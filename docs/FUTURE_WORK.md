# Future work

Ideas for what comes next, after the lore checklist (`LORE_CHECKLIST.md`) was finished. Tick items off as they're done.

## Maps (building on the explorer)

- [ ] **Fog of war per campaign.** Each campaign keeps its own map, where places stay dim or hidden until the party discovers them, and the GM reveals them during a live session. The most Kingdom Come idea of all, and it fits the rule that the Ethereal chart can't be trusted.
- [ ] **Party marker and travel log.** Show where the party is now, with a dotted trail of where they've been, drawn from the session logs.
- [ ] **Region outlines on hover.** Light up a region's borders when its name is hovered, and click to open its codex page.
- [ ] **Player notes on the map.** Players drop their own pins and notes, saved only on their own device.
- [ ] **The same explorer in the GM Atlas,** with drag-to-place pins, so pinning the dominion fortresses or Kaelmar's vaults later takes seconds.

## At the table

- [ ] **Opening adventures for all three starting points.** Equal first-level hooks and a fleshed-out first session each for Hollow Refuge, Ashvale and the Nameless Asylum. Still the biggest content gap.
- [ ] **Random encounter tables** by region, and by depth in the Ethereal Realm, built from the bestiary.
- [ ] **Faction standing tracks.** Renown with each faction in the campaign tools, with what each rank unlocks, as the archive's rules describe.
- [ ] **A handbook that works offline.** Players install it on their phones and use it at the table without a signal.

- [ ] **Mysteries for the newer secrets.** The families', towns', Asylum's and relics' secrets, as clues in the Mysteries tool.
- [ ] **More people** for Kaelholm, the eastern mountains and the Ethereal factions.
