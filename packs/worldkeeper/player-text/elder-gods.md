# The Ones Before

## How to use this

The scraps of legend people tell about whoever made the world. They disagree. That's the point.

## The Ones Before

*Older than the gods: the makers of the world, in the stories nobody quite believes.*

Every people on both continents has a story about who came before the gods, and no two stories agree. Most say there were makers: some say seven, some say one with seven faces, some say they were never more than a story the gods tell to frighten themselves. Most say they made the world, and then went away, or went to sleep, or were sent away. A few say the gods are their children, and don't know it.

Priests of Sephane don't like the question. Scholars of the Consortium collect the stories and argue about them. In Kaelmar, the elders say "the ones before the before," and change the subject. The only people who claim to know more are the Black Sun, who say an eternal eclipse is coming, and that something older than the gods will come out of it.

Children on both continents skip rope to the same rhyme, and nobody knows where it came from:

> *Grey Mother sleeping in the grass,*
> *keep the lost ones as they pass.*
> *Seven made us, three went far,*
> *one will call the falling star.*

**What people say**

- Seven made the world. Or one did, with seven faces. Or none did, and the gods made it all up.
- The gods are the Ones Before's children. That's why priests won't talk about it.
- The Ones Before are asleep, under the world, and must never be woken.
- Three of them were cast out, for teaching people things they shouldn't know.
- They made the world as a prison, and we're what they locked up.
- The Black Sun says the eclipse is the Ones Before coming home. The Black Sun says a lot of things.
- If you say one of their names aloud, something hears you. Nobody knows their names, which is probably for the best.
- The Grey Mother rhyme is older than every kingdom. It's the same, word for word, in Kaelmar.
