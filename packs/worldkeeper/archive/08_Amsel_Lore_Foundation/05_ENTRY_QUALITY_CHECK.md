# Entry Quality Check

Apply to each document before moving it into the canonical Lore collection.

- [ ] Every factual statement is supported by `Aetheris_Setting_Bible.md`, `Kaelmar_Setting_Bible.md`, or an approved owner decision.
- [ ] New ideas are marked PROPOSED and are not silently treated as canon.
- [ ] Secrets, beliefs, rumors, and confirmed facts are distinct.
- [ ] Entry has one stable unique ID; other pages link to it rather than duplicating it.
- [ ] Source section is named (not an invented page number).
- [ ] Any mechanical rules are copied accurately, preserve HOMEBREW / OFFICIAL / OUTSIDE 2024 CORE distinctions, and are not assumed rules-verified without checking primary books.
- [ ] No removed content returns: Aetherfall reset play, five Primary Shards, campaign engine, Kaelmar firearms or modern technology.
- [ ] 300 AB / Year 31 Unbound chronology is respected.
- [ ] Aetheris / Kaelmar / Ethereal Realm relationship is consistent.
- [ ] Player-safe pages do not accidentally reveal GM-only facts.
- [ ] Unknown locations, distances, names or dates remain UNDEFINED.
- [ ] Entry uses a shared template and has references of the form `Name [ID]`.
- [ ] Adventure hooks are references to Tome rather than pre-decided events in Lore.

When completing a batch, record: new files, source headings checked, source details not yet transferred, possible conflicts, and broken references.
