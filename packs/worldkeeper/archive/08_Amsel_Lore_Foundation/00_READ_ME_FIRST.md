# Amsel Lore Foundation: Setup Instructions

**Status:** DOCUMENT DESIGN ONLY. No new world facts are approved or implied by these templates. The original two setting bibles and their owner-approved decisions remain authoritative.

## What this package adds

- 17 reusable Markdown entity templates, each using the nine metadata fields in `Amsel_Suite_Implementation_Guide.md`.
- Five worked examples grounded in the source bibles and existing world documents.
- A page hierarchy, tag and field specification, entry sequence, and manual-entry checklist.
- A quality checklist to check future content against the source bibles.

## How to use in Lore

1. Create **one** world page, with child pages for Aetheris, Kaelmar, and the Ethereal Realm. Use `01_PAGE_HIERARCHY.md`.
2. Verify which features your installed version of Amsel Lore actually supports. The field and tag design below works as text even without custom properties, templates, or automatic links. It is **not** a proprietary import format.
3. Make the 17 entry layouts only where they are supported. Otherwise create one ordinary page per entry and paste metadata at the top.
4. Copy a worked example, check the intended player/GM visibility, and turn `[ID]` references into app links by hand wherever possible.
5. Start with `04_MANUAL_ENTRY_CHECKLIST.md`. Keep the originals in `References/` unchanged.

## Rules for canon and information loss

- CANON: present in Aetheris or Kaelmar source bibles or the documented owner-approved reconciliation.
- BELIEF/RUMOR: in-world account; not objective truth unless explicitly confirmed.
- SECRET: GM only. Keep secret material on restricted pages when player-facing pages may be shared.
- HOMEBREW: preserved rules from the bibles. Do not silently update to official 2024 wording.
- UNDEFINED: a gap, not permission to fill it.
- No adventure outcome becomes canon until play establishes it.
- Do not revive removed Aetherfall resets, five Primary Shards, Kaelmar firearms, or the campaign engine.

## Source priority

1. `References/Aetheris_Setting_Bible.md` and `References/Kaelmar_Setting_Bible.md` with their settled owner decisions.
2. `References/Project_Context_Addendum.md` and `References/Phase_1_Verification_Audit.md` for approved reconciliation and known limitations.
3. Existing world files for derived structure, never as a reason to override the two bibles.

World date: **300 AB = Year 31 Unbound**. Aetheris is east; Kaelmar is west, across the Black Tides. The two share an Ethereal Realm.
