# Ashvale

## Overview
A living town founded in 2 AB beside the Rift [LOC-901], around the thirteen standing stones of the Rite Circle [LOC-301].

## Known locations
Meeting hall, Bloom-Speakers' sanctuary on the west edge, grafted orchard on the east, east gate, west road and ford, and the Rite Circle [LOC-301].

- Bloom-Speakers: Master Speaker Tevan Lin.
