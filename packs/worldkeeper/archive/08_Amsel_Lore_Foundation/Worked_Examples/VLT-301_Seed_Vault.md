# The Seed Vault

## Overview
The deepest of Kaelmar's thirteen vaults, beneath the Rift [LOC-901]. It contains the sleeping Root Core, which the Shrouding's failsafes did not kill.

## The key and what it asks
Blood, glyph and spore: what was made, what grew, and what joins them. Existing key rules require the participating minds to be awake, willing, and honest. No roll opens the key; the established conditions do.

## Consequences
Opening each heart awakens that vault's strand. Opening all thirteen in one shape wakes the whole web and the Root Core. No new rule or numerical DC has been added here.
