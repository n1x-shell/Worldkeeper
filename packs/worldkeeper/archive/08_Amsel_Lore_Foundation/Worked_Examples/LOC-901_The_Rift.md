# The Rift

## Overview
One wound with two open ends, one in the Hollow Marches [REG-001] and one beside Ashvale [SET-301]. The endpoints meet within the Ethereal Realm [CON-501].

## Course
From the Hollow Marches it runs west beneath the Eternal Veil [REG-005], descends to a trench below the Black Tides [LOC-902], continues beneath northern Kaelmar, and opens beside Ashvale.

## Travel
Crossing uses the Ethereal Realm's border layer [ETH-501]. The source guide suggests this route for level 11 and above; that is adventure guidance, not a new hard rule.

## GM-only secret
The Forgotten One [NPC-901] tore the Rift in an earlier life, centuries before Kaelith. Their last crossing was about 270 AB. Keep this off any player-facing version.
