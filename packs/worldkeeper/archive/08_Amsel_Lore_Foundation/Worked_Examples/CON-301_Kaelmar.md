# Kaelmar

## Overview
A post-collapse continent three centuries after the Shrouding, caused when the Kaelith civilisation attempted to end death. The resulting change remains paused. At the default start date of 300 AB the vaults are sealed and the guardians sleep.

## Geography
- North-west: The Rift [LOC-901], Ashvale [SET-301], Seed Vault [VLT-301].
- Centre and east of centre: Bloom country, the under-roads [LOC-303], the memory-house [LOC-302].
- East: Eastern Deepwood [REG-301], eastern mountains [REG-302].
- Far east: Kaelholm [SET-302] and the Black Tides [LOC-902].
- North: cold highlands and ice. South: fens, lakes and rivers.

## Magic
The Bloom [COS-301] and glyph-work [COS-302] are two expressions of a shared grammar. Their existing mechanics remain HOMEBREW where indicated in the bible.

## Chronology
300 AB is the present. The novel's 927 AB is a possible future, not recorded history; see Possible Futures [WLD-908] (GM only).
