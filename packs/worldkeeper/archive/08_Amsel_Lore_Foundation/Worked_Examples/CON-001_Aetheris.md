# Aetheris

## Overview
A shattered high-fantasy continent where five great factions fight over Crystal Shards. The Cycle of Aetherfalls has ended, but shards remain powerful and factions still seek them. No reality reset occurs during play.

## Layout and regions
- Centre: Hollow Marches [REG-001], containing Hollow Refuge [SET-001].
- North: Radiant Ascendancy [REG-002].
- East: Tyrant's Dominion [REG-003].
- North-west: Fractured Nexus [REG-004].
- West: Eternal Veil [REG-005], along the Black Tides [LOC-902].
- South: Verdant Rebirth [REG-006].

## History
The five dominions are realities left side by side after the Cycle ended; see World Cosmology [WLD-903].

## Relationships
- Kaelmar [CON-301] lies beyond the Black Tides [LOC-902].
- Ethereal Realm [CON-501] is accessible through several established thresholds.
