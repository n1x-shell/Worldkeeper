Use one authoritative entry per entity and link from other places. Place NPC entries in the People directory, not under a town page, to avoid duplicates if they travel. Avoid making pages for one-line mentions until needed at the table.

**Important:** The Rift is ONE world landmark with two ends, not two unrelated landmarks. The Ethereal Realm is ONE shared realm, with a border and deep layer. The Loom affects Aetheris, not Kaelmar.

**Player safety:** Do not assume all entries in a parent page share visibility. Separate GM-secret facts into restricted entries or avoid sharing that parent page.
