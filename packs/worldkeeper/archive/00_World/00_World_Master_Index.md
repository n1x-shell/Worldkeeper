## Enter each entry once

The combined world files hold copies of entries that also have their own page. The text is the same. Type the standalone page and skip the copy:

- Cosmology [WLD-903] holds COS-901 to COS-915. Use `00_World/Cosmology_Entries/`.
- Geography [WLD-905] holds REG-901, LOC-901 and LOC-902. Use `00_World/Geography_Entries/`.

`08_Amsel_Lore_Foundation/Worked_Examples/` also repeats five entries as examples. Don't type those.

## Use in Amsel

Create one world. Add Aetheris, Kaelmar and Ethereal Realm as major branches, and keep world-wide entries under The World. Names followed by `[ID]` are manual cross-reference targets. Consult `References/Amsel_Suite_Implementation_Guide.md` for field templates and its unverified-app-capability caveats.
