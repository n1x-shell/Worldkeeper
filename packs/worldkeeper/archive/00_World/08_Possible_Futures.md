# Possible Futures

## GM-only future status

The events of the novel *Persistence Through Ruin* take place in **927 AB**, more than six centuries after the default campaign beginning in 300 AB. The source bible explicitly identifies them as **one possible future, not established history**. None of its living cast has yet been born at the campaign start, except the sleepers that already exist.

## Possible sequence, not predetermined

The source bible describes a possible future in which the Seventh Ward wakes, the web rings and Ashvale turns to glass. The memory-house and seers may glimpse such events. The setting does not require them to happen.

## Branches held open by the setting

Kaelmar's unfinished change might be guided, freely chosen, imposed from above, left asleep, or resolved in an unforeseen way. In Aetheris, someone might attempt to relink the Crystal Shards to the Loom and restart the Cycle. These are risks and choices, not scheduled outcomes.

## Do not convert into past-tense canon

- Do not place 927 AB events on World Timeline [WLD-904] as completed events.
- Do not import novel characters as living 300 AB NPCs unless the setting bible specifically describes them as present sleepers.
- Do not use future knowledge to overwrite NPC goals or faction conditions in 300 AB.
