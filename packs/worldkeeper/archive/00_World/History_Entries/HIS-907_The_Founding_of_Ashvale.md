# The Founding of Ashvale

## When

2 AB.

## Established event

Refugees led by a woman with burned hands [NPC-303] settled at the stone ring, the Rite Circle [LOC-301].

- She taught the children a four-line keeping song and wrote the rite.
- Ashvale [SET-301] was founded as a living lock. Everyone who sleeps there is "a hand on the key".

## Secrets [SECRET]

- The founder is probably Arborhold's [VLT-303] last vault-keeper.
- Her sealed warning is held in Ashvale. See the founder [NPC-303].
