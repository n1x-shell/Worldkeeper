# The Raising of the Thirteen Stones

## When

Before Kaelith.

## Established event

- **The stones.** Unknown hands raised thirteen leaning standing stones over a sleeping thing at the Rift [LOC-901].
- **Older than Kaelith.** The pattern behind all later glyph-work is older than Kaelith, which "only learned to read it".
- **What they became.** The stones became the Rite Circle [LOC-301] of Ashvale. No frost falls inside it, and it is a lock, not an altar.

## Secret [SECRET]

The sleeping thing is the Root Core [NPC-302], under the Seed Vault [VLT-301].

## Open questions [UNDEFINED]

Who raised the stones, and when.
