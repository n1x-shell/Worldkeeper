# The First Regular Crossings of the Black Tides

## Established event [SECRET where marked]

**When:** By 300 AB / Year 31 Unbound.

Regular ships from Aetheris have only recently begun reaching the Kaelmar east coast and Kaelholm [SET-302]. Voyages between Kaelholm and the unnamed Eternal Veil ports take about two weeks. Sailors extinguish lanterns over the Rift trench.
