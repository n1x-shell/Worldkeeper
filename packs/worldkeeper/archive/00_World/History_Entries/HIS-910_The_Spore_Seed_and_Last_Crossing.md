# The Spore-Seed and Last Crossing

## Established event [SECRET where marked]

**When:** About 270 AB.

The Forgotten One [NPC-901] crossed the Rift to Kaelmar for the last time and planted a spore-seed in a communion pool. It waits for a particular blood not yet born. The Forgotten One then returned to Aetheris and severed the shards from the Loom, ending the Cycle [COS-907].

## Limit of the record

The location of the communion pool and identity of the destined future person are UNDEFINED.
