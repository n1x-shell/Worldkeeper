# The Warding

## When

The Kaelith era, late.

## Established event

- **The volunteers.** Thirteen volunteers were lifted out of their bodies at Arborhold [VLT-303]. Twelve "scattered".
- **The Seventh Ward.** The seventh held, and became the guardian called the Seventh Ward [NPC-301].
- **The divided mind.** That mind was later split across many guardian bodies, on purpose, so that no single road could be forced on the world. [SECRET]
- **What came of the twelve.** The twelve who scattered are among Kaelmar's undead and spirits.
