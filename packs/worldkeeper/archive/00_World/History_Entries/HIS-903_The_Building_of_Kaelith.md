# The Building of Kaelith

## When

The Kaelith era.

## Established event

**What Kaelith built.**

- Cities and rune-gated roads.
- Thirteen vaults, each holding one strand of the work.
- The under-roads [LOC-303] joining the vaults.
- A glyph web written in the First Grammar [COS-302].

Its Great Work was Persistence. See Kaelith's Great Work and the Shrouding [COS-912].

**Who built it.** Kaelith was a civilisation of many peoples. Dwarves were its masons, and they cut the vaults from everstone.
