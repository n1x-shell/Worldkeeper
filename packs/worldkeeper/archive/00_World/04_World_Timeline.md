# World Timeline

## Chronology policy

Kaelmar uses years After Bloom (AB). Aetheris uses years Unbound. **300 AB = Year 31 Unbound**; for conversion, Unbound year = AB − 269. Dates without a source-supported number are left relative. Aetheris's pre-Unbound Cycle events cannot be forced into Kaelmar's dated sequence without inventing dates.
