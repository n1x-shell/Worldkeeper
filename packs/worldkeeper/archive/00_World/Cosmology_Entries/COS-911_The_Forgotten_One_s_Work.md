## Established lore

One mortal of Aetheris learned the truth, was erased, returned without memory, and learned it again, over many lives. At last they tore a piece from their own Thread and made the thirteenth shard [SHD-013]. It cost them their name and their place in history.

What they did, in order:

1. **Tore the Rift.** In an earlier life, centuries before Kaelith, they tore open the Rift [LOC-901] and crossed it. They crossed it many times over many lives. (A: Phase 1 audit decisions; K: History)
2. **Planted the spore-seed.** On the last crossing, about 270 AB, they planted a spore-seed in a communion pool in Kaelmar. It waits for one particular blood that has not been born yet. (K: Magic and technology)
3. **Broke the Cycle.** They came back and cut the shards from the Loom. (COS-907)
4. **Were forgotten.** Nobody remembers any of it. The twelfth shard has been missing ever since, and no record names it.

## Present conditions

They live unremembered in the Empty House [LOC-001] at Hollow Refuge, with the thirteenth shard. (A: The Hollow Marches)
