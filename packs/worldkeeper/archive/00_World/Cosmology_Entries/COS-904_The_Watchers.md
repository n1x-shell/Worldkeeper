## Established lore

- **What they are.** Something beyond the Ethereal Realm that perceives whole histories instead of individuals.
- **Where they are.** Somewhere past the Black Horizon [ETH-507].
- **What they did.** The first civilisation [COS-901] drew their attention, and almost nothing survived.
- **What the Hidden Ones do.** They hide the world from the Watchers and feed them discarded histories as tribute. The cards' line that the Hidden Ones "serve something greater that watches and does not yet interfere" is the myth version of that tribute. [RUMOR] (A: Phase 1 audit decisions)
- **In Kaelmar.** The Watchers are the "others" who dream in the between. (A: Place in the world, Bridges)

## Present conditions

Aetheris Unbound has just become permanent, and "something notices". Nothing hides either continent from the Watchers any more. (A: Place in the world, Shared history)

## Game use

- A Watcher's Fragment is a glance from a Watcher, not the Watcher itself. It is built from a Death Tyrant. [OFFICIAL baseline] (A: 5.5e adaptation)
