## Established lore

Both continents worship the same gods. The gods are real and grant spells.

Each Aetherfall rewrote the gods along with everything else, so no god remembers the earlier worlds, and no priest can answer questions about the Cycle. [SECRET: why the gods can't answer]

Three gods are dead, and their remains are places in the cards:

- **Maruun, the Sundered Tide** [DEI-907]. The sea deity. She fell at the Titan's Fall [LOC-005].
- **Aurex, the Throne-Breaker** [DEI-908]. He tried to sit the Shattered Throne [LOC-012] and failed.
- **The Nameless Smith** [DEI-909]. Maker of the gods' weapons at the Nameless Forge [LOC-011]. Its name was the first thing ever erased.
