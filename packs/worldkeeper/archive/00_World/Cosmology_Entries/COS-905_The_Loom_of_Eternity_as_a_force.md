## Established lore

- **What it is.** A living web of silver threads. Each thread is a soul, a destiny or a moment. Vyrithar, the Weaver of Veils [NPC-002], keeps it. It lies beyond mortal reach, and some say beyond even Vyrithar's. The two Loom cards are merged here. [RUMOR: the "beyond even Vyrithar's" part]
- **What it does.** The reforged Crystal tells the Loom which pattern to impose on the world. Rewriting fate is possible there. The Unraveled King tried it and was unmade.
- **The unwritten thread.** One thread connects to nothing. It is an unwritten life that should not exist. [SECRET]
- **Since the Cycle broke.** The shards are cut from the Loom. Anyone who relinks them could restart the Cycle. (A: Cosmology and history; A: Adventure hooks)

## Relationships

- **As a place:** The Loom of Eternity [ETH-505], in the deep realm. Twelve colossal crystals pierce it, and one of them is dark. (A: Phase 1 audit decisions)
- **Keepers:** Vyrithar [NPC-002] keeps it. His brother and rival, Vyrathus [NPC-003], keeps the Crucible of Renewal [LOC-010].
