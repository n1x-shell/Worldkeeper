## Established lore

- **The mark.** Anyone who touches any shard is marked for good: a scar, a glowing vein, a recurring dream, or nothing visible at all.
- **What it does.** A Shardscarred creature resists the rewriting of reality.
- **How factions treat them.** The Order may monitor them, the Legion recruit them, the Consortium study them and the Pact hide or use them.
- **Aether Scars.** People call the leftover contradictions in ruins and records Aether Scars.

## Rule [HOMEBREW]

A Shardscarred creature has Advantage on saves against effects that alter memory or rewrite reality. (A: 5.5e adaptation)
