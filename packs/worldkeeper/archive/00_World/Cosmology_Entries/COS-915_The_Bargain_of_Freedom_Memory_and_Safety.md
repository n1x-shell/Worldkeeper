## Established lore

The Hidden Ones say Aetheris can be free, remembered or safe, and only two at once. Whether that is true is left to the GM.

Kaelmar's glass offers safety and harmony at the price of names. That is the same bargain from the other shore.

## Campaign use

This bargain underlies the endgame choices on both continents:

- whether to relink the shards in Aetheris
- how Kaelmar's change should finish: guided, free, held from the top, left asleep, or something nobody has proposed (K: Adventure hooks)
