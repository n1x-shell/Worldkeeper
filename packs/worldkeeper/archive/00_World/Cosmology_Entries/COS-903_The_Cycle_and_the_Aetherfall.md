## Established lore

**What people saw.** Again and again a faction gathered the shards and reforged the Crystal. The world then restarted as that faction's reality, in an event called an Aetherfall. (A: Overview; A: Cosmology and history)

**How it worked.** [SECRET]

- The reforged Crystal only tells the Loom of Eternity [COS-905] which pattern to impose. Then it shatters again, so no faction is allowed a permanent victory.
- Whenever Aetheris grows stable, people divide around five answers: order, conquest, knowledge, secrecy and nature. The shards surface, wars begin, someone reforges the Crystal, and history starts over.
- The Cycle is a prison, a quarantine and a tribute. It keeps the world from growing, hides it from the Watchers [COS-904], and feeds them the discarded histories.
- The Hidden Ones [FAC-007] guided each turn. Their weapon is forgetting: they redirect shards, suppress discoveries and erase people. They are architects, custodians and jailers, not gods.

**Its end.** The Forgotten One [NPC-901] broke the Cycle by cutting the shards from the Loom. This is Aetheris Unbound [COS-907].

## Present conditions

There are no resets in play. (A: Overview) The books' Cyclebound Return, the reset loop and Souls-like death are removed. (World Canon Rules [WLD-902])
