## Established lore

**One realm, two shores.** Both continents describe a place behind the world where lost things linger and bodies can be carried. Aetheris reaches it through the Hollow Verge. Kaelmar calls it the between: the place beneath the world where minds touch by harmony instead of nearness.

**Two layers.**

- **The border layer** [ETH-501]: the Hollow Verge, the between, the Rift crossing and the root-roads. Parties reach it from level 5 by root-road, and from about level 11 by the Verge or the Rift.
- **The deep realm**: the Fields of Lost Realities [ETH-502], the Ossuary of Kings [ETH-503], the Silent Archive [ETH-504], the Loom [ETH-505], the Citadel of the Hidden [ETH-506] and the Black Horizon [ETH-507]. Reaching it needs the thirteenth shard, or an invitation from someone who lives there. Uninvited, it suits levels 17 to 20.

**Not the default D&D Ethereal Plane.** [HOMEBREW] The Impossible Door opens into the "Border Ethereal", which is this realm's border layer. (A: Shards and relics, Treasures written for 5.5e)

**Different understandings on each shore.** [BELIEF]

- In Aetheris it is where those who slipped the Cycle gathered, and where the Hidden Ones keep their Citadel.
- In Kaelmar it is where guardians dream, roots open roads, and travellers are pulled "into many" unless anchored.
