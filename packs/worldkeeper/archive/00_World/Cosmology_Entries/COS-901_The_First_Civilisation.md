## Established lore

Before any Aetherfall, Aetheris had a civilisation that lasted for ages. It conquered disease, then ageing, then death. It found the Ethereal Realm [CON-501], and then what lies beyond it.

That drew the attention of the Watchers [COS-904], and almost nothing survived.

## Relationships

- **Kaelith** [COS-912] made the same attempt on the other continent much later, and caused the Shrouding. Both lands tried to conquer death. (A: Place in the world, Shared history)
- **The Shattering** [COS-902] was the answer to what this civilisation drew down.
