## Established lore

About 270 AB, the Forgotten One [NPC-901] cut the shards from the Loom [COS-905], and the Cycle ended. This happened after their last crossing of the Rift [LOC-901]. Nobody remembers them for it. [SECRET: who did it]

Aetheris Unbound is one of the GM Lorebook's own endings, used here as the starting state. (A: Overview)
