## Established lore

**The cards' myth.** The Elders of the Ethereal Throne broke the Ethereal Crystal into twelve shards and set the rules of the Aetherfall, so the world would grow through conflict. A whisper says something greater forced them. [RUMOR]

**The truth.** [SECRET]

- The Elders were the first of the Hidden Ones [FAC-007].
- The Shattering was a defence against the Watchers [COS-904].
- They built the Aetherfall [COS-903] around the twelve shards, so that no civilisation would ever again last long enough to be seen.
