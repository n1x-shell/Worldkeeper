## Established lore

Before any Aetherfall, Aetheris had a civilisation that lasted for ages. It conquered disease, then ageing, then death. It found the Ethereal Realm [CON-501], and then what lies beyond it.

That drew the attention of the Watchers [COS-904], and almost nothing survived.

## Relationships

- **Kaelith** [COS-912] made the same attempt on the other continent much later, and caused the Shrouding. Both lands tried to conquer death. (A: Place in the world, Shared history)
- **The Shattering** [COS-902] was the answer to what this civilisation drew down.

## Established lore

**The cards' myth.** The Elders of the Ethereal Throne broke the Ethereal Crystal into twelve shards and set the rules of the Aetherfall, so the world would grow through conflict. A whisper says something greater forced them. [RUMOR]

**The truth.** [SECRET]

- The Elders were the first of the Hidden Ones [FAC-007].
- The Shattering was a defence against the Watchers [COS-904].
- They built the Aetherfall [COS-903] around the twelve shards, so that no civilisation would ever again last long enough to be seen.

## Established lore

**What people saw.** Again and again a faction gathered the shards and reforged the Crystal. The world then restarted as that faction's reality, in an event called an Aetherfall. (A: Overview; A: Cosmology and history)

**How it worked.** [SECRET]

- The reforged Crystal only tells the Loom of Eternity [COS-905] which pattern to impose. Then it shatters again, so no faction is allowed a permanent victory.
- Whenever Aetheris grows stable, people divide around five answers: order, conquest, knowledge, secrecy and nature. The shards surface, wars begin, someone reforges the Crystal, and history starts over.
- The Cycle is a prison, a quarantine and a tribute. It keeps the world from growing, hides it from the Watchers [COS-904], and feeds them the discarded histories.
- The Hidden Ones [FAC-007] guided each turn. Their weapon is forgetting: they redirect shards, suppress discoveries and erase people. They are architects, custodians and jailers, not gods.

**Its end.** The Forgotten One [NPC-901] broke the Cycle by cutting the shards from the Loom. This is Aetheris Unbound [COS-907].

## Present conditions

There are no resets in play. (A: Overview) The books' Cyclebound Return, the reset loop and Souls-like death are removed. (World Canon Rules [WLD-902])

## Established lore

- **What it is.** A living web of silver threads. Each thread is a soul, a destiny or a moment. Vyrithar, the Weaver of Veils [NPC-002], keeps it. It lies beyond mortal reach, and some say beyond even Vyrithar's. The two Loom cards are merged here. [RUMOR: the "beyond even Vyrithar's" part]
- **What it does.** The reforged Crystal tells the Loom which pattern to impose on the world. Rewriting fate is possible there. The Unraveled King tried it and was unmade.
- **The unwritten thread.** One thread connects to nothing. It is an unwritten life that should not exist. [SECRET]
- **Since the Cycle broke.** The shards are cut from the Loom. Anyone who relinks them could restart the Cycle. (A: Cosmology and history; A: Adventure hooks)

## Relationships

- **As a place:** The Loom of Eternity [ETH-505], in the deep realm. Twelve colossal crystals pierce it, and one of them is dark. (A: Phase 1 audit decisions)
- **Keepers:** Vyrithar [NPC-002] keeps it. His brother and rival, Vyrathus [NPC-003], keeps the Crucible of Renewal [LOC-010].

## Note

Kaelmar was never covered by the Loom. (A: Place in the world)

## Established lore

- **The mark.** Anyone who touches any shard is marked for good: a scar, a glowing vein, a recurring dream, or nothing visible at all.
- **What it does.** A Shardscarred creature resists the rewriting of reality.
- **How factions treat them.** The Order may monitor them, the Legion recruit them, the Consortium study them and the Pact hide or use them.

A Shardscarred creature has Advantage on saves against effects that alter memory or rewrite reality. (A: 5.5e adaptation)

## Established lore

One mortal of Aetheris learned the truth, was erased, returned without memory, and learned it again, over many lives. At last they tore a piece from their own Thread and made the thirteenth shard [SHD-013]. It cost them their name and their place in history.

What they did, in order:

1. **Tore the Rift.** In an earlier life, centuries before Kaelith, they tore open the Rift [LOC-901] and crossed it. They crossed it many times over many lives. (A: Phase 1 audit decisions; K: History)
2. **Planted the spore-seed.** On the last crossing, about 270 AB, they planted a spore-seed in a communion pool in Kaelmar. It waits for one particular blood that has not been born yet. (K: Magic and technology)
3. **Broke the Cycle.** They came back and cut the shards from the Loom. (COS-907)
4. **Were forgotten.** Nobody remembers any of it. The twelfth shard has been missing ever since, and no record names it.

## Present conditions

They live unremembered in the Empty House [LOC-001] at Hollow Refuge, with the thirteenth shard. (A: The Hollow Marches)

## Established lore

**The attempt.** The Kaelith civilisation tried to end death by lifting minds out of bodies and pouring them into new ones. Its Great Work was called Persistence. It worked too well.

**The breaking.** It started in the Seed Vault [VLT-301] and ran the web in seconds. Minds slipped their vessels, and matter started answering will. Failsafes written into the Original Directive paused the change half-finished.

**The Shrouding.** Spellwork, flesh and forest grew into each other. The sky came down on the cities for years, and the rain carried ash. Then the Bloom [COS-301] grew up through the wreck, and the calendar restarted as After Bloom (AB).

**What it was not.** The Shrouding was not an invasion or a judgment. Kaelith caused it, and its own failsafes froze it.

## Present conditions (300 AB)

The freeze still holds. The change sleeps, and almost nobody alive knows it can wake.

## Relationships

- Aetheris's first civilisation [COS-901] tried the same thing and drew the Watchers. (A: Place in the world, Shared history)
- "Balance is enforced" by the Hidden Ones, and Kaelith's failsafes paused the change: both lands are held unfinished by a hidden design. (A: Place in the world, Bridges)

## Established lore

Kaelmar has two magics that turn out to be one grammar: Kaelith glyph-work [COS-302] and the living Bloom [COS-301]. Both run on call and answer, and both charge the user in blood, memory or self.

- **The pattern is older than Kaelith.** Unknown hands raised thirteen standing stones over a sleeping thing at the Rift, and Kaelith "only learned to read it". (K: History)
- **It is carried in blood.** The makers planted splinters of the double grammar in bloodlines and let generations bend them. That is how a glyph can grow on an arm nobody cut. Tieflings in Kaelmar descend from the glyph-carved. (K: Peoples and creatures)
- **Keys are made of people.** The failsafes included thirteen keys. A key is a shape of joined minds, not an object, and the web recognises it on contact. (K: Magic and technology)

## Established lore

**One realm, two shores.** Both continents describe a place behind the world where lost things linger and bodies can be carried. Aetheris reaches it through the Hollow Verge. Kaelmar calls it the between: the place beneath the world where minds touch by harmony instead of nearness.

**Two layers.**

- **The border layer** [ETH-501]: the Hollow Verge, the between, the Rift crossing and the root-roads. Parties reach it from level 5 by root-road, and from about level 11 by the Verge or the Rift.
- **The deep realm**: the Fields of Lost Realities [ETH-502], the Ossuary of Kings [ETH-503], the Silent Archive [ETH-504], the Loom [ETH-505], the Citadel of the Hidden [ETH-506] and the Black Horizon [ETH-507]. Reaching it needs the thirteenth shard, or an invitation from someone who lives there. Uninvited, it suits levels 17 to 20.

**Not the default D&D Ethereal Plane.** [HOMEBREW] The Impossible Door opens into the "Border Ethereal", which is this realm's border layer. (A: Shards and relics, Treasures written for 5.5e)

**Different understandings on each shore.** [BELIEF]

- In Aetheris it is where those who slipped the Cycle gathered, and where the Hidden Ones keep their Citadel.
- In Kaelmar it is where guardians dream, roots open roads, and travellers are pulled "into many" unless anchored.

## Rules [HOMEBREW]

- Death in the deep realm is permanent, and the dead are Unmade.
- Unmaking is Thinning taken to 6 in one step. See SYS-907.
