## Overview

From east to west, the world runs:

1. Aetheris [CON-001]
2. The Eternal Veil's dusk coast [REG-005]
3. The Black Tides [LOC-902], a sunless sea
4. Kaelmar's east coast, with the port of Kaelholm [SET-302]
5. Kaelmar [CON-301]

The Rift [LOC-901] runs beneath all of it, from the centre of Aetheris to the north-west of Kaelmar.

## Overview

One wound with two open ends, one on each continent. Only the ends are open, and they meet in the Ethereal Realm.

## Its course, east to west

1. **The eastern end.** A chasm in the Hollow Marches [REG-001] of Aetheris.
2. **Under the Eternal Veil.** It runs west under the Eternal Veil's coast [REG-005].
3. **Under the sea.** It drops into a trench beneath the Black Tides [LOC-902]. That trench is why the sea is sunless.
4. **Under northern Kaelmar.** It rises in Kaelmar as a buried fault, and the under-roads [LOC-303] cross it.
5. **The western end.** It opens again as a chasm beside Ashvale [SET-301].

## The eastern end, in the Hollow Marches

- **What travellers see.** A bottomless chasm humming with strange energy. Parts of it brush the edge of the Ethereal Realm. (A: Geography; A: The Hollow Marches)
- **Getting there.** One day from Blackveil Fortress, or half a day from the Sunken Archive or Wither's Hollow. Every route there is at danger 4. (A: The Hollow Marches, Routes)
- **Site.** Difficulty 14. Its ready adventure is "Where the Road Falls Up".
- **Who watches it.** Mother Thorn, a Wildborn elder, fears "what the land remembers beneath the Rift". (A: Notable NPCs)

## The western end, at Ashvale

- **What travellers see.** A crack in the world that runs out of sight both ways and breathes warm air. Glowing fungus and watching mold line its lip. (K: Geography; novel Prologue)
- **What is near it.** Ashvale sits on its lip. The Rite Circle [LOC-301] stands at the stone ring, and the Seed Vault [VLT-301] lies under its eastern lip.
- **Its riders.** The Convergence Collective's first Riftwalker is Maro Grane. (K: Factions and powers)

## History

- **Who tore it.** The Forgotten One [NPC-901], in an earlier life, centuries before Kaelith. They crossed it many times, and the last crossing was about 270 AB. See HIS-902 and HIS-910. [SECRET]
- **What Aetheris says.** The Rift is "said to be tied to the Aetherfall's origin". [RUMOR]

## Travel [HOMEBREW]

- Go in at one open end, cross the Ethereal Realm's border layer [ETH-501], and come out at the other.
- Suits level 11 and up. (A: Adventures anywhere, Ways to cross)

## Campaign use

- Cross the Rift through the between and come out in the Hollow Marches. (K: Adventure hooks, levels 17 to 20)
- A ship from Aetheris brings a Consortium surveyor with a map of the Rift. (K: Adventure hooks)

## Map

There are two pins, one at each open end. A dashed line on the world map marks the buried and undersea course. No coordinates are given.

## Overview

A sunless sea between Aetheris and Kaelmar.

- **On the Aetheris side** it is one of the Eternal Veil's sites. (A: Geography)
- **On the Kaelmar side** it lies beyond Kaelholm. (K: Geography)

## Established lore

- **Why it is dark.** The Rift's trench runs beneath it, which is why it is sunless.
- **The crossing.** Ships run between Kaelholm [SET-302] and the ports of the Eternal Veil. The crossing takes about two weeks in the dark, and the crew lights no lanterns over the trench.
- **What people believe.** In Aetheris, some believe it leads to another world. [BELIEF, and true]
- **Tidecant.** Sailors mix Common and Kaelish into a pidgin called Tidecant. Smugglers move people across it. (A: Everyday life; A: Adventures anywhere)

## Present conditions

The first regular ships have only just begun to cross. See HIS-912.
