# World Canon Rules

These rules decide what counts as true in this world, and how every entry in the library is labelled.

## 1. Where canon comes from

1. **The two bibles are the source.** The Aetheris bible and the Kaelmar bible say what is true. Each entry cites them by heading, as "A: heading" or "K: heading".
2. **The older sources sit behind the bibles.**
   - Aetheris was built from AI Dungeon story cards and three ICRPG books. Where the two disagree, the cards win and the books fill the gaps. (A: Sources and canon)
   - Kaelmar was built from the novel *Persistence Through Ruin* (Draft 6). (K: Overview)

   These older sources appear only as secondary citations.
3. **Owner decisions are final.** Every decision in the bibles' "Settled", "Also settled", "Review" and "Phase 1 audit decisions" lists is canon. None of them is reopened. They are listed in full in Resolved_By_Owner.md (Phase 8).
4. **Approved "New" material is canon.** Anything marked "New" was written to carry out an owner decision. It was approved on 10 November 2026, including the "Adventures anywhere" list. (A: Open questions, Review; K: Open questions, Review status)

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| CANON | Stated in a bible, including approved "New" material. |

## 5. Voice and style

The Master Rewrite Protocol (§9.10 of the novel's revision bible) governs writing in this library. (K: Overview, Voice and style)

- **Banned words, everywhere.** Delve, tapestry, testament, navigate, weave, echo (as a verb or a metaphor), foster, palpable, visceral, nuance.
- **Names from the sources keep their original form,** even when they contain a banned word. Examples: the Echoborn, the Rift of Echoes, Vyrithar, the Weaver of Veils, the Veilweaver, the Archive Delver background.
- **The full protocol applies to in-world prose.** That means quoted documents, read-aloud text and dialogue:
  - no project-management or clinical jargon
  - no stacked adjectives in front of a noun
  - varied cadence
  - no neat summarizing lines
  - em dashes only for interruptions and self-corrections
  - gritty, specific sensory detail
  - no therapy-speak
  - messy dialogue with contractions, except in the Seventh Ward's formal register
- **Kaelith institutional documents** may use flat bureaucratic euphemism, such as "scattering" for death. They may not use data-speak such as "parameters".
- **Reference text** is plain, with no filler.

## 6. Rules for the library

- **One entry per thing.** When two documents mention the same person, place or event, one entry holds the detail and the others link to it.
- **Facts are never invented to fill a template.** A missing fact is marked UNDEFINED.
- **Adventure material belongs in Tome.** It lives in 06_Campaign and links back to Lore entries.
