## Established contacts

- Ships from Aetheris have begun arriving at Kaelholm [SET-302]. The named Eternal Veil ports remain UNDEFINED.
- Sailors use Tidecant, a trade pidgin mixing Common and Kaelish. Smugglers cross the Black Tides.
- The Arcane Consortium would cross for an intact Kaelith vault; the Wildborn Clans regard the Bloom as close to the world they seek. These are established faction attitudes, not proof that either has already acted.
- Aetheris's Celestial Order would call the glass heresy and treat Kaelmar's Ascendancy as a rival. This is a source-established expected attitude, not a declaration of current war.
- Kaelmar's settlements know Aetheris chiefly through rumors about warring crystal-kings [BELIEF].

## Shared institutions and daily life

Both continents recognize the shared pantheon [COS-909], a 363-day calendar [SYS-901] and standard D&D coinage [SYS-902]. Common and Kaelish are cousin languages [SYS-903], and their mutual intelligibility can be treated as one language at the table. Distinct local systems and cultures remain intact.

## Shared secret history [GM ONLY]

- Both continents attempted to conquer death: Aetheris's first civilisation drew the Watchers; Kaelith brought about the Shrouding.
- The Forgotten One [NPC-901] created the Rift connection and left a spore-seed in Kaelmar. Its intended recipient is not yet born.
- The Watchers [COS-904] are the 'others' mentioned in Kaelmar's accounts of the between.
- Aetheris's Loom did not control Kaelmar. Kaelmar's history was never reset.
- Thinning and Unmaking share a setting rule: Unmaking takes Thinning to six in one step [SYS-907]. [HOMEBREW]

## Still undefined

The relation between Kaelmar's glass and the thirteenth shard [SHD-013] is left undecided. Permanent diplomatic missions, treaties, port names, tariffs and trading goods have not been established. Do not insert them as canon.
