# The World

## Overview

This is one material world with two continents: Aetheris [CON-001] in the east and Kaelmar [CON-301] in the west. The Black Tides [LOC-902], a sunless sea, separate them. Both shores touch the same Ethereal Realm [CON-501], called *the between* in Kaelmar. The Rift [LOC-901] is one wound whose two open ends meet through that realm.

## Date at campaign start

The shared present is **300 After Bloom (AB)** in Kaelmar, **Year 31 Unbound** in Aetheris. The calendar has 363 days: twelve 30-day months and three festival days outside the months. The gods, coinage and much of the language base are shared. See Calendar [SYS-901], Money [SYS-902], Languages [SYS-903] and The Gods [COS-909].

## Aetheris [CON-001]

The broken Cycle of Aetherfalls once let a faction reforge the Ethereal Crystal and impose its vision through the Loom. The Forgotten One cut the shards from that Loom. Now five dominions stand side by side, and the twelve shards remain powerful but cannot rewrite existence while severed. The twelfth is lost; a thirteenth shard belongs to the Forgotten One. The Hollow Marches [REG-001] and Hollow Refuge [SET-001] are the default start.

## Kaelmar [CON-301]

Three hundred years ago, the Kaelith civilisation tried to overcome death through Persistence. Its experiment failed in the Shrouding. The Bloom and Kaelith glyph-work are two expressions of one grammar. The change has been paused, not undone; the thirteen vaults are sealed, and the Seventh Ward and Root Core sleep. Ashvale [SET-301] is the default start.

## How the continents meet

The Black Tides crossing between Kaelholm [SET-302] and ports of the Eternal Veil [REG-005] takes about two weeks; regular ships have only recently begun to cross. The Rift also connects the continents through the Ethereal Realm's border layer. Root-roads in Kaelmar provide another approach to the border layer.

## Shared threat [GM ONLY, SECRET]

The Watchers [COS-904] lie beyond the Black Horizon. Aetheris's Cycle hid the world and fed the Watchers discarded histories. Since the Cycle ended, nothing hides either continent from them. The Forgotten One [NPC-901] tore the Rift, planted the spore-seed in Kaelmar, then broke the Cycle.

## Established limits

Aetherfall resets do not happen in play. Kaelmar was never subject to the Loom. The events of *Persistence Through Ruin* at 927 AB are one possible future, not established history. The cause of the relationship, if any, between Kaelmar's glass and the thirteenth shard remains UNDEFINED.
