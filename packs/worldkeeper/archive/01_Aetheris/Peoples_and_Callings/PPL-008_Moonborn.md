# Moonborn

## Established lineage

Moonborn appear in Aetheris character cards as Wildborn druids who move between the physical and spirit worlds. Faelyn Moondancer is described as the last of the Moonborn.

## Rules mapping [OUTSIDE 2024 CORE where applicable]

The source proposes representing a Moonborn with the Elf species and the Fey Touched feat. This is a character-build interpretation from the setting bible, **not** a distinct official species or a verified 2024 legal character build.
