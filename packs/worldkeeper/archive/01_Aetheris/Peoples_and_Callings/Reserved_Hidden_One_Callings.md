# Reserved Callings of the Hidden Ones

## Non-player callings
These are reserved for servants of the Hidden Ones and are **not playable**.

- **Echo Revenant (The Remnant of Forgotten Eras):** A leftover of an erased reality, neither alive nor dead.
- **Arbiter (The Final Judge):** Removes those who defy the Cycle by unmaking them.
- **Chronomancer (The Weaver of Time):** Adjusts past and future to keep history on course.
- **Veilweaver (The Keeper of Forgotten Truths):** Erases memories and rewrites events unseen.
