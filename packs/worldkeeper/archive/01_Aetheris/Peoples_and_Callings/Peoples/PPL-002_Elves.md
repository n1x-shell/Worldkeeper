# Elves

## Established identity
Ancient, attuned to magic and nature.

**Strengths and flaws as described by the cards:** Graceful casters and archers. Proud and distant.

## Culture and names from the source
Scholarship, artistry and long memory. Found in Consortium academies, Wildborn enclaves and ruin communities.

**Common names:** Avel, Iria, Leth, Nael, Oris, Sera, Therin, Vale
