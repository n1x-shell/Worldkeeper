# Reptilians

## Established identity
Scaled hunters of swamp, desert and jungle.

**Strengths and flaws as described by the cards:** Tough ambush predators. Cold and hard to read. Also called lizardfolk.

## Culture and names from the source
Observation, patience, memory and ritual.

**Common names:** Issik, Karra, Reth, Saal, Serek, Tass, Vesh, Zikka

## D&D adaptation [SOURCE-PROVIDED; OPTIONAL RULES WHERE NOT CORE]
