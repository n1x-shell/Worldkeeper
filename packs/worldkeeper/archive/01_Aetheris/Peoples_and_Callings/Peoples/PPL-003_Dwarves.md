# Dwarves

## Established identity
Stoneborn mountain folk.

**Strengths and flaws as described by the cards:** Enduring warriors and master crafters. Stubborn.

## Culture and names from the source
Engineering, stonecraft and record-keeping. Their records do not always agree with the world.

**Common names:** Brom, Dara, Harn, Kella, Orra, Vara, Venn, Yorr

## D&D adaptation [SOURCE-PROVIDED; OPTIONAL RULES WHERE NOT CORE]
