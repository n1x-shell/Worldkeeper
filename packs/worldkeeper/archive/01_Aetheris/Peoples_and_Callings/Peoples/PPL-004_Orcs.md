# Orcs

## Established identity
Warborn.

**Strengths and flaws as described by the cards:** Unmatched strength and battle rage. Value loyalty and honour among their own. Impatient.

## Culture and names from the source
Military clans, merchant houses and monastic communities. Many emphatically do not belong in the Legion.

**Common names:** Drak, Hessa, Kor, Maraak, Ruun, Torga, Varek, Zhar

## D&D adaptation [SOURCE-PROVIDED; OPTIONAL RULES WHERE NOT CORE]
