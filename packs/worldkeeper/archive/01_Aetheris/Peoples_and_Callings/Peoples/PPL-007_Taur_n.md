# Taur'n

## Established identity
Horned nomads seven to nine feet tall, with rune-marked horns.

**Strengths and flaws as described by the cards:** Warriors and storm-calling shamans. Honoured by the Wildborn, feared in the Legion.

## Culture and names from the source
Migration, family oaths, craftsmanship and spiritual memory. Many live in cities. Assuming they are all Wildborn annoys them.

**Common names:** Arrok, Bera, Dorran, Kala, Mhor, Sava, Torren, Yara

## D&D adaptation [SOURCE-PROVIDED; OPTIONAL RULES WHERE NOT CORE]
