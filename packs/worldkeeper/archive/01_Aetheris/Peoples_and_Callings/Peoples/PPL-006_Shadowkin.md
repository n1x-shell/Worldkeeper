# Shadowkin

## Established identity
Born from an erased past, living between light and dark.

**Strengths and flaws as described by the cards:** Masters of stealth. Distrusted everywhere.

## Culture and names from the source
Hidden identity, chosen family, performance. Some families say their ancestors came from places that no longer exist.

**Common names:** Ash, Eris, Kell, Nira, Orren, Seth, Vey, Zara
