# The Nine Types

## Source-defined options

The books replace the card callings with nine Types. Each picks one of three starting abilities, then grows through Milestones and Mastery rewards.

| Type | Card calling | Three starting abilities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Iron Vanguard | Warrior | Slayer (press an injured foe), Defender (shield allies), Pit Fighter (hit back harder after being hit) |
| Silent Blade | Rogue | Assassin (first strike on the unaware never misses), Thief (stealth), Scout (find traps and ambushes) |
| Arcane Scholar | Mage | Spell Scholar (learn more from found magic), Wild Power (exploding spell damage), Dark Pact (spend life to improve a roll) |
| Hunter of Beasts | Ranger | Dead Eye (aimed shots), Trailwise (tracking and pathfinding), Beast Bond (animal companion) |
| Divine Champion | Paladin | Healer, Holy Ward (protection from undead and the corrupt), Smiting Blade |
| Harbinger of Death | Necromancer | Bone Servant (a skeleton), Grave Drain (heal from harm dealt), Death Scholar |
| Arcane Swordsman | Spellblade | Spellstrike (spell then empowered blow), Warding Stance, Arcane Duelist (fight with intellect) |
| Way of the Fist | Monk | Iron Fist, Inner Sight (fight with wisdom), Flowing Form (move when missed) |
| Spiritwalker | Shaman | Elemental (nature and weather magic), Spirit Guide (question local spirits), Healing Wind |

The Mystic appears only in the cards and is playable. The four callings reserved for the Hidden Ones are not.

The Mystic is playable but has no matching book Type. The reserved four callings are not player options. See [CAL-900].

Existing build mappings are documented in [RUL-004].
