# Earned Titles and Reputations

## Established names and context

The cards also define nine earned titles and nine reputations that change how people react to a character.

- **Titles.** Aetherbreaker, Veilpiercer, Warlord Ascendant, Arbiter of Balance, Bloodforged, Shadowborne, Immortal Sentinel, Herald of the Deep, Phoenix Reborn.
- **Reputations.** The Forsaken Bloodline, Blood Owed, the Shadow of Betrayal, the Duelist's Mark, the Unseen Hand, Protector of the Fallen, Outcast of the Veil, Oathbound, the Voice of the Depths.

See [RUL-006] for the approved gameplay treatment. Effects of each title are not specified in this source; do not generate them as canon.
