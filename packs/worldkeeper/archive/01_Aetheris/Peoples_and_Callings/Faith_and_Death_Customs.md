# Faith and Death Customs

## Established rites and beliefs

Aetheris has no agreed answer about the afterlife. The Order teaches purification toward a radiant unity. The Wildborn hold that the dead stay in the land as spirits and memory.

- **Name Stones.** The dead person's name is carved in stone in case memory fails.
- **Ash Threads.** A strand of the dead person's clothing is burned and shared among loved ones.
- **Last Questions.** The dying are asked who they were, who they loved, and what must not be forgotten.
- **Empty Chairs.** Families keep a place at the table for anyone lost in an Aetherfall.

This section describes local funerary beliefs. The shared pantheon is documented at World Cosmology [COS-909], not duplicated here.
