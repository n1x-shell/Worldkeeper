# The Tyrant's Dominion

## Identity
The Tyrant's Dominion is one of the five coexisting dominions of Aetheris Unbound. This is a region shaped by the faction named below, not a replacement timeline or a future Aetherfall.

## Faction
Crimson Legion. Its faction page records doctrine, leaders, signature forces and political pressures.

## Capital and chief fortress
Varkhaal, the Iron Citadel, with its Great Arena. Warlords meet in the Warlord's Hall. Chief fortress: the Red Bastion.

## Other sites
The Bloodfields (permanent warfront), the Ruins of the Old Kings, the Anvil of Dominion (warforges under the mountains).

## Rival enclaves
The Last Sanctuary (Order), the Ember Archives (Consortium), the Hollow Blades' Den (Pact), the Bloodfang Warcamp (Wildborn).

## Travel and maps
The dominion borders the Hollow Marches [REG-001]. The bible sets the border at about a week's travel from Hollow Refuge [SET-001] and its capital at roughly a further week. Detailed road routes and coordinates are UNDEFINED.
