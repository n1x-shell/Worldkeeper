# Aetheris Dominions Directory

The five dominions coexist in Aetheris Unbound. All are geographically distinct. The following cross-reference table uses the source's capitals and fortresses.

| Dominion | Ruling faction | Capital and fortress |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Radiant Ascendancy [REG-002] | Celestial Order [FAC-001] | Empyrean Spire, a floating city of white stone. The High Theurgists rule from the Throne of Light. Chief fortress: the Golden Aerie. |
| The Tyrant's Dominion [REG-003] | Crimson Legion [FAC-002] | Varkhaal, the Iron Citadel, with its Great Arena. Warlords meet in the Warlord's Hall. Chief fortress: the Red Bastion. |
| The Fractured Nexus [REG-004] | Arcane Consortium [FAC-003] | Zepharyos, City of the Unbound, among the clouds. The council sits in the Nexus Spire. Chief fortress: the Nexus Collegium. |
| The Eternal Veil [REG-005] | Shadow Pact [FAC-004] | Noctis, the City of Masks, in endless dusk. The rulers meet in the Midnight Court. Chief fortress: Nightglass. |
| The Verdant Rebirth [REG-006] | Wildborn Clans [FAC-005] | The Rootspire, a colossal tree-city over a ruined capital. The elders meet at the Den of Elders. Chief fortress: the Verdant Crown. |

## Continental layout
Radiant Ascendancy north; Fractured Nexus north-west; Tyrant's Dominion east; Verdant Rebirth south; Eternal Veil west against the Black Tides. Each borders the Hollow Marches.

## Travel baseline
From Hollow Refuge to the dominion border: roughly one week. From border to faction capital: roughly another week. Exact routes and country borders remain UNDEFINED.
