# The Verdant Rebirth

## Identity
The Verdant Rebirth is one of the five coexisting dominions of Aetheris Unbound. This is a region shaped by the faction named below, not a replacement timeline or a future Aetherfall.

## Faction
Wildborn Clans. Its faction page records doctrine, leaders, signature forces and political pressures.

## Capital and chief fortress
The Rootspire, a colossal tree-city over a ruined capital. The elders meet at the Den of Elders. Chief fortress: the Verdant Crown.

## Other sites
The Green Expanse (roadless wilds), Fangholt (a behemoth's skeleton), the Ravaged Pass (haunted canyon).

## Rival enclaves
The Hollow Shrine (Order), the Bloodfang Pit (Legion), the Verdant Sanctum (Consortium), the Hollow Grove (Pact).

## Travel and maps
The dominion borders the Hollow Marches [REG-001]. The bible sets the border at about a week's travel from Hollow Refuge [SET-001] and its capital at roughly a further week. Detailed road routes and coordinates are UNDEFINED.
