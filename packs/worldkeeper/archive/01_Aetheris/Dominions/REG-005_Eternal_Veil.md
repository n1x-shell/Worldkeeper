# The Eternal Veil

## Identity
The Eternal Veil is one of the five coexisting dominions of Aetheris Unbound. This is a region shaped by the faction named below, not a replacement timeline or a future Aetherfall.

## Faction
Shadow Pact. Its faction page records doctrine, leaders, signature forces and political pressures.

## Capital and chief fortress
Noctis, the City of Masks, in endless dusk. The rulers meet in the Midnight Court. Chief fortress: Nightglass.

## Other sites
The Umbral Paths (shifting roads that lead anywhere), the Veilborn Archive (hall of stone masks), the Black Tides (a sunless sea).

## Rival enclaves
The Last Beacon (Order), the Bleeding Arena (Legion), the Woven Sanctum (Consortium), the Fading Grove (Wildborn).

## Travel and maps
The dominion borders the Hollow Marches [REG-001]. The bible sets the border at about a week's travel from Hollow Refuge [SET-001] and its capital at roughly a further week. Detailed road routes and coordinates are UNDEFINED.
