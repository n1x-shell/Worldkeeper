# The Radiant Ascendancy

## Identity
The Radiant Ascendancy is one of the five coexisting dominions of Aetheris Unbound. This is a region shaped by the faction named below, not a replacement timeline or a future Aetherfall.

## Faction
Celestial Order. Its faction page records doctrine, leaders, signature forces and political pressures.

## Capital and chief fortress
Empyrean Spire, a floating city of white stone. The High Theurgists rule from the Throne of Light. Chief fortress: the Golden Aerie.

## Other sites
The Radiant Expanse (golden plains), the Luminara Gate (an arch of light at the world's edge), the Purged Halls (a city erased for rebellion).

## Rival enclaves
Bastion of the Unbroken (Legion), Sanctum of the Everseeing (Consortium), the Eclipsed Chamber (Pact), the Thornward Grove (Wildborn). The Grove is New: an old grove on the edge of the Radiant Expanse, tolerated as a shrine to Solenne while its keepers quietly shelter Wildborn fleeing the Order.

## Travel and maps
The dominion borders the Hollow Marches [REG-001]. The bible sets the border at about a week's travel from Hollow Refuge [SET-001] and its capital at roughly a further week. Detailed road routes and coordinates are UNDEFINED.
