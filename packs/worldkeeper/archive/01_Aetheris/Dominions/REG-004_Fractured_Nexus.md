# The Fractured Nexus

## Identity
The Fractured Nexus is one of the five coexisting dominions of Aetheris Unbound. This is a region shaped by the faction named below, not a replacement timeline or a future Aetherfall.

## Faction
Arcane Consortium. Its faction page records doctrine, leaders, signature forces and political pressures.

## Capital and chief fortress
Zepharyos, City of the Unbound, among the clouds. The council sits in the Nexus Spire. Chief fortress: the Nexus Collegium.

## Other sites
The Rift of Unmaking (elemental vortex), the Echoing Halls (mirror labyrinth), the Repository of the Unwritten (forbidden vault).

## Rival enclaves
The Last Unbroken Chapel (Order), the Warbound Coliseum (Legion), the Veilfracture (Pact), the Living Wilds (Wildborn).

## Travel and maps
The dominion borders the Hollow Marches [REG-001]. The bible sets the border at about a week's travel from Hollow Refuge [SET-001] and its capital at roughly a further week. Detailed road routes and coordinates are UNDEFINED.
