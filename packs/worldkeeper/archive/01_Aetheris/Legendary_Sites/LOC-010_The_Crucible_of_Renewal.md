# The Crucible of Renewal

## Established description
Vyrathus's eternal forge under the volcanic heart of Aetheris. Its deepest fires open into a realm of flame where weapons, worlds and souls are broken down and reforged. It reforges anything, for a price. The two Crucible cards have already been merged.
