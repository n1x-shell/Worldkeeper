# Aetheris Regional Encyclopedia: First Populated Pack

**Status:** SOURCE-DERIVED ENTRY DRAFTS, ready for manual adaptation to Lore.

## Included in this pack

- The Hollow Marches region with its ten routes, danger values, and existing scene targets.
- Hollow Refuge settlement and its eight named buildings, plus the Empty House as a separate GM-only record.
- The Bloodmarked Fields and Crossroads of Fate as separate site entries.
- A grouped register of other sites and a directory of twelve Marches locals with their source-described relationships.
- All five dominion pages, with capitals, fortresses, sites, rival enclaves, and travel baseline.
- The five ruling faction pages as their own authority records.
- A Tome adventure link index, separate from permanent Lore.

## Canon safeguards

The five dominions and their leaders predate this pack and come from `Aetheris_Setting_Bible.md`. Mechanics are not newly invented. All secrets must stay GM-only in Lore; do not share the region/settlement entries unfiltered with players.

**ID note:** `REG-001-SITES`, `REG-001-PEOPLE`, and `CON-001-DOM` are DOCUMENT GROUP IDs for intermediate indexes, not new lore entities. The existing canonical entity IDs are unchanged.

## Outstanding Aetheris work

- Individual profiles of major named NPCs (existing bible has 96 unique).
- Eight cult pages, Ethereal groups, and other secondary organizations.
- Shards, relics and items with mechanics copied exactly and marked HOMEBREW.
- The legendary and unplaced sites, if enough source detail supports distinct entries.
- Additional 5e mechanics checks for anything used in encounters.
