# Titles Reputations and Faction Standing

## Use and verification
Includes source-described earned rewards, reputations and faction Renown. No specific title powers are invented.

## Source content

- **Titles** are earned rewards. Treat each as an Epic Boon or a story award granted when its condition is met.
- **Reputations** work as a second, smaller background. Each player may pick one at creation for a social benefit and a matching complication.
- **Faction standing** uses the Renown rules in the Dungeon Master's Guide, tracked separately for each of the five factions.
- **Bases.** From level 5 the party can run a Bastion at the Hollow Refuge or in any faction camp that accepts them.
