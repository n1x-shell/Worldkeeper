# Dominion Regional Effects

## Use and verification
Existing **HOMEBREW** regional effects, applying inside each named region. They are not official rules.

## Source content

These use the Lorebook's world rule for each Reality, converted with the key above. Each applies inside that faction's dominion.

| Dominion | Lorebook world rule | Regional effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Broken World and the Hollow Marches | Ordinary gear is unstable. | On a natural 1 while using a mundane item, the GM may mark it damaged until repaired. |
| The Radiant Ascendancy | Healing is easy. Dark, corruptive and necromantic magic is hard and illegal. | Advantage on checks to heal, and healing spells reroll 1s. Necromancy spell attacks have Disadvantage and their targets save with Advantage. |
| The Tyrant's Dominion | Melee weapon damage explodes on a maximum roll. | When a melee weapon damage die shows its maximum, roll it again and add. |
| The Fractured Nexus | Spells hit harder. A spell roll of 1 to 3 causes a dimensional anomaly. | Damaging spells add 1d12. Each time a spell of level 1 or higher is cast, roll a d20, and on 1 to 3 an anomaly occurs. |
| The Eternal Veil | Stealth is easy. Supernatural illusions are hard to detect. | Advantage on Stealth checks. Disadvantage on checks and saves to see through illusions. |
| The Verdant Rebirth | Survival and tracking are easy. Machinery and arcane spells are hard. Natural and spiritual magic is unaffected. | Advantage on Survival checks. Disadvantage on checks to use machines. Wizard, Sorcerer and Warlock spell attacks have Disadvantage and their save DCs drop by 2. |
