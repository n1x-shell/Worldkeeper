# Shard and Shardscarred Mechanics

## Use and verification
The mechanics below are existing **HOMEBREW**, approved setting content. Eleven shards are known, the twelfth is unknown, and the thirteenth is separate. The individual SHD files are the authoritative item entries.

## Source content

The eleven known shards are legendary items that need attunement, grant one signature power and carry the cost from their cards. The twelfth has no statistics, because nobody has found it. The thirteenth is an artifact:

- **Attunement.** None. It chooses who can carry it, and it cannot be destroyed.
- **Unforgetting.** The bearer is immune to any effect that alters or removes memory.
- **Recall.** Once per Long Rest, the bearer learns one useful truth about the place they stand in, as *Legend Lore* with no casting time or cost.
- **Beyond the Verge.** In the Ethereal Realm it answers one question at a time: "What did the world forget here?"
- **Limits.** It never raises the dead, and it cannot stand in for one of the twelve.
- **Cost.** Each use of Recall erases one small true thing about the bearer from everyone else's memory. The GM chooses what.

Anyone who touches any shard becomes Shardscarred: they have Advantage on saves against effects that alter memory or rewrite reality. The eleven known shards follow.

| Shard | Signature power | Cost at the table |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dominion | *Command* at will. *Dominate Monster* once a day. | One level of Exhaustion each dawn while any creature is under its control. |
| Infinity | *Dimension Door* at will. *Foresight* once a day. | On a natural 1 on any d20 Test that day, the bearer vanishes until the next dawn. |
| Ruin | Fire the bearer creates ignores resistance and cannot be put out for one minute. | Wisdom save each dawn or destroy something of value before sunset. |
| Sacrifice | Turn any failed d20 Test into a success. | Each use permanently costs a memory, a Hit Die or a point of Hit Point maximum, chosen by the GM. |
| Eternity | *Time Stop* once a day. | The bearer is Incapacitated later for as many rounds as were stolen. |
| Illusions | Illusions the bearer casts are real to anyone who fails the save. | Psychic damage to the bearer whenever one is disbelieved. |
| Renewal | *Heal* at will. *True Resurrection* once a month. | Whatever is restored comes back changed in one way the GM chooses. |
| Balance | Turn one creature's Advantage into Disadvantage, or the reverse, each round. | Every benefit granted is matched by an equal harm to an ally within a day. |
| Shadows | *Greater Invisibility* at will. *Etherealness* once a day. | After each use, one person who knew the bearer forgets them. |
| Chaos | Reroll any die once a round. | Roll on the Wild Magic Surge table each time. |
| Knowledge | *Legend Lore* at will, with no cost or time. | Wisdom save each use or gain a lasting madness. |
