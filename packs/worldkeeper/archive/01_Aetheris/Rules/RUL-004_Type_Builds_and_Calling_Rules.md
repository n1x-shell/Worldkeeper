# Type Builds and Calling Rules

## Use and verification
Source-provided D&D build equivalents; mechanical choices and optional source material need checking before play.

## Source content

Each Type's three starting abilities point to a different 5.5e build.

| Type | First ability | Second ability | Third ability |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Iron Vanguard | Slayer: Fighter (Champion) | Defender: Fighter (Battle Master) with the Protection fighting style | Pit Fighter: Barbarian (Path of the Berserker) |
| Silent Blade | Assassin: Rogue (Assassin) | Thief: Rogue (Thief) | Scout: Rogue (Thief) with Expertise in Perception, or Ranger (Gloom Stalker) |
| Arcane Scholar | Spell Scholar: Wizard | Wild Power: Sorcerer (Wild Magic) | Dark Pact: Warlock (Fiend patron) |
| Hunter of Beasts | Dead Eye: Ranger (Hunter) with Sharpshooter | Trailwise: Ranger with Expertise in Survival | Beast Bond: Ranger (Beast Master) |
| Divine Champion | Healer: Cleric (Life Domain) | Holy Ward: Paladin (Oath of Devotion) | Smiting Blade: Paladin (Oath of Vengeance) |
| Harbinger of Death | Bone Servant: Wizard with *Summon Undead* and *Animate Dead* | Grave Drain: Warlock with *Vampiric Touch* | Death Scholar: Wizard with *Speak with Dead* |
| Arcane Swordsman | Spellstrike: Fighter (Eldritch Knight) | Warding Stance: Eldritch Knight with the Defense fighting style and *Shield* | Arcane Duelist: Eldritch Knight using *True Strike* |
| Way of the Fist | Iron Fist: Monk (Warrior of the Open Hand) | Inner Sight: Monk with high Wisdom | Flowing Form: Monk (Warrior of Shadow) |
| Spiritwalker | Elemental: Druid (Circle of the Land) | Spirit Guide: Druid with *Speak with Animals* and *Commune with Nature* | Healing Wind: Druid (Circle of the Land) or Cleric (Life Domain) |

Named Milestones convert one at a time into feats. Examples: Intervention becomes the Interception fighting style, Disappear becomes Skulker, Counterspell becomes the spell, Stormcaller becomes *Sleet Storm* once per Long Rest.
