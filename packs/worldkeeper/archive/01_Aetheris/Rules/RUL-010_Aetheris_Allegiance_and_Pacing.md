# Aetheris Allegiance and Pacing

## Use and verification
The source proposes setting Renown values and spell adaptation notes. A suggestion that spells might be written fresh is not an existing spell or a license to add mechanics.

## Source content

- **Allegiance.** Track renown per faction: Hostile at -10, Distrusted at -3, Neutral at 0, Initiate at 3, Trusted at 10, Exalted at 25, Champion at 50.
- **Spells.** Most of the books' 52 spells match existing ones. A few are worth writing fresh, such as Thread Mark, Stone Memory, Corpse Gate and Sepulchral Gate.
