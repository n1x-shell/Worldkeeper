# ICRPG Conversion Reference

## Use and verification
This is the bible’s conversion guidance, **HOMEBREW**, not a verified rule of the 2024 Player’s Handbook. Especially monster HEART conversions are encounter estimates, never validated stat blocks.

## Source content

Use this to convert any rule, item or monster from the three books.

| In the books | In 5.5e |
| --- | --- |
| TARGET 11 to 16 | A DC of the same number. |
| EASY and HARD | Advantage and Disadvantage. |
| CLOSE, NEAR, FAR | Within 5 feet, within 30 feet, within 120 feet. |
| One HEART on an obstacle | One success in a group check, or 10 hit points. |
| Hearts on a monster | One heart is about CR 1. Two is CR 2 to 4. Three is CR 5 to 8. Four is CR 9 to 13. Five is CR 15 or more. |
| WEAPON, MAGIC and ULTIMATE EFFORT | Normal weapon damage, normal spell damage, and one extra d12. |
| +1 to +4 DEFENSE | Standard armour and shields. Reserve a true AC bonus for items worth +1 or better. |
| +1 to a STAT | +1 to the ability score, capped at 20. |
| Once per round, encounter, session | Once per turn, per Short Rest, per Long Rest. |
| TIMER of d4 rounds | Unchanged. |
| Milestone | A feat, or a class feature the character already gets at that level. |
| Mastery reward | A boon granted for a story achievement. |
| Hero Coin | Heroic Inspiration. |
| DYING timer | Death Saving Throws. |
| "Magical weapon" | A +1 weapon. |
