# Aetheris Removed Mechanics

## Removed by owner decision

- The reset loop. No Aetherfall happens during play.
- Souls-like death, bonfires and Cyclebound Return. Death and resurrection follow the core rules everywhere except beyond the Hollow Verge.
- The books' five Primary Shards and the Orb. The cards' twelve shards replace them.
- The Campaign Guide's engine: Shard Pressure, faction clocks, the clue ladder, campaign tiers and the Crown Game.

These are not available to reinstate silently during Amsel Lore entry.
