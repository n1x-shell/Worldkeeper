# Aetheris Threat Baselines

## Use and verification
These are conversion suggestions only; **no creature is mechanically verified**. Consult the rendered official Monster Manual before use. Some names and older baselines may need substitutions under the 2025 rules.

## Source content

| Aetheris threat | Start from |
| --- | --- |
| Restless dead of the Scar and the Cairn | Skeleton, Wight, Specter, Revenant |
| Legion gladiators and warlords | Gladiator, Berserker, Warrior Veteran |
| Order templars and inquisitors | Knight, Priest, Deva for angelic sentinels |
| Consortium casters and anomalies | Mage, Archmage, elementals |
| Pact assassins and spies | Spy, Assassin, Shadow |
| Wildborn warriors and beasts | Berserker, Druid, dire and giant beasts |
| Ashen Covenant zealots | Cultist stat blocks with fire damage, Fire Elemental, Salamander |
| Drowned Saints | Cultist stat blocks, Merrow, Sahuagin |
| Creatures of the Rift | Aberrations such as the Gibbering Mouther and Chuul |
| Echo Revenant | Revenant, with one memory-based power |
| Arbiter | Planetar, with radiant damage changed to force |
| Chronomancer | Archmage with *Slow*, *Haste* and *Time Stop* |
| Veilweaver | Archmage with *Modify Memory*, *Mislead* and *Greater Invisibility* |
| Titans and fallen gods | Empyrean |
| Vyrithar and Vyrathus | No stat block. They are forces, not opponents. |
