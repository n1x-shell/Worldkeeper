# Hollow Refuge

## Overview
Neutral settlement built of structures from several historical periods. All five factions sometimes need its neutrality. Residents remember things they should not.

## Sites and services
- **The Crooked Nail.** Inn, mercenary exchange and rumour market, run by Mara Venn. Its sign is a bent nail through a crown.
- **Old Vara's Forge.** Repairs gear. Vara recognises metals that should not exist and prefers favours to coin.
- **The Lantern House.** Hospital, shrine and mortuary. Weapons stay outside, and even faction soldiers comply.
- **The Market of Lost Things.** Battlefield salvage, old maps, strange currencies and unidentified objects.
- **The Seer's Tower.** Home of the Blind Seer, who is High Prophet Azhir of the Black Sun living under no name. It has no windows, because "windows work both ways".
- **The Empty House.** Nobody enters, and nobody remembers who lives there. Smoke rises from its chimney every morning. The Forgotten One lives here, unremembered in plain sight, with the thirteenth shard.
- **The Old Wall.** Built over centuries. One section commemorates a battle that has not happened.
- **The East Gate.** Where faction diplomats, and most problems, come in.

## Relevant people
Mara Venn owns the Crooked Nail; Old Vara runs the forge; the Blind Seer lives in his windowless tower. The twelve Hollow Marches locals are indexed separately in `Hollow_Marches_People_and_Relationships.md`.

## GM-only truths
The Blind Seer is High Prophet Azhir of the Black Sun. The Forgotten One [NPC-901] lives in the Empty House [LOC-001] with the thirteenth shard [SHD-013]; nobody remembers that resident. The Old Wall has a memorial to a battle that has not happened.

## Starting connections
