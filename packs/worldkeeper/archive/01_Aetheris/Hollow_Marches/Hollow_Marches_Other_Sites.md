# Other Sites of the Hollow Marches

These sites are grouped until further information warrants dedicated entries. The Rift has its authoritative record at The Rift [LOC-901], under World Geography.

## Ashen Spire
The ruined capital of the last kingdom. Scavengers, mercenaries and restless spirits.

## The Scar
An endless battlefield of rusted steel where the dead soldiers do not rest.
Scene target: DC 12. GM clue: Some of the dead fought in wars that never happened.. Adventure: The War That Never Happened.

## Blackveil Fortress
An abandoned stronghold. Its vaults may hold relics, and nobody returns from the depths.
Scene target: DC 12. GM clue: One hall lists the names of soldiers who are still alive.. Adventure: The Names of the Living.

## The Sunken Archive
An underground library labyrinth. Reading too deeply brings back memories that were erased.
Scene target: DC 13. GM clue: Contradictory historical records are all authentic.. Adventure: The Book That Knows Your Name.

## Wither's Hollow
A dead, mist-bound forest whose paths shift behind travellers.
Scene target: DC 13. GM clue: One unstable path leads toward the Hollow Verge.. Adventure: The Forest That Knows You.

## Cairn of the Forgotten
A field of unmarked graves for people whose histories were erased. Some stir.
