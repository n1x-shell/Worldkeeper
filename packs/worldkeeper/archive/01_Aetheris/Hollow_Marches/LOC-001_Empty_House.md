# The Empty House

## What others see
A house no one enters and whose resident nobody remembers. Smoke rises from the chimney every morning.

## GM-only secret
The Forgotten One [NPC-901] lives here unremembered and keeps the thirteenth shard [SHD-013]. Mother Thorn refuses to enter.
