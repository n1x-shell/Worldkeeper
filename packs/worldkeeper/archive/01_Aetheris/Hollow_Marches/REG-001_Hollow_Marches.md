# The Hollow Marches

## Identity and ground
The contested heartland of Aetheris, where forgotten kingdoms once met. The five dominions border this region. The land contains older battlefields, erased histories, faction camps, and the neutral settlement of Hollow Refuge [SET-001].

## Places
- **Ashen Spire:** The ruined capital of the last kingdom. Scavengers, mercenaries and restless spirits.
- **The Scar:** An endless battlefield of rusted steel where the dead soldiers do not rest.
- **Blackveil Fortress:** An abandoned stronghold. Its vaults may hold relics, and nobody returns from the depths.
- **The Sunken Archive:** An underground library labyrinth. Reading too deeply brings back memories that were erased.
- **The Rift:** A bottomless chasm humming with strange energy. People say it is tied to the Aetherfall's origin. In truth the Forgotten One tore it.
- **Wither's Hollow:** A dead, mist-bound forest whose paths shift behind travellers.
- **Cairn of the Forgotten:** A field of unmarked graves for people whose histories were erased. Some stir.
- **The Hollow Refuge:** A neutral settlement built from the ruins of past worlds. Its people remember things they should not. A blind seer lives here. Starting site.
- **The Bloodmarked Fields:** A battlefield where every faction fights over newly surfaced shards. Starting site.
- **The Crossroads of Fate:** A waystone where roads shift but the crossing never changes. All factions pass through. Starting site.

## Faction camps
- **Sanctuary of the Last Light** (Celestial Order): A ruined hilltop cathedral used as a holy bastion.
- **The Warlord's Rest** (Crimson Legion): A war camp on an old battlefield, with duels for rank.
- **The Nexus Outpost** (Arcane Consortium): A ruined tower where reality is fragile.
- **The Veiled Chamber** (Shadow Pact): A hall under a dead city, hidden by illusion.
- **The Wolf's Den** (Wildborn Clans): An overgrown ruin shared with wolves.
- **The Hollow Verge** (Seekers of the Ethereal Realm): A rift between realities whose entrance is different for everyone who finds it.

## People and faction pressure
The Celestial Order, Crimson Legion, Arcane Consortium, Shadow Pact and Wildborn Clans contest the Marches; the Factionless and Ethereal Ones are also present in the setting. The starting settlement requires enough neutrality that all sides have use for it.
