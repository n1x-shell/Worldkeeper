# Hollow Marches People and Relationships

## Established relationship links
- Vara's daughter serves under Vaal.
- Elyra suspects Orin of forbidden research. Orin pays Vess for stolen documents.
- Mother Thorn refuses to enter the Empty House.
- Mara trusts Sera without knowing who she works for.
- Brann once served Vaal, who considers him a deserter.
- Illa owes Vess money, and the Blind Seer knows what her relic does.
- Torren found something in the Scar that Orin wants.
