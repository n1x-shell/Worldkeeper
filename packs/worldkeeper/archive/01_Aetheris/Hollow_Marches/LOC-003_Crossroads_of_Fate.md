# The Crossroads of Fate

## Overview
A waystone where roads shift but the crossing never changes. All factions pass through. Starting site. The Shard of Balance lies hidden inside the waystone. Everyone passes it. [GM SECRET]

## Leads and local travel
Half a day from Hollow Refuge (danger 1); half a day to the Scar (danger 3); one day to the Sunken Archive (danger 2).
