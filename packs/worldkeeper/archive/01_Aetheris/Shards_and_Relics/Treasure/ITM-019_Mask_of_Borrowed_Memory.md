# Mask of Borrowed Memory

## Rarity and attunement (existing homebrew)
Very rare, attunement

## Existing effect [HOMEBREW; not independently rules verified]
Touch the face of a willing or Incapacitated creature to take one memory and its appearance. While you wear the mask you have both, and the creature lacks the memory until you take the mask off.
