# Concept Chain

## Rarity and attunement (existing homebrew)
Very rare, attunement

## Existing effect [HOMEBREW; not independently rules verified]
Once per dawn, as a Magic action, choose a creature within 30 feet and a concept: one damage type, one sense, one language, or movement. On a failed DC 17 Wisdom save, it loses that concept for 1 hour.
