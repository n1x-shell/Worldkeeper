# Shard of Chaos

## Lore power
Breaks rules and reshapes reality unpredictably.

## Lore cost
Fortune or ruin. It answers to no master.

## Canon location (GM)
At the eye of the Rift of Unmaking

## Closest interested party
The Arcane Consortium

## Existing 5.5e homebrew mechanics [HOMEBREW; not independently rules verified]
**Signature power:** Reroll any die once a round.

**Cost at the table:** Roll on the Wild Magic Surge table each time.

## Shard traits
Attunement is required for the eleven known shards. Touch marks a creature as Shardscarred, as established in the source. None can rewrite existence while severed from the Loom.
