# Shard of Eternity

## Lore power
Suspends moments and holds back fate.

## Lore cost
Nothing stays frozen forever.

## Canon location (GM)
At the still centre of the Hollowed Veil

## Closest interested party
The Whispering Circle

## Existing 5.5e homebrew mechanics [HOMEBREW; not independently rules verified]
**Signature power:** *Time Stop* once a day.

**Cost at the table:** The bearer is Incapacitated later for as many rounds as were stolen.

## Shard traits
Attunement is required for the eleven known shards. Touch marks a creature as Shardscarred, as established in the source. None can rewrite existence while severed from the Loom.
