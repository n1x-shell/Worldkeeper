# The Thirteenth Shard

## Appearance and origin
Transparent crystal around a tiny black star that seems infinitely far away. Made by the Forgotten One [NPC-901] from a piece of their own Thread. It is outside the original twelve.

## Location (GM)
The Forgotten One holds it in the Empty House [LOC-001] at Hollow Refuge [SET-001].

## Existing 5.5e rules [HOMEBREW; not independently rules verified]
- **Attunement:** None. It chooses who may carry it and cannot be destroyed.
- **Unforgetting:** The bearer is immune to effects altering or removing memory.
- **Recall:** Once per Long Rest, learns one useful truth about their current location, as *Legend Lore* without casting time or cost.
- **Beyond the Verge:** Answers one question at a time: “What did the world forget here?”
- **Cost:** Each Recall erases one small true thing about the bearer from everyone else's memory, chosen by the GM.
- **Limits:** Cannot raise the dead or replace one of the twelve shards.

## Continuity
The version that says once per session was reconciled: the once-per-Long-Rest version above is authoritative.
