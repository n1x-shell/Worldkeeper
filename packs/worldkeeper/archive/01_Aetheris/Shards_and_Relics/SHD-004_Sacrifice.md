# Shard of Sacrifice

## Lore power
Fuels the wielder's strength.

## Lore cost
Always takes something dear: memory, blood, soul or a piece of existence.

## Canon location (GM)
At the top of the Bloodmoon Spire, reachable only when the moon turns red

## Closest interested party
The Black Sun

## Existing 5.5e homebrew mechanics [HOMEBREW; not independently rules verified]
**Signature power:** Turn any failed d20 Test into a success.

**Cost at the table:** Each use permanently costs a memory, a Hit Die or a point of Hit Point maximum, chosen by the GM.

## Shard traits
Attunement is required for the eleven known shards. Touch marks a creature as Shardscarred, as established in the source. None can rewrite existence while severed from the Loom.
