# Shard of Illusions

## Lore power
Makes the false true by bending perception.

## Lore cost
Every illusion eventually meets the truth.

## Canon location (GM)
Behind one of the stone masks in the Veilborn Archive at Noctis

## Closest interested party
The Shadow Pact, which does not know which mask

## Existing 5.5e homebrew mechanics [HOMEBREW; not independently rules verified]
**Signature power:** Illusions the bearer casts are real to anyone who fails the save.

**Cost at the table:** Psychic damage to the bearer whenever one is disbelieved.

## Shard traits
Attunement is required for the eleven known shards. Touch marks a creature as Shardscarred, as established in the source. None can rewrite existence while severed from the Loom.
