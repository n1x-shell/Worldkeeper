# Shard of Ruin

## Lore power
A fire that cannot be put out. Reduces anything to ash.

## Lore cost
Its hunger tempts the wielder to burn everything.

## Canon location (GM)
In the Ashen Wastes, beside the First Fire

## Closest interested party
The Ashen Covenant

## Existing 5.5e homebrew mechanics [HOMEBREW; not independently rules verified]
**Signature power:** Fire the bearer creates ignores resistance and cannot be put out for one minute.

**Cost at the table:** Wisdom save each dawn or destroy something of value before sunset.

## Shard traits
Attunement is required for the eleven known shards. Touch marks a creature as Shardscarred, as established in the source. None can rewrite existence while severed from the Loom.
