# Shard of Shadows

## Lore power
Lets the wielder move unseen and exist beyond perception.

## Lore cost
The more one fades, the harder it is to be remembered.

## Canon location (GM)
Behind the scratching door of the Vault of the Nameless

## Closest interested party
The Nameless

## Existing 5.5e homebrew mechanics [HOMEBREW; not independently rules verified]
**Signature power:** *Greater Invisibility* at will. *Etherealness* once a day.

**Cost at the table:** After each use, one person who knew the bearer forgets them.

## Shard traits
Attunement is required for the eleven known shards. Touch marks a creature as Shardscarred, as established in the source. None can rewrite existence while severed from the Loom.
