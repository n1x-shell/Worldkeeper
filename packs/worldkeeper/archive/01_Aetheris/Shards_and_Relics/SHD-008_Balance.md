# Shard of Balance

## Lore power
Weighs all things and stops any side tilting too far.

## Lore cost
Balance is not fair, and it cuts both ways.

## Canon location (GM)
Inside the waystone at the Crossroads of Fate

## Closest interested party
Nobody. Everyone walks past it.

## Existing 5.5e homebrew mechanics [HOMEBREW; not independently rules verified]
**Signature power:** Turn one creature's Advantage into Disadvantage, or the reverse, each round.

**Cost at the table:** Every benefit granted is matched by an equal harm to an ally within a day.

## Shard traits
Attunement is required for the eleven known shards. Touch marks a creature as Shardscarred, as established in the source. None can rewrite existence while severed from the Loom.
