# Shard of Infinity

## Lore power
Sees unwritten futures and steps between realities.

## Lore cost
The farther the gaze, the greater the risk of never returning.

## Canon location (GM)
Somewhere along the Rift of Echoes, in the past, present or future

## Closest interested party
Nobody

## Existing 5.5e homebrew mechanics [HOMEBREW; not independently rules verified]
**Signature power:** *Dimension Door* at will. *Foresight* once a day.

**Cost at the table:** On a natural 1 on any d20 Test that day, the bearer vanishes until the next dawn.

## Shard traits
Attunement is required for the eleven known shards. Touch marks a creature as Shardscarred, as established in the source. None can rewrite existence while severed from the Loom.
