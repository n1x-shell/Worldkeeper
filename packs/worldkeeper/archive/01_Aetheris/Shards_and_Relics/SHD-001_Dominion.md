# Shard of Dominion

## Lore power
Bends others to the wielder's will.

## Lore cost
The weight of everything the wielder must now control.

## Canon location (GM)
Beneath the Bloodmarked Fields

## Closest interested party
All five factions

## Existing 5.5e homebrew mechanics [HOMEBREW; not independently rules verified]
**Signature power:** *Command* at will. *Dominate Monster* once a day.

**Cost at the table:** One level of Exhaustion each dawn while any creature is under its control.

## Shard traits
Attunement is required for the eleven known shards. Touch marks a creature as Shardscarred, as established in the source. None can rewrite existence while severed from the Loom.
