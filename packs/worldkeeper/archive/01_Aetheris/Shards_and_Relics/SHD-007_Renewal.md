# Shard of Renewal

## Lore power
Heals and restores beyond mortal limits.

## Lore cost
Not everything should be restored.

## Canon location (GM)
Grown into the skeleton of Fangholt

## Closest interested party
The Wildborn Clans

## Existing 5.5e homebrew mechanics [HOMEBREW; not independently rules verified]
**Signature power:** *Heal* at will. *True Resurrection* once a month.

**Cost at the table:** Whatever is restored comes back changed in one way the GM chooses.

## Shard traits
Attunement is required for the eleven known shards. Touch marks a creature as Shardscarred, as established in the source. None can rewrite existence while severed from the Loom.
