# Shard of Knowledge

## Lore power
Reveals truths never meant to be known.

## Lore cost
Some truths break the mind that learns them.

## Canon location (GM)
The deepest stacks of the Sunken Archive

## Closest interested party
The Arcane Consortium

## Existing 5.5e homebrew mechanics [HOMEBREW; not independently rules verified]
**Signature power:** *Legend Lore* at will, with no cost or time.

**Cost at the table:** Wisdom save each use or gain a lasting madness.

## Shard traits
Attunement is required for the eleven known shards. Touch marks a creature as Shardscarred, as established in the source. None can rewrite existence while severed from the Loom.
