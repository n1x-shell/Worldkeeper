# Aetheris

## Identity

Aetheris is a shattered high-fantasy continent of war-torn ruins, scavengers, warbands, zealots and conspiracies. Five great factions contend over the twelve shards of the Ethereal Crystal. Power takes a price in memory, name, blood or sanity. Its Cycle of Aetherfalls has ended: in the present, **no reset happens during play**.

## Geography

The Hollow Marches [REG-001] occupy the contested centre, with Hollow Refuge [SET-001] as a neutral starting settlement. The Radiant Ascendancy [REG-002] lies north; the Tyrant's Dominion [REG-003] east; the Fractured Nexus [REG-004] north-west; the Eternal Veil [REG-005] west by the Black Tides; and the Verdant Rebirth [REG-006] south.

## Shards

The Ethereal Crystal has twelve original shards: eleven described and placed, one lost and forgotten. A thirteenth crystal made by the Forgotten One is separate. The shards remain extraordinarily powerful but cannot rewrite the world unless relinked to the Loom [COS-905]. See The Shards [COS-906].

## At campaign start

The Cycle ended about thirty years before the current Year 31 Unbound. History accumulates; the five dominions no longer take turns replacing one another. The Hollow Marches stay contested; the first regular ships cross from the Eternal Veil to Kaelmar.

## GM-only truth [SECRET]

The Forgotten One [NPC-901] severed the shards from the Loom and lives unremembered in the Empty House [LOC-001] at Hollow Refuge. The Watchers Beyond [COS-904] have noticed that the world is no longer hidden.
