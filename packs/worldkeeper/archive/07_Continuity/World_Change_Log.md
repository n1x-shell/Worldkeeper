## v10: Campaign, history and cleanup (2026-11-10)

- **Campaign.** Added 48 Tome pages in `06_Campaign/`. The Campaign Directory [HOOK-000] replaces the old Aetheris Starting Adventure Links index (HOOK-001-INDEX). It covers the ten starter hooks, the Vault of the Shard of Dominion, the six site adventures, the major arcs and ways to start, eight level-tier hook lists (four per continent), all 29 adventures anywhere and the crossing routes. Premises come straight from the bibles. Level ranges the books don't give are marked as suggested.
- **History.** Added nine event pages: HIS-903 to HIS-909, HIS-911 and HIS-913 (The Raising of the Thirteen Stones). The World Timeline now links every event.
- **Master index.** Removed the stale "not yet extracted" note for SYS-904 and SYS-907 and the old "Extraction remaining" paragraph. Added history, campaign and enter-once sections.
- **Style.** Removed every em dash outside `References/`. Calling pages now use the plain calling name, with the title as the alternate name, e.g. Mage (alternate name: The Arcane Scholar).
- No new world canon.
