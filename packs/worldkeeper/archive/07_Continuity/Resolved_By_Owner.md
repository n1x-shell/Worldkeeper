# Resolved by Owner

## Approved decisions

This register preserves settled decisions and does not reopen them. Full history and rationales remain in the two original bibles and Phase 1 audit.

- Aetheris's story cards take precedence over its tabletop books, which fill specifically approved gaps.
- Aetheris Unbound is the starting state. The Forgotten One broke the Cycle; the reset and bonfire mechanics are removed.
- Five faction dominions coexist. The story-card cities are capitals; tabletop fortresses remain nearby.
- Twelve original shards exist (eleven known and placed, the twelfth missing), plus the thirteenth Forgotten One shard.
- Aetheris and Kaelmar are two continents in one world, separated by the Black Tides and connected by one Rift and one Ethereal Realm.
- The Rift was torn in a Forgotten One's earlier life before Kaelith; the last crossing was about 270 AB.
- The Forgotten One planted the Kaelmar spore-seed and resides at Hollow Refuge's Empty House.
- The Watchers are the beings glimpsed in the Kaelmar between. The Hidden Ones hid the world from them by tribute.
- The Ethereal Realm has a border and deep layer; deep access requires the thirteenth shard or a resident's invitation.
- The Loom has twelve colossal crystals, one dark.
- Shared species include Aetheris's Taur'n/minotaur, Shadowkin/shadar-kai, and Reptilians/lizardfolk.
- Hollowbell's thirteen voices form an exception to Kaelmar's other three-mind vault keys.
- Unmaking corresponds to Thinning 6 [HOMEBREW].
- Shared deities, standard coins and the 363-day calendar are canon.
- Kaelmar starts at 300 AB, before the possible 927 AB novel future; modern technology is removed.
- Approved new material in both revised bibles is canon, including the 'Adventures anywhere' section approved after the audit.
- Existing homebrew mechanics remain, labeled HOMEBREW; no new mechanics are authorized by this register.
