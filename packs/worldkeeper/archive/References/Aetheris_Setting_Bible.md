# Aetheris — 5.5e Setting Bible

Nov 9, 2026 · @Nix

## Overview

Aetheris is a shattered high-fantasy continent where five great factions hunt twelve Crystal Shards, each hoping to remake the world in its own image.

This bible is built from your three tabletop books (Player's Handbook, GM Campaign Guide, GM Lorebook) and all 266 AI Dungeon story cards. Where they disagree the cards win, and the books fill the gaps. The early sections record what the sources say. Where I reshaped something to fit decisions you have already made, it is marked **Adaptation**. The 5.5e sections are approved homebrew.

- **Premise.** The Ethereal Crystal was broken into twelve shards. Reforged, they rewrite the world in an event called an Aetherfall. Eleven shards are known and the twelfth is lost. Kingdoms have fallen in the fighting, and the land is ruins, warbands and scavengers.
- **Adaptation: no resets.** In the cards, the winner triggers an Aetherfall and the world restarts as that faction's reality. Here no reset ever happens in play. The default campaign is set in Aetheris Unbound, one of the Lorebook's own endings: the link between the shards and the Loom has been cut. The Forgotten One cut it, and nobody remembers them for it. The shards are still powerful relics and the factions still fight over them, but nothing can rewrite the world.
- **Adaptation: five dominions.** The five alternate realities become five regions that exist side by side, each already shaped by its faction. The Lorebook calls this a Patchwork Reality. Here it is the state the world was in when the Cycle broke.
- **Also dropped.** Souls-like death, bonfire checkpoints, and the books' Cyclebound Return, in which the dead come back at the next Aetherfall without their memories. The Campaign Guide's tracking engine is also left out.
- **Layers of conflict.** Five factions fight in the open. Eight cults work underneath. The Hidden Ones quietly keep any side from winning. Something older watches the Hidden Ones.
- **Tone.** War-torn ruins, zealots and conspiracies, with cosmic secrets under them. Power always takes something: memory, name, blood or sanity.
- **Default start.** The Hollow Marches, the contested heartland around the neutral settlement of Hollow Refuge.

**Adaptation.** The Cycle is real history, and in this campaign it has ended. The world is Aetheris Unbound: history accumulates for the first time, the dead stay dead, and beyond the Black Horizon something has noticed.

**The truth behind the myths**

- **The first civilisation.** Before any Aetherfall, Aetheris had a civilisation that lasted for ages. It conquered disease, then ageing, then death. It found the Ethereal Realm, and then what lies beyond it.
- **The Watchers.** Something beyond the Ethereal Realm perceives whole histories instead of individuals. That civilisation drew its attention, and almost nothing survived.
- **The Shattering was a defence.** The Elders of the Ethereal Throne, the first of the Hidden Ones, broke the Ethereal Crystal into twelve shards and built the Aetherfall around them. No civilisation would ever again last long enough to be seen.
- **Prison, quarantine and tribute.** The Cycle keeps the world from growing, hides it from the Watchers, and feeds them the discarded histories.
- **The shards pull the world apart.** Whenever Aetheris grows stable, people divide around five answers: order, conquest, knowledge, secrecy and nature. The shards surface, wars begin, someone reforges the Crystal, and history starts over.
- **The Loom does the work.** The reforged Crystal only tells the Loom of Eternity which pattern to impose. Then it shatters again. No faction is allowed a permanent victory.
- **The weapon is forgetting.** The Hidden Ones redirect shards, suppress discoveries and erase people. They are architects, custodians and jailers, not gods.
- **Shardscarred.** Anyone who touches a shard is marked, and resists the rewriting of reality. People call the leftover contradictions in ruins and records Aether Scars.
- **The Forgotten One.** One mortal learned the truth, was erased, returned without memory, and learned it again, over many lives. At last they tore a piece from their own Thread and made the thirteenth shard. It cost them their name and their place in history.
- **The thirteenth shard is refusal.** It stands for the right not to choose among the five answers. It is crystallised continuity and cannot forget.
- **The Hidden Ones' equation.** Aetheris can be free, remembered or safe. Choose two. Whether that is true is left to the GM.

**What the Forgotten One did**

These three acts are your decisions, and they set the starting state of both continents.

1. In an earlier life, centuries before Kaelith, they tore open the Rift. They crossed it many times over many lives. On the last crossing, about 270 AB, they planted a spore-seed in a communion pool in Kaelmar.
2. They came back and broke the Cycle, cutting the shards from the Loom. That is how Aetheris became Unbound.
3. Nobody remembers any of it. The twelfth shard has been missing ever since, and no record names it.

In Aetheris Unbound the shards are still the most powerful objects in the world, and the five factions still fight for them. They can no longer rewrite existence unless someone relinks them to the Loom.

**The powers behind it**

- **The Hidden Ones.** Ancient, unseen architects who are neither gods nor mortals. They shape reality and keep the struggle going.
- **Vyrithar, the Weaver of Veils.** Keeps the Loom of Eternity, a living web of silver threads in which each thread is a soul or a moment.
- **Vyrathus, the Keeper of Flame.** His brother and rival. Tends the Crucible of Renewal, where whatever must be destroyed is reforged.
- **The Watchers Beyond.** The cards say the Hidden Ones serve something greater that watches and does not yet interfere. In truth they hide the world from it and pay it tribute.

**Forbidden truths (for the GM)**

- Balance is enforced. Something makes sure no faction ever holds power for good.
- A thirteenth shard exists. It does not glow, does not answer magic and is not part of the Ethereal Crystal.
- One thread in the Loom connects to nothing. It is an unwritten life that should not exist.
- There is a ritual that is a key, not a spell. If it is completed, something steps through.
- Something in the dark remembers everything the world has forgotten.
- Once every thousand years, on the Whispering Night, the Veil lifts and the attuned see what should not be seen.
- The Silent Archive is a library whose books read the visitor. It holds a book with no name.

## Geography

The 80 location cards sort into a lawless heartland, five dominions, a set of legendary sites and the Ethereal Realm. Only the heartland has travel times, given in the Hollow Marches section below.

**Layout in words**

The cards and books give no compass directions, so these positions are new and are now fixed.

- **Centre.** The Hollow Marches, the lawless heartland. Every dominion borders it.
- **North.** The Radiant Ascendancy, golden plains running up to the Luminara Gate at the world's edge.
- **North-west.** The Fractured Nexus, a high country of storms and floating rock.
- **East.** The Tyrant's Dominion, iron mountains with the Bloodfields on the border facing the Marches.
- **South.** The Verdant Rebirth, roadless forest.
- **West.** The Eternal Veil, a dusk coast on the Black Tides.
- **Beyond the west.** The Black Tides, a sunless sea. Kaelmar lies on its far shore.
- **The Rift's course.** The Rift is one wound running between both continents. It opens as the chasm in the Hollow Marches, runs west under the Eternal Veil's coast, and drops into a trench beneath the Black Tides, which is why that sea is sunless. It rises in Kaelmar as a buried fault under the north and opens again as a chasm beside Ashvale. Only the two ends are open, and they meet in the Ethereal Realm.
- **Scale.** Each dominion's border is about a week's travel from Hollow Refuge, and each capital is about another week beyond its border.

**Adaptation.** In the cards, each dominion's locations are "hidden unless" that faction has won. Here all five dominions exist at once, and each contains small enclaves of its rivals.

**Hollow Refuge**

A neutral settlement of 250 to 400 people, built from structures of several historical periods. Every faction sometimes needs its neutrality.

- **The Crooked Nail.** Inn, mercenary exchange and rumour market, run by Mara Venn. Its sign is a bent nail through a crown.
- **Old Vara's Forge.** Repairs gear. Vara recognises metals that should not exist and prefers favours to coin.
- **The Lantern House.** Hospital, shrine and mortuary. Weapons stay outside, and even faction soldiers comply.
- **The Market of Lost Things.** Battlefield salvage, old maps, strange currencies and unidentified objects.
- **The Seer's Tower.** Home of the Blind Seer, who is High Prophet Azhir of the Black Sun living under no name. It has no windows, because "windows work both ways".
- **The Empty House.** Nobody enters, and nobody remembers who lives there. Smoke rises from its chimney every morning. The Forgotten One lives here, unremembered in plain sight, with the thirteenth shard.
- **The Old Wall.** Built over centuries. One section commemorates a battle that has not happened.
- **The East Gate.** Where faction diplomats, and most problems, come in.

**The Silent Accord in detail**

- It knows the Aetherfall is artificial, that memories are manipulated, that Mnemonic Relics and the Hidden Ones exist, and that evidence points to a thirteenth shard.
- It does not know the full purpose of the Cycle, the Watchers, how the Loom works, or that death in the Ethereal Realm is permanent.
- It works in cells of three to eight: a Scribe, a Witness, a Keeper, a Guide and a Silent, who destroys evidence that would expose the cell.

The books' Crimson Pact, an independent mercenary brotherhood, is renamed the Bloodbound Company, and the Crimson Raven is now the Grey Raven. Only the Legion is called Crimson.

**Faith and death**

Aetheris has no agreed answer about the afterlife. The Order teaches purification toward a radiant unity. The Wildborn hold that the dead stay in the land as spirits and memory.

**Everyday life on both continents**

Both continents share one system for money and time, kept simple for the table. The Kaelmar bible points back to this section.

**Money.** Standard D&D coins everywhere: copper (cp), silver (sp), gold (gp) and platinum (pp), at their usual values. Each dominion and Kaelholm strike their own faces on them, and anyone takes anyone's coin by weight. Western Kaelmar still trades mostly in barter.

**Calendar.** One calendar on both continents, following the design Mystic Arts uses for its world Orethon. The year has 363 days: twelve months of 30 days in four seasons, plus three festival days that belong to no month. Weeks have seven days.

| Season | Months | Festival |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spring | Spring Dawn, First Seed, Monsoon | **Spring Equinox.** A day of its own before Spring Dawn 1. It is New Year's Day. |
| Summer | Midnight Sun, Dry Wind, Harvest | **Midsummer.** Held on Midnight Sun 15, so it adds no day. |
| Autumn | Fall, Last Seed, Old Winds | **Autumn Equinox.** A day of its own before Fall 1. |
| Winter | Snowfall, Evernight, Winter's Dusk | **Evernight Festival.** A day of its own between Evernight 30 and Winter's Dusk 1, the longest night of the year. |

Write a date as "Midnight Sun 15, 300 AB". Kaelmar counts years After Bloom. Aetheris counts them from the night the Cycle broke, so 300 AB is Year 31 Unbound there. The month names are Mystic Arts' own, so rename them if you ever publish the setting.

**Languages.** The standard 5.5e languages exist on both continents. Common in Aetheris and Kaelish in Kaelmar are distant cousins. Treat them as one language at the table unless you want a barrier. Sailors are already mixing them into a trade pidgin called Tidecant. Local tongues:

- **Veilspeech.** The Shadow Pact's cant. Use the rules for Thieves' Cant.
- **Root-tongue.** Spoken by the Wildborn. Druids use it as Druidic, and everyone else as Sylvan.
- **The First Grammar.** Kaelith's dead script. It is read, never spoken, and counts as a rare language.
- **Circle-tongue.** The Spore Circle's language, spoken only during communion.

**The thirteenth shard**

- It cannot be destroyed. The Silent Accord thinks it can keep the Cycle ended. The Hidden Ones want it erased.
- Beyond the Hollow Verge it answers one question at a time: "What did the world forget here?"
- It never raises the dead.

**The shards as artifacts**

The eleven known shards are legendary items that need attunement, grant one signature power and carry the cost from their cards. The twelfth has no statistics, because nobody has found it. The thirteenth is an artifact:

- **Attunement.** None. It chooses who can carry it, and it cannot be destroyed.
- **Unforgetting.** The bearer is immune to any effect that alters or removes memory.
- **Recall.** Once per Long Rest, the bearer learns one useful truth about the place they stand in, as *Legend Lore* with no casting time or cost.
- **Beyond the Verge.** In the Ethereal Realm it answers one question at a time: "What did the world forget here?"
- **Limits.** It never raises the dead, and it cannot stand in for one of the twelve.
- **Cost.** Each use of Recall erases one small true thing about the bearer from everyone else's memory. The GM chooses what.

Anyone who touches any shard becomes Shardscarred: they have Advantage on saves against effects that alter memory or rewrite reality. The eleven known shards follow.

**Death beyond the Verge**

Past lives are still real in Aetheris Unbound, so the books' memory rules stay.

- **Old memories.** Meeting something from a forgotten life gives a character a fragment of memory, with no roll.
- **Awakening.** Crossing the last threshold of the Hollow Verge restores every past life at once and cannot be undone. The Hidden Ones treat the Awakened as threats.
- **True Death.** In the true Ethereal Realm, Death Saving Throws work normally, but no spell, item or wish returns the dead.
- **Unmaking.** A character who dies there is erased from history. Statues change and family remember someone else. Awakened companions keep only a sense of loss. Unmaking is the same loss as Kaelmar's Thinning: it is Thinning taken to 6 in one step.
- **Memorial Card.** Each surviving companion writes one true sentence about the lost character. It grants nothing. The table remembers what the world does not.

## Place in the world

Aetheris and Kaelmar are two continents of one world, joined by one Ethereal Realm. Kaelmar lies west of Aetheris across the Black Tides. Ashvale is a town in Kaelmar. Both bibles describe the same year: 300 After Bloom in Kaelmar's calendar, one generation after the Cycle broke in Aetheris.

**The Ethereal Realm as a playable place**

- **Adaptation.** In the cards the Ethereal Realm opens only after someone ends the Cycle. Here the Cycle is already broken, and the realm exists at all times. The realm has two layers. The border layer is the Hollow Verge, Kaelmar's between, the Rift crossing and the root-roads. Parties can reach it from level 5 by root-road, and from about level 11 by the Verge or the Rift. The deep realm lies past the Verge's last threshold: the Ossuary of Kings, the Silent Archive, the Loom, the Citadel of the Hidden and the Black Horizon. Reaching it needs the thirteenth shard, or an invitation from someone who lives there, such as a summons from the Colosseum of Echoes or from Magistrate Althros. Uninvited, it suits levels 17 to 20.
- **It is Kaelmar's "between".** Both settings describe a place behind the world where lost things linger and bodies can be carried. They are the same realm seen from two shores.
- **Ways in from Aetheris.** The Hollow Verge, the Rift, the Umbral Paths, the Luminara Gate, the Rift of Echoes, and any place on the Whispering Night.
- **Who lives there.** The Cycle Breakers, Architects of Eternity, Veilborn, Titans of the Rift and Echoborn, with Magistrate Althros keeping the realm's laws.
- **What it holds.** Fragments of erased worlds at the Rift's Edge, and the Nameless Sepulcher, whose seals are breaking.

**Bridges between the continents**

| Aetheris | Kaelmar | How they connect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Rift, a chasm tied to the origin of the Aetherfall. | The Rift at Ashvale town. | One wound with two open ends, joined by a buried fault and a trench beneath the Black Tides. The Forgotten One tore it open to cross. Whoever enters one side can come out of the other, by way of the Ethereal Realm. |
| The Forgotten One, whom nobody remembers. | The spore-seed lying in a communion pool. | The Forgotten One planted it. It has not woken yet. |
| The thirteenth shard, which does not glow or answer magic. | Kaelmar counts everything in thirteens, and its glass is "made, not grown". | The shard's maker walked Kaelmar. Whether the glass grew from the shard is the GM's secret. |
| The Black Tides, a sunless sea some believe leads to another world. | Kaelmar's eastern coast and the port of Kaelholm. | The sea route between the continents. The Rift's trench runs beneath it, which is why it is sunless. |
| "Balance is enforced" by the Hidden Ones. | Kaelith failsafes paused the change half-finished. | Both lands are held unfinished by a hidden design. |
| The Whispering Circle remembers what was erased. The Nameless lost their names. | Kaelmar's Thinning costs memories and names. | The same loss, understood differently on each shore. |
| The Watchers Beyond. | "Others dream the same dream" in the between. | The same observers. |

**Shared history**

- **Both lands tried to conquer death.** The first civilisation of Aetheris did, and drew the Watchers. Kaelith tried three centuries ago, and caused the Shrouding. The Loom never covered Kaelmar, which is why its history has run unbroken.
- **Both are now exposed.** Aetheris Unbound has just become permanent, and "something notices". Nothing hides either continent from the Watchers any more.
- **Crystal that remembers.** The thirteenth shard is crystallised continuity: a clear crystal around a black star. Kaelmar's glass is living crystal that keeps people.
- **Rebirth and Bloom.** The Wildborn want the Verdant Rebirth. Kaelmar has a Bloom that already swallowed its cities.
- **The same bargain.** The Hidden Ones say a world can be free, remembered or safe, and only two at once. Kaelmar's glass offers safety and harmony at the price of names.
- **Two kinds of erasure.** Unmaking in the Ethereal Realm and Thinning in Kaelmar are the same loss met from two sides, and they share one rule.

**How each continent sees the other**

- The Arcane Consortium would cross the Black Tides for an intact Kaelith vault.
- The Wildborn Clans would see the Bloom as their promised world, already arrived.
- The Celestial Order would call the glass a heresy and the Ascendancy a rival.
- To Kaelmar's settlements, Aetheris is a rumour of warring crystal-kings from across the sunless sea.

## Adventure hooks

The books and cards support a full campaign: scavenging in the heartland, faction wars in the dominions, the conspiracy behind them, and the Ethereal Realm at the end.

**Ready-made adventures from the books**

- **Vault of the Shard of Dominion.** A five-room starter under the Bloodmarked Fields: the Ashen Gates, the Hall of Shifting Floors, the Runic Crucible, the Heart Vault and the Threshold of War. It ends with the Shard of Dominion in the party's hands. The books wrote it for their Blood Shard.
- **Six site adventures.** The Names of the Living, The Book That Knows Your Name, The War That Never Happened, The Red Excavation, Where the Road Falls Up, and The Forest That Knows You.
- **Ten starter hooks.** The Dead Man at the Crooked Nail, The Road That Wasn't There, The Legion Wants Its Daughter Back, The Book That Knows Your Name, The Empty Grave, The Blackveil Job, Vault of the Shard of Dominion, The Saint Who Remembers Drowning, Five Assassins One Crystal, and Someone Has Been Erased.
- **Major arcs.** The War for the Shard of Dominion, the War for the Hollow Marches, the thirteenth shard, and the Door Beyond.
- **Three ways to start.** As wanderers looking for work in Hollow Refuge, in the middle of a battle on the Bloodmarked Fields, or as strangers who share one dream of a black star and twelve crystals.

The books' fifth arc, the First Aetherfall, is left out because no reset happens in this version.

**Levels 1 to 4: the Broken World**

- Start at the Hollow Refuge, the Bloodmarked Fields or the Crossroads of Fate, as the cards intend.
- The Shard of Dominion stirs beneath the Bloodmarked Fields and all five factions send someone.
- Salvage Ashen Spire before a rival crew, with restless spirits giving warnings nobody understands.
- A grave at the Cairn of the Forgotten bears a party member's family name.
- The blind seer at the Hollow Refuge asks for a book from the Sunken Archive.

**Levels 5 to 10: the dominions**

- Carry a message from Exile Liora Voss into the Radiant Ascendancy, under Inquisitor Malrik Vael's eye.
- Win a place in Varkhaal's Great Arena to reach Darion Kael, who wants a way out of his father's war.
- Retrieve a tome from the Ember Archives before the Legion burns it.
- Cross Noctis by the Umbral Paths, where Kaelen Voris may be anyone in the party's company.
- Decide what to do with a first full shard, knowing every bearer pays its cost.

**Levels 11 to 16: the conspiracy**

- The Silent Accord shares proof that balance is enforced. The Hollow Tribunal starts erasing its members.
- An Arbiter comes for a character who has earned the Outcast of the Veil reputation.
- Stop the Ashen Covenant reaching the First Fire, or help Elder Thaelon tell them what it really is.
- The Drowned Saints find the other half of the Tidecaller's Heart.
- Step through the Hollow Verge and meet Eryndor the First Shard.

**Levels 17 to 20: beyond the Veil**

- Someone is gathering the shards to reconnect them to the Loom and restart the Cycle.
- The seals on the Nameless Sepulcher break.
- Pull the unfinished thread from the Loom of Eternity, with Vyrithar watching.
- Follow the thirteenth shard across the Black Tides to Kaelmar.
- Find the twelfth shard, which no record names, before the Hidden Ones do.
- Learn who the Forgotten One was, and decide whether the world should remember.
