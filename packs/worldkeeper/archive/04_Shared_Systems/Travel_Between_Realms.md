# Travel between realms

## Border access

Aetheris: Hollow Verge [ETH-501], Rift [LOC-901], Umbral Paths, Luminara Gate, Rift of Echoes and access on Whispering Night.

Kaelmar: root crossroads, Nexus crystal, seven seeps of glass and Rift [LOC-901]. Root pathways can transport a body and mind through the between, but a traveler can be pulled 'into many' without an anchor. A Nexus crystal requires someone to stay behind and hold the door.

## Deeper access

The deep realm requires the thirteenth shard [SHD-013] or an invitation from a resident, such as a Colosseum or court summons. Source guidance places uninvited expeditions around levels 17–20.

## Existing mechanics

Travel and Thinning mechanics are already described in both bibles. Keep those exact rules marked HOMEBREW when converting their entries; this page does not alter them.

Journey lengths in the Ethereal Realm and the full limitations of each entry method are not comprehensively defined.
