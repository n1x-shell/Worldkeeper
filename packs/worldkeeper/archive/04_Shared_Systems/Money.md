# Money

## Established currency

Both continents use standard D&D copper (cp), silver (sp), gold (gp) and platinum (pp) coins at their standard game values. The dominions and Kaelholm strike different faces on the coins, but coins are accepted by weight across political borders.

Western Kaelmar relies heavily on barter.
