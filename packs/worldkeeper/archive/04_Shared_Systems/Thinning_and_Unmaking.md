# Thinning and Unmaking

## Existing approved rule [HOMEBREW]

**Thinning** is a track from **0 to 6**. After opening a glass door, riding a root-road, communing too deeply or trading with a memory-house, make a **Charisma saving throw, DC 12 + current Thinning**. On failure, gain one level and permanently strike one named memory from the character’s notes.

At Thinning **3**, a character no longer answers to their name without effort. At **6**, they join the harmony and become an NPC.

**Names pull people back.** Hearing one’s true name from someone who knows it grants Advantage on the saving throw. Spending a month among people who use that name removes one Thinning level. Lost memories do not return.

**Root-roads.** Travel between crossroads the roots know takes minutes instead of days. Each traveller makes the Thinning saving throw unless an anchor takes it for them.

**Unmaking.** Dying in the deep Ethereal Realm [ETH-502–ETH-507] sets Thinning to 6 immediately, with the additional history-erasure and irreversible-death consequences described by Aetheris. Death Saving Throws operate normally before death; no spell, item, or wish restores that character.

**Memorial Card.** Every surviving companion writes a true sentence about the lost character. No mechanical effect.

## Canon boundaries

This combines two **existing approved** mechanics, without changing their values. The text does not add any saving throw for simply being present in the Ethereal border layer. The price an anchor pays is established in principle but not mechanically quantified [UNDEFINED].
