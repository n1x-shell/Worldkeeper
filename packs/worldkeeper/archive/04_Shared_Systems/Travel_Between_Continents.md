# Travel between continents

## Sea crossing

Ships travel across the Black Tides [LOC-902] between Kaelholm [SET-302] and unnamed Eternal Veil ports [REG-005]. The passage takes **about two weeks**. Crew extinguish lanterns over the trench where the Rift runs below the sunless sea. Regular crossings are newly established by campaign start; smugglers also use the route.

## Rift crossing [HOMEBREW]

The Rift [LOC-901] has an open end in the Hollow Marches and another by Ashvale [SET-301]. Travellers can enter one end, cross the Ethereal Realm's border layer [ETH-501], and emerge from the other. The source suggests this route for parties around level 11 and above.
