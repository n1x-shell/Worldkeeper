# Solenne, the Dawn Mother

## Established lore

**State:** Living.

**Portfolio:** Light, law and mercy.

**Cleric domains named in the setting bible:** Light; Life. These are source-provided associations, not independently validated 2024 class choices.

## Cosmological relationship [SECRET]

Each Aetherfall rewrote the gods along with the rest of Aetheris; no god remembers the prior worlds. The cause is unknown to worshippers. Refer to The Gods [COS-909] for the shared account.
