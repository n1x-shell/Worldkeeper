# Ancestries Across the World

## World-wide identification

Some peoples have distinct names and accounts of their origin on the two continents, but use the same species rules (approved reconciliation decision).

| Aetheris name | Kaelmar name | Optional rules mapping stated by the bibles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Taur’n | Minotaur | Monsters of the Multiverse; fallback reskinned Goliath |
| Shadowkin | Shadar-kai | Monsters of the Multiverse; fallback Elf with Drow lineage |
| Reptilians | Lizardfolk | Monsters of the Multiverse; fallback reskinned Dragonborn |

Humans, Elves, Dwarves and Orcs exist in both settings. Kaelmar's bible also lists Gnomes, Halflings, Goliaths, Aasimar, Tieflings and additional optional-source species; use its Peoples and Creatures chapter for the full approved list. Aetheris's Stormborn and Moonborn appear as card lineages; the approved 5.5e mappings are Goliath (storm giant ancestry) and Elf with Fey Touched respectively.

## Handling in Amsel Lore

Keep one ancestry entry per shared people, with continent-specific language and origin sections. Do not silently declare the two continents' origin stories identical. Species options outside the 2024 core remain labelled **OUTSIDE 2024 CORE**. These are the source bibles' mappings, not newly verified official legality.
