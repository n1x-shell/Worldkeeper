# The Forgotten One

## Identity
One mortal of Aetheris learned the truth, was erased and returned without memory, then learned it again over many lives. Their name, face and people remain UNDEFINED.

## Established acts [SECRET]
- In an earlier life, centuries before Kaelith, tore the Rift between continents.
- Crossed it repeatedly, returning for the last time about 270 AB.
- Left a spore-seed in a Kaelmar communion pool for a bloodline not yet born.
- Cut the shards from the Loom, ending Aetheris's Cycle.
- Tore a piece from their own Thread to make the thirteenth shard.
