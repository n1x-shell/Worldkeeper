# The Blind Seer

## Established description
Prophet of Hollow Refuge, in a windowless tower.

Put out his own eyes to see the Eternal Eclipse.

## Associated groups
Black Sun, Hollow Marches

## Wants
The truth discovered slowly enough to survive.

## Fears / pressure
The thirteenth shard being found too early.

## Secrets (GM only)
Has survived more than one Aetherfall.

## Hidden identity (GM only)
The Blind Seer is High Prophet Azhir of the Black Sun. He survived more than one Aetherfall and lives in the Seer's Tower under no name. Most Black Sun followers do not know where he goes between eclipses.
