# The Vale Family

## Background
A minor house of Ashen Spire that scattered when the Cycle broke. Four Vales serve four masters.

## Family members
- Brother Altharion Vale [NPC-020]
- Tobias Vale [NPC-051]
- Professor Orin Vale [NPC-007]
- Sister Elyra Vale [NPC-006]

## Established tensions
Altharion prays for the other three; Tobias watches them; Orin keeps research from Elyra, who suspects him. Each faction believes it has the only Vale worth having.
