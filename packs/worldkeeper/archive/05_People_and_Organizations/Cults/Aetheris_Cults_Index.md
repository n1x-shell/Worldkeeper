# Aetheris Cults

Eight cults are source-established. Their approved 2026 leaders are included where the bible provides them.

- The Ashen Covenant [CLT-001]
- The Black Sun [CLT-002]
- The Drowned Saints [CLT-003]
- The Bloodbound Company [CLT-004]
- The Nameless (the Nameless Choir) [CLT-005]
- The Silent Accord [CLT-006]
- The Hollow Tribunal [CLT-007]
- The Whispering Circle [CLT-008]

The Bloodbound Company name supersedes the Crimson Pact, as explicitly established.
