# The Silent Accord

## Established belief
The Aetherfall is a prison built by something older than the Hidden Ones.

## Known figures
None on the cards

## Approved leader
Scribe-Mother Ondine Marrow

## What members know (GM)
The Aetherfall was artificial; memories were manipulated; Mnemonic Relics and the Hidden Ones exist; evidence points to a thirteenth shard.

## What they do not know
The full purpose of the Cycle, the Watchers, the Loom's working, and permanent death in the Ethereal Realm.

## Organization
Cells of three to eight include Scribe, Witness, Keeper, Guide, and Silent roles.
