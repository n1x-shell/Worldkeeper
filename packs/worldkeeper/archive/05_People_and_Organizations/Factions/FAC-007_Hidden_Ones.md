# The Hidden Ones

## Identity [SECRET]

The Elders of the Ethereal Throne were the first Hidden Ones. They broke the Ethereal Crystal into twelve shards after the earlier deathless civilisation attracted the Watchers [COS-904]. They are architects and custodians of the Cycle, not gods. They are described as ancient, unseen architects who are neither gods nor mortals.

## Purpose and methods [SECRET]

The Cycle hid Aetheris from the Watchers by preventing any stable civilisation from lasting long enough to attract notice. Discarded histories were offered as tribute. The Hidden Ones redirected shards, suppressed discoveries, erased people and guided the repeated Aetherfalls so that no faction held permanent power.

With the Cycle severed by the Forgotten One [NPC-901], the Watchers have noticed. The Hidden Ones want the thirteenth shard [SHD-013] erased.

## Persons and places

- Vyrithar [NPC-002] guards the Loom of Eternity [COS-905; ETH-505].
- Vyrathus [NPC-003] tends the Crucible of Renewal [LOC-010].
- The Citadel of the Hidden [ETH-506] contains corridors and archives behind reality.
- Their true stronghold is the Shadowed Sanctum [LOC-505]. Older Aetheris listings also call it [LOC-015]; do not create a second site.

## Views and uncertainty

- The myth that the Hidden Ones *serve* those who watch is a [RUMOR]. The GM truth is that they hide the world and pay tribute.
- Their claim that the world can be free, remembered or safe, but only two at once, is not an established universal law; its truth is left to the GM [UNDEFINED]. See [COS-915].
- Individual goals, numbers, internal ranks and rules statistics are not specified.
