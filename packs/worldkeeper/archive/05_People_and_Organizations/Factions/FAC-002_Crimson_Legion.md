# Crimson Legion

## Creed
Only the strong deserve to rule.

## The world it wants
Endless war. Every settlement a fortress, every dispute a duel, an arena in every city.

## Leaders
Warlord Garron Kael, General Orana Volkash

## Promise to adherents
Strength capable of surviving anything.

## How rivals describe it
It defines strength as whatever benefits the Legion. [BELIEF: hostile perspective]

## Chief fortress
The Red Bastion, near Varkhaal

## Emblem
A crimson blade or clenched fist

## Signature forces
Shock Troopers, Centurions, Scouts, gladiators and siege companies.

## Domain and rivals
Main dominion: The Tyrant's Dominion [REG-003]. Each dominion has enclaves of the other great factions; consult its region entry. All five factions retain an interest in the shards, even though the Cycle has already ended.

## Continuity and limits
No current objectives, deployment counts or faction clocks have been established beyond the source's general position. Do not invent them as canonical ongoing events.
