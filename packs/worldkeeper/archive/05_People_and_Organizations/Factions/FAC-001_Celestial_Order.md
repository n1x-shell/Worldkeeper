# Celestial Order

## Creed
Divine rule, purity and absolute law.

## The world it wants
A golden theocracy under a sun that never sets. Undead, demons and forbidden magic erased. Dissent erased with them.

## Leaders
Highlord Theos Valcairn, Grand Theurgist Selara Dawnveil, Inquisitor Malrik Vael

## Promise to adherents
Peace through enlightened order.

## How rivals describe it
It calls obedience "harmony". [BELIEF: hostile perspective]

## Chief fortress
The Golden Aerie, near Empyrean Spire

## Emblem
A rising golden sun

## Signature forces
Radiant knights, inquisitors, seraphs and missionary healers.

## Domain and rivals
Main dominion: The Radiant Ascendancy [REG-002]. Each dominion has enclaves of the other great factions; consult its region entry. All five factions retain an interest in the shards, even though the Cycle has already ended.

## Continuity and limits
No current objectives, deployment counts or faction clocks have been established beyond the source's general position. Do not invent them as canonical ongoing events.
