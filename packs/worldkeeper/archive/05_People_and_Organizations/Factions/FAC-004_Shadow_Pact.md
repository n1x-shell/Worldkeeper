# Shadow Pact

## Creed
Rule unseen. Truth is whatever is believed.

## The world it wants
Perpetual twilight where identities shift, histories are rewritten and assassins run the cities.

## Leaders
Grandmaster Lysander Morrow, Spymaster Tobias Vale

## Promise to adherents
Freedom from permanent central control.

## How rivals describe it
It fights control by controlling everything in secret. [BELIEF: hostile perspective]

## Chief fortress
Nightglass, near Noctis

## Emblem
A broken black circle

## Signature forces
Silent Blades, illusionists, Shadow Lashers and executioners.

## Domain and rivals
Main dominion: The Eternal Veil [REG-005]. Each dominion has enclaves of the other great factions; consult its region entry. All five factions retain an interest in the shards, even though the Cycle has already ended.

## Continuity and limits
No current objectives, deployment counts or faction clocks have been established beyond the source's general position. Do not invent them as canonical ongoing events.
