# Arcane Consortium

## Creed
Reality is a puzzle to be rewritten.

## The world it wants
Limitless, unstable magic. Floating islands, shifting time, cities that move between dimensions.

## Leaders
Archmagister Veylan Orithis, Archivist Lissara Wynne

## Promise to adherents
A world no longer limited by ignorance.

## How rivals describe it
It treats catastrophe as an acceptable research cost. [BELIEF: hostile perspective]

## Chief fortress
The Nexus Collegium, near Zepharyos

## Emblem
A violet geometric eye or prism

## Signature forces
Scholars, constructs, dimensional mercenaries and Eye Beasts.

## Domain and rivals
Main dominion: The Fractured Nexus [REG-004]. Each dominion has enclaves of the other great factions; consult its region entry. All five factions retain an interest in the shards, even though the Cycle has already ended.

## Continuity and limits
No current objectives, deployment counts or faction clocks have been established beyond the source's general position. Do not invent them as canonical ongoing events.
