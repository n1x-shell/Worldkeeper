# Wildborn Clans

## Creed
Civilisation is a disease.

## The world it wants
Cities swallowed by forest. No kings, weakened magic, the law of the wild.

## Leaders
High Shaman Raethan Stormcaller, Elder Myrris Thornveil

## Promise to adherents
A world able to go on living.

## How rivals describe it
It would sacrifice cities to save trees. [BELIEF: hostile perspective]

## Chief fortress
The Verdant Crown, near the Rootspire

## Emblem
A green crown of roots

## Signature forces
Beast hunters, shamans, primal spirits and living constructs of root and bone.

## Domain and rivals
Main dominion: The Verdant Rebirth [REG-006]. Each dominion has enclaves of the other great factions; consult its region entry. All five factions retain an interest in the shards, even though the Cycle has already ended.

## Continuity and limits
No current objectives, deployment counts or faction clocks have been established beyond the source's general position. Do not invent them as canonical ongoing events.
