# Ashvale

## Overview
Ashvale was founded in 2 AB by refugees led by a woman whose palms had been burned. The town stands on the lip of the Rift [LOC-901]. It is a living lock: everyone who sleeps here is a hand on the key, though few remember why.

## Streets, services and locations
A half-grown meeting hall; the Rite Circle [LOC-301] of thirteen stones; the Bloom-Speakers’ sanctuary on the western edge; an orchard of grafts taking root on the eastern side; the east gate; the west road and its ford; and a ruined waygate [LOC-305] beneath that road.

## Three local factions
- Boundary Keepers [FAC-306], led by Orla Hesk [NPC-304], keep the rite and the song.
- Bloom-Speakers [FAC-307], led by Tevan Lin [NPC-305], teach careful communion.
- Wrights [FAC-308], led by Brenna Halvor [NPC-306], make working tools and tend the orchard.

The groups agree the rite must be kept, but disagree on almost everything else.

## Neighbouring settlements
Coldwater, Haskett's Mill, Greyfold, Low Fenn, Oakhallow, Sallow Cross, Kettleby, Brannock and Thornmere lie in the hills west and south. Their exact positions and populations are UNDEFINED. Odran Reeve [NPC-312] trades salt from Low Fenn.

## The sealed warning [SECRET]
The founder wrote a warning now held by Orla Hesk under lead beneath the meeting hall. Only each incoming Ritekeeper reads it. It names the Root Core [NPC-302] indirectly, warns of children without a husk and of thirteen keys gathered as one shape. For the exact original wording, see K: Notable NPCs, The founder's warning. The source does not give the four lines of the keeping song.

## Current pressures
At 300 AB the vaults are sealed, the Root Core sleeps, and the rites continue. The waygate offers salvage, the Eastern Tendril is watching the town, and the first seep of glass could expose the secret.
