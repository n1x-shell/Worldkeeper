# Kaelmar History and Chronology

**Source:** Kaelmar_Setting_Bible.md: History. **Canon status:** CANON, except 927 AB which is a possible future.

The Shrouding was not an invasion or a judgment. Kaelith caused it, and its own failsafes froze it half-done. Three centuries later the freeze still holds.

| When | What happened | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before Kaelith | The Forgotten One, a mortal of Aetheris, tears the Rift open from the far side, in one of their earlier lives. | Your decision |
| Before Kaelith | Unknown hands raise thirteen standing stones over a sleeping thing at the Rift. The pattern behind all later glyph-work is older than Kaelith, which "only learned to read it". | Prologue, Ch. 17 |
| Kaelith era | Kaelith builds cities, rune-gated roads and thirteen vaults, linked by the under-roads and a glyph web written in the First Grammar. Its Great Work, called Persistence, sets out to unmake death. | Ch. 1, 5, 7 |
| The Warding | Thirteen volunteers are lifted out of their bodies at Arborhold. Twelve "scatter". The seventh holds and becomes the guardian called the Seventh Ward. That mind is later divided across many guardian bodies, on purpose, so no single road can be forced on the world. | Ch. 5, 15 |
| The breaking | It starts in the Seed Vault and runs the web in seconds. Minds slip their vessels and matter starts answering will. Failsafes written into the Original Directive pause the change. The vault-keepers seal Arborhold and Cradlehorn. | Ch. 5, 12, 14 |
| The Shrouding | The sky comes down on the cities for years, and the rain carries ash. Then the Bloom grows up through the wreck and the calendar restarts as After Bloom (AB). | Ch. 1 |
| First years AB | The Continuity is founded to watch the web and wait for the old orders to wake. | Ch. 12 |
| 2 AB | Refugees led by a woman with burned hands settle at the stone ring. She teaches the children a four-line keeping song and writes the rite. Ashvale town is founded as a living lock: everyone who sleeps there is "a hand on the key". | Prologue, Ch. 16 |
| 22 AB | A Kaelholm repair crew enters Arborhold and never returns. Its log is the vault's last entry. | Ch. 3 |
| 2 to 300 AB | Kaelholm and the Spore Circle take shape. The Convergence Collective and the Ascendancy begin as small movements. Ashvale records the circle "listening" once. | Ch. 1, 10, 12, with the dates New |
| About 270 AB | The Forgotten One crosses the Rift for the last time and leaves a spore-seed in a communion pool. Then they go home and break the Cycle of Aetheris. Nobody on either continent remembers them. | Your decision |
| 300 AB | Now. Every vault is sealed. The Seventh Ward and the Root Core sleep. | New |
| 927 AB | The events of the novel: the Seventh Ward wakes, the web rings, and Ashvale turns to glass. Treat this as one possible future, which seers and the memory-house may glimpse. | Ch. 4 to 17 |
