# Peoples and Species

Source: Kaelmar_Setting_Bible.md: Peoples and creatures. Canon status: CANON.

**Peoples**

| People | Book | Place in Kaelmar |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Human | Player's Handbook | The most numerous people. Most of Ashvale and the nine settlements. |
| Dwarf | Player's Handbook | Kaelith's masons. They cut the vaults from everstone, and hold the northern highlands around Anvilreach. |
| Elf | Player's Handbook | The Spore Circle's oldest members. An elf past 300 saw the Shrouding. |
| Gnome | Player's Handbook | Scribes and glyph-wrights of Kaelholm. |
| Halfling | Player's Handbook | River people and salt traders of the south. |
| Orc | Player's Handbook | Herders of the ash barrens, and most of the Collective's Warband. |
| Goliath | Player's Handbook | Clans of the eastern mountains, and the first to meet the Ascendancy. |
| Aasimar | Player's Handbook | Children born with light under the skin, to parents the between has touched. |
| Tiefling | Player's Handbook | Descendants of the glyph-carved. Their marks are inherited splinters of the double grammar. |
| Shifter, Tabaxi, Lizardfolk, Tortle, Aarakocra | Monsters of the Multiverse | Beast-folk. Their ancestors were rewritten by spore-storms in the first years of the Bloom. Tortles and lizardfolk keep the fens, and aarakocra the eastern peaks. |
| Minotaur, Centaur | Monsters of the Multiverse | Beast-folk of the barrens, who ride with the Warband or pull its constructs. |
| Kenku | Monsters of the Multiverse | A people who lost their names and voices to Thinning, and speak in borrowed sounds. |
| Duergar, Deep Gnome | Monsters of the Multiverse | Descendants of vault crews sealed into the under-roads at the breaking. |
| Shadar-kai | Monsters of the Multiverse | Elves who lived too long beside the between. Many serve the Continuity. |
| Goblin, Hobgoblin, Bugbear | Monsters of the Multiverse | Salvage crews, the Ascendancy's drilled ranks, and Deepwood ambushers. |
| Kobold | Monsters of the Multiverse | Tunnel folk who tend circuit-roots and run errands for the memory-house. |

Not used as peoples: Dragonborn, the plant-and-fungus peoples and the far-realm peoples in Monsters of the Multiverse.

## Handling in Amsel
Maintain one shared ancestry page when an Aetheris name and Kaelmar name describe the same species. Do not replace this setting lore with assumed official species traits.
