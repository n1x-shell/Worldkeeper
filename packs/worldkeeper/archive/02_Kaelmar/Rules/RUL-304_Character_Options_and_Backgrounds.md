# Character Options and Backgrounds

**Mechanics classification:** HOMEBREW for setting changes and custom rules; named 2024 spell/item/stat baselines are references only, not independently verified here. Options outside the 2024 core remain labeled OUTSIDE 2024 CORE.

**Character options**

| Setting role | 5.5e expression |
| --- | --- |
| Any people of Kaelmar | Any species from the table in Peoples and creatures. |
| Guardian or vessel character | Warforged if you own a book with it, otherwise a reskinned Goliath or Dwarf. Rare in 300 AB: it would be the first to wake. |
| Glyph-wright, vault-breaker | Artificer if available, otherwise Rogue (Thief) or Wizard, with Tinker's Tools and Thieves' Tools. |
| Spore-bound druid, Bloom-Speaker | Druid (Circle of the Land, or Circle of Spores from older books) or Ranger. |
| Boundary Keeper | Bard (College of Lore) or Cleric of Sephane. The keeping song is their spellcasting focus. |
| Wright | Artificer, Fighter or Rogue with the Crafter feat. |
| Warband rider, Riftwalker | Barbarian (Path of the World Tree) or Paladin (Oath of the Ancients). |
| Glyph-burned visionary | Warlock (Great Old One patron) or Sorcerer (Aberrant Sorcery). |
| Continuity watcher | Rogue or Wizard (Diviner). |
| Ascendancy remade soldier | Fighter, with grafts described as armour and weapons. |
| Priest | Cleric of any god in the shared pantheon (see the Aetheris bible). |

**Custom backgrounds**

Each grants the usual three ability score choices, two skills, one tool and one origin feat.

| Background | Skills | Tool | Origin feat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Burned Scholar (Kaelholm exile) | Arcana, Investigation | Tinker's Tools | Skilled |
| Circle Initiate | Nature, Medicine | Herbalism Kit | Magic Initiate (Druid) |
| Stone-Singer (Boundary Keeper) | History, Performance | A musical instrument | Musician |
| Graft-Keeper (orchard Wright) | Nature, Survival | Woodcarver's Tools | Crafter |
| Glyph-Cut (Warband) | Religion, Intimidation | Calligrapher's Supplies | Tough |
| Quiet Watcher (Continuity) | Insight, Stealth | Cartographer's Tools | Alert |
| Camp Healer | Medicine, Persuasion | Herbalism Kit | Healer |
