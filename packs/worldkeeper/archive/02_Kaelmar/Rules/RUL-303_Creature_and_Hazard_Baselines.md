# Creature and Hazard Baselines

**Mechanics classification:** HOMEBREW for setting changes and custom rules; named 2024 spell/item/stat baselines are references only, not independently verified here. Options outside the 2024 core remain labeled OUTSIDE 2024 CORE.

**Monster baselines**

| Kaelmar creature | Start from this stat block | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Half-made | Zombie or Animated Armor | They act on one shared initiative and never flee. |
| Hunter | Shambling Mound | Add regeneration and a climb speed. Engulfed targets are restrained, not damaged. |
| Lesser guardian or iron vessel (asleep in 300 AB) | Helmed Horror or Shield Guardian | Add a force ray and the ability to change shape to Medium. |
| Ward guardian | Stone Golem or Iron Golem | Add telepathy with other fragments and core fire. |
| Flesh vessel | Flesh Golem | Calm and curious, not berserk. |
| Warband walker | Mammoth or Triceratops | Treat as a mount with a rider's glyph aura. |
| A war-vessel | A mobile location, not a monster | Give it rooms, a dais and lair-style actions. |
| Thinking rot | Hazard | Difficult terrain. A controlling will can restrain creatures in it. |
| Spore-storm | Hazard | Constitution save, or Poisoned. A critical failure adds a Thinning save. |
| Dead zone | Hazard | Necrotic damage each hour without plates. Hostile plants without adaptive spores. |
| The glass | Hazard | No stat block. Attackers make the Thinning save at disadvantage. |
