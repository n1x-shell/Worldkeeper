# Kaelmar

## Identity

Kaelmar is a post-collapse fantasy continent, three hundred years after the Shrouding. Kaelith's Great Work attempted to move minds between bodies and conquer death. Its failure joined spellwork, flesh and forest. Failsafes paused the transformation; the Bloom grew through the ruins. The setting's central conflict asks who may choose what shape another life takes.

## Layout

Ashvale [SET-301] lies at the north-western Rift [LOC-901], with nine settlements west and south of it. The north has cold highlands and ice; the south has fens, lakes and rivers; the centre is Bloom country above the under-roads [LOC-303]. The eastern Deepwood [REG-301] holds Arborhold; the eastern mountains [REG-302] hold Cradlehorn. Kaelholm [SET-302], the largest city, stands in the far east near the Black Tides. The Rift to east-coast scale is about 1,500 km.

Each vault's key is a shape of willing minds. Hollowbell, with thirteen voices, is the documented exception to the three-minds pattern. At 300 AB all thirteen vaults remain sealed.

## Factions and pressures

Kaelholm wants the old works catalogued. The Spore Circle tends the Bloom and its Eastern Tendril burns Kaelith work. The Continuity watches the old web. The Convergence Collective advocates voluntary completion of the change, while the Ascendancy wants rule by a chosen few. In Ashvale, Boundary Keepers, Bloom-Speakers and Wrights disagree on how to keep their town safe.

## Magic and supernatural conditions

Kaelith glyph-work [COS-302] and the Bloom [COS-301] express a common grammar of call and answer. Their cost is blood, memory or self. The glass [COS-303] exists in limited seeps. Under-roads and root paths reach the Ethereal Realm's border layer.

## Present conditions

The Seventh Ward [NPC-301] and the Root Core [NPC-302] sleep. Ashvale's keeping rite continues. Ships from Aetheris have recently begun to reach Kaelholm. The 927 AB novel is a possible future, not the current world.
