# The Glass

## Established nature
The glass is made, not grown. At 300 AB it appears only in small seeps at seven places where the sleeping change approaches the surface.

## Practice and boundaries
A glass door answers intention. An open hand is asked a question; a fist is taken. Whoever opens a door comes back with slightly less of themselves. Opening a glass door requires the established Thinning save. The seven sites’ precise positions are UNDEFINED.
