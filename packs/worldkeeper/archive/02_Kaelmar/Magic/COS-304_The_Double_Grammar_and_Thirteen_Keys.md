# The Double Grammar and Thirteen Keys

## Established nature
Kaelmar’s glyph-work and living Bloom are expressions of one pattern of call and answer, older than Kaelith. The makers seeded its fragments into bloodlines.

## Practice and boundaries
The thirteen vault keys are shapes of joined minds, not objects. Blood, glyph and spore comprise the Seed Vault’s key. Consent is required. Hollowbell is the thirteen-voice exception. See the vault key index and COS-913 for cosmic context.
