# The Bloom

## Established nature
The Bloom is a weather of minds in the mycelium, not a single speaking mind. It thinks by pulse and growth. Communion opens a person to it, at a cost in self; those who go too deep may cease to know where they end.

## Practice and boundaries
Spore-bound people listen through roots, read memories in old mycelia, grow living houses and heal with membranes that remember the body’s intended form. The membranes cannot take iron. Adaptive spores permit temporary passage through hostile Bloom. The Bloom drank Kaelith constructs and learned from them; circuit-roots and Arbors followed.
