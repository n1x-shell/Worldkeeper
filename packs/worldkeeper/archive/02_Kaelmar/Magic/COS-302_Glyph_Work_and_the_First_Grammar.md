# Glyph-Work and the First Grammar

## Established nature
Kaelith glyph-work follows a First Grammar: intention wakes resonance; resonance makes a pattern; pattern becomes the world. Kaelith doors listen, and outer gates may demand thirteen notes, a tuning fork, a key or marked blood.

## Practice and boundaries
Glyph-scars can be carved into flesh, and some spontaneously grow. Everstone does not rot; hatch-metal drives roots away. At the table, this magic uses ordinary 2024 arcane spells where the bible maps them. See the Kaelmar glyph-work rule reference, RUL-302.

## Mechanics note
The original bible supplies the setting homebrew and spell mappings. The standalone page does not introduce new mechanics.
