# The Continuity

## Aim
The change guided by minds awake to it.

## Methods and identity
A hidden order since the first years AB. "Observation without exposure." Clean outposts, hatch-metal, seventeen locks cut for the key's shape.

## Status at 300 AB
Hidden and patient. It has watched a silent web for three hundred years. Led by Warden Ilsabet Crane.

## Associated people and places
Ilsabet Crane [NPC-309]

## Tensions
It fears premature changes to the web and avoids public exposure.
