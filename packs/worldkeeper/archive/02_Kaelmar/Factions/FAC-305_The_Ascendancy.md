# The Ascendancy

## Aim
A ladder: a few minds raised through the change, the rest held beneath them.

## Methods and identity
Drilled columns, siege constructs and soldiers remade with iron and graft.

## Status at 300 AB
A doctrine inside a mercenary legion in the eastern mountains, not yet an army. It is searching for Cradlehorn. Led by Marshal Dace Corvane.

## Associated people and places
Dace Corvane [NPC-311]

## Tensions
It fights the Collective’s ideal of consent, and searches for Cradlehorn [VLT-304].

## Relationships
Kaelmar [CON-301]; the relevant settlements and vaults listed above. This is a permanent faction entry; changing agendas during a campaign belong in Tome.
