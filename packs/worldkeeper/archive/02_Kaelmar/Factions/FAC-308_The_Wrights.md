# The Wrights

## Aim
Tools that work.

## Methods and identity
Ashvale's artisans, called Technologists in the novel. They make warding stones, listening bowls, walkers and grafted orchards.

## Status at 300 AB
Led by Chief Wright Brenna Halvor.

## Associated people and places
Brenna Halvor [NPC-306]

## Tensions
They need usable salvage from the waygate while other factions fear waking old work.
