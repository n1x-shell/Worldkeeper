# The Convergence Collective

## Aim
The change finished as first intended, chosen and never forced.

## Methods and identity
A faith with Root Texts, elders and "cycles". Its Warband rides half-living constructs. Members are cut with glyphs and grafts.

## Status at 300 AB
A young faith. Its first Riftwalker, Maro Grane, is still writing the Root Texts.

## Associated people and places
Maro Grane [NPC-310]

## Tensions
It rejects the Ascendancy’s hierarchy and argues that the change must be freely chosen.

## Relationships
Kaelmar [CON-301]; the relevant settlements and vaults listed above. This is a permanent faction entry; changing agendas during a campaign belong in Tome.
