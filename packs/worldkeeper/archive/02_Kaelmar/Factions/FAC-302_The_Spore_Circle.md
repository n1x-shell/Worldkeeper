# The Spore Circle

## Aim
To tend the conversation between people and the Bloom.

## Methods and identity
Druids bound by communion, ranked in Tendrils. The Sixth Tendril goes deepest. The Eastern Tendril burns Kaelith work and carries lamps grown in gourds.

## Status at 300 AB
Well established. Its oldest elves saw the Shrouding as children. Elder Sorrel leads the Eastern Tendril.

## Associated people and places
Elder Sorrel [NPC-307]

## Tensions
Its Eastern Tendril opposes Kaelholm’s vault expeditions.

## Relationships
Kaelmar [CON-301]; the relevant settlements and vaults listed above. This is a permanent faction entry; changing agendas during a campaign belong in Tome.
