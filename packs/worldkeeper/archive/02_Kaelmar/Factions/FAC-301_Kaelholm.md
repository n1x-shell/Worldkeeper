# Kaelholm

## Aim
The change turned back and the world sorted "into its drawers".

## Methods and identity
A scholar corps, monasteries, archives and tribunals of elders in grey. It "burns" offenders by scraping their names off every roll.

## Status at 300 AB
The strongest power on the continent. It wants every vault opened and catalogued. Led by Archivist-Elder Venn Tull.

## Associated people and places
Venn Tull [NPC-308]

## Tensions
It seeks to open and catalogue every vault; the Eastern Tendril burns Kaelith work on sight.

## Relationships
Kaelmar [CON-301]; the relevant settlements and vaults listed above. This is a permanent faction entry; changing agendas during a campaign belong in Tome.
