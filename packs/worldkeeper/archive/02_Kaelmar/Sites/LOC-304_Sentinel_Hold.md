# Sentinel Hold

## Established description
A hidden Continuity base lies in a scraped hillside near the memory-house, with pruned roots, a light-map of the web, guardian cradles and a cell that silences root and glyph.

## Connections and use
Warden Ilsabet Crane [NPC-309] runs it. About 12 km north of the memory-house by tunnel and about 70 km west of Cradlehorn.
