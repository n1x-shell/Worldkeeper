# The Survey Garden

## Established description
An abandoned Kaelith herbarium was consumed by its own test plots. Its plants record what passes them.

## Connections and use
The garden is three days from Arborhold’s exit. The nearest under-road crossing is about 170 km south and a little west through the between.
