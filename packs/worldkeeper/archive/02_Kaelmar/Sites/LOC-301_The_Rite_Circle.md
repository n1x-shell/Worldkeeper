# The Rite Circle

## Established description
Thirteen leaning stones stand within Ashvale. They are older than Kaelith’s cities, and no frost falls inside the ring. They are a lock, not an altar.

## Connections and use
The founder [NPC-303] established Ashvale at the ring in 2 AB and taught the keeping song. The stones contain workings in the First Grammar. Their exact operation is SECRET; the four-line song is UNDEFINED.
