# The Under-Roads

## Established description
Ancient glyph-lit tunnels link the thirteen vaults. Rune-sleds once ran through them, and circuit-roots hold up many galleries.

## Connections and use
The Bloom cathedral is a hall of root columns at a tunnel junction, and must be asked for passage. At a rot crossroads the roots can carry travellers through the between [ETH-501], at a cost.
