# The Dead Zone

## Established description
Crimson and black-crystal Bloom lies over a split Kaelith forge-heart between the gate and Arborhold. Its burn comes and goes.

## Connections and use
Travellers need warded plates and adaptive spores. The source describes the hazard but gives no independently verified creature stat block.
