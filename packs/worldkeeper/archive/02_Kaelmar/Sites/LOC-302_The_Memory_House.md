# The Memory-House

## Established description
A chapel-sized sphere hangs in a root-lit canyon, about 800 km south-east of Ashvale, near the foot of the eastern mountains. It holds three centuries of gathered minds.

## Connections and use
The house seeks new experiences and trades knowledge for how the visitor saw a memory. Nib [NPC-313] tends its roots and explains the price. Sentinel Hold [LOC-304] is about 12 km north by tunnel.
