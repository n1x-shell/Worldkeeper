# Ashvale Waygate

## Established description
A ruined under-road halt lies beneath Ashvale’s west road on the old line from Cradlehorn.

## Connections and use
The waygate is salvage country at the campaign’s beginning. Brenna Halvor [NPC-306] wants usable material from it.
