# The Southern Fens, Lakes and River Country

## Established geography
Southern Kaelmar consists of fens, lakes and river country. Halfling traders use the waterways.

## Sites and inhabitants
Mirewell [VLT-302] is in the fens, Glassmere [VLT-312] in the lakes, and Hollowbell [VLT-313] in the river country. Tortles and lizardfolk keep the fens.
