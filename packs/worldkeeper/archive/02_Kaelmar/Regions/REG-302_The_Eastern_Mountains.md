# The Eastern Mountains

## Established geography
Mountain country east of the Deepwood; Cradlehorn [VLT-304] lies in it.

## Sites and inhabitants
Cradlehorn [VLT-304] is about 70 km east of Sentinel Hold [LOC-304]. Goliath clans live in the mountains, where the Ascendancy began as a mercenary doctrine.
