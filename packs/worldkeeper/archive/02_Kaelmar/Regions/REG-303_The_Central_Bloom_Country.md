# The Central Bloom Country

## Established geography
Open Bloom country lies over the under-roads [LOC-303]. The memory-house [LOC-302] lies east of centre, toward the eastern mountains.

## Sites and inhabitants
Sentinel Hold [LOC-304] sits about 12 km north of the memory-house by tunnel. Deepcall [VLT-308] lies deep below the centre.
