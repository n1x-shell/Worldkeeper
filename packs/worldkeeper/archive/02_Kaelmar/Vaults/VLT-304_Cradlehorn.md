# Cradlehorn

## Overview
Cradlehorn is one of the thirteen Kaelith vaults, all sealed at the campaign opening in 300 AB.

**Established region:** The eastern mountains.  
**Strand of the Great Work:** Growing and building vessels.

## Site details
In the eastern mountains, the vault of vessel-making has vat-halls, the Vessel Nexus and a shielded sanctuary. Its vessels remain asleep. The Ascendancy seeks the site.

## Its key [HOMEBREW]
**Name / shape:** A body, a mind, and the one who lets them go.  
**What it asks:** Someone must release a person they want to keep.

At a vault heart, the key is made of joined minds acting awake, willing and honestly. Except for Hollowbell, three participate. A lie, charm or forced hand keeps the heart closed for one day. No ability check substitutes for the required participation.

## What opening does
Opening the heart wakes the vault’s strand. The bibles do not define the full outcomes for every individual vault. Opening all thirteen hearts together in one shape wakes the whole web and the Root Core [NPC-302].
