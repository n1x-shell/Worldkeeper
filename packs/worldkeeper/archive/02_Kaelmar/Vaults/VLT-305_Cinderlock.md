# Cinderlock

## Overview
Cinderlock is one of the thirteen Kaelith vaults, all sealed at the campaign opening in 300 AB.

**Established region:** The ash barrens, east of centre.  
**Strand of the Great Work:** The bound fire that powers the web.

## Site details
Its bound fire powered the web before the Shrouding. No further interior rooms or original occupants are named.

## Its key [HOMEBREW]
**Name / shape:** A fire, someone it burned, and someone who forgave it.  
**What it asks:** An enemy reconciled.

At a vault heart, the key is made of joined minds acting awake, willing and honestly. Except for Hollowbell, three participate. A lie, charm or forced hand keeps the heart closed for one day. No ability check substitutes for the required participation.

## What opening does
Opening the heart wakes the vault’s strand. The bibles do not define the full outcomes for every individual vault. Opening all thirteen hearts together in one shape wakes the whole web and the Root Core [NPC-302].
