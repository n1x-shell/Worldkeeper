# Hollowbell

## Overview
Hollowbell is one of the thirteen Kaelith vaults, all sealed at the campaign opening in 300 AB.

**Established region:** The southern river country.  
**Strand of the Great Work:** The thirteen keys and the notes that open locks.

## Site details
The vault of the thirteen keys and notes that open locks. Unlike the other twelve keys, it needs thirteen consenting voices singing one note.

## Its key [HOMEBREW]
**Name / shape:** Thirteen voices singing one note.  
**What it asks:** The keeping song, sung by thirteen people at once.

At a vault heart, the key is made of joined minds acting awake, willing and honestly. Except for Hollowbell, three participate. A lie, charm or forced hand keeps the heart closed for one day. No ability check substitutes for the required participation.

## What opening does
Opening the heart wakes the vault’s strand. The bibles do not define the full outcomes for every individual vault. Opening all thirteen hearts together in one shape wakes the whole web and the Root Core [NPC-302].
