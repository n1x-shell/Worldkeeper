# Deepcall

## Overview
Deepcall is one of the thirteen Kaelith vaults, all sealed at the campaign opening in 300 AB.

**Established region:** Deep under the centre.  
**Strand of the Great Work:** The web itself, and roads through the between.

## Site details
Holds the web and roads through the between. Its key requires an awake participant physically in the Ethereal Realm’s border layer.

## Its key [HOMEBREW]
**Name / shape:** A voice, its answer, and a listener in the between.  
**What it asks:** One of the three must stand in the Ethereal Realm.

At a vault heart, the key is made of joined minds acting awake, willing and honestly. Except for Hollowbell, three participate. A lie, charm or forced hand keeps the heart closed for one day. No ability check substitutes for the required participation.

## What opening does
Opening the heart wakes the vault’s strand. The bibles do not define the full outcomes for every individual vault. Opening all thirteen hearts together in one shape wakes the whole web and the Root Core [NPC-302].
