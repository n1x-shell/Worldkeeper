# Watchbarrow

## Overview
Watchbarrow is one of the thirteen Kaelith vaults, all sealed at the campaign opening in 300 AB.

**Established region:** The western hills.  
**Strand of the Great Work:** Guardian cradles and the long watch.

## Site details
A guardian cradle is associated with this long-watch vault. Its interior details are UNDEFINED.

## Its key [HOMEBREW]
**Name / shape:** The watcher, the watched, and the one who sleeps.  
**What it asks:** A guardian, or someone who has kept a long vigil.

At a vault heart, the key is made of joined minds acting awake, willing and honestly. Except for Hollowbell, three participate. A lie, charm or forced hand keeps the heart closed for one day. No ability check substitutes for the required participation.

## What opening does
Opening the heart wakes the vault’s strand. The bibles do not define the full outcomes for every individual vault. Opening all thirteen hearts together in one shape wakes the whole web and the Root Core [NPC-302].
