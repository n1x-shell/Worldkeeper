# Glassmere

## Overview
Glassmere is one of the thirteen Kaelith vaults, all sealed at the campaign opening in 300 AB.

**Established region:** The southern lakes.  
**Strand of the Great Work:** The glass, which is "made, not grown".

## Site details
This vault’s strand is the glass, made rather than grown. Its key tests a person who can strike and chooses not to.

## Its key [HOMEBREW]
**Name / shape:** An open hand, a closed fist, and the choice between them.  
**What it asks:** Someone who could strike, and chooses not to.

At a vault heart, the key is made of joined minds acting awake, willing and honestly. Except for Hollowbell, three participate. A lie, charm or forced hand keeps the heart closed for one day. No ability check substitutes for the required participation.

## What opening does
Opening the heart wakes the vault’s strand. The bibles do not define the full outcomes for every individual vault. Opening all thirteen hearts together in one shape wakes the whole web and the Root Core [NPC-302].
