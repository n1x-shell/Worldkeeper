# Arborhold

## Overview
Arborhold is one of the thirteen Kaelith vaults, all sealed at the campaign opening in 300 AB.

**Established region:** The Eastern Deepwood.  
**Strand of the Great Work:** The Warding: lifting a mind out of its body.

## Site details
The site of the Warding. It has an Input Arbor, a Core Arbor, a rune gate, a marker stone, and 273 steps. Its blood threshold was cut for two bloods. A Kaelholm crew vanished there in 22 AB. The Seventh Ward [NPC-301] sleeps inside.

## Its key [HOMEBREW]
**Name / shape:** Two bloods given together, and a guardian's fire.  
**What it asks:** Two people who trust each other, and a guardian who agrees.

At a vault heart, the key is made of joined minds acting awake, willing and honestly. Except for Hollowbell, three participate. A lie, charm or forced hand keeps the heart closed for one day. No ability check substitutes for the required participation.

## What opening does
Opening the heart wakes the vault’s strand. The bibles do not define the full outcomes for every individual vault. Opening all thirteen hearts together in one shape wakes the whole web and the Root Core [NPC-302].
