# The Seed Vault

## Overview
The Seed Vault is one of the thirteen Kaelith vaults, all sealed at the campaign opening in 300 AB.

**Established region:** Under the Rift, north-west.  
**Strand of the Great Work:** The Root Core and the pattern everything else grew from.

## Site details
The Root Core [NPC-302] sleeps in the deepest vault under the eastern lip of the Rift. The thirteen stones and the living lock at Ashvale are part of its long defence.

## Its key [HOMEBREW]
**Name / shape:** Blood, glyph and spore.  
**What it asks:** What was made, what grew, and what joins them.

At a vault heart, the key is made of joined minds acting awake, willing and honestly. Except for Hollowbell, three participate. A lie, charm or forced hand keeps the heart closed for one day. No ability check substitutes for the required participation.

## What opening does
Opening the heart wakes the vault’s strand. The bibles do not define the full outcomes for every individual vault. Opening all thirteen hearts together in one shape wakes the whole web and the Root Core [NPC-302].
