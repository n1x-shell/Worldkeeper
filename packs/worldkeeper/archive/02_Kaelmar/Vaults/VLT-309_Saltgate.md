# Saltgate

## Overview
Saltgate is one of the thirteen Kaelith vaults, all sealed at the campaign opening in 300 AB.

**Established region:** The east coast.  
**Strand of the Great Work:** Far-speaking and the sea-roads.

## Site details
Its key requires a traveller from Kaelmar and a traveller from Aetheris. First intercontinental ships have only recently reached Kaelholm.

## Its key [HOMEBREW]
**Name / shape:** Two strangers from opposite shores, and the sea between.  
**What it asks:** One person of Kaelmar and one of Aetheris.

At a vault heart, the key is made of joined minds acting awake, willing and honestly. Except for Hollowbell, three participate. A lie, charm or forced hand keeps the heart closed for one day. No ability check substitutes for the required participation.

## What opening does
Opening the heart wakes the vault’s strand. The bibles do not define the full outcomes for every individual vault. Opening all thirteen hearts together in one shape wakes the whole web and the Root Core [NPC-302].
