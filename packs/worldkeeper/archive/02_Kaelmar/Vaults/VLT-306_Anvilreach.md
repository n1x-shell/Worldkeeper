# Anvilreach

## Overview
Anvilreach is one of the thirteen Kaelith vaults, all sealed at the campaign opening in 300 AB.

**Established region:** The northern highlands.  
**Strand of the Great Work:** Forging the thirteen rebirth cores.

## Site details
The thirteen rebirth cores were made for guardians, and naming the restored result is part of the repair. The vault’s interior remains UNDEFINED.

## Its key [HOMEBREW]
**Name / shape:** The smith, the ore, and the name given to what was forged.  
**What it asks:** Something made by the party and named aloud.

At a vault heart, the key is made of joined minds acting awake, willing and honestly. Except for Hollowbell, three participate. A lie, charm or forced hand keeps the heart closed for one day. No ability check substitutes for the required participation.

## What opening does
Opening the heart wakes the vault’s strand. The bibles do not define the full outcomes for every individual vault. Opening all thirteen hearts together in one shape wakes the whole web and the Root Core [NPC-302].
