# The Thirteen Vault Keys: GM Index

Canon status: CANON; mechanics HOMEBREW. Source: Kaelmar_Setting_Bible.md: Magic and technology, The thirteen keys and How the keys work in play.

A key is a choice made by joined minds rather than an object. No roll can replace the willing participants. Twelve vaults need three consenting minds. Hollowbell [VLT-313] alone requires thirteen voices. The key specifications below are also reproduced on the corresponding vault pages to make each usable in Lore.

| The Seed Vault [VLT-301] | Blood, glyph and spore | What was made, what grew, and what joins them |

## Consequences
Opening a vault heart awakens that vault's strand; all thirteen opened together in one shape wake the whole web and the Root Core. Forced participation, dishonesty or magical compulsion fail, and the vault stays closed for a day. At Glassmere, the bible adds that the glass takes whoever tried to force it.

## Play guidance
Use social, exploration and encounter checks to bring the required people together, never as substitutes for consent. No new DC or additional ritual is introduced.
