# Mirewell

## Overview
Mirewell is one of the thirteen Kaelith vaults, all sealed at the campaign opening in 300 AB.

**Established region:** The southern fens.  
**Strand of the Great Work:** Taking the impression of a living mind.

## Site details
The key requires a dream to be surrendered. The Collective’s first Riftwalker, Maro Grane, seeks witnesses for a reading of the Root Texts here in a proposed adventure.

## Its key [HOMEBREW]
**Name / shape:** The dreamer, the waker, and one who remembers both.  
**What it asks:** Someone must give up a dream to open it.

At a vault heart, the key is made of joined minds acting awake, willing and honestly. Except for Hollowbell, three participate. A lie, charm or forced hand keeps the heart closed for one day. No ability check substitutes for the required participation.

## What opening does
Opening the heart wakes the vault’s strand. The bibles do not define the full outcomes for every individual vault. Opening all thirteen hearts together in one shape wakes the whole web and the Root Core [NPC-302].
