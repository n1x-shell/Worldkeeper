# Rimeholt

## Overview
Rimeholt is one of the thirteen Kaelith vaults, all sealed at the campaign opening in 300 AB.

**Established region:** The northern ice.  
**Strand of the Great Work:** The failsafes and the Original Directive.

## Site details
Home of failsafes and the Original Directive that paused the change. Its exact text is not supplied.

## Its key [HOMEBREW]
**Name / shape:** Three who disagree, and all say yes.  
**What it asks:** Consent from people who want different things. "Nobody decides alone.".

At a vault heart, the key is made of joined minds acting awake, willing and honestly. Except for Hollowbell, three participate. A lie, charm or forced hand keeps the heart closed for one day. No ability check substitutes for the required participation.

## What opening does
Opening the heart wakes the vault’s strand. The bibles do not define the full outcomes for every individual vault. Opening all thirteen hearts together in one shape wakes the whole web and the Root Core [NPC-302].
