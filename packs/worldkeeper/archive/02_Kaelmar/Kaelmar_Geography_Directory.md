## Geographic constraints
Kaelmar is about 1,500 km from the Rift to the eastern coast. The memory-house is approximately 800 km south-east of Ashvale and east of centre, near the eastern mountains. No coordinates were assigned. The Rift has two open ends across the world but the buried and undersea fault between them is not a walkable route.
