# Kaelholm

## Overview
Kaelholm is the renamed Kaelith-East, the enclave that kept Kaelith's name, archives, scholar corps and tribunals. It is the largest city on Kaelmar and the first port for ships arriving from Aetheris.

## Authority and methods
The Kaelholm power [FAC-301] is led in 300 AB by Archivist-Elder Venn Tull [NPC-308]. It wants every vault catalogued and the change turned back. Its tribunals can punish a person by scraping their name from every roll, a practice called being "burned".

## Sites and connections
Greyhall [VLT-310], the archive of the First Grammar, lies beside Kaelholm. Saltgate [VLT-309] lies on the east coast. The Black Tides [LOC-902] are beyond the city and the far eastern coast.

## Current conditions
The first regular ships from Aetheris are arriving. Kaelholm wants to be first to examine any surviving guardian or old vault. City wards, population, port name and exact internal geography are UNDEFINED.
