# Ways Into and Out of the Ethereal Realm

## Two distinct depths

The border layer [ETH-501] is accessible by the routes below. Deep regions [ETH-502] through [ETH-507] require the thirteenth shard [SHD-013] or an invitation from an inhabitant; a Colosseum summons or Magistrate Althros’s summons qualifies. Root-roads and the Rift do **not** automatically bypass that restriction.

## Cost and limitations [HOMEBREW]

Travellers using a root-road make the Thinning save unless an anchor takes it for them. The between tends to pull what it carries “into many”; an anchor such as the seed holds travellers apart and bears a cost. See Thinning and Unmaking [SYS-907].

Exact duration of Ethereal journeys, exhaustive conditions for other routes, and whether any exit lies beyond the Black Horizon are UNDEFINED. Sylas Vaerin [NPC-064] only **claims** such an exit exists.
