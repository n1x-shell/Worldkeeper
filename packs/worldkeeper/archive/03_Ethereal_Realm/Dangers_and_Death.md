# Dangers and Death in the Ethereal Realm

## The threshold [HOMEBREW]

**Old memories.** Meeting something from a forgotten life gives a fragment of memory without a roll.

**Awakening.** Crossing the last threshold of the Hollow Verge restores every past life at once. It cannot be undone; the Hidden Ones regard the Awakened as threats.

**True Death.** In the true, deep Ethereal Realm, Death Saving Throws operate normally. No spell, item or wish returns the dead.

**Unmaking.** Someone who dies there is erased from history. Statues change and families remember another person. Awakened companions retain only a sense of loss. This is Thinning taken to 6 in a single step.

**Memorial Card.** Surviving companions each write one true sentence about the lost character. It grants no mechanical benefit.

## Border travel and Thinning [HOMEBREW]

Kaelmar’s root-roads, deeper communion and the glass can trigger Thinning. Its approved rule is transcribed into the single shared mechanics record [SYS-907]. The sources do not declare that simply entering the border layer causes True Death or Unmaking.
