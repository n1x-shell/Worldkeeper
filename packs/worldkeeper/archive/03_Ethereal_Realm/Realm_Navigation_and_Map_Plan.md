# Ethereal Realm Navigation and Map Plan

## Diagram instead of physical map

The setting bible establishes **seven regions in a sequence travellers meet them**, not coordinates, miles, or a conventional map. Build a non-scaled relationship diagram: **Hollow Verge [ETH-501] → Fields of Lost Realities [ETH-502] → Ossuary of Kings [ETH-503] → Silent Archive [ETH-504] → Loom of Eternity [ETH-505] → Citadel of the Hidden [ETH-506] → Black Horizon [ETH-507].**

This is **an ordering**, not a claim that travellers must cross each node in a straight line or that travel times are equal.

## Site groups

- Verge: Nameless Asylum [LOC-506], Hollow Verge entrance (same domain, not a second place).
- Fields: Rift’s Edge [LOC-501], Crucible of Creation [LOC-502].
- Ossuary: Fractured Throne [LOC-503], Colosseum of Echoes [LOC-504].
- Archive: the Silent Archive itself [ETH-504].
- Loom: the Loom itself [ETH-505].
- Citadel: Shadowed Sanctum [LOC-505].
- Horizon: Veil of Eternity [LOC-507], Observatory of the Last Question [LOC-508], Nameless Sepulcher [LOC-509].

## Link types

Use parent/child for region-to-site; cross-reference for resident/faction involvement; distinguish **known access route** from **deep realm access permission**. Never invent Cartesian coordinates, travel time, additional gates or additional realm boundaries.
