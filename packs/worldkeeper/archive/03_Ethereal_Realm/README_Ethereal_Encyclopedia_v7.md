# Ethereal Realm encyclopedia: v7

This package extends the previously approved world. The Ethereal Realm is one shared homebrew realm, called the *between* on Kaelmar. Its seven regions have stable IDs ETH-501 through ETH-507.

## New pages

- 7 regions under `Domains/`
- 9 subordinate named sites under `Sites/`
- 5 existing ethereal groups under `Groups/`
- An index linking the 12 pre-existing resident/keeper NPC records, without creating duplicate NPCs
- Access, death, map and navigation guides
- Shared Thinning/Unmaking [SYS-907] and Ancestries [SYS-904] references

## Canon boundaries

- No source bibles have been rewritten.
- Entity ID assignments for previously unnumbered groups and sites are organizational choices, **not** new lore.
- No new mechanics, destinations or Watcher motivations were invented.
- Use `Realm_Overview.md` as the realm overview [CON-501]; the new pages supply the detail.
- `The Hollow Verge` is ETH-501. There is no second site record merely for its entrance.
- `The Silent Archive` and `The Loom of Eternity` are each a **single** ETH domain entry also describing the place; do not create duplicate LOC pages.
- The Crucible of Creation (ethereal LOC-502) is distinct from Vyrathus’s Crucible of Renewal (Aetheris LOC-010).
- Cross-reference existing character records in `05_People_and_Organizations/NPCs/Ethereal/`; do not enter them twice.
- `The Shadowed Sanctum` is associated with ETH-506 as stated in the original regions table.
- The deep-realm requirement remains the thirteenth shard or invitation. Levels are guidelines.

## Unresolved canon

The Watchers’ purpose, the twelfth shard’s identity, the unnamed occupant of the Sepulcher, internal travel time, and any exit beyond the realm remain UNDEFINED.

## Using Amsel Lore

Make seven child region pages beneath The Ethereal Realm. Place subordinate sites beneath their parent region; create the five groups under People and Organizations, and link their records from the region pages. Manual Markdown entry only, as in the implementation guide.
