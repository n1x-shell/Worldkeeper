# The Ossuary of Kings

## Established nature

Rulers who learned the truth of the Cycle crossed here. They can truly die now for the first time in centuries, and they are afraid.

## Associated places

The Fractured Throne [LOC-503], the Colosseum of Echoes [LOC-504].

## Access and campaign considerations

A summons to the Colosseum can grant an exception to the general deep-realm access restriction. A summon is an invitation, not possession of the thirteenth shard.
