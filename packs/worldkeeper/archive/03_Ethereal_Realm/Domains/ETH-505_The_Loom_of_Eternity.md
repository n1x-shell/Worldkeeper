# The Loom of Eternity

## Established nature

A black ocean hangs vertically in nothing, crossed by billions of luminous Threads, each a life. Twelve colossal crystals pierce it, and one is dark. The living web of fate is kept by Vyrithar [NPC-002].

## Associated places

The Loom of Eternity [ETH-505] (one entry serves as region and place).

## Access and campaign considerations

The reforged Ethereal Crystal once told the Loom which pattern to impose. The Forgotten One cut the shards from the Loom, ending the Cycle. Kaelmar was never covered by it. Someone could relink the shards and restart the Cycle. See The Loom as a force [COS-905].
