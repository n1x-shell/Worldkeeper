# The Fields of Lost Realities

## Established nature

Discarded worlds pile up here. A golden cathedral lies sideways inside a Legion fortress. Travellers may encounter monuments to themselves.

## Associated places

The Rift’s Edge [LOC-501], the Crucible of Creation [LOC-502].

## Access and campaign considerations

A deep-realm region. Reaching it requires the thirteenth shard [SHD-013] or an invitation from an inhabitant. Uninvited travel is suited to levels 17–20.
