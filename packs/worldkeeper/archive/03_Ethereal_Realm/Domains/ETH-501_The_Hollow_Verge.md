# The Hollow Verge

## Established nature

The border between Aetheris and the realm. Its entrance changes: a door in an empty house or a stair under a dry well. Refugees who remember too much live inside. The warning at its last threshold reads: “Beyond this point, the world will no longer dream you back.”

## Associated places

The Hollow Verge [ETH-501] (this domain), the Nameless Asylum [LOC-506].

## Access and campaign considerations

The Hollow Verge is the border layer. Kaelmar’s between, the Rift crossing, and the root-roads connect to that layer. Reaching it by Kaelmar root-road can occur around level 5; access by the Verge or the Rift is suited to about level 11. These are level guidelines, not a universal prohibition.
