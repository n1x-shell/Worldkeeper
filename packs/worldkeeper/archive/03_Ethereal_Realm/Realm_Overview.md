# The Ethereal Realm

## One realm, two shores

Aetheris calls the passage through the Hollow Verge the Ethereal Realm. Kaelmar calls this place *the between*, where minds touch by harmony rather than distance. These are names for the same realm, not separate planes. This is a homebrew setting cosmology and not the default D&D Ethereal Plane.

## Border layer [ETH-501]

The Hollow Verge, the between, the Rift crossing and Kaelmar's root-roads belong to the border layer. A Kaelmar party may reach it by root-road from around level 5; the Verge or Rift route is suited to about level 11 and above. These levels are guidance, not a universal access prohibition. The Rift's two open ends meet here.

Uninvited travel to the deep realm is intended for levels 17–20. Access requires the thirteenth shard [SHD-013] or an invitation from an inhabitant, such as a summons to the Colosseum or a court summons.

## Routes

From Aetheris: the Hollow Verge, Rift, Umbral Paths, Luminara Gate, Rift of Echoes and sites open on Whispering Night. From Kaelmar: root crossroads, Nexus crystal, seven seeps of glass and Rift. Access conditions for each route should remain on its own eventual entry rather than be presumed identical.

## Residents and groups

The Cycle Breakers, Architects of Eternity, Veilborn, Titans of the Rift and Echoborn occupy the realm. Magistrate Althros keeps its laws. The Watchers are beyond the Black Horizon, not ordinary inhabitants with a monster stat block.

## Dangers and rules [HOMEBREW]

Kaelmar's between threatens to pull travelers 'into many' without an anchor. In the deep realm, death is permanent and produces Unmaking, defined as Thinning taken to six in one step [SYS-907]. The source bibles contain additional existing mechanics; no new rules are introduced here.

## Undefined

Travel durations within the realm, the nature and aims of the Watchers, and any exit beyond the realm remain UNDEFINED.
