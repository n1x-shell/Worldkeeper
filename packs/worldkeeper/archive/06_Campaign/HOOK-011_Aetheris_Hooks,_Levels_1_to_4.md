# Aetheris Hooks, Levels 1 to 4

## Levels

1 to 4.

The Broken World tier: scavenging in the heartland.

## Hooks

1. Start at Hollow Refuge [SET-001], the Bloodmarked Fields [LOC-002] or the Crossroads of Fate [LOC-003], as the cards intend.
2. The Shard of Dominion [SHD-001] stirs beneath the Bloodmarked Fields, and all five factions send someone.
3. Salvage Ashen Spire before a rival crew does, while restless spirits give warnings nobody understands.
4. A grave at the Cairn of the Forgotten bears a party member's family name.
5. The Blind Seer [NPC-001] at Hollow Refuge asks for a book from the Sunken Archive.
