# Vault of the Shard of Dominion

## Premise

A five-room starter dungeon under the Bloodmarked Fields [LOC-002]. It ends with the Shard of Dominion [SHD-001] in the party's hands. The books wrote it for their Blood Shard. Here it is the Shard of Dominion.

## Rooms

1. The Ashen Gates.
2. The Hall of Shifting Floors.
3. The Runic Crucible.
4. The Heart Vault.
5. The Threshold of War.

The bible names the rooms and nothing more. Room contents come from the original book, if you have it, or from your prep. [UNDEFINED]

## Before and after

- **Before.** The Red Excavation [HOOK-004] leads into it.
- **After.** The War for the Shard of Dominion, in Major Arcs and Ways to Start [HOOK-010].

## Levels

A starter, so levels 1 to 4. The bible gives no exact level. [PROPOSED]

## Running notes

- All five factions are sending someone to the Fields. Captain Marrek Vaal [NPC-005] wants the shard.
- Whoever carries the shard pays its cost. See the Shard of Dominion [SHD-001].
- Site difficulty is 12. Use it as the DC for scene checks.
