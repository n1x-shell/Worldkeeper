# Kaelmar Hooks, Levels 1 to 4

## Levels

1 to 4.

In 300 AB nothing has woken yet, so the campaign is about who wakes it, and how. This tier stays in Ashvale and the nine settlements.

## Hooks

1. Chief Wright Brenna Halvor [NPC-306] pays for salvage from the waygate [LOC-305] under the west road, where something still hums.
2. A child in Coldwater dreams of thirteen stones and wakes with frost on her hands.
3. A seep of glass opens in a Sallow Cross barn and takes in a cow. Orla Hesk [NPC-304] wants it hidden. Tevan Lin [NPC-305] wants it studied.
4. Escort Odran Reeve's [NPC-312] salt barge through the fens, past a drifting half-made.
5. A green gourd-lamp appears at the treeline every night. The Eastern Tendril of the Spore Circle [FAC-302] is watching Ashvale.
