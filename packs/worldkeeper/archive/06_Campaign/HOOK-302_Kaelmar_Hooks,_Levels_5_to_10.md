# Kaelmar Hooks, Levels 5 to 10

## Levels

5 to 10.

The under-roads and the vaults.

## Hooks

1. Archivist-Elder Venn Tull [NPC-308] hires the party to open Arborhold's [VLT-303] rune gate, which no one has passed since 22 AB.
2. Marshal Dace Corvane's [NPC-311] scouts are mapping the mountains for Cradlehorn [VLT-304]. Get there first, or take his coin.
3. Ask the memory-house [LOC-302] where the nine lost vaults lie, and decide who pays its price.
4. Maro Grane [NPC-310] wants a witness for the first reading of the Root Texts at Mirewell [VLT-302].
5. A ship from Aetheris docks at Kaelholm [SET-302] carrying a Consortium surveyor and a map of the Rift.
