# Kaelmar Hooks, Levels 11 to 16

## Levels

11 to 16.

The sleepers.

## Hooks

1. Reach the Seventh Ward [NPC-301] before Kaelholm [SET-302] does, and decide whether to wake it.
2. Open Rimeholt [VLT-307] and read the Original Directive, which says what the failsafes are waiting for.
3. Find the communion pool where the spore-seed lies, and learn that someone from Aetheris left it.
4. The Ascendancy [FAC-305] breaks into Cradlehorn [VLT-304], and the first vessel opens its eyes.
