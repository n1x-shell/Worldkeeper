# Crossing Between the Continents

## Overview

Every adventure in the shared list can start a party anywhere and carry it anywhere else. These are the routes. The full travel rules are in Travel between continents [SYS-905] and Travel between realms [SYS-906].

| Way | Levels | How it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Passage over the Black Tides [LOC-902] | Any | Ships run between Kaelholm [SET-302] and the ports of the Eternal Veil [REG-005]. The crossing takes about two weeks in the dark, and the crew lights no lanterns over the trench. |
| The Crossroads of Fate [LOC-003] | Any | The roads around the waystone shift. Now and then one comes out on the other continent, and the travellers on it don't know until they ask the date. |
| The Umbral Paths | 5+ | The Shadow Pact's [FAC-004] roads lead anywhere, for a price. |
| Root-roads | 5+ | Kaelmar's root crossroads [LOC-310] carry travellers through the between. It costs a Thinning save [SYS-907]. |
| The Hollow Verge [ETH-501] | 11+ | Into the Ethereal Realm, then out wherever the realm allows. |
| The Rift [LOC-901] | 11+ | Go in at one open end, cross the Ethereal Realm, and come out at the other. |
