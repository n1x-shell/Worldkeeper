## Three ways to start

1. **Wanderers.** The party arrives in Hollow Refuge [SET-001] looking for work.
2. **In the fight.** The party starts in the middle of a battle on the Bloodmarked Fields [LOC-002].
3. **The dream.** The party are strangers who all had the same dream of a black star and twelve crystals. It points at the Thirteenth Shard [SHD-013].
