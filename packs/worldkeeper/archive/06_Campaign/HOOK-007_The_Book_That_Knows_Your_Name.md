# The Book That Knows Your Name

## Premise

One of the six site adventures from the books. It takes place at The Sunken Archive, in the Hollow Marches [REG-001].

## Hidden truth [SECRET]

Contradictory historical records are all authentic.

## Levels and difficulty

- **Levels.** The books set none. The Hollow Marches is the level 1 to 4 tier, so start there. [PROPOSED]
- **Site difficulty.** 13. The books' scene target works unchanged as a 5.5e DC.

## Links onward

It is also one of the Ten Starter Hooks [HOOK-002]. The Shard of Knowledge [SHD-011] lies in the archive's deepest stacks.
