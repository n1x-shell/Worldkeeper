# Campaign Directory

## How to use this in Tome

Each page here is one Tome adventure or one list of hooks. The sources give premises, not scenes. Build scenes when you prep, and mark new details [PROPOSED] until you approve them.

The books and cards support a full campaign: scavenging in the heartland, faction wars in the dominions, the conspiracy behind them, and the Ethereal Realm at the end. Kaelmar's campaign is about who wakes the sleepers, and how. The shared list lets any party go anywhere.
