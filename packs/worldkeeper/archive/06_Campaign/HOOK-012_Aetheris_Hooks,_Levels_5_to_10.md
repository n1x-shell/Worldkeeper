# Aetheris Hooks, Levels 5 to 10

## Levels

5 to 10.

The dominions tier: faction wars.

## Hooks

1. Carry a message from Exile Liora Voss [NPC-023] into the Radiant Ascendancy [REG-002], under Inquisitor Malrik Vael's [NPC-017] eye.
2. Win a place in Varkhaal's Great Arena to reach Darion Kael [NPC-033], who wants a way out of his father's war.
3. Get a tome out of the Ember Archives before the Legion burns it.
4. Cross Noctis by the Umbral Paths, where Kaelen Voris [NPC-046] may be anyone in the party's company.
5. Decide what to do with a first full shard, knowing every bearer pays its cost.
