4. Follow the Thirteenth Shard [SHD-013] across the Black Tides [LOC-902] to Kaelmar [CON-301].
5. Find the twelfth shard [SHD-012], which no record names, before the Hidden Ones [FAC-007] do.
6. Learn who the Forgotten One [NPC-901] was, and decide whether the world should remember.
