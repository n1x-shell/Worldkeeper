Note: Adventure hooks from the residents of the world's landmarks, vaults and fortresses (GM only). Each hook grows from a resident's secret; the matching mystery in the Mysteries tool has the clues. Pick one, or roll.

# Hooks from the residents: the fortresses
1. **Stone for the Sanctuary** (levels 1 to 4). Odile hires the party to escort a quarry cart up the hill. Legion scouts want to know what the stone is for, and so, soon, will the Refuge. See Sister-Captain Odile Brightwater [NPC-405], at the Sanctuary of the Last Light [LOC-036].
2. **A Deserter at the Door** (levels 1 to 4). A wounded Legion deserter begs the party to get him to Brother Abelard before the Legion catches up. See Brother Abelard Ashgrove [NPC-406], at the Sanctuary of the Last Light [LOC-036].
3. **The Night Watch** (levels 1 to 4). Durro pays the party to sit the night watch over the trenches, and to tell nobody what they see. See Duel-Marshal Durro Oruun [NPC-407], at the Warlord's Rest [LOC-037].
4. **Read While I Measure** (levels 1 to 4). Pim needs someone to read in the Sunken Archive while he measures the tower. He won't say what he expects to happen. See Pim Quillfeather [NPC-408], at the Nexus Outpost [LOC-038].
5. **Who Sold the List** (levels 1 to 4). Vess the Grey hires the party to find out who is selling the Veiled Chamber's guest list. See Merrin Rell [NPC-409], at the Veiled Chamber [LOC-039].
6. **The Old Wolf's Curse** (levels 5 to 10). Ghent asks the party to find whoever laid the old wolf's curse, before the old wolf dies. See Ghent Two-Wolves [NPC-410], at the Wolf's Den [LOC-040].
7. **What the Griffons Fear** (levels 5 to 10). Ysabeau quietly asks the party to go to Empyrean Spire and find out what the griffons are afraid of. See Griffon-Master Ysabeau Lark [NPC-411], at the Golden Aerie [LOC-041].
8. **Keep the Beat** (levels 5 to 10). Okko has fallen ill. Someone has to keep the beat on the deep drum for three nights, without missing once. See Drum-Sergeant Okko Grall [NPC-412], at the Red Bastion [LOC-044].
9. **Below the Lowest Terrace** (levels 5 to 10). Ilda needs someone small, or brave, to go below the lowest terrace and see who's working down there. See Forge-Mother Ilda Cragg [NPC-413], at the Anvil of Dominion [LOC-047].
10. **The Sun for Kip** (levels 1 to 4). Kip begs the party to smuggle him up to see the sun. The forge-masters say he's needed, and they mean it. See Kip [NPC-414], at the Anvil of Dominion [LOC-047].
11. **The Student Who Failed** (levels 5 to 10). A student's family pays the party to find out what happened to their daughter after she failed the final test. See Dean Melisande Ithil [NPC-415], at the Nexus Collegium [LOC-048].
12. **Proof** (levels 5 to 10). Bix wants proof, and needs the party to go through a Collegium door that opens somewhere else. See Bix Tallowmere [NPC-416], at the Nexus Collegium [LOC-048].
13. **The Last Chapter** (levels 11 to 16). Saska asks the party to read the newest chapter of the book that's writing her, before she does. See Warden Saska Ironvow [NPC-417], at the Repository of the Unwritten [LOC-051].
14. **A Stolen Face** (levels 5 to 10). Someone is walking around Noctis wearing a party member's face, or a friend's. The trail leads to Nightglass. See Lisle Vantarro [NPC-418], at Nightglass [LOC-052].
15. **A Mask at the Door** (levels 5 to 10). A stone mask with a party member's face is left at their door. The Mask-Warden wants to know why it appeared. See The Mask-Warden [NPC-419], at the Veilborn Archive [LOC-054].
16. **Where the Crown Walks** (levels 11 to 16). Bramwell asks the party to scout ahead of the Verdant Crown and find out what it's walking toward. See Rootwarden Bramwell Barkmantle [NPC-420], at the Verdant Crown [LOC-055].
