Note: Encounters on the crossing of the Black Tides, and with the people of Kaelmar's far north, southern fens and ash barrens.

# Encounters: Crossing the Black Tides
Region: LOC-902
1. **Sign.** At dusk, Tollrock's light goes out behind the ship. The crew stops talking above a whisper.
2. **Travellers.** The *Lodestar* passes the other way. Captain Wilhelmina Corr hails across the water for news, and asks the party's names, as she asks everyone's.
3. **Fight.** A lantern shows for a moment, and 2 Drowned Saints [CRE-032] come up the anchor chain.
4. **Fight.** A Pact cutter, lanternless, comes alongside to "inspect the cargo": 2 Silent Blade Agents [CRE-014] who want the dark toll a second time.
5. **Strange.** The Kneelers stand on the horizon. Count them going out. Count them coming back.
6. **Travellers.** The *Long Dark*, the first regular ship, crossing home. Its crew trades news for tobacco and won't talk about the trench.
7. **Fight.** Something hunts under the keel: 1d4 Hunter Sharks (Monster Manual), drawn by a sailor's bleeding hand.
8. **Travellers.** Kittiwake fishers far from home, selling fish and asking in Tidecant what Aetheris is like.
9. **Strange.** Every lantern aboard lights itself for a heartbeat, then goes out. The water round the ship goes very still.
10. **Fight.** One of the Drowned Saints' lanternless ships, with a Cultist Fanatic and 4 Cultists (Monster Manual) aboard, come to offer the passengers to the sea.
11. **Sign.** Wreckage drifts past: a mast, a ship's bell with no clapper, a coat with someone's name stitched inside.
12. **Strange.** A voice from the bilges calls one passenger by their true name. Nobody is down there.

# Encounters: Kaelmar's far places, residents on the road
Region: CON-301
1. **Travellers.** Kaunna [NPC-467], walking south for supplies, who says the weather will turn in three days. It will.
2. **Travellers.** Harbour-mother Hettie Goodbarrel [NPC-468]'s barge, singing its way downriver. She offers passage for news.
3. **Travellers.** Elder Gorrum [NPC-469], a day late with the answer to a question the party hasn't asked yet.
4. **Travellers.** Speaker Ghora Coalsong [NPC-470]'s caravan bound for Ashgather, stopped to settle a quarrel by the road. Ghora asks the party to witness the judgement.
5. **Travellers.** A Hearthdeep iron caravan heading south, dwarves with crossbows, who trade iron for food and nothing else.
6. **Fight.** Bandits who've heard the Hearthdeep caravans carry iron: 4 Bandits and a Bandit Captain (Monster Manual), waiting at a ford.
7. **Sign.** Steam on the horizon in the ash barrens: Cinderwell, and the herds walking toward it.
8. **Strange.** The river market at Tumblewater has moved overnight. Where it stood is a ford, and a single forgotten crate.
9. **Fight.** An Eastern Tendril patrol that wants to burn the party's Kaelith salvage: a Druid and 2 Scouts (Monster Manual). They fight only if refused.
10. **Travellers.** Nib [NPC-313], on a rare errand from the Memory-House, asking travellers for one memory each, and naming the price first.
11. **Sign.** A small cairn of white stones by the road: Kaunna's mark for someone who didn't listen.
12. **Strange.** Passing Mossback, one of the island-turtles opens an eye, looks at the party, and closes it again.
