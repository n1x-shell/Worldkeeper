# Crystal Shard

One of the shards of a broken source of world-changing power, each with its own gift and its own price. Use an Artifact for a powerful object that is not part of the set.

## Facts

- **Power**: Say what it does, in a few words. Example: Bends others to the wielder's will (Shard of Dominion)
- **Cost**: Say what it takes from the wielder. Example: The weight of everything the wielder must now control (Shard of Dominion)
- **Closest interested party**: Name who wants it most. Example: All five factions (Shard of Dominion)
- **Signature power**: Name the spell or effect it gives at the table. Example: Command at will. Dominate Monster once a day. (Shard of Dominion)
- **Cost at the table**: Give the price in game terms. Example: One level of Exhaustion each dawn while any creature is under its control. (Shard of Dominion)

## In brief

> Guidance: The shard in one line that a player could remember. Pair the gift with the price.
> Example (Shard of Shadows, SHD-009): "Walk unseen, and be forgotten."

## What it does

> Guidance: Its power in plain words, as the stories describe it. Say what it does before saying how it works in the rules.
> Example (Shard of Eternity, SHD-005): "The Shard of Eternity suspends moments and holds back fate."

## What it costs

> Guidance: What it takes from whoever carries or uses it. The price should grow with use.
> Example (Shard of Shadows, SHD-009): "The more they fade, the harder they are to remember."

## Who wants it

> Guidance: The groups and people hunting for it, and why. This is what makes carrying it dangerous.
> Example (Shard of Shadows, SHD-009): "The Nameless Choir wants it. Of course they do."

## What people say

> Guidance: Rumours about where it is and what it has done. Some should lead the party towards it.
> Example (Shard of Dominion, SHD-001): "Every Legion warlord has dreamed of it."

## Rules at the table

> Guidance: The exact effects and costs in game terms, including attunement. Write it so a player can use it without asking.
> Example (Shard of Shadows, SHD-009): "After each use, one person who knew the bearer forgets them."

## Where it is (GM only)

> Guidance: Where the shard lies now and what guards it. Decide this even if nobody is close yet.
> Example (Shard of Eternity, SHD-005): "At the still centre of the Hollowed Veil"
