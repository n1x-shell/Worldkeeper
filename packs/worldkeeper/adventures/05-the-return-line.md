Title: The Return Line
Arc: The Loose Thread
Hook: HOOK-931
Levels: 2 to 4 (written for level 3)
Linked: SET-301, LOC-305, LOC-303, NPC-448, NPC-447, NPC-306, CRE-309, CRE-306, CRE-305, CRE-311, VLT-304, NPC-454, NPC-338, FAC-305, NPC-376, HOOK-931

## Premise
After the courier's voice is freed from the waygate under Ashvale's west road, something else under there wakes. Clem Cogburn [NPC-448], salvaging for the Wrights, finds a rune-sled with power in it, pointed east down the old line, with one glyph lit on its prow: *return*. Joss Underhill [NPC-447], who rides the under-roads for a living, knows that sled. It always stops for him. It carries passengers he can't see.

The truth: the passengers are the Scattered [CRE-309], minds that slipped their bodies when Kaelith broke and never found others. The old line runs east to Cradlehorn [VLT-304], where Kaelith grew iron vessels for minds that never came. The freed voice, a mind without a body of its own, called them, and now they're going home, and they want it to come with them. At the end of the line, a vessel has already walked out to meet them. The mercenary Darra Vosk [NPC-454] is hiding it, and the Ascendancy [FAC-305] wants it.

## Hook
Clem comes to the party (she knows they freed the voice) with a lantern and a problem: "There's a sled down there that wants to go somewhere, and it hums your tune." Brenna Halvor [NPC-306] will pay for anything Kaelith-made that comes back up the line. Joss offers to drive.

If the party carries the voice, the hook is simpler: one night it stops humming the courier's tune and starts humming a new one, going east.

## Stakes
- **The voice.** If it's put into a vessel, it becomes a person that is only a voice: the thread's second end is gone, and tying it back costs what losing an end costs (see The Loose Thread [HOOK-931]).
- **The Scattered.** Restless minds that have waited three hundred years. Most just want to be someone again. Some will take any body they can get.
- **The vessel.** Whatever mind enters it becomes a new person in the world, with a body made for war. Whoever controls that choice controls them.

## Opposition
- **The Scattered [CRE-309]**, riding the sled unseen, who try to possess the party one by one on the long ride.
- **Half-made [CRE-306]** at the abandoned halts, who want to be finished, and think the vessel is for them.
- **Darra Vosk [NPC-454] and her company** (use Veteran and Thug, 4 to 6 of them), guarding the vessel at the end of the line, frightened and well paid.
- **Scout-Captain Teague Dunmarsh [NPC-338]** and Ascendancy scouts (Legion Scout statistics [CRE-006], reskinned), coming down from the mountains for the vessel.

## Clues
- The sled's benches are warm when it pulls in, and dented as if people were sitting on them.
- Joss has counted the dents: eleven passengers, every ride, and one more each time the voice hums.
- Painted on the halt walls, in old Kaelish: the stops on the line, ending at "CRADLEHORN, vessel works. Passengers alight here for embodiment."
- A Half-made at the second halt says, in pieces, "We were next. We were next in line." They were waiting for vessels too.
- Darra's camp at the end of the line has one tent nobody sleeps in, and it's guarded (her secret).
- The vessel has a name stamped on its chest plate in Kaelith script: none. The place for a name is blank.

## Obstacles
- The under-roads are long and dark, and the sled stops where it wants. Joss can coax it (a DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion) check with the sled, which he swears listens), or the party can walk.
- Each halt has a glass door. Opening one costs a Thinning save [SYS-907].
- A character who sits on a warm bench must make a DC 12 Wisdom save or hear eleven people talking about going home.

## Encounters
Three fights, and a choice at the end.
- **The Ride (the first night).** Two of the Scattered [CRE-309] try to possess the party during the ride. Possessed characters try to put the voice down "where it belongs", on the sled's prow. At level 2, use one.
- **The Second Halt.** Four Half-made [CRE-306] wait on the platform in lockstep. A Walker [CRE-305] in halt livery checks tickets. Settle it by ticket, by kindness (Half-made want to be finished: telling them the vessels are full ends their fight), or by force.
- **The Last Halt (climax).** Darra's mercenaries hold the platform. Teague's scouts come down the mountain stair at the same time. The Scattered pour off the sled toward the vessel. Three sides, plus a crowd of minds nobody can see. At level 2, halve the mercenaries and drop the scouts.

## Rewards
- Brenna's pay for every working Kaelith part brought back up the line (up to 100 gp), and a **halt key** for anyone who hasn't one.
- Whatever the party decides about the vessel (see the last scene).
- Joss Underhill's friendship: a driver who knows the under-roads, and a sled that stops for the party now too.
- Standing: +1 with the Wrights; +1 or -1 with the Ascendancy, depending on whether Teague gets the vessel.

## Approaches
- **Ride and talk.** The Scattered can be reasoned with. A character who listens to them on the ride (a DC 13 Wisdom (Insight) check) learns their names, and that most of them don't want to take anyone's body, just their own.
- **Ride and fight.** Turned, or forced out, the Scattered fall behind the sled, and catch up at the end.
- **Don't ride.** The party can walk the line in three days instead of one night, and arrives after the vessel has already been taken by someone.

## Consequences
- **A mind takes the vessel.** A new person stands up in a body made for war, and remembers being someone three hundred years ago. If the party chose who (a Scattered they talked to, kind or not), they have a powerful friend. If they let the strongest take it, they have a powerful problem.
- **The vessel is destroyed.** The Scattered scatter again, angrier. The voice is safe. Darra's company goes unpaid, and remembers who did that.
- **The Ascendancy gets it.** Teague takes it up the mountain. A month later, the Ascendancy has an iron soldier with no mind in it, and is looking for one. The party has made an enemy of the Scattered.
- **The voice went into it.** The thread's second end is lost; see The Loose Thread [HOOK-931]. A vessel walks the under-roads humming a courier's tune, looking for a room someone kept for it.

## If ignored
The sled leaves without the party. A week later, a vessel walks out of the mountains with a mind in it, and Darra's company and the Ascendancy fight over it in the foothills. The voice hums east every night until someone goes.

## Scaling
- **Level 4.** Add a second Walker [CRE-305] at the Second Halt, and give Teague one more scout per character.
- **Levels 5 to 10.** The vessel wakes before the party arrives, with the strongest of the Scattered inside it: run it as a Vessel [CRE-311] with that mind's Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma. Teague brings a Guardian [CRE-310], and sends a Hunter [CRE-307] ahead of him, built to capture whoever touched the vessel.

# Scene: The Sled With Power in It
Kind: social
Linked: NPC-448, NPC-447, LOC-305

> Clem Cogburn's lantern swings over a sled that shouldn't work: brass runners, cracked benches, and a prow with one glyph glowing a steady blue. When you come close, the courier's voice you carry stops humming. Then, very softly, it starts a new tune. The sled's glyph brightens.

Joss arrives with his goggles up and says he's driven this sled before. Ask him what it carries, and he looks at the benches for a long time before he answers.

# Scene: The Ride East
Kind: encounter
Linked: LOC-303, CRE-309

> The sled moves without a sound. Glyph-lights slide past on the tunnel walls, then fewer, then none. Behind you, the benches creak as if someone has sat down. Then someone else. Somebody behind you sighs, like a traveller settling in for a long journey.

Run the possession attempts in the dark. Let the players hear the passengers before they see anything: a woman counting stops, a child asking if they're nearly there.

# Scene: The Second Halt
Kind: encounter
Linked: CRE-306, CRE-305

> The sled slows by a platform of fitted stone. Four figures stand on it in a row, facing up the line, grey as wet clay, in clothes three hundred years out of date. As the sled stops, all four heads turn at once. Behind them, a tall brass figure in halt livery holds out a hand for tickets.

The Half-made have waited longest. If the party tells them the truth, that the vessels are for the Scattered, they argue among themselves in pieces, and one of them sits down on the platform and doesn't get up again.

# Scene: The End of the Line
Kind: climax
Linked: VLT-304, NPC-454, NPC-338, CRE-311

> The tunnel opens into a cavern of iron cradles, rows and rows of them, empty. At the end of the platform a camp has been pitched: tents, a cook-fire, a dozen tired mercenaries with crossbows. Behind them, under a tarpaulin, something stands taller than any of them, perfectly still. Above, from the mountain stair, comes the sound of boots.

Darra will talk if the party talks first. She didn't take the vessel: it walked out of the mountain to meet her company, and she doesn't know what it wants. Teague arrives two rounds after the party does, whether anyone's fighting or not. The Scattered pour off the sled the moment it stops.

# Scene: Who Gets the Body
Kind: scene
Linked: CRE-309, NPC-376, HOOK-931

The vessel can take one mind. Whoever stands closest to it when the fighting stops can choose who: a Scattered the party has come to know, the strongest of them, none (destroy it), or, if they're careless or desperate, the courier's voice.

> The vessel's chest plate, where a name should be stamped, is blank. A hand can write there in chalk, or in blood, or in glyph-ink. Whatever name is written there is the name it wakes with.
