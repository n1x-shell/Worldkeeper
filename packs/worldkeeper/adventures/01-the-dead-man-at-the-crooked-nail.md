Title: The Dead Man at the Crooked Nail
Arc: The Loose Thread
Hook: HOOK-001
Levels: 1 to 10 (written for level 1)
Linked: SET-001, NPC-010, NPC-004, NPC-006, NPC-008, NPC-005, NPC-012, NPC-014, NPC-013, LOC-003, LOC-001, NPC-376, HOOK-931

## Premise
At first light a dead man is found at the corner table of the Crooked Nail, a full cup in front of him. Nobody saw him come in, the doors were barred all night, and his clothes are thirty years out of fashion. Sewn into his coat is a sealed courier's tube, and by noon every faction agent in Hollow Refuge wants it. Mara Venn wants something else: to know who he is before the Refuge's neutrality breaks over him.

The truth: he is no murder victim. He is one end of a loose thread. He is the body of Lemmy Carrow [NPC-376], a courier who was crossing the Crossroads road on the night the shards were cut from the Loom, coming back thirty-one years late. He wasn't killed. He was never allowed to finish dying.

## Hook
Mara Venn [NPC-010] pays 10 gp each, and a room at the Nail for a month, for a name and a reason by sundown. "Quietly. If the Legion and the Pact start breaking furniture over a dead stranger, nobody will trust this town again."

If the party isn't at the Nail, the Lantern House sends for them instead: a body that won't go cold needs someone who isn't a faction.

## Stakes
- **The Refuge's neutrality.** If a faction takes the tube by force inside the walls, the others answer, and the Crooked Nail closes its doors to all five for a season.
- **The thread.** If nobody follows the dead man at dusk, he walks to the Crossroads of Fate and is gone, and this end of the thread is lost.
- **Mara.** She doesn't know why the dead man frightens her. The answer is in her own locked room.

## Opposition
Nobody here is a villain, but everyone wants the tube.
- **Vess the Grey [NPC-008]**, of the Shadow Pact, wants it unopened and in Pact hands, and will steal it.
- **Captain Marrek Vaal [NPC-005]**, of the Crimson Legion, thinks the seal is a Legion seal from before the break, and will ask for it, then demand it.
- **Sister Elyra Vale [NPC-006]**, of the Celestial Order, wants the body kept at the Lantern House and the thread on its hand recorded.
- **Sera Noll [NPC-012]**, of the Silent Accord, wants to know who in the party the dead man looks at. (He does look at one of them.)

## Clues
- The body has no wound and no smell, and stays exactly as warm as the room.
- The coat and boots are of a cut nobody has sewn for about thirty years. Old Vara [NPC-004] knows it: "courier's rig, from before."
- A silver thread is wound three times round the dead man's left hand. It can't be cut, and it is warm.
- Written in the spilled ale on the table, in a hand that isn't his: HOL. It dries, then is there again in the morning.
- The courier's knot on the satchel is a Crossroads knot, tied by the people who walked the shifting road for pay.
- Illa Reed [NPC-014] has seen that satchel style once, on a courier who used to sell her maps "from the other side of the road."
- Brann Kest [NPC-013] has dreamed this man's face, sitting at the same table, every night this week.
- Mara's locked room holds a courier's cap with the same knot on it, and a ledger page of a regular booking under the letter L.
- The tube holds no letter, just a waybill for three stops: Hollow Refuge, "the ring of thirteen, west road," and "the lamp in the grey." Under that: "Deliver to myself."

## Obstacles
- The Lantern House keeps weapons outside, and so do the people the party must question there.
- The tube's seal is old Consortium wax, and it bites. Opening it without the right word deals 1d6 necrotic damage, and the waybill is legible either way.
- The factions watch each other more than they watch the party. Being seen with the tube makes the party the next thing they watch.
- The body can't be moved past the walls before dusk. It is too heavy to lift outside the Refuge, and as light as a coat inside it.

## Encounters
Three fights, and the last one is big.
- **The Old Wall (late morning).** The thread draws echoes. When the party passes the stretch of the Old Wall that commemorates a battle that hasn't happened, two Ghost Artillerists [CRE-029] fire on whoever has touched the thread, from a gun-line that isn't there. At level 1 use one Artillerist and two Forgotten Legionnaires [CRE-027] climbing out of the memorial's carved frieze. They fall back into the stone when beaten, and the frieze has one more carved figure than before: a man in a courier's cap.
- **The alley (afternoon).** Vess's people try to take the tube: two Silent Blade Agents [CRE-014], and a Shadow Lasher [CRE-016] on the roofs. They fight hard but flee at half strength. Nobody wants a killing in the Refuge.
- **The Legion at the gate (optional).** Marrek Vaal and two Legion Scouts [CRE-006] block the East Gate and ask nicely. This can be talked down, since Marrek prefers a bargain, or fought. If it's fought, the Lantern House shuts its doors to the party for a week.
- **On the Crossroads road (climax).** At dusk the dead man stands and walks east, and four Forgotten Legionnaires rise from the verge to march beside him. Halfway to the waystone, whoever didn't get the tube makes a last grab: Vess's surviving agents, or Marrek's scouts, or both, ambush the party in the dusk. The Legionnaires turn on anyone who touches the dead man or the thread, so the fight has three sides, and a careful party can steer the dead to the ambushers.

## Rewards
- Mara's pay, and a room at the Nail that is always kept.
- The **thread's end**: when the dead man fades at the waystone, the silver thread uncoils from his hand into the hand of whoever walked closest to him. It tugs, gently, toward the other two ends. (See The Loose Thread [HOOK-931].)
- The waybill, which works as a map: at a crossroads, its three stops faintly warm when you face toward them.
- Whatever goodwill the party earned. See Faction standing: +1 with any faction whose agent they helped without bloodshed, and -1 with any they embarrassed.

## Approaches
- **Investigators** follow the clues to Mara's locked room and work out who he was before dusk.
- **Diplomats** play the four agents against each other, and keep the peace by giving each one something true.
- **The direct** sit with the body until it stands up, then follow it.
- **The careful** ask the Blind Seer [NPC-001]. He won't come down, but says through the door: "He's late for three appointments. Don't make him late for the fourth."

## Consequences
- If the tube goes to a faction, it opens it, learns nothing it understands, and starts watching the Crossroads. That faction remembers the party helped.
- If the party gets the thread's end, Sera Noll takes an interest in them for good.
- Mara, once she has seen her own locked room, remembers one thing: she kept a room for someone who never came back. She doesn't remember who. She wants to.

## If ignored
The dead man sits at the corner table until dusk, then walks out. The next morning he is there again, a little more faded, and the factions get a little rougher with each other every day. After the seventh day he doesn't come back, the thread's end is lost, and so is the chance of tying it back without great cost.

## Scaling
- **Levels 3 and 4.** The alley has three Silent Blade Agents and a Veil Adept [CRE-012]. On the road, a Forgotten Captain [CRE-028] leads the Legionnaires. Marrek brings a Centurion [CRE-007].
- **Levels 5 to 10.** The Pact sends a Nightglass Executioner [CRE-015], and the factions stop asking politely. The dead man is no longer the only echo on the road: a Rift Stalker [CRE-024] has followed the thread out of the between and wants it. Raise the seal's bite to 3d6, and the stakes: a faction that takes the tube by force tries to follow the waybill itself.

# Scene: The Corner Table
Kind: scene
Linked: SET-001, NPC-010

> The Crooked Nail smells of last night's smoke and this morning's bread. The chairs are still on the tables, all of them but one. In the corner, under the bent-nail sign, a man sits with a full cup in front of him, his cap pulled low, as if he's waiting for someone who is only a little late. He hasn't touched the ale. He hasn't moved. Mara Venn stands at the bar with her arms folded, looking at him the way you look at a dog you don't know.

Mara barred the doors at midnight herself, and unbarred them at dawn. Nobody slept in the common room. The man is dead, and has been since before anyone found him.

Let the party look. Give them the warmth, the clothes, the knot, the thread on his hand and the letters in the ale. Mara offers the job once they've seen him. She doesn't explain why she looks frightened. She can't.

# Scene: The Lantern House
Kind: social
Linked: NPC-006

Weapons stay at the door. Sister Elyra Vale [NPC-006] has the body brought in, if the party allows it, and will write down everything about it for the Order, openly.

- She confirms he has been dead for a long time, and is not decaying.
- If anyone touches the thread on his hand, they see three things at once: a ring of thirteen standing stones in green hills, a grey hall full of beds lit by one small lamp, and this same table at the Nail, from his side. Wisdom save, DC 10: on a failure, that character can't sleep tonight without dreaming them again.
- Elyra wants the thread recorded, not taken. She can become a friend, or a quiet rival.

# Scene: The Market of Lost Things
Kind: clue
Linked: NPC-014, NPC-004

Illa Reed [NPC-014] sells the party a courier's token for 2 gp, if they show her the satchel. It's stamped with a waystone. "Crossroads couriers. They'd walk the shifting road for anyone who'd pay, and come back with maps of places that weren't there. Haven't seen one since the year everything broke."

Old Vara [NPC-004] at her forge knows the boots: Refuge-made, before. The nails in the heels are a metal she says "shouldn't be, yet". She wants a favour for that, not coin.

Brann Kest [NPC-013] is in the market, grey from not sleeping. He has dreamed this man every night this week, sitting at the Nail, "waiting to be served." In the dream he says one word: Lemmy. Brann doesn't know anyone by that name, and it frightens him that he almost does.

# Scene: The Old Wall
Kind: encounter
Linked: SET-001, CRE-029, CRE-027

> The Old Wall is a patchwork of every age the Refuge has lived through. One long stretch is carved with a battle: soldiers in no army anyone recognises, charging up a hill under banners nobody has flown. The plaque says it commemorates the Battle of the Hollow Gate. There has never been a battle at the Hollow Gate. As you pass, the carved guns on the hilltop turn, very slowly, to follow whoever touched the dead man's thread.

The fight is short and loud. When it's over, the frieze has one figure more than it did: a man in a courier's cap, at the back of the carved column, looking over his shoulder.

# Scene: Four Agents
Kind: social
Linked: NPC-008, NPC-005, NPC-012

By noon the factions know. Each agent finds the party, in this order:

1. **Sera Noll**, as a scribe asking to sketch the body. She watches which party member the dead man's head has turned toward. It has turned.
2. **Marrek Vaal**, at the East Gate, polite: the seal is Legion business. It's not. It's Consortium, which he'll discover the moment he sees it.
3. **Vess the Grey**, who never meets them, but leaves a note: "Sell it before it's taken."
4. **Elyra**, again, asking them to keep the thread from the other three.

# Scene: The Alley
Kind: encounter
Linked: NPC-008, CRE-014, CRE-016

The next time the party carries the tube anywhere quiet, Vess's people come for it: two Silent Blade Agents from both ends of a narrow lane behind the Market of Lost Things, and a Shadow Lasher on the roofs. They want the tube, not a death. If one of them falls, the rest break off. Each carries a grey silk ribbon, the Pact's mark for "this was business."

# Scene: The Locked Room
Kind: clue
Linked: NPC-010

Mara has a room at the back of the Nail that she keeps locked and can't say why. If the party asks her about it, she gives them the key without wanting to: "I don't go in. I don't know why I don't go in."

> Dust, and a bed that someone made carefully and nobody has slept in. On hooks along the wall hang coats and hats and a child's shoe, things belonging to people the house forgot. On the last hook hangs a courier's cap, its band tied in the same knot as the dead man's satchel. On the table, a ledger page held down by a stone: a room booked every ninth night, for years, under a single letter, L. Then the bookings stop.

When Mara sees the cap, she sits down on the bed and says, "I kept the room for him." Then: "For who?" She won't remember more, but she now wants the party to find out.

# Scene: The Waybill
Kind: clue

Whoever opens the tube finds a single waybill in courier's shorthand. Three stops:

1. **Hollow Refuge, the Crooked Nail.** Ticked.
2. **The ring of thirteen, west road.** (Ashvale, across the sea.)
3. **The lamp in the grey.** (The Nameless Asylum, in the Hollow Verge.)

Consignee: "myself." Date of dispatch: the night the Cycle broke.

A character who knows Crossroads lore (Intelligence (History), DC 12) knows that couriers who walked the shifting road wrote "myself" when they were carrying a message they meant to deliver in person.

# Scene: Dusk on the Crossroads Road
Kind: climax
Linked: LOC-003

> As the sun touches the walls, the dead man puts both hands flat on the table and stands up. The chair doesn't scrape. He takes his cap from wherever it is now, puts it on, and walks out of the East Gate toward the Crossroads, at a courier's steady pace, the silver thread trailing from his hand.

He walks for three days and nights without stopping or sleeping: the Crossroads is about three days from the Refuge. Four Forgotten Legionnaires rise from the verge to march beside him. They only fight anyone who tries to stop him or seize the thread.

On the second night, halfway to the waystone, the ambush comes: whichever faction didn't get the tube, with the agents who survived the alley and whatever it could add (two Legion Scouts, or a Veil Adept [CRE-012] casting from the ditch). They want the tube, or the thread, or both. The dead man doesn't stop walking. If the party falls behind, he reaches the waystone without them and the thread's end is lost, so the fight must move. Run it as a running battle along the road, a round or two at each bend.

He reaches the waystone [LOC-003] at dusk on the third day. He stops, turns to the party member he has been looking at, and fades like breath on glass. The thread uncoils into that character's hand. For a moment, every road around the waystone points west, across the sea.

End the session there, or with a single line from somewhere nobody can see: "Two more stops."
