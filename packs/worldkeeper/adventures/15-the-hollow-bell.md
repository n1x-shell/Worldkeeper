Title: The Hollow Bell
Arc: The Thirteen Vaults
Hook: VLT-313
Levels: 9 to 10 (written for level 9)
Linked: VLT-313, NPC-463, NPC-468, SET-331, FAC-306, NPC-304, RUL-650, FAC-305, NPC-311, FAC-301, CRE-312, CRE-310, CRE-308, CRE-307, NPC-301, NPC-302, REG-305

## Premise
Hollowbell [VLT-313], in the southern river country [REG-305], is the vault of the thirteen keys and the notes that open locks. It is the one exception to every vault's rule: its key is not three minds but thirteen voices singing one note. What it asks is the keeping song, sung by thirteen people at once.

The truth: whoever opens Hollowbell learns the notes that open every lock, every vault's included. That is why Marshal Dace Corvane [NPC-311] wants it. And it is why the founder of Ashvale wrote "never thirteen" into the keeping song [RUL-650]: thirteen minds joined as one shape is the danger. Hollowbell's thirteen voices are not quite that. They are close enough to frighten anyone who knows the song.

## Hook
- Oona Reedwater [NPC-463], who leads the river folk's singing to Hollowbell on Evernight, comes to the party afraid: once, Hollowbell sang back to her, alone, and now someone has been asking her to sing again.
- Harbour-mother Hettie Goodbarrel [NPC-468] of Tumblewater [SET-331] has heard that the Ascendancy is buying singers, and doesn't like it.
- Ritekeeper Orla Hesk [NPC-304] of Ashvale sends word: someone is teaching the keeping song to strangers in the south, and they're teaching it wrong.

## Stakes
- **Every lock.** Opened, Hollowbell gives up the notes that open the vaults. With them, someone could open all thirteen, in one shape, and wake the whole web and the Root Core [NPC-302].
- **The singers.** Thirteen people singing as one note, willing and honest. Corvane has been gathering his own thirteen, and they aren't willing.
- **The song.** Sung wrong, the keeping song frays. Ashvale's Boundary Keepers [FAC-306] feel it at the stones.

## Opposition
- **The Ascendancy** [FAC-305]: Corvane, his thirteen bought singers, and his soldiers (use 6 Veterans and 2 Hunters [CRE-307]). On his barge, asleep, lies a War-vessel [CRE-308] he has brought out of Cradlehorn; it wakes only if he opens the vault.
- **Kaelholm** [FAC-301], who want the notes catalogued and locked away, and will take them by writ if they can.
- **The vault's wards:** 2 Vault Wards [CRE-312] and a Guardian [CRE-310] that guards the notes, and lets only a willing thirteen pass.

## Clues
- Corvane's singers are paid, and frightened. Payment isn't willingness: the vault hasn't opened for them, three nights running.
- Oona remembers the note Hollowbell sang back to her. It's the last line of the keeping song, a half-step flat.
- The vault door has thirteen hollows in a ring, each the size of a person's palm.
- Hettie Goodbarrel knows every family on the river, and which of them would sing for a friend.

## Obstacles
- **The key.** Thirteen voices, one note, all awake, willing and honest. The party must find and persuade thirteen people (themselves included) who will sing, knowing what it opens. A forced or paid voice keeps the vault shut for a day.
- **The warning.** Anyone who knows the keeping song knows "never thirteen". Each singer from Ashvale will ask the party what the warning means, and the party must answer honestly.
- **The river.** Hollowbell lies in the river country; Tumblewater moves, and the barges are the only fast road.

## Encounters
- **The Bought Choir.** Corvane's thirteen singers on a barge at Hollowbell, rehearsing, guarded. Freeing them, or persuading them, empties Corvane's choir.
- **The Gathering.** The party gathers their own thirteen: Oona, Hettie's river folk, Ashvale singers, themselves. Each needs a reason, and each has a fear.
- **The River Battle.** When he loses his choir, Corvane comes upriver with his Veterans and his two Hunters, to take the vault by force before the party's thirteen can sing.
- **The Hollow Bell (climax).** Thirteen voices, one note, and the vault opens. The notes are there, written in the air in the First Grammar. Corvane and Kaelholm both reach the door. What the party does with the notes decides how the arc ends.

## Rewards
- The notes, or their destruction. With the notes, any one vault can be opened without its key, once. All thirteen together would wake the web.
- Oona's voice back, and the river folk's friendship.
- The Boundary Keepers' gratitude, if the song is sung right.
- Standing: +2 with the Boundary Keepers if the song is kept; -3 with the Ascendancy, whatever happens.

## Approaches
- **Sing it right.** Thirteen willing voices, the warning explained honestly, and the notes taken or destroyed.
- **Break Corvane's choir.** Persuade his singers to walk away, and the Ascendancy can't open the vault at all.
- **Leave it shut.** Hollowbell stays closed, and the notes with it, and Corvane goes on trying.

## Consequences
- **The vault opens.** The notes are known. Whoever holds them can open one vault each, without its key, and all thirteen together would wake the web and the Root Core. That is the Root Core's arc, for later.
- **If the notes are destroyed,** every vault still opens by its own key, and only that way. Some people will never forgive the party. Others will be very relieved.
- **If Corvane gets the notes,** the War-vessel on his barge wakes, and the Ascendancy can open Cradlehorn's heart without its key, and start building an army of vessels with Kaelith minds.
- **The Seventh Ward wakes.** For an hour, in Arborhold, it is awake, and it asks the Continuity's watcher whether the thirteen have come back in one shape. They haven't. It sleeps again, less deeply.

## If ignored
Corvane finds thirteen people willing to sing for him, which takes a year. Then the Ascendancy has the notes, and every vault in Kaelmar is his to open.

## Scaling
- **Level 10.** Corvane fights (use his fight card).
- **Levels 11 to 14.** The War-vessel wakes at the River Battle, and the Guardian of the notes tests the thirteen with one question each; one wrong answer silences a voice.

# Scene: The Hollow Bell
Kind: climax
Linked: VLT-313, CRE-310, CRE-308

> Thirteen voices find the note, and hold it. The grey door doesn't open: it rings, like a bell the size of a hill, and the ringing goes on after the singing stops. Inside, in the air of a round, empty room, lines of light hang like music. Behind you, on the river, something huge and iron stirs in its sleep on a barge.
