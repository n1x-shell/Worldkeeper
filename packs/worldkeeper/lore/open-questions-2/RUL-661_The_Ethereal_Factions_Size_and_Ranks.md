# The Ethereal Factions: Size and Ranks

## What this is
How large each of the Ethereal Realm's five factions is, how it is organised and what it has (GM only). Starting attitudes are on their standing tracks ([RUL-611] to [RUL-615]).

## The Cycle Breakers [FAC-501]
- **Size.** About 300, all people who remember the Cycle and broke free of it.
- **Ranks.** None. Anyone who stands on the broken steps of the Fractured Throne has one voice. A **Speaker** is chosen for each debate by lot. Eryndor the First Shard is honoured as **the Eldest**, but he doesn't lead, and won't.
- **Resources.** Memories of every turn of the Cycle; the Fractured Throne; friends in every other faction of the realm. Very little that can be carried.

## The Architects of Eternity [FAC-502]
- **Size.** About 2,000: the Crucible of Creation is a city.
- **Ranks.** The **First Architect** (Kaelos "the Forgotten Star"); a dozen **Architects** who each own a district's design; **Masons**, who build; **Salvagers**, who bring in the remains of lost worlds from the Fields; and **Wardens**, who guard the gates.
- **Resources.** Salvage from the Fields of Lost Realities; the Fixing Yards, where salvage is made so that nothing can unmake it; the best workshops in the realm.

## The Veilborn [FAC-503]
- **Size.** About 150 living at the Nameless Asylum, and about 300 more scattered through the Verge.
- **Ranks.** The **Keeper** (Keeper Lantern); **Rememberers**, who listen in the Remembering Rooms and write lives down; **Runners**, who guide and carry messages through the Verge (Dannick Thrale is one); and **Wards**, the people the Asylum shelters.
- **Resources.** The Asylum itself; the list of names read every evening; the Runners' knowledge of the Verge's moving paths; the Lost Shelf.

## The Titans of the Rift [FAC-504]
- **Size.** About 500 warriors, and a crowd for every bout.
- **Ranks.** The **Titan-King** (Varuun), who rules by being the strongest; **Champions**, who have beaten their own echo; **Challengers**, who haven't yet; and **the Summoned**, outsiders invited to fight.
- **Resources.** The Colosseum of Echoes; the summons, which are invitations into the deep realm; their echoes, which they can send to fight for them once each.

## The Echoborn [FAC-505]
- **Size.** About 80 at the Observatory of the Last Question, and a handful of Walkers out on the Veil of Eternity.
- **Ranks.** None they'll admit to. In practice, **Questioners** (who ask the last question), **Lens-keepers** (who tend the lenses) and **Walkers** (who walk out onto the Veil, like the Wanderer, and don't come back).
- **Resources.** The Observatory's lenses, turned outward; star-charts of the Black Horizon going back thirty years.
- **Secret.** The charts show the Horizon's stars going out, one by one, faster lately. The Echoborn think it's a door opening. It's the Return.
