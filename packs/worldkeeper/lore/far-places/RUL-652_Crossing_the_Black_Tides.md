# Crossing the Black Tides

## What this is
The width of the Black Tides, the cost of passage, the tolls on the crossing and what is worth carrying. Standard D&D prices still apply to everything else (SYS-902). The player-facing text is in the handbook.

## Width
About 1,000 km (600 miles) from the Eternal Veil [REG-005] to Kaelholm [SET-302]. Ships cross slowly, under short sail in the dark middle, which is why it takes two weeks.

## Passage, tolls and the catalogue
- Passage is at the book rate, 1 sp per mile: 70 gp, with food. Smugglers charge double. Working your passage is free, if a captain will have you.
- The Shadow Pact [FAC-004] takes one coin in ten (10% of declared value) on cargo at Tollrock [LOC-903], both ways. Undeclared cargo it finds is forfeit.
- Kaelith salvage landed in Kaelmar must be shown at Kaelholm's [FAC-301] catalogue office, in the city or on Kittiwake [LOC-905]. Kaelholm can buy anything it wants at the book price. Salvage not shown is contraband.

## Trade goods
Goods from the other continent sell at double their book price the first time they are sold across the sea. Aetheris to Kaelmar: Legion steel, Order relic-oil, Consortium paper and inks, Veil masks and lacquer, Wildborn furs, wine. Kaelmar to Aetheris: graftwood, living cord, glow-fungus (packed dark for the crossing), glyph-ink, Low Fenn salt, and Kaelith salvage Kaelholm let go.

## Western Kaelmar barter
A sack of Low Fenn salt is worth 1 gp; a day's work on the orchard or the walls, 2 sp; a working Kaelith part, whatever the Wrights will give for it.
