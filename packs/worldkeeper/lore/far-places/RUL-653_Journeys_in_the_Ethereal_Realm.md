# Journeys in the Ethereal Realm

## What this is
How long journeys take in the Ethereal Realm, which measures them by harmony, not distance. The player-facing text is in the handbook.

## The rule [HOMEBREW]
- The Hollow Verge [ETH-501]: each leg between known places takes 1d4 days; with a guide, use the lower of two rolls; without one, add a day.
- The Rift crossing, from one open end of the Rift [LOC-901] to the other through the border layer: 2d6 days.
- Root-roads: minutes, with a Thinning save [SYS-907] (canon).
- The deep realm: past the last threshold, a journey to a region takes 1d6 days; 1d3 if the party travels with one shared purpose, said aloud each morning; add 1d6 if they quarrel.
- The Black Horizon [ETH-507] can't be reached by travel. Only a summons or the thirteenth shard [SHD-013] brings anyone there.

## Limits
What lies beyond the Black Horizon, and the Watchers' aims, remain UNDEFINED.
