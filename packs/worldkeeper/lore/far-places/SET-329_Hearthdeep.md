# Hearthdeep

## Overview
The dwarf hold built over Anvilreach [VLT-306], kept by Hold-Warden Brisa Deepmantle. Forges, hot springs, and halls cut deep against the cold. It trades iron south for food.

## Leaders
Hold-Warden Brisa Deepmantle
