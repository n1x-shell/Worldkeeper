# Standing with the Titans of the Rift

## What they value
Strength that decides fate, and anyone who'll fight their own copy and win.

## Hostile
Your copies are already in the Colosseum of Echoes [LOC-504], and the Titans send them after you.

## Distrusted
You may fight in the Colosseum, but only against odds.

## Initiate
A place in the Colosseum's lists, and a Titan sparring partner who'll train you.

## Trusted
Victory in the Colosseum earns favours: once per adventure, a Titan fights beside you for one battle. From level 5, a Bastion beneath the Colosseum stands.

## Exalted
Titan-King Varuun [NPC-069] names you a worthy foe, and the Titans will escort you anywhere in the realm.

## Champion
Named Titan-Blooded. Once, you can call the Titans to war, and they come.
