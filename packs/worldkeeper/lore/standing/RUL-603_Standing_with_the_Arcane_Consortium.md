# Standing with the Arcane Consortium

## What they value
Discoveries shared, rare things brought in, and patience with the cost of progress.

## Hostile
Your names are on the Collegium's list of "variables to be removed". Consortium constructs treat you as a hazard.

## Distrusted
Barred from Consortium libraries. Its scholars won't identify your finds, or will lie about them.

## Initiate
Your finds identified for free at any Consortium outpost, and access to its public libraries.

## Trusted
Spell scrolls and arcane components at cost, and a Nexus Researcher [CRE-011] to study a problem for you. From level 5, a Bastion at a Consortium outpost.

## Exalted
Use of a Consortium teleportation circle, once per adventure. Archivist Lissara Wynne will answer one question a month from the deep archives.

## Champion
Named Fellow of the Collegium. A seat on a Consortium research council, an Arcane Sentinel [CRE-009] as a permanent guard, and first claim on any artifact you help them recover. Every other great faction marks you as the Consortium's.
