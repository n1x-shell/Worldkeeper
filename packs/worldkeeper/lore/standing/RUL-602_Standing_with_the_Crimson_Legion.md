# Standing with the Crimson Legion

## What they value
Strength shown, agreements kept, fights finished. The Legion respects an enemy who stands more than a friend who runs.

## Hostile
A bounty on every head in the party, payable in Varkhaal. Legion patrols attack on sight in the Tyrant's Dominion.

## Distrusted
Legion roads charge you a toll in blood: a duel, or a week's labour on the walls.

## Initiate
Hired as auxiliaries. Legion quartermasters sell to you, and you can enter Varkhaal's Great Arena as a fighter.

## Trusted
A Legion Scout [CRE-006] guide wherever the Legion marches. Siege gear and mounts on loan. From level 5, a Bastion in a Legion fort.

## Exalted
A squad of Shock Troopers [CRE-005] answers your call for one battle per adventure. Captain Marrek Vaal and officers like him will negotiate through you.

## Champion
Named Blood-Sworn. You can demand a duel of any Legion officer, and the Warlord's door is open. The Legion will break a siege for you, once. Every other great faction marks you as the Legion's.
