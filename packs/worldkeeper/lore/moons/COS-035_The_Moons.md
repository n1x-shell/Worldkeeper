# The Moons

## What this is
The two moons over both continents. The player-facing text is in the handbook; the cycles are in the campaign calendar.

## The cycles
- **The Lamp:** large and white, a 30-day cycle. It keeps nearly in step with the months, slipping three days a year against them.
- **The Wanderer:** small and red, a 49-day cycle (seven weeks), so it drifts against the months and the Lamp.

## GM notes [SECRET]
- **Both dark.** On a night when both moons are new (every three or four years), the Hollow Verge's doors open more easily: anyone looking for an entrance finds one, whether they meant to or not. The Thinning save DC for anything done that night is 2 lower.
- **The Wanderer** is not on the Loom. It's the only thing in the sky that wasn't made with the world. Nobody knows what it is. The Echoborn [FAC-505] have been watching it for thirty years.
