Kind: species
Name: Shadowkin
Creature Type: Humanoid
Size: Medium
Speed: 30 feet
People: Shadowkin (PPL-006)
Stands in for: the shadar-kai rules, for GMs without that book

Born from an erased past, living between light and dark: masters of stealth, distrusted everywhere.

## Darkvision
You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

## Fey Ancestry
You have Advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Charmed condition.

## Between
You have Resistance to Necrotic damage, and proficiency in the Stealth skill.

## Shadow Step
As a Bonus Action, you teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see that is in Dim Light or Darkness, and you have Resistance to all damage until the start of your next turn. You can do so a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
