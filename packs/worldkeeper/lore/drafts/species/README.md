# The world's own species: drafts for review

Status: **drafts, not shipped** (docs/PLAN_0.9.md, v0.9.0). Nothing here reaches the app until the owner approves it; the app skips `drafts/` folders.

40 species rules for the Worldkeeper world's peoples that today play as a book species, so the world is whole on the SRD alone. Written fresh, never book text, from what each people's page says they are like, and balanced against the SRD 5.2.1's nine species: a size and speed, two to five traits, a darkvision where the page suggests it, and at most one or two limited uses (Proficiency Bonus times, or once, per Long Rest). Innate spells are all SRD spells of level 2 or lower, at character levels 3 and 5, as the SRD's own species give them.

**Names** (owner, November 2026): the people's own name in the world where it has one; its alternate name where it has one; a name already open (in the SRD 5.2.1, or plain English) kept as it is; otherwise a proposed name, marked below, for you to accept or change. **The book wins:** a GM who has the book gets the book's species; these are the fallback, and Books only sets them aside.

Each file is in the rules-pack format (docs/RULES_PACKS.md) with two extra lines for review: which people it's for, and which book species it stands in for.

**To review:** for each, approve, change (a name, a number, a trait), or drop. Say which in any form ("all fine except: Ironhide → Riverhorn; Shell-folk AC 16").
