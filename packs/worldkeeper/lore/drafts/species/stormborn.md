Kind: species
Name: Stormborn
Creature Type: Humanoid
Size: Medium or Small (chosen when you select this species)
Speed: 35 feet
People: PPL-013 Stormborn
Stands in for: the air genasi rules, for GMs without that book

Rare souls born in a thunderstorm, with the storm still in them: hair that drifts, eyes that flicker, and a temper that brings the weather.

## Darkvision
You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

## Breath of the Storm
You can hold your breath indefinitely while you aren't Incapacitated, and you have Resistance to Lightning damage.

## Weather in the Blood
You know the Shocking Grasp cantrip. Starting at character level 3 you can cast Feather Fall, and at level 5 Levitate, each once without a spell slot; you regain the ability to cast them this way when you finish a Long Rest. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for these spells (choose when you select this species). You can also cast them with any spell slots you have of the right level.
