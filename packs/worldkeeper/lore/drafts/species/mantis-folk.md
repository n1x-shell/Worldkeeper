Kind: species
Name: Mantis-folk
Creature Type: Monstrosity
Size: Medium or Small (chosen when you select this species)
Speed: 30 feet
People: PPL-052 Thri-kreen
Stands in for: the thri-kreen rules, for GMs without that book

Six-limbed mantis-folk found on every desert, in every world.

## Darkvision
You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

## Chitin
Your Armor Class is 13 + your Dexterity modifier while you aren't wearing armor. As an action, you can change the color of your carapace to match your surroundings, giving you Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks to hide in them.

## Extra Arms
You have two smaller arms below your main pair. They can wield only Light weapons or hold, manipulate or stow objects, and they don't give you extra attacks.

## No Sleep
You don't need to sleep, and magic can't put you to sleep. During a Long Rest you can stay alert and active, doing only light activity.

## Mind Speech
You can speak telepathically to any creature you can see within 120 feet that shares a language with you, and it can answer you the same way.
