Quick, twitchy rabbit-folk, born lucky and usually already leaving.

## Quick Start
You can add your Proficiency Bonus to your Initiative rolls.

## Twitchy
You have proficiency in the Perception skill.

## Born Lucky
When you fail a Dexterity saving throw, you can take a Reaction to roll a d4 and add it to the result, possibly turning it into a success. You can't use this Reaction if you have the Prone condition or your Speed is 0.

## Spring Away
As a Bonus Action, you can jump a number of feet equal to five times your Proficiency Bonus without provoking Opportunity Attacks. You can do so a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
