Kind: species
Name: Cat-folk
Creature Type: Humanoid
Size: Medium or Small (chosen when you select this species)
Speed: 30 feet
People: PPL-040 Tabaxi
Stands in for: the tabaxi rules, for GMs without that book

Curious cat-folk who travel for stories and keep the best ones to themselves.

## Darkvision
You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

## Cat's Claws
You have a Climb Speed equal to your Speed, and your unarmed strikes can be claws that deal 1d6 + your Strength modifier Slashing damage.

## Curious
You have proficiency in two of these skills of your choice: Investigation, Perception or Stealth.

## Burst of Speed
When you move on your turn, you can double your Speed until the end of that turn. Once you use this trait, you can't do so again until you spend a turn without moving, or finish a Short or Long Rest.
