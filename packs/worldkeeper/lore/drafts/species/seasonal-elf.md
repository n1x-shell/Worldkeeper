Kind: species
Name: Seasonal Elf
Creature Type: Humanoid
Size: Medium
Speed: 30 feet
People: PPL-021 Eladrin
Stands in for: the eladrin rules, for GMs without that book

Elves whose moods turn with the seasons, and who sometimes forget which world they're standing in.

## Darkvision
You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

## Fey Ancestry
You have Advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Charmed condition.

## Trance
You don't need to sleep, and magic can't put you to sleep. You can finish a Long Rest in 4 hours if you spend them in a light, aware trance.

## Season's Step
As a Bonus Action, you teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see. Choose your current season when you finish a Long Rest (spring, summer, autumn or winter); after you teleport, one creature within 10 feet of where you arrive feels it: spring, it gains Temporary Hit Points equal to your Proficiency Bonus; summer, it takes Fire damage equal to your Proficiency Bonus; autumn, it has Disadvantage on its next attack roll before the end of its next turn; winter, its Speed is halved until the end of its next turn. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
