Kind: species
Name: Bird-folk
Creature Type: Humanoid
Size: Medium
Speed: 30 feet
People: PPL-011 Aarakocra
Stands in for: the aarakocra rules, for GMs without that book

Feathered folk of the high passes, light-boned and long-sighted, who would rather cross a mountain than walk around it.

## Wings
You have a Fly Speed equal to your Speed. You can't use it while wearing Medium or Heavy armor, or while carrying more than half your carrying capacity.

## Talons
Your unarmed strikes can deal 1d6 + your Strength modifier Slashing damage instead of the usual damage.

## Far Sight
You have Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight to see something more than 60 feet away.

## Wind Rider
Starting at character level 3, you can cast Feather Fall once without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to do so when you finish a Long Rest. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for these spells (choose when you select this species). You can also cast them with any spell slots you have of the right level.
