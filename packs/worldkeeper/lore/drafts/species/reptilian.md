Kind: species
Name: Reptilian
Creature Type: Humanoid
Size: Medium
Speed: 30 feet
People: Reptilians (player text)
Stands in for: the lizardfolk rules, for GMs without that book

Scaled hunters of swamp, desert and jungle, patient and hard to read.

## Scales
Your Armor Class is 13 + your Dexterity modifier while you aren't wearing armor.

## Bite
Your unarmed strikes can be bites that deal 1d6 + your Strength modifier Piercing damage.

## Hold Breath
You have a Swim Speed equal to your Speed, and you can hold your breath for up to 15 minutes.

## Hungry Jaws
When you hit with your bite, you gain Temporary Hit Points equal to your Proficiency Bonus. You can do so a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
