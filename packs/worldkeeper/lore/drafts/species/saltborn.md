Kind: species
Name: Saltborn
Creature Type: Humanoid
Size: Medium or Small (chosen when you select this species)
Speed: 30 feet
People: PPL-045 Water Genasi
Stands in for: the water genasi rules, for GMs without that book

People with the sea in their blood, calm as still water until they aren't.

## Darkvision
You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

## Sea Blood
You have a Swim Speed equal to your Speed, you can breathe air and water, and you have Resistance to Acid damage.

## Tide Gifts
You know the Elementalism cantrip. Starting at character level 3 you can cast Create or Destroy Water, and at level 5 Fog Cloud, each once without a spell slot; you regain the ability to cast them this way when you finish a Long Rest. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for these spells (choose when you select this species). You can also cast them with any spell slots you have of the right level.
