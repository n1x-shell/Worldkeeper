Warriors from beyond the world, who came in through a crack in it and never went home.

## Rift Lore
You gain proficiency in one skill and with one weapon or tool of your choice. You know one language of your choice.

## Mind Shield
You have Resistance to Psychic damage.

## Rift Gifts
You know the Mage Hand cantrip, and the hand is invisible when you cast it this way. Starting at character level 3 you can cast Jump, and at level 5 Misty Step, each once without a spell slot; you regain the ability to cast them this way when you finish a Long Rest. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for these spells (choose when you select this species). You can also cast them with any spell slots you have of the right level.
