Hippo-headed soldiers from a world nobody can find, loud, literal and very fond of explosions.

## Hippo Strength
You count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can push, drag or lift, and you have Advantage on Strength saving throws.

## Drilled
You have proficiency with Martial Ranged weapons.

## Big Swim
You have a Swim Speed equal to your Speed, and you can hold your breath for up to 1 hour.

## Steady Aim
Once per turn when you roll a 1 on a damage die for a ranged weapon attack, you can reroll the die and must use the new roll.
