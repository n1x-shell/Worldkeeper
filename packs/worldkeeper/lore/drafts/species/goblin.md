Creature Type: Humanoid
Size: Small

People: PPL-028 Goblins
Stands in for: the goblin rules, for GMs without that book

Small, quick and underestimated by everyone, which goblins find very useful.

## Darkvision
You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

## Slip Away
You can take the Disengage or Hide action as a Bonus Action on each of your turns.

## Get Even
When you damage a creature larger than you with an attack or a spell, you can deal extra damage to it equal to your Proficiency Bonus. You can do so a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, but only once per turn, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

## Fey Ancestry
You have Advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Charmed condition.
