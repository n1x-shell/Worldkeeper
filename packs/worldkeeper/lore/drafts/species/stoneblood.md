Kind: species
Name: Stoneblood
Creature Type: Humanoid
Size: Medium or Small (chosen when you select this species)
Speed: 30 feet
People: PPL-020 Earth Genasi
Stands in for: the earth genasi rules, for GMs without that book

People with the earth in them: skin like stone, patience like a mountain, and a grip nobody breaks.

## Darkvision
You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

## Sure Footing
Difficult Terrain made of earth or stone doesn't cost you extra movement, and you have Advantage on saving throws and checks to avoid being knocked Prone or moved against your will.

## Stone Skin
As a Bonus Action, you can give yourself Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing and Slashing damage for 1 minute. You can do so a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

## Earth Gift
You know the Elementalism cantrip. Starting at character level 3 you can cast Spike Growth once without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to do so when you finish a Long Rest. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for these spells (choose when you select this species). You can also cast them with any spell slots you have of the right level.
