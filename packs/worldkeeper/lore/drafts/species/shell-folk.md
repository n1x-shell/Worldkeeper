Name: Shell-folk
Creature Type: Humanoid
Size: Medium
Speed: 30 feet
People: PPL-042 Tortles
Stands in for: the tortle rules, for GMs without that book

Patient, wandering turtle-folk, who carry their homes on their backs and see no reason to hurry.

## Shell
Your Armor Class is 17 while you aren't wearing body armor (you can still use a Shield). Your Dexterity modifier doesn't add to it.

## Withdraw
As an action, you pull into your shell. Until you come out (a Bonus Action), you gain +4 to your Armor Class and Advantage on Strength and Constitution saving throws, but you have the Prone condition, your Speed is 0, and you can't take Reactions.

## Hold Breath
You can hold your breath for 1 hour.

## Claws
Your unarmed strikes can be claws that deal 1d6 + your Strength modifier Slashing damage.
