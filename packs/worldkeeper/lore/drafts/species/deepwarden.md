Name: Deepwarden
Creature Type: Humanoid
Size: Medium
Speed: 30 feet
People: PPL-043 Tritons
Stands in for: the triton rules, for GMs without that book

Guardians of the deep, who came up to the surface with a sense of duty and a lot of opinions.

## Darkvision
You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

## Amphibious
You have a Swim Speed equal to your Speed, you can breathe air and water, and you have Resistance to Cold damage.

## Speaker for the Sea
You can communicate simple ideas with any Beast that has a Swim Speed.

## Tide Gifts
Starting at character level 3 you can cast Fog Cloud, and at level 5 Gust of Wind, each once without a spell slot; you regain the ability to cast them this way when you finish a Long Rest. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for these spells (choose when you select this species). You can also cast them with any spell slots you have of the right level.
