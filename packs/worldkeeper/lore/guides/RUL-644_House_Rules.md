# House Rules

## What this is
The world's own rules in plain words for players: Thinning and Unmaking [SYS-907], Shardscarred [COS-908], and a spoiler-free version of Elder Gods at the Table [RUL-621]. The player-facing text is in the handbook.

## GM notes
Elder Gods at the Table appears only as 'Things you can't kill' and 'Some things you shouldn't look at'. The handbook never names the elder gods, their Laws of Existence, their save DCs or the Return [COS-032].
