# Vault of the Shard of Dominion: the Rooms

## What this is
The contents of the five rooms of the Vault of the Shard of Dominion [HOOK-003], the starter dungeon under the Bloodmarked Fields [LOC-002].

## The rooms
A starter dungeon for levels 1 to 4, under the Bloodmarked Fields. DC 12 for scene checks, as the books set. Whoever carries the shard pays its cost.

1. **The Ashen Gates.** A stairway under a crater the dig crews broke open, ending at bronze doors packed with grey ash. Ash-wraiths (use 2 Shadows, Monster Manual) rise from it when the doors are forced. Sweeping the ash out with water lays them to rest without a fight. *Clue:* the doors are carved with five banners kneeling to a sixth, blank one.
2. **The Hall of Shifting Floors.** A long hall of tiles that slide when weight lands on them. The tiles obey whoever gives the most commanding voice: a DC 12 Charisma (Intimidation) or Persuasion check stills them for a round. Failure drops a character 10 feet into the hall below (1d6 Bludgeoning damage), with a climb back. *Clue:* the hall obeys whoever commands it, a taste of the shard.
3. **The Runic Crucible.** A round room around a cold crucible, its walls carved with the five factions' sigils. Lighting the sigils in the order the factions came to the Marches (DC 12 Intelligence (History) to recall it) opens the way. A wrong order wakes a Gargoyle (Monster Manual) built into the wall. *Setback:* lighting all five at once floods the room with fire (2d6 Fire, DC 12 Dexterity save for half).
4. **The Heart Vault.** The shard lies in a red crystal heart that beats. Its last bearer, a Legion warlord dead for centuries, still stands guard (use a Wight, Monster Manual), commanded by the shard. Whoever takes the shard can order the Wight to stand down, at the shard's price.
5. **The Threshold of War.** The way out comes up in the middle of the Fields, and all five factions are waiting: the shard has been calling them since the party touched it. Captain Marrek Vaal offers terms first. The other four don't. *Twist:* whoever holds the shard can command one faction's soldiers for a day, and everyone there knows it.
