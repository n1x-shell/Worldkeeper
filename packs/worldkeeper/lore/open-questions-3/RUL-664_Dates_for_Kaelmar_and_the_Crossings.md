# Dates for Kaelmar and the Crossings

## What this is
Dates for Kaelith, the Warding and the rise of Kaelmar's powers, and the dates and ships of the first regular crossings of the Black Tides.

## Counting years before the Shrouding
Years before the Shrouding are counted **Before the Bloom (BB)**, matching After Bloom (AB).

## The dates
- **The Building of Kaelith** [HIS-903]. When Kaelith began stays unknown, as the World Timeline keeps it. It stood for at least nine centuries before the Shrouding. Kaelith had no kings: it was ruled by **the Chancellery**, thirteen Chancellors, one for each vault's strand, who chose a **First Chancellor** every thirteen years. The last First Chancellor's name is the one name Kaelholm's archive doesn't keep.
- **The Warding** [HIS-904]. 17 BB: seventeen years before the Shrouding.
- **The Founding of the Continuity** [HIS-906]. 3 AB.
- **The Rise of Kaelmar's Powers** [HIS-909].
  - **Kaelholm:** 1 AB, when Kaelith-East's scholars renamed their city and swore to keep the rolls.
  - **The Spore Circle:** 12 AB, from the first communions in the Bloom. Its Eastern Tendril broke away in 41 AB.
  - **The Convergence Collective:** 284 AB, and **the Ascendancy:** 287 AB (both already in the history).

## The first regular crossings [HIS-912]
- **When.** The first regular ship sailed from Mothhaven on Spring Dawn 1, 298 AB (Year 29 Unbound), after nearly twenty years of smugglers.
- **Ships.** The ***Long Dark*** made the first regular crossing. The ***Quiet Answer*** followed within the month. Captain Wilhelmina Corr's ship is the ***Lodestar***.
- **Ports.** Mothhaven in the Eternal Veil; the Tollmouth at Kaelholm.
