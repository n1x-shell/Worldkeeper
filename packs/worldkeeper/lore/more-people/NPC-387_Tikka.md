# Tikka

## Public role and identity
- Flies messages between the aarakocra eyries, the goliath clans and the Halfcrown.
- Works for Aerie-Speaker Kree [NPC-358] when it's needed.

## Goal
To be the fastest courier on the peaks, and to be believed.

## Known relationships and locations
The Eastern Mountains [REG-302]; Aerie-Speaker Kree [NPC-358]

## Appearance and voice
A small aarakocra with grey-and-gold feathers, a satchel bigger than their body and one bent flight feather. Fast-talking, eager and easily offended.

## Pressure and secrets
There is a door on a nameless peak: a door frame with no door and no wall, standing in the snow. It's the Door-Maker's sign (Kth-Varaneh [DEI-916]), and it's the first of several. Tikka has looked through it.
