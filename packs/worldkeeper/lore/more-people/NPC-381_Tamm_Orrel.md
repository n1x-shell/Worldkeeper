# Tamm Orrel

## Public role and identity
- Ran away from the Copyhouses at eleven.
- Forges letters, passes and seals for anyone with a coin.

## Goal
A name that's theirs, on a roll nobody can burn.

## Known relationships and locations
Kaelholm [SET-302]; the Fair Copies

## Appearance and voice
A small, quick kid of about thirteen with ink to the elbows and a too-big Copyhouse smock cut short. Cocky, funny and frightened of being found.

## Pressure and secrets
Copying an old roll for a client, Tamm traced the Roll of the Lost Crew [ART-310], and the scraped-out name came through on the tracing, as a shadow. Tamm knows the burned Tull cousin's name, and doesn't know what it means yet. The Tulls would pay anything for that tracing, or for Tamm.
