# Orrin Halfcrown

## Public role and identity
- Keeps the Halfcrown, the only inn on the high pass to Cradlehorn.
- Pays the Ascendancy's toll so his guests don't have to.

## Goal
A quiet winter, and his guests alive in the morning.

## Known relationships and locations
The Eastern Mountains [REG-302]; the Halfcrown

## Appearance and voice
A short, round dwarf with a red beard, a cook's apron over mail, and a crossbow behind the bar. Cheerful, nosy and protective.

## Pressure and secrets
Orrin pays the Ascendancy in information: who goes up the pass, and what they carry. He hates it, and keeps a second list for whoever might one day want to stop them.
