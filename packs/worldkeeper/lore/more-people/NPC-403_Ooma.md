# Ooma

## Public role and identity
- Listens in the Nameless Asylum's Remembering Rooms, where people say their other lives aloud.
- Takes the shape of whoever's story is being told, which helps more people than you'd think.

## Goal
To hear every story there is.

## Known relationships and locations
The Nameless Asylum [LOC-506]; the Veilborn [FAC-503]; Inkling [NPC-375]

## Appearance and voice
A soft, faintly glowing shape, the colour of weak tea, that settles into the face of whoever is talking. Gentle, curious, and very good at silence.

## Pressure and secrets
Ooma remembers every story word for word, including the ones people asked to have forgotten. Inkling's written records leave those out; Ooma doesn't. The Hollow Tribunal has heard there's a listener in the Asylum who forgets nothing, and wants it.
