# Angle-Walker

## Overview
Herald of the Choir Between Angles. It steps out of the corner of a room, and folds it shut behind it.

## Appearance
A tall, narrow figure made of the space where two walls meet, all edges and no middle, that is always facing you no matter where you stand. It hums the Choir's note.

## Where
The Citadel of the Hidden [ETH-506], then old houses in the world, where a corner has grown too deep.

## Game statistics (GM)
Start from: Nothic (Monster Manual) with double its hit points, or a Phase Spider for a lesser one. About CR 6 to 9. It teleports between any two corners it can see, and a creature it hits makes a DC 15 Constitution save or is pushed into a corner, Restrained, until the end of its next turn.

## Secret (GM only)
Rooms it has walked through are bigger inside than out ever after. A house with three of them in it is a door.
