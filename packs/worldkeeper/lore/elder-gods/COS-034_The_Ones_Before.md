# The Ones Before

## What this is
The legends mortals tell about the elder gods [COS-030]. Players can read these; none of them is the whole truth, and some are wrong.

## Which legends are true [SECRET]
- **"Seven made the world":** true.
- **"The gods are the children of the Ones Before":** true. The gods don't know it.
- **"They are asleep, and must not be woken":** true of four.
- **"Three were cast out for teaching":** true.
- **"They made the world as a prison":** false. It's a hiding place. The people are what's hidden, not what's held.
- **The Black Sun's eclipse:** true, and they're wrong about what it brings.
- **The Grey Mother rhyme:** about Ysh'thaleen [DEI-911], and accurate.
