# Kth-Varaneh, the Door-Maker

## What he is [SECRET]
He built the Ethereal Realm's walls and corridors, and the Citadel of the Hidden [ETH-506] is the workshop he left behind, which the stewards moved into. He taught the first civilisation how to build doors out of the world. They built them, walked through, and found the Ethereal Realm.

## Sign
A door frame with no door and no wall.

## In the schism
The second teacher. He left with the first and has spent the time since building a door back in, from the outside.

## What he wants
A door the seed can walk out of, into everything beyond, with nothing hunting it.

## His Guest
The Choir Between Angles. See The Guests [COS-031].

## His return
Corridors in the Citadel lead to places that weren't there. Doors appear in the world, standing alone in fields, and they're locked from the other side. Hidden Wardens go missing.

## His name
Spoken at any door, his name opens it, and also opens it onto somewhere else.

## Laws of Existence
In addition to Immutable Existence and the Unseen Gaze (Elder Gods at the Table [RUL-621]). Save DCs follow its reach.

| Law | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Architecture of Elsewhere** | Every door in his reach opens somewhere else. Roll a d6 when one is opened: 1 to 3, where expected; 4 or 5, another room in his reach; 6, another depth. |
| **Rooms Without End** | A creature that tries to leave a room the way it came in makes an Intelligence save. On a failure, it finds another room instead. |
| **Sealed** | Teleportation, Plane Shift and Dimension Door fail in his reach unless the caster knows the name of a door to aim for. |
| **Reality Erosion** | Corners are doors. Any creature that ends its turn next to a corner can be moved to any other corner in his reach by his heralds, as a free action on their turn. |

## Manifestation
**The Architect.** A figure built like a door frame, three times a man's height, with the Choir's note coming through it. Start from the Marut (Monsters of the Multiverse), with its Justify ability replaced by Rebuild: once per round, it moves a wall. Defeating it doesn't kill the god; it pushes the god's reach down a step for a year and a day.

## How it can be stopped
- **Sever the way.** Shut his door at the Black Horizon from the inside, and lock it. The Worldscar Key [ITM-020] opens any seal; turned the other way, it closes one for good, and costs its bearer's name.
- **Break the vessel.** The keystone of the Citadel's first corridor is the first stone he set. Pull it, and the Citadel stops obeying him, and starts falling down.
- **Speak the true name.** Spoken at a door, his name opens it somewhere else. Spoken at his own door, it opens onto him, and he must come through and be bound.
