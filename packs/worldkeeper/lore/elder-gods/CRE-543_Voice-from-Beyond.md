# Voice from Beyond

## Overview
Herald of the Shepherd of Quiet Stars. A gentle voice in the dark that calls you home, and the thing that comes when you go.

## Appearance
You hear it first: someone you love, calling from just out of sight. Then the dark nearby is darker, and the dark has a shape like a long-legged animal, blind, patient, made of the space between stars.

## Where
The Black Horizon [ETH-507], then the Hollow Verge, then the world's darkest nights.

## Game statistics (GM)
Start from: Balhannoth (Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the Multiverse). About CR 11. Its call: each creature that can hear it makes a DC 16 Wisdom save at the start of its turn or must move toward it. A creature it carries off falls asleep, and dreams of a warm field.

## Secret (GM only)
Those it takes aren't dead. They are in the Shepherd's fold, beyond the Horizon, asleep. Getting them back is the hardest thing in the realm.
