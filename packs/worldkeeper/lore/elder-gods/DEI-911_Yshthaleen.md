# Ysh'thaleen, the Sleeper in the Grass

## What she is [SECRET]
The shallowest of the seven, and the gentlest. She sleeps under the Hollow Verge [ETH-501], and the Verge is her dream: a world misremembered, grey grass and things you almost recall. She dreams of everyone who has ever been forgotten, which is why the forgotten wash up there.

## Sign
A blade of grey grass, bent, as if someone just stepped on it.

## In the schism
She loved the seed most, and grieved most when the teachers drew the Watchers. She chose to sleep closest to the world, to keep its forgotten.

## What she wants
That nobody is lost entirely. She is why the Nameless Asylum's lamps feel warm, though nobody there knows it.

## If she wakes
The Verge brightens. The grey grass greens. The forgotten start remembering who they were, all at once, and many of them can't bear it. Greylings stop whispering. Anyone Unmade in the realm could, perhaps, be asked for back.

## Her name
Spoken in the Hollow Verge, her name makes the grass lie flat toward the speaker, and every Greyling within a mile turns to listen.

## Laws of Existence
In addition to Immutable Existence and the Unseen Gaze (Elder Gods at the Table [RUL-621]). Save DCs follow its reach.

| Law | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Unremembering** | Each day in her reach, a creature makes a Wisdom save. On a failure it forgets one named person or place (the player chooses; strike it from their notes) until it spends a long rest outside her reach. |
| **The Kept** | A creature that dies in her reach rises as a Greyling [CRE-501] in 1d4 hours, unless someone who knew it speaks its name over the body. |
| **Dream Contagion** | A creature that finishes a long rest in her reach makes a Charisma save. On a failure it dreams a forgotten stranger's life: it wakes with one true clue the GM chooses, and regains no Hit Dice from that rest. |
| **Grey Grass** | Nothing that passes through her reach leaves no trace. Checks to track always succeed here, and nothing can hide its trail. A thing dropped or lost here is always found again, eventually. |

## Manifestation
**The Grey Mother.** A woman of grey grass the size of a hill, lying asleep across the Verge, one hand open. Start from the Empyrean (Monster Manual), with radiant damage replaced by psychic. Any creature she hits forgets the last minute. Defeating it doesn't kill the god; it pushes the god's reach down a step for a year and a day.

## How it can be stopped
- **Return her to dormancy.** Sing her back to sleep. The Grey Mother rhyme is the first verse of her lullaby. The rest is in the Silent Archive, and one line is in Keeper Lantern's lamp. Sung in the Verge with an Asylum lamp lit, the whole lullaby puts her down for a thousand years.
- **Rewrite a Law.** Give her a true memory you'll never get back (one the player chooses, permanently lost) to strike out one of her Laws for good.
- **Speak the true name.** Her name is half-known in the rhyme. It binds her while it's held.
