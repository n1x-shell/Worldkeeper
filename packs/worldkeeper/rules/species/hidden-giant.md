Gentle giants of the deep forests, who'd much rather hide than fight.

## Forest Speech
You can communicate simple ideas with Beasts and Plants, and you have Advantage on Wisdom (Animal Handling) checks.

## Powerful Build
You have Advantage on any saving throw or check you make to end the Grappled condition, and you count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity.

## Hidden Step
As a Bonus Action, you can turn Invisible until the start of your next turn, or until you attack, deal damage or force a creature to make a saving throw. You can do so a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

## Forest Magic
Starting at character level 3 you can cast Detect Magic, and at level 5 Disguise Self, each once without a spell slot; you regain the ability to cast them this way when you finish a Long Rest. When you cast Disguise Self this way, you can seem up to 3 feet shorter or taller. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for these spells (choose when you select this species). You can also cast them with any spell slots you have of the right level.
