Name: Satyr
Creature Type: Fey
Size: Medium
Speed: 35 feet
People: PPL-037 Satyrs
Stands in for: the satyr rules, for GMs without that book

Goat-legged revellers with a song for every occasion and a joke at the worst time.

## Ram
Your headbutt is a natural weapon for your unarmed strikes, dealing 1d6 + your Strength modifier Bludgeoning damage.

## Hard to Charm
You have Advantage on saving throws against spells.

## Leaping Hooves
Whenever you make a long or high jump, you can roll a d8 and add the result to the number of feet you cover, even when making a standing jump.

## Reveller
You have proficiency in the Performance and Persuasion skills, and with one musical instrument of your choice.
