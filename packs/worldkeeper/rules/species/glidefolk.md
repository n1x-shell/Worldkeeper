Kind: species
Name: Glidefolk
Creature Type: Humanoid
Size: Medium or Small (chosen when you select this species)
Speed: 30 feet
People: PPL-050 Hadozee
Stands in for: the hadozee rules, for GMs without that book

Long-armed gliding sailors from a forest world, fearless at any height.

## Climber
You have a Climb Speed equal to your Speed, and you can use a Bonus Action to take the Use an Object action.

## Gliding Membranes
When you fall and aren't Incapacitated, you can reduce the fall's damage to 0, and you can move up to 5 feet horizontally for every 1 foot you descend.

## Twist Away
When you take damage, you can take a Reaction to reduce it by 1d6 + your Proficiency Bonus. You can do so a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
