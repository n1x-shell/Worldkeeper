Kind: species
Name: Riftborn Seeker
Creature Type: Humanoid
Size: Medium
Speed: 30 feet
People: PPL-026 Githzerai
Stands in for: the githzerai rules, for GMs without that book

Calm, disciplined people from beyond the world, who think very carefully before they act.

## Disciplined Mind
You have Advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Charmed or Frightened condition.

## Mind Shield
You have Resistance to Psychic damage.

## Seeker's Gifts
You know the Mage Hand cantrip, and the hand is invisible when you cast it this way. Starting at character level 3 you can cast Shield, and at level 5 Detect Thoughts, each once without a spell slot; you regain the ability to cast them this way when you finish a Long Rest. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for these spells (choose when you select this species). You can also cast them with any spell slots you have of the right level.
