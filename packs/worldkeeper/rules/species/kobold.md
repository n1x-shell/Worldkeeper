Kind: species
Name: Kobold
Creature Type: Humanoid
Size: Small
Speed: 30 feet
People: PPL-036 Kobolds
Stands in for: the kobold rules, for GMs without that book

Small, clever trap-makers, devoted to each other and to dragons nobody else can see.

## Darkvision
You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

## Trapwright
You gain proficiency with Tinker's Tools, and you have Advantage on Intelligence (Investigation) checks to find or disarm traps.

## Pack Tactics
Once per turn, you have Advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of your allies is within 5 feet of it and isn't Incapacitated.

## Draconic Spark
You know one of these cantrips of your choice: Mending, Prestidigitation or Fire Bolt. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for these spells (choose when you select this species). You can also cast them with any spell slots you have of the right level.
