Kind: species
Name: Deep Gnome
Creature Type: Humanoid
Size: Small
Speed: 30 feet
People: PPL-017 Deep Gnomes
Stands in for: the deep gnome rules, for GMs without that book

Small, stony people of the deep places, who trust rock more than anyone who lives above it.

## Darkvision
You have Darkvision with a range of 120 feet.

## Gnomish Cunning
You have Advantage on Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma saving throws.

## Stone Camouflage
You have Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks you make while in rocky or underground places.

## Hidden Ways
Starting at character level 3, you can cast Disguise Self, and at level 5 Pass without Trace, each once without a spell slot; you regain the ability to cast them this way when you finish a Long Rest. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for these spells (choose when you select this species). You can also cast them with any spell slots you have of the right level.
