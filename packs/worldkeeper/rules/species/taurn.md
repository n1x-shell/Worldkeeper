Horned nomads seven to nine feet tall, with runes cut into their horns: warriors and storm-calling shamans.

## Horns
Your horns are natural weapons for your unarmed strikes, dealing 1d6 + your Strength modifier Piercing damage.

## Goring Rush
When you take the Dash action and move at least 20 feet, you can make one attack with your horns as a Bonus Action.

## Hammering Horns
When you hit a creature with a melee attack as part of the Attack action, you can use a Bonus Action to push it up to 10 feet away from you if it is no more than one size larger than you.

## Storm-Caller
Starting at character level 3 you can cast Thunderwave once without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to do so when you finish a Long Rest. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for these spells (choose when you select this species). You can also cast them with any spell slots you have of the right level.
