Made people of iron and old work: leftovers of erased worlds in Aetheris, and Kaelith's sleeping vessels in Kaelmar.

## Built
You gain a +1 bonus to your Armor Class. You don't need to eat, drink or breathe, and you have Resistance to Poison damage and Advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Poisoned condition.

## Sentry's Rest
You don't sleep. During a Long Rest you spend at least 6 hours in an inactive, motionless state, during which you aren't Unconscious and can see and hear as normal.

## Made for a Purpose
You gain proficiency in one skill and with one tool of your choice.

## Old Work
When you finish a Long Rest, choose one: Advantage on your next death saving throw before your next Long Rest, or Temporary Hit Points equal to your character level.
