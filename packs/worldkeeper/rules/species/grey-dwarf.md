Grey dwarves of the deep forges, hard as their iron and twice as stubborn.

## Darkvision
You have Darkvision with a range of 120 feet.

## Iron Mind
You have Resistance to Poison damage, and Advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Charmed or Stunned condition.

## Deep-Forge Gifts
Starting at character level 3, you can cast Enlarge/Reduce on yourself (enlarging only), and at level 5 Invisibility on yourself, each once without a spell slot; you regain the ability to cast them this way when you finish a Long Rest. Neither needs material components when cast this way. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for these spells (choose when you select this species). You can also cast them with any spell slots you have of the right level.
