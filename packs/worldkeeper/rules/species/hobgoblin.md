Disciplined, ambitious goblin-kin, who keep order and expect it.

## Darkvision
You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

## Fey Ancestry
You have Advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Charmed condition.

## Drilled Together
When you take the Help action, the creature you help also gains Temporary Hit Points equal to your Proficiency Bonus. You can do so a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest. You can take the Help action as a Bonus Action.

## Keep Face
When you miss with an attack roll or fail an ability check or saving throw, you can gain a bonus to that roll equal to the number of allies you can see within 30 feet of you (maximum +5). Once you use this trait, you can't do so again until you finish a Long Rest.
