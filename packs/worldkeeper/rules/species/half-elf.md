Creature Type: Humanoid
Size: Medium
Speed: 30 feet
People: PPL-030 Half-elves
Stands in for: the half-elf rules, for GMs without that book

Children of two peoples, at home in both and wholly in neither.

## Darkvision
You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

## Fey Ancestry
You have Advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Charmed condition.

## Two Worlds
You gain proficiency in two skills of your choice, and you have Advantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks to find common ground between two people who distrust each other.
