People who can wear any face, and who sometimes forget which one was theirs.

## Borrowed Face
As an action, you change your appearance and voice: your height and build within your size, your features, hair, skin and voice, and how old you look. You choose each detail; you can't take on another creature's game statistics, and your clothes don't change. You stay in the new form until you use this trait again or die.

## Read the Room
You have proficiency in two of these skills of your choice: Deception, Insight, Intimidation, Performance or Persuasion.
