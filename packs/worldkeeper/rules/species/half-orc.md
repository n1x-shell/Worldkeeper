Stands in for: the half-orc rules, for GMs without that book

Children of orcs and humans, with an orc's strength and a temper they keep on a short leash.

## Darkvision
You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

## Hard to Put Down
When you're reduced to 0 Hit Points but not killed outright, you can drop to 1 Hit Point instead. Once you use this trait, you can't do so again until you finish a Long Rest.

## Short Leash
When you score a Critical Hit with a melee attack, you can roll one of the attack's damage dice one more time and add it to the extra damage. You have proficiency in the Intimidation skill.
