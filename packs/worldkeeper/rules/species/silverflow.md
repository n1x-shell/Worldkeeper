Kind: species
Name: Silverflow
Creature Type: Ooze
Size: Medium or Small (chosen when you select this species)
Speed: 30 feet
People: PPL-051 Plasmoids
Stands in for: the plasmoid rules, for GMs without that book

Shapeless, curious folk of the silver void, easily mistaken for a monster.

## Darkvision
You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

## Amorphous
You can squeeze through a space as narrow as 1 inch wide, provided you are wearing and carrying nothing. You have Advantage on ability checks to start or escape a grapple.

## Pseudopod
As a Bonus Action, you can extend a pseudopod up to 10 feet away that can lift up to 10 pounds, manipulate an object, open an unlocked door or container, or stow or retrieve an item. It can't attack or activate magic items, and it retracts at the end of the turn.

## Ooze Body
You can hold your breath for 1 hour. You have Resistance to Acid and Poison damage, and Advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Poisoned condition.
