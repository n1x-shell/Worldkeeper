Kind: species
Name: Emberborn
Creature Type: Humanoid
Size: Medium or Small (chosen when you select this species)
Speed: 30 feet
People: PPL-024 Fire Genasi
Stands in for: the fire genasi rules, for GMs without that book

People with fire in their blood, warm to the touch and quick to burn.

## Darkvision
You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

## Fire in the Blood
You have Resistance to Fire damage, and you never suffer from cold weather.

## Flame Gifts
You know the Produce Flame cantrip. Starting at character level 3 you can cast Burning Hands, and at level 5 Flame Blade, each once without a spell slot; you regain the ability to cast them this way when you finish a Long Rest. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for these spells (choose when you select this species). You can also cast them with any spell slots you have of the right level.
