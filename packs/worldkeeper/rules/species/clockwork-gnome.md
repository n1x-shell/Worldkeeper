Name: Clockwork Gnome

People: PPL-048 Autognomes
Stands in for: the autognome rules, for GMs without that book

Little made folk, built by gnomes and left with a job to do.

## Built Tough
Your Armor Class is 13 + your Dexterity modifier while you aren't wearing armor. You have Advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Poisoned condition, and Resistance to Poison damage.

## Made for Work
You gain proficiency with two tools of your choice. You don't need to eat, drink or breathe.

## Running Repairs
As a Bonus Action you can regain Hit Points equal to 1d6 + your Proficiency Bonus. You can do so a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

## Sentry's Rest
You don't sleep. During a Long Rest you spend at least 6 hours in an inactive, motionless state, during which you aren't Unconscious and can see and hear as normal.
