Kind: species
Name: Bugbear
Creature Type: Humanoid
Size: Medium
Speed: 30 feet
People: PPL-014 Bugbears
Stands in for: the bugbear rules, for GMs without that book

Big, quiet and much stealthier than anyone expects, until they decide to be noticed.

## Darkvision
You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

## Long Arms
When you make a melee attack on your turn, your reach for it is 5 feet greater than normal.

## Quiet Bulk
You have proficiency in the Stealth skill, and you count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can push, drag or lift.

## Out of Nowhere
If you hit a creature with an attack roll during the first round of a combat, and it hasn't yet taken a turn, the attack deals an extra 2d6 damage of the weapon's type. You can deal this extra damage only once per combat.
