Kind: species
Name: Bloodbound
Creature Type: Humanoid
Size: Medium
Speed: 30 feet
People: PPL-039 Shifters
Stands in for: the shifter rules, for GMs without that book

People with a beast in their blood, who let it out when it matters.

## Darkvision
You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

## Keen Instincts
You have proficiency in one of these skills of your choice: Acrobatics, Athletics, Intimidation or Survival.

## Let It Out
As a Bonus Action, you let the beast show for 1 minute. You gain Temporary Hit Points equal to twice your Proficiency Bonus, and one of these, chosen when you select this species: Long-tooth (your bite is a natural weapon for unarmed strikes, dealing 1d6 + your Strength modifier Piercing damage, and you can make it as a Bonus Action while shifted); Swift (your Speed increases by 10 feet and you can climb at your Speed); Thick-hide (+1 to your Armor Class); or Wild-eyed (Advantage on Wisdom checks and saving throws). You can do so a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
