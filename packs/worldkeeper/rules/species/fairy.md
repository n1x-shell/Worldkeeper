Tiny winged folk with old magic and a very strict sense of fairness.

## Wings
You have a Fly Speed equal to your Speed. You can't use it while wearing Medium or Heavy armor.

## Old Magic
You know the Druidcraft cantrip. Starting at character level 3 you can cast Faerie Fire, and at level 5 Enlarge/Reduce, each once without a spell slot; you regain the ability to cast them this way when you finish a Long Rest. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for these spells (choose when you select this species). You can also cast them with any spell slots you have of the right level.

## Fair Dealing
You have Advantage on Wisdom (Insight) checks to tell whether someone is lying to you or breaking a bargain.
