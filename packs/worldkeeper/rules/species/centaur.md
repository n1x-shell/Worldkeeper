Name: Centaur
Creature Type: Fey

People: PPL-015 Centaurs
Stands in for: the centaur rules, for GMs without that book

Horse-bodied wanderers of the open plains, who measure the world in a day's gallop.

## Hooves
Your hooves are natural weapons for your unarmed strikes, dealing 1d6 + your Strength modifier Bludgeoning damage.

## Charge
If you move at least 20 feet straight toward a target and then hit it with a melee attack on the same turn, you can make one attack with your hooves against it as a Bonus Action.

## Horse's Back
You count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can push, drag or lift. Climbing costs you 4 extra feet of movement for every foot instead of 1, and a ladder or narrow stair is Difficult Terrain for you.

## Plainsfolk
You have proficiency in one of these skills of your choice: Animal Handling, Medicine, Nature or Survival.
