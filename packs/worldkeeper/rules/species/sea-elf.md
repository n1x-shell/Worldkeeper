Kind: species
Name: Sea Elf
Creature Type: Humanoid
Size: Medium
Speed: 30 feet
People: PPL-038 Sea Elves
Stands in for: the sea elf rules, for GMs without that book

Elves of the deep water, who came up from under the sea when the sea god died.

## Darkvision
You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

## Fey Ancestry
You have Advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Charmed condition.

## Child of the Deep
You have a Swim Speed equal to your Speed, you can breathe air and water, and you have Resistance to Cold damage.

## Speaker for the Sea
You can communicate simple ideas with any Beast that has a Swim Speed. It can understand your words, though you have no special ability to understand it in return.
