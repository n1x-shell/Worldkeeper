Name: Serpent-folk
Creature Type: Humanoid
Size: Medium or Small (chosen when you select this species)
Speed: 30 feet
People: PPL-046 Yuan-ti
Stands in for: the yuan-ti rules, for GMs without that book

Serpent-folk with cold blood, sharp minds and a reputation they've mostly earned.

## Darkvision
You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

## Cold Blood
You have Resistance to Poison damage, and Advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Poisoned condition.

## Hard to Bewitch
You have Advantage on saving throws against spells.

## Serpent Gifts
You know the Poison Spray cantrip. Starting at character level 3 you can cast Animal Friendship (targeting only snakes), and at level 5 Suggestion, each once without a spell slot; you regain the ability to cast them this way when you finish a Long Rest. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for these spells (choose when you select this species). You can also cast them with any spell slots you have of the right level.
