Creature Type: Humanoid
Size: Medium
Speed: 30 feet
People: PPL-047 Astral Elves
Stands in for: the astral elf rules, for GMs without that book

Elves who went looking for their gods among the stars, and stayed so long the starlight stayed in them.

## Darkvision
You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

## Fey Ancestry
You have Advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Charmed condition.

## Starlight
You know the Light cantrip, and you can cast it with a range of Self, making your eyes or skin shine. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for these spells (choose when you select this species). You can also cast them with any spell slots you have of the right level.

## Long Watch
You don't need to sleep, and magic can't put you to sleep. You can finish a Long Rest in 4 hours if you spend them in quiet watch, conscious. When you finish one, you gain proficiency in one skill of your choice until you finish your next Long Rest.
