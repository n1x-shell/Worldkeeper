Kind: species
Name: Dawn-touched
Creature Type: Humanoid
Size: Medium or Small (chosen when you select this species)
Speed: 30 feet
People: PPL-012 Aasimar
Stands in for: the aasimar rules, for GMs without that book

People with a little of the divine in them, who glow when they're angry and are never quite sure why.

## Darkvision
You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

## Steady Light
You have Resistance to Radiant damage, and you know the Light cantrip. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for these spells (choose when you select this species). You can also cast them with any spell slots you have of the right level.

## Mending Touch
As a Magic action, you touch a creature and roll a number of d4s equal to your Proficiency Bonus. The creature regains Hit Points equal to the total. Once you use this trait, you can't do so again until you finish a Long Rest.

## Anger's Glow
Starting at character level 3, as a Bonus Action you can make yourself shine for 1 minute: you shed Bright Light in a 10-foot radius, and once on each of your turns, one creature you hit with an attack or a spell takes extra Radiant damage equal to your Proficiency Bonus. Once you use this trait, you can't do so again until you finish a Long Rest.
