Crow-folk who speak in borrowed sounds, and remember every one they've heard.

## Perfect Echo
You can mimic any sound or voice you have heard. A creature that hears your mimicry can tell it isn't real only with a successful Wisdom (Insight) check against a DC of 8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your Charisma modifier.

## Long Memory
You can remember, word for word and sound for sound, anything you've seen or heard in the last month. You gain proficiency in two of these skills of your choice: Acrobatics, Deception, Sleight of Hand or Stealth.

## Remembered Moment
When you make an ability check or attack roll, you can give yourself Advantage on it, recalling a time you did it before. You can do so a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
