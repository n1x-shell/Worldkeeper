# Encounters: The Fields of Lost Realities (depth 2), the new peoples
Region: ETH-502
1. **Fight.** A press-gang off a pirate driftship: 2 Hadozee Warriors and a Plasmoid Boarder (Spelljammer), short of crew and not fussy.
2. **Fight.** Giff deserters from the Twelfth Regiment of Nowhere (2 Giff Shock Troopers (Spelljammer)), guarding a cask of stolen powder.
3. **Fight.** A thri-kreen hunting clutch from a desert wreck (3 Thri-kreen Marauders (Spelljammer)), who take the party for prey until someone speaks.
4. **Fight.** An Astral Elf Honor Guard (Spelljammer) challenges whoever insulted the Starlit Court. It may have been an accident.
5. **Fight.** A wreck-island already claimed by 2 Plasmoid Explorers (Spelljammer), who don't share.
6. **Travellers.** Captain Chiri Akko [NPC-402] and the *Rope-and-Wish*, offering passage deeper into the Fields.
7. **Travellers.** Major Halloway Stroud [NPC-401] and a column of the Twelfth Regiment, asking every salvager about the giff homeworld.
8. **Travellers.** An envoy of Lady Sennith Velarys [NPC-399]'s Starlit Court (an Astral Elf Aristocrat (Spelljammer)), with an invitation, asking for news of the gods.
9. **Travellers.** Gauge [NPC-400], measuring a wreck-island, who asks the party to hold one end of the line.
10. **Sign.** A petrified god drifts by in the silver void, vast and grey, with a giff regiment's banner planted on its shoulder.
11. **Strange.** Yazir, the hadozee's forest world, passes overhead, and hadozee glide down from it to trade.
12. **Strange.** An autognome sits on a wreck, still at work for a maker who isn't coming back. It asks the party to sign for the delivery.

# Encounters: The Hollow Verge (depth 1), the new peoples
Region: ETH-501
1. **Travellers.** Tch'kala [NPC-404] on a hunt, who offers to share the kill and the way back.
2. **Travellers.** Ooma [NPC-403], out walking in a borrowed shape, who would like to hear the party's story.
3. **Travellers.** Two autognomes sent from Kaelholm, lost, with maps that are all wrong.
4. **Travellers.** A hadozee crew from a wrecked driftship, walking to the Nameless Asylum.
5. **Fight.** 2 Thri-kreen Marauders (Spelljammer) who think the party is following them. By chance, it is.
6. **Fight.** A Plasmoid Boarder (Spelljammer) that has taken the shape of the door the party just opened.
7. **Sign.** Giff boot-prints in neat rows, marching toward the last threshold.
8. **Strange.** An astral elf sits in the grey grass, weeping starlight. She has just remembered how long it's been.
