Note: Random encounters for Kaelmar. At levels 1 to 4 parties stay around Ashvale and its nine settlements, as the archive's hooks suggest, so the six regions start at level 5. About half are fights; the rest are travellers, signs and strange sights. Creature numbers are for a party of four.

# Encounters: Ashvale and the Western Hills, levels 1 to 4
Region: SET-301
1. **Fight.** 1d4 + 1 twig blights [CRE-302] creeping out of a hedge that was a fence last week.
2. **Fight.** A Half-made [CRE-306] drifting along the west road in its slow lockstep, toward the nearest light.
3. **Fight.** 1d4 Thread Mites [CRE-033] clustered on a fence post where someone said goodbye forever.
4. **Fight.** A pack of 3 wolves, driven out of the Deepwood by something worse.
5. **Fight.** Salvage thieves (3 bandits and a scout) robbing a Wrights' cart on the Coldwater road.
6. **Travellers.** Odran Reeve's [NPC-312] salt barge, poling up from Low Fenn. He'll pay for an escort.
7. **Travellers.** Jory Hesk [NPC-341] and a line of children, practising the keeping song on the way to the stones.
8. **Travellers.** A Bloom-Speaker with copper lines on her forearms, kneeling to listen to a field of grass.
9. **Sign.** A green gourd-lamp at the treeline. The Eastern Tendril is watching.
10. **Sign.** A cow standing perfectly still in a Sallow Cross field, staring at a patch of ground that reflects nothing.
11. **Strange.** For a moment the hum from under the west road can be heard right across the hills.
12. **Strange.** A child in Coldwater with frost on her hands, who says she dreamed of thirteen stones.

# Encounters: Ashvale and the Western Hills, levels 5 to 10
Region: SET-301
1. **Fight.** 2 Walkers [CRE-305] from an old Kaelith station, still guarding a road that leads nowhere now.
2. **Fight.** 3 Half-made [CRE-306] and a Vessel [CRE-311] coming up out of a sinkhole that opened overnight.
3. **Fight.** A Thing from the Between [CRE-313] climbing out of a seep of glass [CRE-354].
4. **Fight.** An Ascendancy recruiting party (a veteran and 4 thugs) who don't like being told no.
5. **Fight.** A shambling mound [CRE-303] in the Low Fenn reeds, wearing an old Wright's coat.
6. **Travellers.** Chief Wright Brenna Halvor [NPC-306] with a cart of salvage and a broken axle.
7. **Travellers.** A Kaelholm survey team, polite and armed, asking about the waygate.
8. **Sign.** One of the nine settlements has stopped answering its neighbours' letters.
9. **Sign.** A new crack in the ground at the Rite Circle, running toward the thirteenth stone.
10. **Strange.** Every well in Kettleby rings like a bell at midnight.
11. **Strange.** The Bloom in a meadow writes a word in its flowers. Tevan Lin [NPC-305] wants to know which one.
12. **Strange.** A traveller from Aetheris, dazed, who came the wrong way down the Crossroads road and doesn't know the date.

# Encounters: The Eastern Deepwood, levels 5 to 10
Region: REG-301
1. **Fight.** A Myconid [CRE-301] circle defending its ring from intruders, with spore-servants.
2. **Fight.** 2 needle blights and a vine blight [CRE-302], grown together around a Kaelith ruin.
3. **Fight.** A Hunter [CRE-307], built to capture, still following orders from a vault that no longer exists.
4. **Fight.** Thinking rot [CRE-350] across the path, with a hostile will behind it: 2 shambling mounds [CRE-303].
5. **Fight.** Eastern Tendril zealots (a druid and 3 scouts) burning Kaelith work, and anyone carrying it.
6. **Travellers.** A Spore Circle druid with a gourd-lamp, who'll guide you past a spore-storm for a story.
7. **Travellers.** A Kaelholm surveyor who has been lost in the Deepwood for a month and has started to like it.
8. **Sign.** A fungus-covered tower with a door at the top. The stairs are on the outside, made of shelf-mushrooms.
9. **Sign.** Copper-veined ferns all pointing the same way, toward Arborhold.
10. **Strange.** A spore-storm [CRE-351] blows through. Constitution save, or a lasting change.
11. **Strange.** The party hears its own conversation from yesterday through a circuit-root [CRE-353].
12. **Strange.** In the dead zone the Bloom doesn't grow, and nothing makes a sound, not even your footsteps.

# Encounters: The Eastern Deepwood, levels 11 to 20
Region: REG-301
1. **Fight.** An ancient treant [CRE-304] that has listened to the Bloom for three centuries and come to a conclusion.
2. **Fight.** A War-vessel [CRE-308] woken in Arborhold's outer works, its minds arguing over whether you are an enemy.
3. **Fight.** A Guardian [CRE-310] with a rebirth core: it reforms once after it's destroyed.
4. **Fight.** A Myconid sovereign and its circle, spore-bound to something much older under the roots.
5. **Fight.** A Bloom-shift [CRE-352] in the dead zone, and 2 Hunters [CRE-307] driving the party into it.
6. **Travellers.** Elder Sorrel [NPC-307] of the Eastern Tendril, walking to Thornhaven with a grave face.
7. **Travellers.** A Convergence Collective envoy who speaks for many voices at once.
8. **Sign.** The survey garden in full bloom out of season, and its keeper missing.
9. **Sign.** Every tree within a mile has turned its leaves toward the party.
10. **Strange.** A Kaelith door in a tree trunk, standing open. The room inside is bigger than the tree.
11. **Strange.** The party wakes with copper lines on their forearms that weren't there last night.
12. **Strange.** The Bloom speaks, plainly, in one party member's voice: "Not yet."

# Encounters: The Eastern Mountains, levels 5 to 10
Region: REG-302
1. **Fight.** Ascendancy mercenaries (a veteran, 4 thugs) holding a pass and charging a toll.
2. **Fight.** 2 Walkers [CRE-305] from Cradlehorn's outer line, still patrolling.
3. **Fight.** A pair of griffons whose eyrie the party has walked under.
4. **Fight.** A goliath hunting party who've mistaken the party for raiders. They can be talked down.
5. **Fight.** 1d4 + 2 Half-made [CRE-306] in an old mine, mining nothing in lockstep.
6. **Travellers.** A goliath clan herald carrying a challenge from one clan to another (see The Goliath Feud).
7. **Travellers.** A Wright expedition heading for Cradlehorn with maps they shouldn't have.
8. **Sign.** A cairn on every peak, each with a goliath name and a number. The numbers are counting down.
9. **Sign.** The Ascendancy's mark carved into a cliff, where the doctrine began as a mercenary creed.
10. **Strange.** An avalanche that falls upward.
11. **Strange.** A rail line on the mountainside, running straight into the rock toward Cradlehorn.
12. **Strange.** At dawn, the shadow of a horn on the peaks, though nothing up there casts it.

# Encounters: The Eastern Mountains, levels 11 to 20

2. **Fight.** An Ascendancy war-company (2 champions, 8 veterans) marching to settle the Goliath Feud for pay.
3. **Fight.** A Vault Ward [CRE-312] and 2 Walkers guarding Cradlehorn's under-road gate.
4. **Fight.** A stone giant clan defending its peaks against everyone.

11. **Strange.** Snow that falls in perfect hexagons the size of a hand, each one a little map.
12. **Strange.** A goliath who has been carrying the same stone up the same peak for thirty-one years, and won't say why.

# Encounters: The Central Bloom Country, levels 5 to 10

4. **Fight.** Continuity agents (a mage and 3 spies), trying to stop the change spreading, by any means.
5. **Fight.** 1d4 + 2 vine blights [CRE-302] in the open Bloom, moving like wind in grass.
6. **Travellers.** Someone coming out of the memory-house [LOC-302], with a memory that isn't theirs.
7. **Travellers.** A Sentinel Hold patrol, asking whether the party has seen anything coming up from below.
8. **Sign.** An under-road vent breathing warm air that smells of oil and old stone.
9. **Sign.** The Bloom has grown over a road in a perfect copy of the road.
10. **Strange.** For a moment, everyone in the party knows what the others are thinking.
11. **Strange.** A field of Bloom that hums the keeping song.
12. **Strange.** The party passes through the same village twice in a day, half a day's travel apart.

10. **Strange.** The ground under the party goes hollow and sounds like a drum.
11. **Strange.** The party's dreams are shared, and someone else is in them too.
12. **Strange.** A Kaelith voice from the under-roads announces the next train, in a language that used to be spoken here.
