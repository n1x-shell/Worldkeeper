Note: Random encounters for Aetheris, one d12 table per region and tier. About half are fights; the rest are travellers, signs and strange sights. Creature numbers are for a party of four; add or remove one for each player more or fewer.

# Encounters: The Hollow Marches, levels 1 to 4
Region: REG-001
1. **Fight.** 1d4 + 1 Forgotten Legionnaires [CRE-027] march out of the morning mist in step, fighting a war nobody else remembers.
2. **Fight.** 2 Briar Wolves [CRE-018] shadowing the party since dawn, hungry and patient.
3. **Fight.** A scavenger crew (4 bandits and a bandit captain) who think the party's packs look like salvage.
4. **Fight.** 1d4 Thread Mites [CRE-033] drift out of a hollow log toward whichever two characters are closest.
5. **Fight.** A Ghost Artillerist [CRE-029] fires from a ridge where no gun has stood for three hundred years.
6. **Travellers.** A Legion Scout [CRE-006] and a Celestial Order Dawnward Healer [CRE-002] sharing a fire, very carefully. Both want news of the Shard of Dominion.
7. **Travellers.** Torren Kell's [NPC-011] scavengers, hauling a cart of rusted armour. They'll trade, and they'll warn you off the Scar.
8. **Sign.** A cairn of fresh stones on the road, each one painted with a name. One of them is a party member's.
9. **Sign.** Faction banners from all five dominions, burned, staked in a ring around a dead campfire.
10. **Strange.** For an hour, every road the party takes curves back toward Hollow Refuge.
11. **Strange.** A rain of grey ash that smells of incense. Everyone it touches remembers a dream they haven't had yet.
12. **Strange.** A traveller who greets one of the characters by a name they've never used, apologises, and walks on.

11. **Strange.** A dead soldier at the roadside who sits up, says the date in a calendar nobody uses, and lies down again.
12. **Strange.** The party walks for an hour and arrives where they left, but it is the next morning.
