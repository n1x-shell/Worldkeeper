Note: Random encounters for the Ethereal Realm, by depth. The Hollow Verge, the border layer, has three tiers, because a party can live there from level 1. Each depth below has one table: the deeper it goes, the stranger it gets. About half are fights. Remember that death in the deep realm Unmakes [SYS-907].

# Encounters: The Hollow Verge (depth 1), levels 1 to 4
Region: ETH-501
1. **Fight.** 1d4 Greylings [CRE-501] sliding across the grey grass, whispering names.
2. **Fight.** A Lamp-moth Swarm [CRE-503] that puts out the party's light and then comes for them in the dark.
3. **Fight.** 2 Name-Eater spawn [CRE-502] in a puddle that isn't water.
4. **Fight.** A lost Thread Mite [CRE-033] cloud (1d4 + 1), drawn to anyone carrying a loose end.
5. **Fight.** A scavenger from the Fields (a bandit captain and 2 bandits), desperate, with a sack of things that weren't there.
6. **Travellers.** Tessk the Wayfinder [NPC-372], guiding a lost soul back to the Asylum, for a price.
7. **Travellers.** A Veilborn refugee who remembers seven lives and can't decide which one is theirs.
8. **Travellers.** Brindle Quickfoot [NPC-373] with his pack, coming back from the edge of the Fields.
9. **Sign.** A door, standing in the grass, slightly open. Through it, the inside of an empty house in Aetheris.
10. **Sign.** The grey sky darkens to dusk, then lightens again, and the party has lost an hour.
11. **Strange.** A field of grey flowers, each one the face of someone nobody remembers.
12. **Strange.** The party hears, far off, the dawn-bell of the Nameless Asylum, ringing at the wrong hour.

# Encounters: The Hollow Verge (depth 1), levels 5 to 10
Region: ETH-501
1. **Fight.** A Threshold Hound [CRE-504] and 2 Greylings [CRE-501], guarding a way down that shouldn't be open.
2. **Fight.** An Echo [CRE-505] wearing one of the party's faces, which wants to live their life instead.
3. **Fight.** An old Name-Eater [CRE-502] (Adult Oblex), with the faces of three Asylum residents.
4. **Fight.** A Void Feeder [CRE-023] that came in through the Rift and stayed.
5. **Fight.** A githyanki war-party, chasing the path Tessk's war-band took.
6. **Travellers.** Keeper Lantern [NPC-371], walking the Verge's edge at night, counting.
7. **Travellers.** A Kaelmar root-road traveller who came out here instead of at their crossroads, a little Thinner.
8. **Sign.** A stair going down into the grass, under a dry well. At the bottom, a light.
9. **Sign.** Silver threads in the grey grass, all running one way.
10. **Strange.** The party meets someone they knew who died. The person doesn't remember dying.
11. **Strange.** The warning at the last threshold, carved in a stone the party passes: "Beyond this point, the world will no longer dream you back."
12. **Strange.** For a moment the grey sky shows a map of Aetheris and Kaelmar, seen from below.

# Encounters: The Hollow Verge (depth 1), levels 11 to 20
Region: ETH-501
1. **Fight.** A Rift Stalker [CRE-024] hunting whoever crossed the Rift last.
2. **Fight.** 3 Threshold Hounds [CRE-504], set on the party by someone who doesn't want them to go deeper.
3. **Fight.** A Hidden Warden [CRE-035], erasing a refugee who remembers too much.
4. **Fight.** An Abandoned Future [CRE-524], come up from the Citadel looking for the life it was meant to have.
5. **Fight.** A githyanki knight and her war-band, who have heard the party carries a shard.
6. **Travellers.** Kyrra the Shadeborn [NPC-070], who offers to speak to someone the party has lost.
7. **Travellers.** An Architect [FAC-502] buyer, looking for pieces of a world the Hidden Ones erased on purpose.
8. **Sign.** The Asylum's lamps all out at once, and its gate standing open.
9. **Sign.** A tear in the sky, like torn grey cloth, with stars behind it.
10. **Strange.** One of the party is greeted by name at the last threshold, by a voice from beyond it.
11. **Strange.** The Verge's grass grows over one character's feet while they stand still, gently, as if it wants them to stay.
12. **Strange.** A door that opens on Hollow Refuge's Crooked Nail, thirty-one years ago.

# Encounters: The Fields of Lost Realities (depth 2)
Region: ETH-502
1. **Fight.** A Driftwhale [CRE-506] grazing the wreckage, startled into a charge.
2. **Fight.** 2 Wreck-eels [CRE-507] in a sideways sea inside a golden cathedral.
3. **Fight.** A neogi crew of Spindleclaws [CRE-508] with umber hulk slaves, salvaging a world.
4. **Fight.** A Ruin Mimic [CRE-510] pretending to be a building the party once lived in.
5. **Fight.** A Monument [CRE-509] to one of the party, which wants to stand where they stand.
6. **Travellers.** Salvagers from the Crucible of Creation, who'll buy or sell pieces of worlds.
7. **Travellers.** A whole village from a discarded world, still living their last day, over and over.
8. **Sign.** A Legion fortress with a golden cathedral lying sideways inside it.
9. **Sign.** The party's own monuments, in a row, each from a version of them that won.
10. **Strange.** Silver Drift [CRE-511]: an astral current that carries anyone caught in it 1d4 miles a round.
11. **Strange.** A street from a world that ended, with someone sweeping it.
12. **Strange.** The party finds a map that shows the Fields exactly, and it's already out of date.

# Encounters: The Ossuary of Kings (depth 3)
Region: ETH-503
1. **Fight.** An Oathless Blade [CRE-514] company: sword wraiths who swore to kings who were erased.
2. **Fight.** A Bone Herald [CRE-512] announcing the party to a court of the dead.
3. **Fight.** The Crowned Dead [CRE-513], a king who can finally die, and is afraid, and lashes out.
4. **Fight.** 2 Oathless Blade commanders duelling for a throne, who'll unite against the party.
5. **Fight.** Titan challengers from the Colosseum of Echoes, testing anyone who passes.
6. **Travellers.** A queen from a forgotten Aetherfall who wants to know how her kingdom is remembered. It isn't.
7. **Travellers.** Titans of the Rift [FAC-504] escorting a summoned challenger to the Colosseum.
8. **Sign.** Thrones in rows to the horizon, most of them empty, some of them still warm.
9. **Sign.** The Fractured Throne's light, far off, where the Cycle Breakers meet.
10. **Strange.** A crown left on the ground, which fits whoever picks it up perfectly.
11. **Strange.** The party is announced by heralds they can't see, with titles they haven't earned yet.
12. **Strange.** A king's funeral, the first in centuries, with nobody to mourn him. He asks the party to.

# Encounters: The Silent Archive (depth 4)
Region: ETH-504
1. **Fight.** 2 Readers [CRE-515] who want to read one of the party, and won't take no for an answer.
2. **Fight.** A Hungry Lens [CRE-517] drifting through the stacks, draining the magic out of books, and out of spellcasters.
3. **Fight.** A Gibbering Margin [CRE-518] spilling out of a book's notes.
4. **Fight.** A Hollow Scholar [CRE-516], who has been looking for the party's book for a very long time (levels 17 and up).
5. **Fight.** A Hidden Warden [CRE-035], come to erase a book that should have stayed blank.
6. **Travellers.** A librarian with no face, who will fetch any book for a price, paid in memories.
7. **Travellers.** Inkling's [NPC-375] copies, turning up in books that used to be blank. One of them is about the party.
8. **Sign.** A book for every erased soul. Most are blank. One is open, and someone has been reading it.
9. **Sign.** The untitled book at the Archive's centre, glimpsed down an aisle, with its shard-shaped hollow.
10. **Strange.** The books read the visitor: each character hears a sentence from their own life read aloud.
11. **Strange.** A book in which one character's next day is written, in their own hand.
12. **Strange.** The stacks rearrange themselves so the way out is always just behind the party.

# Encounters: The Loom of Eternity (depth 5)
Region: ETH-505
1. **Fight.** 2d4 Thread-grues [CRE-519], eating loose threads, and anything near them.
2. **Fight.** A Weft-mangler [CRE-520] tearing at the weave, which takes the party for rivals.
3. **Fight.** A Fate-seer [CRE-521], who has seen how the party dies and wants to make sure of it.
4. **Fight.** A Thread Ghost [CRE-522] guarding its own thread, the last of a life nobody remembers.
5. **Fight.** A Loom Sentinel [CRE-533], keeping the Loom from anyone who would touch it (levels 20, or flee).
6. **Travellers.** A servant of Vyrithar [NPC-002], who asks politely what the party wants with the Loom.
7. **Travellers.** A Cycle Breaker [FAC-501], come to make sure nobody relinks the shards.
8. **Sign.** Twelve colossal crystals piercing the black ocean of threads, one of them dark.
9. **Sign.** A thread the party recognises: one of their own, glowing.
10. **Strange.** The threads around the party tremble when they speak, as if listening.
11. **Strange.** A loose thread drifts past, connected to nothing. It is warm.
12. **Strange.** For a moment each character sees every choice they didn't make, and the thread each would have spun.

# Encounters: The Citadel of the Hidden (depth 6)
Region: ETH-506
1. **Fight.** A Corridor Cloak [CRE-523] in the passages behind reality.
2. **Fight.** An Abandoned Future [CRE-524] rising from a pool of futures that never happened.
3. **Fight.** 2 Hidden Wardens [CRE-035], faceless, erasing the party's way back.
4. **Fight.** A Watcher's Fragment [CRE-036] the Hidden Ones keep in a cell, now loose.
5. **Fight.** A Hidden One's guard of faceless Wardens and a Fate-seer [CRE-521], closing in.
6. **Travellers.** A Hidden One who wants to explain, and hasn't been allowed to for a very long time.
7. **Travellers.** A person who never existed, from a pool of abandoned futures, who'd like to.
8. **Sign.** Archives of lives, shelf after shelf, labelled in the Hidden Ones' hand.
9. **Sign.** A pool showing the world as it would be if the Cycle had never broken.
10. **Strange.** The party's shadows are filed away in a drawer, and must be taken back.
11. **Strange.** A corridor where every door opens onto a moment of the party's own past.
12. **Strange.** The Shadowed Sanctum's door, closed, with a lock shaped like a name.

9. **Sign.** The Veil of Eternity, a curtain of nothing at the edge of everything.
