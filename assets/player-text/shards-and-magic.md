# The Shards and the magic of Aetheris

## How to use this

What everyone in Aetheris knows about magic, the Cycle and the shards. What's really going on stays with the GM.

## Magic in Aetheris

*Power takes a price here: in memory, name, blood or sanity.*

Magic in Aetheris works as it does anywhere. The gods grant spells, mages study them, and the wild has its own. What marks Aetheris is the price. Power here takes something back, in memory, name, blood or sanity, and the greater the power, the more it takes. Old casters say you can always tell a great mage: they've forgotten something important, and they don't know what.

**Each dominion has its own weather.** Each of the five dominions still carries the rule of the world its faction made, and magic bends to it.

| Where | What it's like |
| --- | --- |
| The Hollow Marches | Ordinary gear is unreliable. Things break. |
| @[The Radiant Ascendancy|REG-002] | Healing comes easily. Necromancy and dark magic struggle, and they're illegal. |
| @[The Tyrant's Dominion|REG-003] | Blades bite deeper. |
| @[The Fractured Nexus|REG-004] | Spells hit harder, and sometimes tear a hole in the world. |
| @[The Eternal Veil|REG-005] | Shadows hide you. Illusions are hard to see through. |
| @[The Verdant Rebirth|REG-006] | The wild is easy. Machines and arcane spells struggle. |

**Aether Scars.** Ruins and records are full of leftover contradictions: a wall that remembers a battle that hasn't happened, a map with two rivers where there's one. Scholars collect them. Sensible people walk round them.

**What people say**

- Every spell costs something. Cheap ones cost things you won't miss.
- If a mage can't remember their mother's name, don't ask how they got so good.

## The Cycle and the Aetherfall

*How the world used to end and begin again, until about thirty years ago.*

For longer than anyone can count, the world of Aetheris kept starting over. Again and again a faction gathered the shards and reforged the Ethereal Crystal, and the world restarted as that faction's reality, in an event called an Aetherfall. Kingdoms, histories and whole peoples were rewritten. Nobody remembered the worlds before, except in scraps.

About thirty years ago it stopped. The world woke up one morning and stayed as it was. Five factions' worlds were left side by side, the dead stayed dead, and history began to pile up for the first time. Nobody knows who ended the Cycle, or how.

**What people say**

- Every faction claims it would have won the next Aetherfall.
- Some people say the Cycle will start again, if someone gathers the shards. Some people are trying.
- Your grandmother's world might not have been this one. Ask her what she remembers. Then ask her again next year.
- The Cycle's over. That doesn't mean nobody's pulling the strings.

## The Shards

*Twelve pieces of the broken Ethereal Crystal: the most powerful objects in the world, and the most dangerous.*

In the oldest myth, the Elders broke the Ethereal Crystal, and it shattered into twelve shards. For as long as the Cycle ran, whoever gathered them remade the world. The Cycle is broken now, and scholars say the shards can't remake the world any more. Every faction still wants them, just in case, and because each one is still the greatest power a person can hold.

Every shard gives great power, and takes something specific in return.

| Shard | What it gives | What it takes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| @[Shard of Dominion] | Bends others to the wielder's will. | The weight of everything the wielder must now control. |
| @[Shard of Infinity] | Sees unwritten futures and steps between realities. | The farther the gaze, the greater the risk of never coming back. |
| @[Shard of Ruin] | A fire that can't be put out. | Its hunger tempts the wielder to burn everything. |
| @[Shard of Sacrifice] | Fuels the wielder's strength. | Always something dear: memory, blood, soul, or a piece of existence. |
| @[Shard of Eternity] | Suspends moments and holds back fate. | Nothing stays frozen forever. |
| @[Shard of Illusions] | Makes the false true. | Every illusion eventually meets the truth. |
| @[Shard of Renewal] | Heals and restores beyond mortal limits. | Not everything should be restored. |
| @[Shard of Balance] | Weighs all things and stops any side tilting too far. | Balance isn't fair, and it cuts both ways. |
| @[Shard of Shadows] | Moves unseen, beyond perception. | The more you fade, the harder you are to remember. |
| @[Shard of Chaos] | Breaks rules and reshapes reality. | Fortune or ruin. It answers to no master. |
| @[Shard of Knowledge] | Reveals truths never meant to be known. | Some truths break the mind that learns them. |
| @[The Lost Twelfth Shard] | Nobody remembers. | Nobody remembers. |

**What people say**

- There's a shard under the Bloodmarked Fields. Every faction has sent someone.
- Nobody's seen all twelve together since the Cycle broke. Nobody wants to be the first, and everybody does.
- A shard marks whoever touches it. Ask anyone with a scar that glows.

## Shardscarred

*Anyone who has touched a shard, marked for good.*

Anyone who touches a shard, even for a moment, is marked for life. The mark might be a scar, a glowing vein, a dream that keeps coming back, or nothing anyone can see. The Shardscarred are harder to fool: their memories hold, and the world's rewriting slides off them.

Every faction wants them, in its own way. The Order watches them. The Legion recruits them. The Consortium studies them. The Pact hides them, or uses them.

**At the table.** A Shardscarred character has Advantage on saves against effects that alter their memory or rewrite reality. Ask your GM before choosing it.

**What people say**

- The Shardscarred remember things other people don't. That's not always a comfort.
- If you're scarred, don't tell a faction. They'll find out anyway.

## Shard of Dominion

*Bends others to the wielder's will, and makes them carry all of it.*

The Shard of Dominion bends other people to its wielder's will. The price is the weight of everything the wielder then has to control.

**What people say**

- It's under the Bloodmarked Fields. That's why the Fields are full.
- Every Legion warlord has dreamed of it.

## Shard of Infinity

*Sees futures that haven't been written, and steps between realities.*

The Shard of Infinity lets its wielder see futures that haven't been written, and step between realities. The farther they look, the greater the risk they never come back.

**What people say**

- Nobody's hunting it. Nobody knows where to start.
- People who look into the future too long come back late, if they come back.

## Shard of Ruin

*A fire that can't be put out, and a hunger to use it.*

The Shard of Ruin makes fire that can't be put out and reduces anything to ash. Its hunger tempts the wielder to burn everything.

**What people say**

- The Ashen Covenant wants it more than anyone.
- Wherever it is, it's warm.

## Shard of Sacrifice

*Strength without limit, paid for with whatever you hold dearest.*

The Shard of Sacrifice fuels its wielder's strength. It always takes something dear in return: a memory, blood, a soul, or a piece of existence.

**What people say**

- The Black Sun wants it very badly.
- Every price it takes is one you'd pay again. That's what makes it dangerous.

## Shard of Eternity

*Holds a moment still, and holds back fate.*

The Shard of Eternity suspends moments and holds back fate. Nothing stays frozen forever, and the time it holds has to be paid back.

**What people say**

- The Whispering Circle has been looking for it longer than anyone.
- Where time stands still, the shard is near. Nobody agrees where that is.

## Shard of Illusions

*Makes the false true, until the truth catches up.*

The Shard of Illusions bends perception until the false is true. Every illusion eventually meets the truth, and the wielder feels it when it does.

**What people say**

- The Shadow Pact wants it more than anything.
- If you're sure you've seen it, you probably haven't.

## Shard of Renewal

*Heals and restores beyond mortal limits.*

The Shard of Renewal heals and restores beyond anything mortal magic can do. Not everything should be restored, and what comes back is never quite the same.

**What people say**

- The Wildborn Clans say the forest is its rightful keeper.
- If you meet someone who came back wrong, ask where they were healed.

## Shard of Balance

*Stops any side tilting too far, and cuts both ways.*

The Shard of Balance weighs all things and stops any side tilting too far. Balance isn't fair: every gift it gives is matched by a loss.

**What people say**

- Nobody's looking for it. That's strange, for a shard.
- Some say it doesn't want to be found, and that's its own kind of balance.

## Shard of Shadows

*Walk unseen, and be forgotten.*

The Shard of Shadows lets its wielder move unseen and exist beyond perception. The more they fade, the harder they are to remember.

**What people say**

- The Nameless Choir wants it. Of course they do.

## Shard of Chaos

*Breaks the rules, and answers to no one.*

The Shard of Chaos breaks rules and reshapes reality, unpredictably. It's fortune or ruin, and it answers to no master.

**What people say**

- The Arcane Consortium wants it, for research.
- Wherever it's been, the weather is strange.

## Shard of Knowledge

*Truths never meant to be known.*

The Shard of Knowledge reveals truths never meant to be known. Some truths break the mind that learns them.

**What people say**

- The Arcane Consortium wants this one even more.
- A scholar who touched it is said to have laughed for a week.

## The Lost Twelfth Shard

*The one nobody remembers.*

There are twelve shards. Everyone knows that. Ask anyone to name them, and they'll stop at eleven. Nobody remembers what the twelfth one did, or where it was last seen, or who held it. Some people find that more frightening than any of the others.

**What people say**

- Even the oldest myths skip it.
- If you remember the twelfth shard, keep it to yourself.
