# Player Adventure Book: Cover Pages

## How to use this

People whose full records are GM-only, but who live public lives. Their pages show only what anyone could learn. Their secrets stay in the GM file.

## The Blind Seer

*The blind Taur'n prophet of Hollow Refuge, in his windowless tower.*

| People | Calling | Pronouns | From |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Taur'n | Mystic | He/him | Nobody knows |

**Looks.** A huge, stooped Taur'n with rune-marked horns worn smooth, and two white scars where his eyes were. He never seems to bump into anything.

**Manner.** Unhurried and kind, with a habit of answering the question you were about to ask.

**Who they are**

- The prophet of @[Hollow Refuge].
- Lives in the Seer's Tower, which has no windows, because "windows work both ways".
- Put out his own eyes to see the Eternal Eclipse.

**What people say**

- People climb the tower for advice and come down quieter.
- He answers the question you were about to ask.
- He is older than the Refuge.

## Old Vara

*Hollow Refuge's dwarf smith, with an iron hand and a taste for strange metals.*

| People | Calling | Pronouns | From |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dwarf | Artificer | She/her | The Anvil of Dominion, the Tyrant's Dominion |

**Looks.** A broad old dwarf with a white braid down her back and a hand of black iron that she built herself. The joints hiss when she grips a hammer.

**Manner.** Gruff, generous with favours, stingy with coin. Calls every blade "her".

**Who they are**

- Runs Old Vara's Forge in @[Hollow Refuge].
- Repairs anything, and would rather be paid in favours than coin.
- Built her own iron hand.

**What people say**

- Vara knows metals that should not exist, just by the look of them.
- She has a daughter somewhere in the Legion.
- She will pay well for any metal from Blackveil Fortress.

## The founder

*The nameless woman with burned palms who founded Ashvale.*

| People | Calling | Pronouns | From |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Human | Unknown | She/her | Unknown |

**Looks.** Remembered as a small woman with burned palms. Ashvale's children draw her with her hands held up.

**Manner.** In the stories: blunt, tired and kind.

**Who they are**
