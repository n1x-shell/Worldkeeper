# Factions and cults of Aetheris

## How to use this

The five great factions and the eight cults, as people in Aetheris know them: what they want, how they work, who leads them, and what it's like to belong. Their secrets aren't here.

## Celestial Order

*The golden theocracy of the Radiant Ascendancy: divine law, purity, and a sun that never sets.*

**What they want.** A golden theocracy under a sun that never sets. Undead, demons and forbidden magic wiped out, and, their rivals add, dissent wiped out with them.

**What they promise.** Peace through enlightened order.

**How they work.** Radiant knights, inquisitors, seraphs and missionary healers. The healers build hospitals in every dominion; the inquisitors follow them.

**Who leads.** @[Highlord Theos Valcairn], with @[Grand Theurgist Selara Dawnveil] and @[Inquisitor Malrik Vael].

**Where.** The @[The Radiant Ascendancy|REG-002], ruled from @[Empyrean Spire]. Its chief fortress is the Golden Aerie.

**Their sign.** A rising golden sun.

**Who opposes them.** Everyone the Order would purify: the Arcane Consortium, the Shadow Pact and the Wildborn Clans most of all.

**Belonging.** Order knights swear to Solenne and to the Highlord, in that order, at least officially. A member gets protection, purpose and a place in a world that makes sense. They also get watched.

**What people say**

- The Order calls obedience "harmony".
- Its healers will treat anyone. Its knights won't.
- Nobody lives in the Purged Halls any more.

## Crimson Legion

*The war-host of the Tyrant's Dominion: only the strong deserve to rule.*

**What they want.** Endless war. Every settlement a fortress, every dispute a duel, an arena in every city.

**What they promise.** Strength that can survive anything.

**How they work.** Shock troopers, centurions, scouts, gladiators and siege companies. Rank is won in fights, and lost the same way.

**Who leads.** @[Warlord Garron Kael], with @[General Orana Volkash]. The Warlord's heir is @[Darion Kael].

**Where.** The @[The Tyrant's Dominion|REG-003], ruled from @[Varkhaal]. Its chief fortress is the Red Bastion.

**Their sign.** A crimson blade, or a clenched fist.

**Who opposes them.** The Celestial Order, which converts Legion captives, and the Wildborn Clans, across the Bloodfields.

**Belonging.** Anyone can join the Legion. Staying in it means winning. Members swear to Karrath on their weapons, and an oath sworn that way can't be taken back.

**What people say**

- The Legion defines strength as whatever helps the Legion.
- Every Legion town has an arena. Most have two.
- The Warlord's son writes poetry. Nobody says it to the Warlord's face.

## Arcane Consortium

*The mages of the Fractured Nexus: reality is a puzzle, and they mean to solve it.*

**What they want.** Limitless magic. Floating islands, shifting time, cities that move between worlds.

**What they promise.** A world no longer limited by ignorance.

**How they work.** Scholars, constructs, dimensional mercenaries and Eye Beasts. The Consortium funds research everywhere and asks very few questions about what it costs.

**Who leads.** @[Archmagister Veylan Orithis], with @[Archivist Lissara Wynne].

**Where.** The @[The Fractured Nexus|REG-004], ruled from @[Zepharyos], City of the Unbound. Its chief fortress is the Nexus Collegium.

**Their sign.** A violet geometric eye, or a prism.

**Who opposes them.** The Celestial Order, which would erase forbidden magic, and the Wildborn Clans, who want less magic in the world, not more.

**Belonging.** The Consortium takes anyone with a talent or a question. Members are graded, ranked and published. Failures are written up too.

**What people say**

- The Consortium treats catastrophe as a research cost.
- Zepharyos is falling, a little, every year. The Consortium says it's fine.
- If you need something identified, ask a Consortium scholar. If you need it to stay yours, don't.

## Shadow Pact

*The masked rulers of the Eternal Veil: rule unseen, because truth is whatever is believed.*

**What they want.** Perpetual twilight, where identities shift, histories are rewritten and assassins run the cities.

**What they promise.** Freedom from anyone's permanent control.

**How they work.** Silent Blades, illusionists, Shadow Lashers and executioners. Mostly, though, information: the Pact knows something about everyone, and trades it.

**Who leads.** @[Grandmaster Lysander Morrow], with @[Tobias Vale] as spymaster.

**Where.** The @[The Eternal Veil|REG-005], ruled from @[Noctis], the City of Masks. Its chief fortress is Nightglass.

**Their sign.** A broken black circle.

**Who opposes them.** The Celestial Order, whose inquisitors hunt Pact agents in every Radiant city.

**Belonging.** Nobody joins the Pact. The Pact finds you. Members wear masks in Noctis and anything they like elsewhere, and they don't give their real names to each other.

**What people say**

- The Pact fights control by controlling everything, quietly.
- It brokered the enclave bargains between the factions, and kept a copy of each.
- If you think you've never met a Pact agent, you have.

## Wildborn Clans

*The clans of the Verdant Rebirth: civilisation is a disease, and the forest is the cure.*

**What they want.** Cities swallowed by forest. No kings, weaker magic, and the law of the wild.

**What they promise.** A world that can go on living.

**How they work.** Beast hunters, shamans, primal spirits, and living constructs of root and bone. Each clan governs itself; the elders meet at the Den of Elders when it matters.

**Who leads.** @[High Shaman Raethan "Stormcaller"], with @[Elder Myrris Thornveil].

**Where.** The @[The Verdant Rebirth|REG-006], around @[The Rootspire]. Its chief fortress is the Verdant Crown.

**Their sign.** A green crown of roots.

**Who opposes them.** The Celestial Order, which hunts them; the Arcane Consortium, which they think is ruining the world; and the Crimson Legion, across the Bloodfields.

**Belonging.** A clan adopts you, or it doesn't. Members swear to Orrowen and to their clan, and they're expected to give back to the land what they take from it.

**What people say**

- The Wildborn would sacrifice a city to save a tree.
- The Thornward Grove takes in anyone fleeing the Order. Nobody asks how the Order doesn't know.
- There are no roads in the Verdant Rebirth. The Wildborn like it that way.

## The Ashen Covenant

*A fire cult: the world must burn before it can be reborn.*

**What they believe.** The world must burn to be reborn. They seek the First Fire, somewhere in the @[The Ashen Wastes].

**How they work.** Preachers, fire-singers and zealots, and the occasional hired builder who knows how to make fire last.

**Who's known.** @[Hadrek "the Emberborn"], who claims to have bathed in the First Fire; @[Sister Kallisa "the Pyre-Singer"]; @[Aedrin "the Cinderfather"]; @[Vexis "the Infernal Architect"]; and @[Elder Thaelon "the Last Spark"], who remembers the First Fire and doesn't preach destruction.

**Who opposes them.** The Celestial Order, and anyone who'd like their city not to burn.

**What people say**

- The Covenant recruits after every fire. There are a lot of fires.
- Not every Covenant elder wants the world to burn. Some just want to see the First Fire.

## The Black Sun

*An eclipse cult: an endless night is coming, and something older than the gods will come out of it.*

**What they believe.** An eternal eclipse is coming, and something older than the gods will emerge from it.

**How they work.** Prophets, oracles and warrior priests. The Black Sun preaches in the dark, and its prophets speak of the end as if they'd seen it.

**Who's known.** @[Seraine "the Eclipse Blade"], a warrior priestess; @[Vhalek "the Silent Watcher"]; @[Cassiel "the False Dawn"], a fallen Order paladin; and @[Isolde "the Last Ember"], an oracle who doesn't want the end she sees. Its high prophet is said to be called Azhir. Nobody claims to have met him.

**Who opposes them.** The Celestial Order, absolutely: an endless eclipse against a sun that never sets.

**What people say**

- The Black Sun doesn't want to end the world. It thinks it's already ending, and wants a good seat.
- Every eclipse, the cult's numbers double.

## The Drowned Saints

*A sea cult: the sunken city will rise, and the sea will take back what was stolen.*

**What they believe.** The sunken city will rise and the sea will take back what was stolen. The sea's god, @[Maruun, the Sundered Tide], is only sleeping.

**How they work.** Preachers on the docks, and converts drowned to wake her. Its holy ones are those who drowned, and came back.

**Who's known.** @[Father Iskar "the Tidecaller"], a triton preacher who drowned and returned; @[Althisa "the Saltborn"]; @[Drennal "the Barnacled Knight"]; @[Sirala "the Deep Mourner"]; and @[Elder Marrow "the Writeless Prophet"].

**Where.** Along the dusk coast of the Eternal Veil, and on the @[The Black Tides].

**What people say**

- They keep the Sundering on Monsoon 1. Sensible people stay off the docks.
- Some of the drowned do come back. That's the problem.

## The Bloodbound Company

*A mercenary brotherhood: strength is the only truth, and blood the only currency.*

**What they believe.** Strength is the only truth and blood is the only currency. Contracts are sealed in blood, and the Company always collects.

**How they work.** Mercenaries, killers and financiers, for hire to any faction that can pay. The Company recruits in the Legion's arenas and works out of the Hollow Marches.

**Who's known.** @[Varik "the Bloodforged"], a mercenary lord who's never lost a duel; @[Selka "the Pactmother"], who may be the Company's real power; @[Lira "the Red Whisper"]; @[Garek "the Coinbinder"], who decides which wars get funded; and @[Yulrath "the Warhound"].

**What people say**

- The Company has worked for every faction. Usually against each other.
- A Bloodbound contract can't be broken. Ask anyone who tried; you'll have to find them first.

## The Nameless (the Nameless Choir)

*A cult of the erased: its members were taken out of history, and they want to know who did it.*

**What they believe.** Their members were erased from history. Nobody remembers their names, their families or their deeds. They want to learn who took them, and why.

**How they work.** A choir of the forgotten, who sing so they can't be lost entirely. They gather where things were erased.

**Who's known.** Its leader is @[The Cantor], who can't keep a name.

**What people say**

- You can't remember a Nameless face. You remember the song.
- The Nameless Choir wants the Nameless Forge opened.

## The Silent Accord

*A secret society: the Aetherfall was a prison, built by something older than anyone guesses.*

**What they believe.** The Aetherfall that remade the world was a prison, built by something older than anyone guesses.

**How they work.** In small cells, quietly. They copy forbidden records so no single burning can destroy them, and they ask people about their earliest memories.

**Who's known.** Its leader is @[Scribe-Mother Ondine Marrow], an archivist who copies the same forbidden ledger in every hand she can learn.

**What people say**

- The Silent Accord whispers that the Aetherfall was a prison.
- If a stranger asks about your earliest memory, you've met the Accord.

## The Hollow Tribunal

*A secret court: it serves the hidden laws of reality, and those it judges guilty vanish.*

**What they believe.** There are hidden laws under reality, and someone has to enforce them.

**How they work.** Masked judges. A verdict comes before any words, and the guilty simply vanish.

**Who's known.** Its judge is @[Justicar Quell], who wears a mask and is rumoured to be very small.

**What people say**

- Nobody knows where the Tribunal sits. The people it judges find out.
- Whose laws, it never says.

## The Whispering Circle

*A circle of seers: time loops, and they don't predict the future, they remember it.*

**What they believe.** Time loops. Their seers don't predict the future, they remember it.

**How they work.** Seers, and those who listen to them. The Circle gathers at the @[The Rift of Echoes], and shares what it remembers with anyone who asks the right way.

**Who's known.** Its leader is @[Pell the Rememberer], an old gnome who says he remembers three Aetherfalls.

**What people say**

- The Circle said the Cycle would break. It said it before it happened.
- Ask a Circle seer what happens next, and they'll ask you what happened last time.
