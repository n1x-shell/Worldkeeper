# Player Adventure Book: Bestiary

## How to use this

Creatures and hazards of both continents: what you see, what you know, and what people say.

## Radiant Squire

*A young knight of the Celestial Order, keen, armoured and very sure of themselves.*

> Polished plate a size too big, a golden sun on the shield, and a stare that is looking for sin.

**What you know**

- The lowest rank of the Order's radiant knights.
- They patrol the Radiant Ascendancy's roads in pairs.

**What people say**

- Squires report everything to the Inquisition.
- Most of them have never seen a real fight.

## Dawnward Healer

*A missionary healer of the Celestial Order, carrying mercy and the law together.*

> White robes, a satchel of bandages and a sun-disc that glows when they pray.

**What you know**

- The Order's missionary healers, who travel with its knights.
- They heal anyone, then preach to them.

**What people say**

- They will heal you whether you want it or not.
- Their mercy has limits, and the limits are the law.

## Celestial Inquisitor

*A hunter of heretics who brands the guilty with a mark that smothers magic.*

> A grey coat over plate, a burning sigil on one palm, and a quiet, patient voice.

**What you know**

- The Order's heretic-hunters.
- Their mark makes magic stumble.

**What people say**

- When an inquisitor arrives, someone is already guilty.
- The mark fades in a year, if you live that long.

## Avenger Seraph

*A winged sentinel of light the Celestial Order calls down in its holiest wars.*

> Wings of white fire, a face too bright to look at, and a sword that sings.

**What you know**

- Angelic sentinels that fight for the Celestial Order.
- They are rarely seen, and never forgotten.

**What people say**

- The Order can call them. Nobody knows what it costs.
- An Avenger Seraph has never lost a fight it was sent to win.

## Shock Trooper

*The Crimson Legion's front-line soldiers, who spit acid when the line closes.*

> Red armour, a heavy shield, and a mask with a grille over the mouth that hisses.

**What you know**

- The Legion's line infantry.
- Alchemy lets them breathe acid at close range.

**What people say**

- They drink something before battle. Nobody else should.
- The masks hide what the acid has done to their mouths.

## Legion Scout

*The Legion's outriders, quick, cruel and always watching the roads.*

> Light leathers, a short bow and red cloth tied at the throat.

**What you know**

- The Legion's scouts and outriders.

**What people say**

- If you see one scout, there are three more you did not see.

## Centurion

*A Legion officer whose bark can move a whole line at once.*

> A plumed helm, a scarred face and a voice like a slammed door.

**What you know**

- The Legion's field officers.
- They command their soldiers in the middle of a fight.

**What people say**

- Kill the centurion and the line wavers.
- Every centurion won the rank in a duel.

## Bloodfang Ravager

*A huge wolf of the Bloodfang packs, or a person who has become one.*

> A wolf the size of a horse, scarred and red-muzzled, with too-clever eyes.

**What you know**

- Great wolves that run with the Wildborn and the Bloodfang camps.

**What people say**

- Some Bloodfang Ravagers were people once.
- They remember faces.

## Arcane Sentinel

*An empty suit of armour that guards Consortium towers and fires bolts of force.*

> Armour with nothing inside, runes glowing at the joints, a palm that crackles.

**What you know**

- Guardians of the Arcane Consortium's towers.

**What people say**

- They never sleep and never forget a face.
- Someone is always watching through their eyes.

## Phase Construct

*A many-legged Consortium construct that steps in and out of the world.*

> Brass legs, a body of humming glass, and a habit of being somewhere else.

**What you know**

- Consortium constructs that blink between here and elsewhere.

**What people say**

- They were built to hunt escaped experiments.
- They are getting better at it.

## Nexus Researcher

*A junior Consortium scholar, curious, careless and dangerous to stand near.*

> Ink stains, singed eyebrows and a satchel of notes that occasionally hums.

**What you know**

- The Consortium's junior scholars.

**What people say**

- Half of them are one mistake from becoming an anomaly.

## Veil Adept

*A Shadow Pact illusionist who makes you see what the Pact wants seen.*

> Grey robes, a half-mask and a voice that seems to come from beside you.

**What you know**

- The Shadow Pact's illusionists.

**What people say**

- If you can see a Veil Adept, they wanted you to.

## Eye Beast of the Sunken Archive

*A floating, many-eyed horror that guards the deep stacks of the Sunken Archive.*

> A great floating eye ringed by smaller eyes on stalks, drifting between the shelves in total silence.

**What you know**

- A many-eyed guardian of the Sunken Archive's deep stacks.
- The Consortium uses lesser Eye Beasts as guards.

**What people say**

- It reads whatever you are thinking about.
- It has been in the Archive longer than the books.

## Silent Blade Agent

*A Shadow Pact agent who could be anyone in the room.*

> Nobody you would notice. That is the point.

**What you know**

- The Shadow Pact's spies and agents.

**What people say**

- There is one in every inn in the Marches.

## Nightglass Executioner

*The Shadow Pact's killers, sent from Nightglass for traitors.*

> Black glass armour that reflects the dusk, and a blade that drinks light.

**What you know**

- The Shadow Pact's executioners, from its fortress of Nightglass.

**What people say**

- They only come for traitors.
- Nobody has ever seen one coming.

## Shadow Lasher

*A shadow-cat of the Shadow Pact that is never quite where it seems to be.*

> A great black cat with tentacles of shadow, and an outline that slides half a step from where it really is.

**What you know**

- Shadow beasts the Pact keeps and sets on its enemies.

**What people say**

- Aim where it is not.

## Root Hunter

*A Wildborn tracker who reads the forest like a page.*

> Bark-dyed leathers, a bow of living wood and bare feet.

**What you know**

- The Wildborn Clans' trackers and scouts.

**What people say**

- They know you are in the forest before you do.

## Briar Wolf

*A wolf grown through with thorns, that runs with the Wildborn.*

> A lean wolf with briars growing through its fur and blood on its thorns.

**What you know**

- Thorned wolves of the Verdant Rebirth.

**What people say**

- Their bite leaves thorns behind.

## Spirit Bear

*A great bear the Wildborn honour as a spirit of the forest.*

> A white-furred bear with a roar that shakes leaves off the trees.

**What you know**

- Sacred bears of the Verdant Rebirth.

**What people say**

- Kill one and the forest remembers.

## Briar Mother

*A hag of thorns who trades favours for memories.*

> An old woman of twisted briar and green skin, sitting in a thicket, smiling with too many teeth.

**What you know**

- A hag of the deep forest who bargains for memories.

**What people say**

- She will give you anything. Ask her what it cost you afterwards, if you can remember.

## Reality-Warped Wolf

*A wolf twisted by unstable magic, that flickers from place to place.*

> A wolf that is here, then there, then behind you, leaving afterimages.

**What you know**

- Wolves of the Fractured Nexus, warped by the magic there.

**What people say**

- Packs of them follow the storms.

## Shard-Back Boar

*A giant boar with crystal growing from its back where a shard's power touched it.*

> A boar the size of a cart, crystal spikes glinting along its spine.

**What you know**

- Great boars warped by shard energy, found where shards have surfaced.

**What people say**

- Where you find a Shard-Back Boar, a shard is near.

## Void Feeder

*A creature of the Rift that feeds on strength itself.*

> Something like a spider made of absence, stepping out of nowhere.

**What you know**

- Creatures that come up out of the Rift.

**What people say**

- Its bite leaves you weaker, and it does not wear off quickly.

## Rift Stalker

*An unseen hunter from the Rift.*

> Nothing. A footprint. A breath of cold air. Then it hits you.

**What you know**

- Invisible hunters that come out of the Rift.

**What people say**

- Once a Rift Stalker is set on you, it does not stop.

## Mirror Stalker

*A thing from the Echoing Halls that wears your reflection.*

> You. Almost exactly you. It smiles a moment too late.

**What you know**

- Creatures from the Echoing Halls that take the shape of anyone they reflect.

**What people say**

- Not every reflection is yours.

## Nameless Walker

*A shape-taker with no name of its own, that borrows other people's.*

> A plain traveller on the road, who knows your name and wants to walk with you.

**What you know**

- Shape-takers of the Hollow Marches who have no name and take others.

**What people say**

- If a stranger already knows your name, be careful.

## Forgotten Legionnaire

*A dead soldier of the Scar who will not stay down.*

> A skeleton in rusted armour of a kind nobody recognises, still holding its line.

**What you know**

- The restless dead of the Scar and the Cairn of the Forgotten.

**What people say**

- Nobody recognises their banners.
- They still march in formation.

## Forgotten Captain

*A dead officer of the Scar who still commands the fallen.*

> A tall corpse in a crested helm, eyes burning cold, shouting orders in a language nobody speaks.

**What you know**

- Dead officers who lead the restless dead of the Scar.

**What people say**

- They give orders for a war nobody remembers.

## Ghost Artillerist

*The ghost of a gunner on the Scar, still firing at an enemy long gone.*

> A flickering figure beside a broken engine of war, which somehow still fires.

**What you know**

- Ghosts of the Scar's artillery.

**What people say**

- You can hear them reloading at night.

## Ashen Zealot

> Ash-grey robes, burn-scarred hands and a smile that does not flinch.

**What you know**

- Fanatics of the Ashen Covenant.

**What people say**

## Black Sun Prophet

*A preacher of the Black Sun who calls the dark down.*

**What you know**

- Preachers of the Black Sun cult.

**What people say**

## Drowned Saint

*A saint of the drowned, who came back from the sea wrong.*

> A figure in rotted vestments, wet and dripping, with the sea in its eyes.

**What you know**

- The Drowned Saints' holy ones, who drowned and returned.

**What people say**

- If you look at one too long, you start to drown on dry land.

## Myconid

*The Bloom's nearest thing to a people, who speak only by communion.*

> Mushroom-folk, waist-high to taller than a person, glowing faintly, standing very still in a ring.

**What you know**

- The Bloom's own people, as near as it has one.
- They speak only by communion.

**What people say**

- The Bloom-Speakers can talk to them. Barely.
- A ring of myconids is a sign the Bloom is listening.

## Blight

*A walking tangle of twigs and thorns: one of the Bloom's angry moods.*

> A bundle of twigs and thorns shaped like a person, creaking as it comes.

**What you know**

- One of the Bloom's moods, given a body.

**What people say**

- Blights rise where the Bloom has been hurt.

## Shambling Mound

*A heap of rot and roots that swallows what it catches: one of the Bloom's hungry moods.*

> A hill of wet leaves and roots that rises and lurches toward you.

**What you know**

- One of the Bloom's moods, given a body.

**What people say**

- It is not angry. It is just hungry.

## Treant

*A walking tree: the Bloom's slowest, oldest mood.*

> A great tree that is suddenly not a tree, looking down at you.

**What you know**

- One of the Bloom's moods, given a body.

**What people say**

- A treant moves when the Bloom has decided something.

## Walker

*A Kaelith work-construct that walks on its own, or one the Wrights have built new.*

> A tall frame of brass and old wood, stepping carefully on long legs.

**What you know**

- Kaelith built walkers to carry and work.
- Ashvale's Wrights build new ones.

**What people say**

- Old walkers still wander the ruins, doing jobs nobody gave them.

## Half-made

*Minds that sank halfway into flesh or scrap during the breaking, and want to be finished.*

> Figures of flesh and scrap welded together, walking in lockstep and singing a single note.

**What you know**

- Minds that sank halfway into flesh or scrap when Kaelith broke.
- They move in lockstep and sing one note.

**What people say**

- They want to be finished.
- One drifts in the deep fen.

## Hunter

*A braided thing of root and wire, bred to track and capture alive.*

> Shifting limbs of root and wire, and a slow whirlpool where a face should be.

**What you know**

- Root and wire, braided and bred to track.
- They are built to capture, not kill.

**What people say**

- They learn their quarry at every touch.
- Whoever holds the leash pours their will into it.

## War-vessel

*A walking hill of hide and iron, shaped by the minds inside it.*

> A hill of hide and iron with a dais in its back, reshaping itself to fit the ground.

**What you know**

- Great war-constructs of Kaelith.
- They take only the shapes all the minds inside agree on.

**What people say**

- The Collective's Warband rides lesser ones.
- A whole war-vessel has not moved in three hundred years.

## The Scattered

*Restless minds that slipped their bodies and found no other.*

> A cold drift of light that tries to speak and cannot.

**What you know**

- Minds that slipped their bodies and found no other.

**What people say**

- Some say the first of them scattered at the Warding.

## Thinking Rot

*Soft, pear-sweet ground where the Bloom's mind runs close to the surface.*

> The ground goes soft and smells of pears. It gives under your boots, then holds on.

**What you know**

- Ground where the Bloom's mind runs close to the surface.
- A hostile will can teach it to grip.

**What people say**

- It remembers everyone who has walked on it.

## Spore-storm

*A fruiting of the Bloom: a cloud of spores that sometimes rewrites people.*

> The air turns gold and sweet, and the light goes strange. Then the spores come down like snow.

**What you know**

- Called fruitings.
- Most pass harmlessly. Some get into the lungs and rewrite a person from inside.

**What people say**

- The beast-folk's ancestors were rewritten by spore-storms.
- Hold your breath and walk, don't run.

## Bloom-shift

*A visible line where the Bloom changes character, and the dead zones beyond it.*

> A line on the ground where the moss changes colour, sharp as a knife cut. Past it, everything is grey and silent.

**What you know**

- Visible lines where the Bloom's growth changes character.
- In a dead zone, the Bloom closes on anyone it reads as a stranger.

**What people say**

- There is a dead zone in the Eastern Deepwood that even the Spore Circle avoids.

## Circuit-root

*Glassy, glowing roots that carry food, memory and gossip.*

> Roots like glass, lit from within, pulsing slowly along the tunnel walls.

**What you know**

- Roots that carry food, memory and gossip through the under-roads.
- Trackers listen through them.

**What people say**

- Say nothing near a circuit-root you would not want heard.
