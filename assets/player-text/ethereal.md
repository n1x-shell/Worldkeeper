# Player Adventure Book: The Ethereal Realm

## How to use this

The Ethereal Realm as the people who live there and the people who've been there know it: its regions, the people you'll meet, the ways in and out, and its dangers. A group can start here, at the Nameless Asylum in the Hollow Verge. What's really going on behind the realm stays with the GM.

## The Ethereal Realm

> There's no sky, only a pale light that comes from everywhere and nowhere. Things that shouldn't be together are: a staircase from one world climbing into the window of another, a field of grass growing on the underside of a bridge. Everything feels remembered rather than real. Somewhere, very far off, someone is saying your name, and you're almost sure it's yours.

**What you know**

- The Ethereal Realm lies behind the world. Aetheris reaches it through the Hollow Verge. Kaelmar calls it the between, the place under the world where minds touch by harmony instead of nearness. They're the same place.
- It's where lost and erased things go: discarded worlds, forgotten people, names nobody remembers.
- Its border, the Hollow Verge, is where most travellers stay. Past it lies the deep realm, which you don't enter without an invitation from someone who lives there.
- People live here. Some slipped out of the Cycle when an Aetherfall remade their world, and woke here. Some came by root-road from Kaelmar. A few were born here.
- In the deep realm, death is final, and worse than final. See @[Dangers and Death in the Ethereal Realm].

| Who | What they want |
| --- | --- |
| @[Cycle Breakers] | Never to be rewritten again. |
| @[Architects of Eternity] | A world built so it can never be erased. |
| @[Veilborn] | Somewhere safe for people who remember too much. |
| @[Titans of the Rift] | Fate decided by strength, not by hidden hands. |
| @[Echoborn] | To find out what lies beyond the realm. |

**What people say**

- Everything that was ever forgotten is here somewhere. Finding it is the hard part.
- Since the Cycle broke, the realm has been filling up more slowly. Nobody's sure that's good news.
- There's something past the Black Horizon. Nobody who's gone to look has said what.

## The Hollow Verge

> The door was in a wall you've walked past a hundred times. Behind it, a corridor of every corridor you've ever been down, going on and on, and then: a quiet country of grey grass and long shadows, full of people who look up when you arrive and are kind enough not to ask how.

**How deep.** Depth 1 of 7: grey, quiet and almost remembered. See @[The Depths of the Ethereal Realm].

**What you know**

- The Hollow Verge is the border of the Ethereal Realm, the part closest to the world.
- Its entrance moves. For one person it's a door that wasn't there; for another, a stair under a dry well.
- Refugees live here, people who remember too much to be comfortable in the world. The @[The Nameless Asylum] is their home.
- The Verge's last threshold has words carved over it: "Beyond this point, the world will no longer dream you back." Past it is the deep realm.

**What people say**

- The Verge finds you when you need it. It doesn't always let you leave when you want.
- Crossing the last threshold makes you remember every life you've ever lived. All at once. People who do it come back changed, if they come back.
- Root-roads from Kaelmar come out here, if you ride them far enough.

## The Nameless Asylum

> A long, low house of grey stone stands in the grey grass, and it's the only thing in the Verge that looks like it was built on purpose. Lamps burn in every window. A painted sign over the door has been scraped clean and repainted so often that the wood is worn into a hollow. Inside, people talk quietly in a dozen languages, and someone at a long table is reading names aloud from a list. A tall iron figure in a grey cloak looks up from the door, and steps aside to let you in.

**What you know**

- The Nameless Asylum is a refuge in the Hollow Verge for people who remember too much: other lives, other worlds, things that were erased.
- It's the closest thing the Ethereal Realm has to a town, and the best place to start.
- Anyone is welcome. Weapons stay sheathed, and names are spoken carefully.
- The @[Veilborn] keep it. Its keeper is @[Keeper Lantern], a warforged who doesn't remember being made.
- The realm's laws are kept by @[Magistrate Althros], who visits when someone has broken one.

**Places in the Asylum**

**What people say**

- Nobody remembers who built the Asylum. The sign over the door had a name once.
- Every evening the list of names is longer.
- Someone who came in last month swears they've been here before, in another life, as the keeper.

## The Fields of Lost Realities

*Where discarded worlds pile up, one on top of another.*

**How deep.** Depth 2 of 7: wreck-islands of dead worlds in a silver void. See @[The Depths of the Ethereal Realm].

Past the Verge, the deep realm begins with the Fields: the wreckage of every world the Cycle threw away. A golden cathedral lies on its side inside a Legion fortress. Rivers run uphill into forests that were cut down in another history. Travellers here sometimes find monuments to themselves, to deeds they never did.

The @[Architects of Eternity] built a city here, the @[The Crucible of Creation], out of the remains of lost worlds.

**What people say**

- If you find your own statue, don't read the plaque.
- Salvagers from the Asylum go to the edge of the Fields and no further. What's further in is worth more, and costs more.

## The Rifts Edge

*An expanse of broken memories and places that no longer exist, at the edge of the Fields.*

At the edge of the Fields of Lost Realities, the ground breaks up into fragments of memory: a doorway with no house, a stretch of road from nowhere to nowhere, a kitchen where someone's bread is still warm. Some say this is where the Rift's two ends meet.

**What people say**

- Everything here belonged to someone. Some of it still does.
- Don't eat the bread.

## The Crucible of Creation

*A city built out of the remains of lost worlds, by people who mean never to be erased again.*

The Architects of Eternity built the Crucible from the wreckage of the Fields: towers from one world, bridges from another, all fitted together so tightly that no Aetherfall could undo them. They say it's the one place nothing can be rewritten.

**What people say**

- The Architects will take anything you salvage, and use it.
- They're building for what comes after. Nobody's sure what they mean.

## The Ossuary of Kings

*Where rulers who learned the truth of the Cycle fled, and where they can finally die.*

**How deep.** Depth 3 of 7: courts of frightened dead kings. See @[The Depths of the Ethereal Realm].

Kings, queens and warlords who found out what the Cycle was came here to escape it. For centuries they couldn't die. Now they can, and they're afraid. Their courts still sit, in halls of bone.

**What people say**

- A dead king's court will invite you in. Be careful what you agree to.
- An invitation from the Ossuary will get you into the deep realm. Getting out is your own problem.

## The Fractured Throne

*A floating ruin where the Cycle Breakers meet to argue about the future.*

The seat of the first rulers floats in pieces over the Ossuary, held together by nothing anyone can see. The @[Cycle Breakers] meet on its broken steps to debate what the world should be now that the Cycle is over.

**What people say**

- They've been arguing for thirty years. They haven't agreed on anything.
- Some say the Shattering happened here. The Breakers won't say.

## The Colosseum of Echoes

*An arena where the Titans of the Rift fight copies of themselves.*

The @[Titans of the Rift] fight here, against echoes of themselves: copies that know every move they know. A summons to fight at the Colosseum is an invitation into the deep realm, and the Titans send them out often.

**What people say**

- If you fight your echo and win, you're never quite the same.
- A Colosseum summons is the easiest invitation into the deep realm. It's also the most dangerous.

## The Silent Archive

*A library with a book for every erased soul. Most of them are blank.*

**How deep.** Depth 4 of 7: where the truly strange begins. See @[The Depths of the Ethereal Realm].

Somewhere deep in the realm there's a library with a book for everyone who was ever erased. Most of the books are blank. The ones that aren't are read by the books themselves: in the Silent Archive, it's the books that read you.

@[Master Ilythis] keeps records of histories that never happened, and is often found here.

**What people say**

- If your book has writing in it, you were someone once.
- Nobody leaves the Archive without having been read.

## The Loom of Eternity

*Where the threads of every life in Aetheris and Kaelmar are woven, still.*

**How deep.** Depth 5 of 7: cosmic, and crawling with things from outside. See @[The Depths of the Ethereal Realm].

Every story of the Cycle ends at the Loom. It's said to be a black ocean hanging in nothing, crossed by countless threads of light, one for every life in Aetheris and Kaelmar, and pierced by great crystals. Every thread is a fate. Rewriting fate is possible there. A king who tried it, long ago, was unmade.

The Loom is guarded. By what, the few who have come back don't agree.

**What people say**

- The Unraveled King tried to seize the Loom. Nobody remembers his real name, which is the point.
- The Cycle is broken. The Loom is still weaving. Nobody knows whose hands are on it.
- Everyone has a thread. Kaelmar's people too, whatever Kaelmar's scholars say.

## The Citadel of the Hidden

*Where the Hidden Ones are said to keep their citadel, behind reality.*

**How deep.** Depth 6 of 7: corridors where the angles don't add up. See @[The Depths of the Ethereal Realm].

Aetheris's oldest stories say the Hidden Ones, who guided every turn of the Cycle, live in a citadel of corridors behind reality, full of archives of lives and pools of abandoned futures. Whether it's real, nobody in the Asylum will say for sure. Those who might know don't talk about it.

**What people say**

- The Hidden Ones erased anyone who came too close to the truth. Maybe they still do.
- If you find the Citadel, it found you first.

## The Black Horizon

*A starless region at the far edge of the realm, where distance stops making sense.*

**How deep.** Depth 7 of 7: the edge of everything. See @[The Depths of the Ethereal Realm].

At the far edge of the realm the light gives out, and distance stops behaving. A day's walk takes a minute; a step takes a year. Even the realm's oldest residents avoid it. The @[Echoborn] go there anyway, to look.

**What people say**

- There's something beyond it. It might be an exit. It might be the reason nobody should look for one.
- Travellers who come back from the Horizon don't talk about the stars.

## The Veil of Eternity

*An endless plain under stars that don't belong.*

Inside the Black Horizon there's a plain that goes on forever, under stars that aren't anyone's stars. Some say it's the edge of reality itself.

**What people say**

- The stars move when you're not looking. Then they're looking at you.

## The Observatory of the Last Question

*A tower where the Echoborn search for what lies beyond the realm.*

The @[Echoborn] built a tower at the Black Horizon and turned every lens they have outward. They're looking for what lies beyond the Ethereal Realm, and asking one question over and over.

**What people say**

## The Nameless Sepulcher

*An obsidian tomb in the Black Horizon, holding something older than the Cycle.*

An obsidian tomb stands alone in the Black Horizon. Whatever is inside is older than the Cycle, and its seals are breaking. You can hear them crack, if you're close.

**What people say**

- Since the Cycle broke, the cracking's got louder.
- Nobody knows what's inside. Several people would like to keep it that way.

## Dangers and Death in the Ethereal Realm

*What the realm does to the people who walk in it.*

**Old memories.** Meet something from a life you've forgotten, and a fragment of it comes back to you.

**The last threshold.** Crossing the Verge's last threshold brings back every life you've ever lived, all at once, and you can't undo it. The Veilborn call it Awakening. Some people in the realm don't like the Awakened.

**True death.** In the deep realm, death is final. No spell, no relic and no wish brings anyone back.

**Unmaking.** Worse, someone who dies in the deep realm is erased from history. Their statues change. Their families remember someone else. Their friends who crossed the threshold with them keep only a sense of loss.

**At the table.** If a character is Unmade, each surviving companion writes one true sentence about them. It changes nothing in the rules, and everything at the table.

**What people say**

- In the Verge, you can die like anywhere else. Past the threshold, you can die like nowhere else.
- Say your friends' names out loud every day. It helps.

## Thinning and Unmaking

*How the realm, the roots and the glass wear away at who you are.*

Opening a glass door, riding a root-road, communing too deeply with the Bloom or trading with a memory-house can wear away at who you are. Kaelmar calls it Thinning.

**At the table.** Thinning is a track from 0 to 6. When something calls for it, make a Charisma saving throw (DC 12 plus your current Thinning). If you fail, you gain a level, and you strike one named memory from your character's notes for good.

- At 3, you stop answering to your name without effort.
- At 6, you join the harmony, and your character becomes the GM's.
- Hearing your true name from someone who knows you gives Advantage on the save.
- A month among people who use your name takes away one level. Lost memories don't come back.
- Dying in the deep Ethereal Realm sets Thinning to 6 at once. Aetheris calls this Unmaking.
