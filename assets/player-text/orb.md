# The Ethereal Orb

## How to use this

The legend every faction knows about the shards. What's really possible stays with the GM.

## The Ethereal Orb

*The Ethereal Crystal made whole: the oldest story in Aetheris, and the reason every faction wants the shards.*

Every child in Aetheris knows the story. The Ethereal Crystal broke into twelve shards. Gather all twelve, make the Crystal whole, and you hold the Ethereal Orb, and whoever holds the Orb becomes a god and remakes the world in their image. That's what an Aetherfall was. That's how the Cycle worked: some faction always got there in the end.

The Cycle is broken now. Scholars say the shards can't remake the world from inside it any more, and that whoever gathered all twelve would have to carry them beyond the world, into the Ethereal Realm, to make the Orb there. Nobody knows who, or what, would make it, or where.

Every faction is trying anyway.

**What people say**

- Whoever makes the Orb becomes a god. Every Aetherfall had one. Nobody remembers their names.
- The new god's world lasts until the god falls. Then the shards scatter, and it all starts again.
- You'd need all twelve. Nobody even remembers what the twelfth one is.
- Carrying one shard costs you. Carrying twelve would cost you everything, long before you got there.
