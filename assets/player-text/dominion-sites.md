# Player Adventure Book: Sites of the Dominions and Legendary Places

## How to use this

The named sites of the five dominions, and the legendary places of the myths, whose locations nobody agrees on.

## The Golden Aerie

*The Celestial Order's chief fortress, on a crag near Empyrean Spire.*

> The fortress sits on a crag of white rock, its walls gilded so heavily that it hurts to look at in daylight. Griffons wheel above the towers. From the gate you can see Empyrean Spire hanging in the sky beyond, and every knight who passes you salutes it.

**What you know**

- The Celestial Order's chief fortress, near Empyrean Spire.
- Radiant knights train here.
- Part of @[The Radiant Ascendancy].

**What people say**

- The Aerie has never been taken.
- Highlord Valcairn trains his knights here himself.

## The Radiant Expanse

*The golden plains of the Radiant Ascendancy, running north to the Luminara Gate.*

> Gold grass to the horizon in every direction, rippling in the wind. Shrines stand at every crossroads, scrubbed white. Far off, a column of pilgrims walks north toward a light on the edge of the world.

**What you know**

- The golden plains of the Radiant Ascendancy.
- They run north to the Luminara Gate.
- The Thornward Grove, a Wildborn enclave, stands at their edge.
- Part of @[The Radiant Ascendancy].

**What people say**

- Nothing casts a shadow on the Expanse at noon.
- The pilgrim road north is watched by the Order every step of the way.

## The Purged Halls

*A city the Celestial Order erased for rebellion.*

> The streets are clean. That is the first wrong thing. No rubble, no bones, no weeds. The buildings stand empty and scrubbed, every carving chiselled smooth, every name taken off every door. It is very quiet, and the quiet feels like it is listening.

**What you know**

- A city the Celestial Order erased for rebellion.
- Nobody lives there now.
- Part of @[The Radiant Ascendancy].

**What people say**

- Inquisitor Malrik Vael was born here, before it was purged.
- Nobody lives in the Purged Halls, but lamps still get lit there.

## The Red Bastion

*The Crimson Legion's chief fortress, a short march from Varkhaal.*

> Red stone, red banners, red-lacquered gates. The Bastion squats on a rise outside Varkhaal like a fist on a table. Drums beat the hours. Every soldier inside it would rather be fighting, and the walls are covered in the marks of those who have.

**What you know**

- The Crimson Legion's chief fortress, near Varkhaal.
- Part of @[The Tyrant's Dominion].

**What people say**

- The Bastion has a cell for every warlord who ever lost a duel.
- The drums never stop.

## The Bloodfields

*The Tyrant's Dominion's permanent warfront, on the border facing the Hollow Marches.*

> Trenches, stakes and churned mud, stretching along the border as far as you can see. Legion banners on one side; on the other, whoever is pushing this year. The fighting never quite stops here. It just changes who it is with.

**What you know**

- A permanent warfront on the Tyrant's Dominion's border, facing the Hollow Marches.
- Not to be confused with the Bloodmarked Fields, in the Marches.
- Part of @[The Tyrant's Dominion].

**What people say**

- The Legion sends its new recruits to the Bloodfields first.
- Nobody remembers who the Legion was fighting when the Bloodfields began.

## The Ruins of the Old Kings

*The broken halls of the rulers before the Legion, in the iron mountains.*

> Statues of kings with their faces knocked off line a broken road into the mountains. The halls at the end of it are roofless, and slave pits have been dug into their floors. Something older than the Legion is carved into the deepest stone.

**What you know**

- The ruins of the rulers who held the iron mountains before the Crimson Legion.
- Balar the Unchained escaped from a slave pit here.
- Part of @[The Tyrant's Dominion].

**What people say**

- The Legion knocked the faces off the statues so nobody would remember who ruled before.
- The deepest carvings are in a script nobody reads.

## The Anvil of Dominion

*The Crimson Legion's war-forges, deep under the iron mountains.*

> Heat hits you like a wall. The forges go down into the mountain in terraces, glowing orange, ringing with hammers. Thousands work here. Smoke climbs out through cracks in the mountainside far above, and every blade the Legion carries was born in this light.

**What you know**

- The war-forges of the Crimson Legion, under the mountains near Varkhaal.
- Tormek Ironheart built his armour here.
- Part of @[The Tyrant's Dominion].

**What people say**

- The deepest forges are older than the Legion.
- Some of the smiths have never seen daylight.

## The Nexus Collegium

*The Arcane Consortium's chief fortress and greatest school, near Zepharyos.*

> Half fortress, half university, and not entirely in one place at a time. The Collegium floats in tiers above a chasm, joined by bridges of light. Students argue on every landing. Wards hum in the walls, and some of the doors open onto rooms that are somewhere else.

**What you know**

- The Arcane Consortium's chief fortress, near Zepharyos, and its greatest school.
- Part of @[The Fractured Nexus].

**What people say**

- Some of its rooms are in other places entirely.
- Students who fail their final test are never seen again. Or they graduate. Nobody is sure which.

## The Rift of Unmaking

*A howling elemental vortex in the Fractured Nexus.*

> The storm turns in place: a wall of wind, fire, water and stone spinning around a dark centre, howling loud enough to feel in your chest. Rocks are torn off the ground and taken in. Nothing comes out.

**What you know**

- An elemental vortex in the Fractured Nexus.
- Velkana Stormclad was born in a thunderstorm over the Nexus.
- Part of @[The Fractured Nexus].

**What people say**

- The Consortium made it by accident.
- Seraine of the Black Sun came out of it.

## The Echoing Halls

*A labyrinth of mirrors in the Fractured Nexus.*

> Mirrors, in every direction, at every angle, reflecting you back a thousand times. Except some of the reflections are a step behind. And one of them, somewhere ahead, is already turning to look at you.

**What you know**

- A labyrinth of mirrors in the Fractured Nexus.
- Part of @[The Fractured Nexus].

**What people say**

- Lirian Althos walked out of the Echoing Halls one day, and nobody remembers him going in.
- Not every reflection is yours.

## The Repository of the Unwritten

*The Consortium's forbidden vault, where dangerous knowledge is kept.*

> A door of black iron, in a cliff, with no handle and no lock. Wards are cut into every inch of the stone around it. Two Consortium guards stand in front of it, and they are not there to keep people out. They are there to keep something in.

**What you know**

- A forbidden vault in the Fractured Nexus.
- Archivist Lissara Wynne keeps the Consortium's most dangerous texts.
- Part of @[The Fractured Nexus].

**What people say**

- What goes into the Repository of the Unwritten does not come out.
- Some of the books in it are still being written.

## Nightglass

*The Shadow Pact's chief fortress, just outside Noctis.*

> A fortress of black glass that reflects the dusk and nothing else. You can see yourself in its walls, but not what is behind them. Inside, the Pact's magistrates sit in judgement on those who betray it.

**What you know**

- The Shadow Pact's chief fortress, near Noctis.
- Magistrate Renn Varros judges traitors here.
- Part of @[The Eternal Veil].

**What people say**

- Nobody who goes into Nightglass as a prisoner comes out with the same name.
- The glass remembers every face it has reflected.

## The Umbral Paths

*Shifting roads in the Eternal Veil that lead anywhere.*

> The road forks, and forks again, and the signposts are blank. You are sure you have passed that tree before. Then the dusk thickens, and the road opens onto somewhere you have never been, somewhere you could not possibly have walked to.

**What you know**

- Shifting roads in the Eternal Veil.
- They lead anywhere.
- Part of @[The Eternal Veil].

**What people say**

- The Umbral Paths will take you anywhere, but not always where you asked.
- The Pact's couriers use them, and never get lost.

## The Veilborn Archive

*A hall of stone masks in the Eternal Veil.*

> The hall is lined with faces: stone masks, thousands of them, set into the walls from floor to ceiling, each one different. Their eyes are empty. Walk slowly, and you will hear them whispering.

**What you know**

- A hall of stone masks in the Eternal Veil.
- Archivist Lissara Wynne learned her trade here.
- Part of @[The Eternal Veil].

**What people say**

- Every mask in the Veilborn Archive belonged to somebody.
- Some of the masks are new.

## The Verdant Crown

*The Wildborn Clans' chief fortress, a short climb from the Rootspire.*

> It is not built. It is grown: a fortress of living trees woven together, their trunks thick as towers, their branches knotted into walls. Wolves pad along the ramparts. Somewhere inside, drums made of hide beat slow.

**What you know**

- The Wildborn Clans' chief fortress, near the Rootspire.
- Living constructs of root and bone guard it.
- Part of @[The Verdant Rebirth].

**What people say**

- The Crown has walked a mile north in the last hundred years.
- No axe has ever cut its walls.

## Fangholt

*The skeleton of a behemoth in the Verdant Rebirth.*

> The ribs rise out of the forest like the beams of a cathedral, each one taller than a tower. Moss covers the bones. Vines hang from the spine. A wolf pack has denned in the skull, and they watch you come.

**What you know**

- The skeleton of a behemoth in the Verdant Rebirth.
- Brynn Wolfsong comes from here.
- Part of @[The Verdant Rebirth].

**What people say**

- Nobody knows what killed the behemoth at Fangholt.
- There are more bones under the forest floor.

## The Ravaged Pass

*A haunted canyon in the Verdant Rebirth.*

> The canyon walls close in, scored with old claw marks and old sword cuts. Bones lie in the scree. The wind sounds like voices here, and after dark it sounds like marching.

**What you know**

- A haunted canyon in the Verdant Rebirth.
- Yvara Ashborn was born here, under an eclipse.
- Part of @[The Verdant Rebirth].

**What people say**

- Travellers who camp in the Ravaged Pass hear an army marching all night.
- The army never arrives.

## The Titan's Fall

*A battlefield of gods, littered with their skeletons and weapons.*

> The ground is bone. Ribs like hills, a skull like a fallen palace, a hand the size of a village closed around the haft of a broken spear. The gods fought here, and they did not get up.

**What you know**

- A battlefield of the gods, littered with their skeletons and weapons.
- Maruun, the Sundered Tide, fell here.
- Nobody agrees on where it is.

**What people say**

- The gods' weapons are still there, if you can lift them.
- The sea has never forgiven the Titan's Fall.

## The Abyssal Hollow

*A drowned city, intact beneath the sea, its people missing.*

> The city lies on the sea floor, perfect, its streets swept clean by the current, its doors standing open. Lamps hang in the windows, unlit. There is nobody here. Something below the city is breathing.

**What you know**

- A drowned city, intact beneath the sea.
- Its people are missing.

**What people say**

- Something waits below it.
- The Drowned Saints say it is the city that will rise.

## The Crucible of Renewal

*An eternal forge under the volcanic heart of Aetheris that reforges anything, for a price.*

> The heat is a living thing. The forge burns at the bottom of a shaft that goes down into the heart of the world, and its fire is white. Weapons, crowns, bones and stranger things lie in the coals, coming apart and coming back together.

**What you know**

- An eternal forge under the volcanic heart of Aetheris.
- It reforges anything, for a price.

**What people say**

- Its deepest fires open into a realm of flame.
- Whatever you put in comes out changed, and so do you.

## The Nameless Forge

*Where the Nameless Smith made the gods' weapons.*

> An anvil the size of a house, cold now, standing alone in a field of slag. Hammers lie around it, too heavy to lift. Someone has scratched a name into the anvil, and someone else has scratched it out.

**What you know**

- Where the Nameless Smith made the gods' weapons.
- The Smith's name was the first thing ever erased.
- Nobody agrees on where it is.

**What people say**

- The anvil still rings if you strike it, and something hears.

## The Shattered Throne

*The throne Aurex, the Throne-Breaker, tried to sit, and failed.*

> The throne stands on a dais in a ruin with no walls, split clean down the middle. The two halves lean apart. The air around it is heavy, and every instinct you have says: do not sit down.

**What you know**

- The throne Aurex, the Throne-Breaker, tried to sit, and failed.
- Nobody agrees on where it is.

**What people say**

- Whoever sits the Throne will rule everything, or be destroyed by it.
- Aurex is still there, in a way.

## The Vault of the Nameless

*A sealed tomb under an ancient city. Something scratches at the door.*

> The stairs go down under the city, and stop at a door of stone sealed with iron bands and older wards. The door is cold. From the other side, slowly, patiently, something is scratching.

**What you know**

- A sealed tomb under an ancient city.

**What people say**

- Something scratches at the door.
- The Nameless Choir wants it opened.
