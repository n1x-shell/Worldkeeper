# Player Adventure Book: The Nine Villages

## How to use this

The nine villages in the hills west and south of Ashvale: its home villages, rivals and trade partners. Each has a short read-aloud, what you know, a few places, who you'll meet, the way to Ashvale, and rumours.

## Coldwater

*A cold, high sheep village where the springs never freeze and the wool is the best in the hills.*

> The track climbs until the trees give up. Stone walls run over the hills in every direction, and sheep crop the grass between them. The village is a huddle of grey houses around a spring that steams in the cold. Somebody's dog has already seen you, and somebody's reeve is already walking down to meet you.

**What you know**

- A shepherds' village high in the hills west of Ashvale.
- Its wool is the best in the hills, and it trades it down to Ashvale and Haskett's Mill.
- Its spring never freezes, even in Evernight.
- @[Reeve Hanna Colt] runs it.

**Places in the village**

| Place | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The Warm Spring | The steaming spring at the heart of the village, where everyone draws water and trades news. |
| The Shearing Barn | The biggest building in Coldwater, used for everything from shearing to weddings. |
| The Fold Stones | A ring of old sheep-folds on the hill above the village, older than anyone remembers. |

**Getting to Ashvale.** About a day's walk east, down out of the hills.

**What people say**

- One of Coldwater's children has been having strange dreams, and the reeve is frightened.
- The spring was warm long before anyone built the village.
- Coldwater sheep will not graze inside the Fold Stones.

## Haskett's Mill

*A busy mill village on the river, grinding grain for half the hills.*

> You hear the wheel before you see the village: a slow, steady creak and splash. The mill stands where the river drops over a shelf of rock, white with flour dust, and the village has grown up around it. Carts queue at the mill door. Children chase each other through the drifting flour.

**What you know**

- A mill village on the river south-west of Ashvale. Its mill grinds for half the villages.
- Named for the Haskett family, halflings who built the mill and still run it.
- @[Miller Tobin Haskett] is headman.

**Places in the village**

| Place | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The Great Wheel | The mill itself, the biggest machine in the hills. |
| The Flour Market | Where grain comes in and flour goes out, every market day. |
| The Weir House | A tavern on the mill pond. Everyone in the hills ends up there eventually. |

**Getting to Ashvale.** Half a day by cart, along the river road.

**What people say**

- Tobin's niece ran off to join the Convergence Collective, and he wants her home.
- The wheel turned all by itself one night last winter.
- The Hasketts are richer than they let on.

## Greyfold

*A quiet village of stone and records, where more children are born with light under their skin than anywhere else.*

> Grey stone houses, grey stone walls, a grey stone shrine with a sun painted on its door in faded yellow. Greyfold is the quietest place in the hills. An old dwarf in spectacles is sitting on the shrine steps with a ledger on his knees, writing something down as you arrive.

**What you know**

- A small stone village in the southern hills.
- Its shrine to Solenne keeps the oldest records of births and deaths in the hills.
- More children are born here with light under their skin than anywhere else.
- @[Elder Mott Greyfold] keeps the shrine and the records.

**Places in the village**

| Place | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The Sun Shrine | Solenne's shrine, with the village records in its crypt. |
| The Record Room | Shelves of births, marriages and deaths, older than Ashvale's. |
| The Lantern Field | A meadow where children with light under their skin glow faintly at dusk. |

**Getting to Ashvale.** A day's walk north-east.

**What people say**

- Greyfold's records go back further than they should.
- Sister Abra of Kaelholm was born here, glowing.
- The between touched Greyfold's grandparents. Nobody agrees how.

## Low Fenn

*A fen village of stilts, salt and flat-bottomed boats, at the edge of the southern marshes.*

> The road turns into a causeway, then into a boardwalk, then into water. Low Fenn stands on stilts above the reeds, house joined to house by plank bridges. Salt is stacked in white pyramids under the eaves. A tortle poles past on a barge, raises a hand, and goes back to arguing prices with the lizardfolk on the next platform.

**What you know**

- A fen village of stilt houses on the edge of the southern marshes, south of Ashvale.
- The salt trade runs through it, up from the coast and on to the hills.
- Tortles, lizardfolk and halflings live here together.
- @[Headwoman Sel Marrow] leads it. @[Odran Reeve] trades salt from here.

**Places in the village**

| Place | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The Salt Stacks | White pyramids of salt under the eaves, waiting for the barges. |
| The Long Platform | The village's market and meeting place, built over open water. |
| The Drowned Stair | Old stone steps that go down into the fen and disappear. Nobody uses them. |

**Getting to Ashvale.** A day and a half: a day by barge, then half a day by road.

**What people say**

- Something half-made drifts in the deep fen.
- The Drowned Stair goes somewhere.
- Odran Reeve knows every safe ford in the south.

## Oakhallow

*A woodland village under ancient oaks, where outsiders are watched closely.*

> The road goes into the trees and the light goes green. Oakhallow is built among the roots of oaks so old their trunks are as wide as houses. Some of the houses are inside the trunks. Somebody is watching you from the branches, and they are not hiding it.

**What you know**

- A woodland village under ancient oaks, in the hills west of Ashvale.
- Its people are foresters, hunters and charcoal-burners.
- It does not trust the Spore Circle's scouts.
- @[Warden Brin Oakhallow] keeps the woods and the village.

**Places in the village**

| Place | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The Hollow Oak | The biggest tree, hollow inside, where the village meets. |
| The Charcoal Clearing | Smoking kilns at the edge of the wood. |
| The Hermit's Path | The trail into the deep wood, where Oda Fernlow lived for forty years. |

**Getting to Ashvale.** A day's walk east, through the woods.

**What people say**

- The oaks are older than Kaelith.
- Brin can track a deer through the Bloom country.
- Something in the deep wood leaves bark-coloured footprints that sprout.

## Sallow Cross

*A crossroads market village where every road in the hills meets, and every traveller pays.*

> Four roads meet at a muddy square full of stalls, carts, animals and shouting. Sallow Cross is all market. Willows droop over the ditches. A tall woman with a ring of keys watches everything from the steps of the biggest house, and the stallholders keep glancing her way.

**What you know**

- A crossroads village south of Ashvale where the hill roads meet.
- Its market is the biggest in the hills outside Ashvale itself.
- @[Goodwife Marta Sallow] runs the village and the market.

**Places in the village**

| Place | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The Cross Market | The square where four roads meet, busy every market day. |
| The Toll Willow | A great willow where every traveller pays the crossing toll. |
| The Sallow Barns | The goodwife's barns. One of them is locked. |

**Getting to Ashvale.** Half a day north, on the best road in the hills.

**What people say**

- Marta Sallow has locked one of her barns, and will not say why.
- A cow went missing from that barn, and nobody saw it leave.
- Nothing crosses Sallow Cross without paying her.

## Kettleby

*A brewing village built half above ground and half below, famous for its beer.*

> The village smells of hops. Kettleby's houses sit low in the hillside, half dug into it, with round doors and chimneys poking out of the grass. Barrels roll down ramps into cellars that go deeper than cellars should. A small red kobold in a leather cap is already pouring you a cup.

**What you know**

- A brewing village dug into the hillside, west of Ashvale.
- Its beer is famous in the hills.
- Its cellars connect to old tunnels, and some of its people came up from them.
- @[Reeve Odo Kettleby], a kobold, is reeve.

**Places in the village**

| Place | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The Brewhouse | Kettleby's great brewhouse, the pride of the village. |
| The Deep Cellars | Cellars that go down further than anyone admits. |
| The Tasting Green | The village green, where every barrel is tried before it is sold. |

**Getting to Ashvale.** A day's walk east.

**What people say**

- Kettleby's deepest cellar opens into the old tunnels.
- Odo still has friends in the tunnels, and trades with them.
- Kettleby has never won the hills' beer contest, and it bothers them terribly.

## Brannock

*A tough hill village of goliaths and humans, grieving its children lost to the east.*

> Brannock sits on a bare ridge where the wind never stops. Its houses are low and thick-walled, roofed with turf. Axes hang beside every door. A huge goliath woman is splitting firewood in the square, and every stroke sounds angry.

**What you know**

- A hill village on a high ridge in the west, where goliaths and humans have lived together for generations.
- Its young people go east to the mountains for work, and some come back changed.
- @[Thane Gerda Brannock] leads it.

**Places in the village**

| Place | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The Ridge Hall | The thane's hall, where the village eats together in winter. |
| The Wind Stones | Standing stones on the ridge, where the young swear oaths before leaving. |
| The East Road Gate | Where those who go east say goodbye. Names are carved on the posts. |

**Getting to Ashvale.** A day and a half east, over the ridges.

**What people say**

- The thane's nephew went east to the Ascendancy and came back with an iron jaw.
- Gerda will not let another Brannock child go east.
- Ascendancy recruiters have been seen on the east road.

## Thornmere

*A lake village of boats, music and full-moon dances.*

> The hills open out and there is the lake, flat and silver. Thornmere is a ring of houses around its shore, their boats pulled up on the shingle. Lanterns hang from every eave, and somebody is playing reed pipes. Thornmere always seems to be getting ready for a party.

**What you know**

- A lake village in the southern hills, on the shore of Thornmere lake.
- Its people fish, farm and dance. It holds a dance every full moon, and everyone is welcome.
- @[Mother Wren Thornmere], a satyr, is headwoman.

**Places in the village**

| Place | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The Moon Green | The lakeside green where the full-moon dances are held. |
| The Boat Hall | Where the boats are mended, and the village gossips. |
| The Still Water | The far end of the lake, where nobody fishes. |

**Getting to Ashvale.** A day's walk north.

**What people say**

- Nobody fishes the Still Water. Nobody says why.
- Wren can talk anyone into anything.
- The lake is deeper than it should be.
