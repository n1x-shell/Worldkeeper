# Player Adventure Book: More People

## How to use this

New people of Kaelholm, the eastern mountains and the Ethereal Realm's factions, in the same layout as the other people pages. Secrets stay with the GM.

## Old Corwin Ambry

*The Lampblack ink-seller whose ink, they say, can't be scraped.*

| People | Class | Known as | Pronouns | From |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Halfling | Artificer | Wright | He/him | Kaelholm |

**Looks.** A tiny, ink-stained halfling with a white beard tucked into his apron and blue fingertips that never wash clean.

**Manner.** Gossipy, kind and much sharper than he lets on.

**Who they are**

- Sells ink, quills and glyph-pigments in Lampblack, Kaelholm's quarter of scribes.
- Has sold ink to every Archivist-Elder for fifty years.

**What people say**

- Corwin's ink doesn't fade. Some say it doesn't scrape, either.
- Never ask Corwin what he writes at night.
