# Player Adventure Book: The Depths of the Ethereal Realm

## How to use this

How the Ethereal Realm changes the deeper you go, and the creatures you'll meet at each depth.

## The Depths of the Ethereal Realm

*The deeper you go, the less the realm remembers being a world.*

The Ethereal Realm lies outside the world. While the Cycle ran, the world was held shut; since it broke, the way in is open, for anyone who finds a door. The Hollow Verge, nearest the world, is the world misremembered: quiet, grey, familiar and wrong. Every region past it is further from anything people were made for. By the Black Horizon, nothing is.

| Depth | Region | What it's like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | @[The Hollow Verge] | Grey grass and long shadows. Things you almost remember. Sad more than frightening. |
| 2 | @[The Fields of Lost Realities] | Wreck-islands of dead worlds drifting in a silver void, crossed by salvagers' driftships, currents and great swimming beasts. |
| 3 | @[The Ossuary of Kings] | Courts of dead kings, all ceremony and bone, and very afraid. |
| 4 | @[The Silent Archive] | A hush, and the feeling of being read. Things here aren't quite like anything in the world. |
| 5 | @[The Loom of Eternity] | Threads of light across a black sea standing on end, and something crawling along them that came from somewhere else. |
| 6 | @[The Citadel of the Hidden] | Corridors behind reality, where the angles don't add up. |
| 7 | @[The Black Horizon] | The edge of everything. Wrong stars, wrong distances, and nothing made for people. |

**Driftships.** In the Fields, salvagers sail ships patched together from the wreckage of several worlds, riding the currents between the islands. Up and down are a matter of opinion.

**Going deeper.** Past the Verge, you need an invitation to enter. Once you're in, the realm doesn't care how you got there.

**What people say**

- The deeper you go, the less you'll be able to describe it when you get back.
- Since the Cycle broke, something from outside has been creeping in from the Black Horizon. It's reached the Loom. Some say the Archive.
- Past the Archive, keep your eyes down and your friends' names in your mouth.
- There are powers out there older than the gods, with their own wars. Mostly they don't notice people. Mostly.
- Whoever holds the deep realm could start the Cycle again, or make a new world, or end this one.

## Greyling

*The faded ghost of someone the world forgot, still looking for anyone who remembers.*

> A grey figure at the edge of sight, its face smudged like a thumbprint on a window. It turns when you say any name at all.

**Where.** The Hollow Verge.

**What you know**

- The forgotten dead of erased worlds, drifting through the Verge.
- Most are harmless. Some are hungry for names.

**What people say**

- Say a greyling's name and it goes quiet. Nobody knows their names.
- The Asylum reads its list every evening to keep them away.

## Name-Eater

*A slick of grey ooze that feeds on memories, and wears the faces of the people it has eaten.*

> A puddle that isn't water, sliding toward you across the grey grass. When it rises, it has your sister's face, and it calls you by name.

**Where.** The Hollow Verge.

**What you know**

- Memory-eating oozes of the Verge.
- They mimic the people whose memories they've eaten.

**What people say**

- If someone you lost calls your name in the Verge, don't answer.
- Kill one and some of what it ate comes back. Not always to the right person.

## Lamp-moth Swarm

*A cloud of pale moths that eats light, and then whatever was standing in it.*

> Grey moths, thousands of them, settling on every lamp until the flame gutters out under their bodies. Then they turn toward the warmest thing left.

**Where.** The Hollow Verge.

**What you know**

- Moths of the Verge that swarm to light and smother it.

**What people say**

- Cover your lamp when you hear the wings.
- The Asylum's lamps have glass so thick you could stand on it. That's why.

## Threshold Hound

*A black hound that guards the Verge's paths, and decides who goes deeper.*

> A dog-shaped hole in the light, padding beside you just out of reach. It stops at a doorway and looks at you, and waits.

**Where.** The Hollow Verge.

**What you know**

- Hounds that walk the Verge's paths, especially near the last threshold.
- They don't attack people who turn back.

**What people say**

- A threshold hound only bites the ones who keep going.
- Tessk the Wayfinder feeds them. She won't say what.

## Echo

*A copy of someone who passed through the Verge, left behind and trying to finish their life.*

> A stranger who looks exactly like someone you know, and who knows things only they would know, except the last few years.

**Where.** The Hollow Verge.

**What you know**

- Copies left behind in the Verge by people who passed through.
- They believe they're the real one.

**What people say**

- If you meet yourself in the Verge, be kind. It's having a worse day than you are.
- Some echoes try to get back to the world. Some make it.

## Driftwhale

*A vast, gentle beast that swims the silver between the wreck-islands of the Fields.*

> Something the size of a ship turns slowly over the wreckage, its belly lit like a lantern, and sings a long low note that you feel in your teeth.

**Where.** The Fields of Lost Realities.

**What you know**

- Great beasts that swim between the islands of the Fields.
- Salvagers follow them, because they always find the way out.

**What people say**

- Driftwhales sing the way home.
- Some of the Fields' salvagers live on their backs.

## Wreck-eel

*A long-jawed eel that hunts through the drifting wreckage of the Fields.*

> A silver eel as long as a wagon, eyeless, sliding through a broken cathedral window with its jaw hanging open.

**Where.** The Fields of Lost Realities.

**What you know**

- Predators of the Fields' wreckage.
- They hunt by sound.

**What people say**

- Never shout in the Fields.
- Wreck-eel teeth sell for good money at the Asylum.

## Spindleclaw

*Spider-bodied slavers that sail the Fields in ships made of other people's worlds.*

> An eel-headed thing on eight jointed legs, wearing a breastplate from one world and a crown from another, with a line of chained captives behind it.

**Where.** The Fields of Lost Realities.

**What you know**

- Slavers and salvagers who sail the Fields in wreck-built ships.
- They take people from every world that comes through.

**What people say**

- Spindleclaws keep slaves the way other people keep coins.
- Their ships are faster than anything else in the Fields. They're stolen.

## Monument

*A statue of you, celebrating something you never did, that would very much like you to stay.*

> A statue twenty feet high, unmistakably you, raising a sword over a plaque that names a battle you've never heard of. Its head turns to follow you.

**Where.** The Fields of Lost Realities.

**What you know**

- Statues in the Fields that honour travellers for deeds they never did.
- They don't like being doubted.

**What people say**

- Don't read the plaque.
- People who read the plaque start to remember the battle.

## Ruin Mimic

*A piece of wreckage from a lost world, which turns out to be hungry.*

> A door with no house, standing on its own. A chest of coins from a mint that never existed. A ladder up to nothing.

**Where.** The Fields of Lost Realities.

**What you know**

- Ordinary-looking salvage that isn't.

**What people say**

- If it's too good to leave, leave it.
- Brindle Quickfoot checks every piece twice. He has scars from the time he didn't.

## Silver Drift

*A current in the silver void between the Fields' islands, which carries off anything it catches.*

> A shimmer in the empty air between the islands, like heat over a road. Loose wreckage drifts into it and is gone.

**Where.** The Fields of Lost Realities.

**What you know**

- Currents in the void between the islands.
- Salvagers use them to travel fast. Carefully.

**What people say**

- Tie yourself to something.
- Every current goes somewhere. Not always somewhere you want.

## Bone Herald

*A dead court's messenger, all claws and courtesy, sent to fetch you.*

> A tall skeletal thing in rotten court livery, with claws like scythes, bowing very low. It has an invitation for you.

**Where.** The Ossuary of Kings.

**What you know**

- The dead courts of the Ossuary send them with invitations.
- Refusing politely usually works.

**What people say**

- A Bone Herald's invitation gets you into the deep realm. It doesn't say you'll leave.
- Refuse it rudely and it stays.

## Crowned Dead

*A dead monarch of an erased kingdom, still holding court, and terrified of finally dying.*

> A skeleton in three crowns, one on top of the other, on a throne of other thrones. Its courtiers are bones. Its voice shakes.

**Where.** The Ossuary of Kings.

**What you know**

- Rulers who fled the Cycle to the Ossuary, and can now truly die.
- They are afraid, and fear makes them cruel.

**What people say**

- They'll offer you anything to protect them. They have nothing left to give.
- Every crown is from a different world.

## Oathless Blade

*The ghost of a soldier still fighting for a kingdom that never happened.*

> Ghostly soldiers in mismatched armour from a dozen worlds, drilling in a courtyard of bone, saluting a banner nobody can read.

**Where.** The Ossuary of Kings.

**What you know**

- The dead armies of the Ossuary's courts.
- They fight for whichever king they last swore to.

**What people say**

- They'll swear to you, if their king is gone. Be careful what you accept.
- Veyra the Unchained hates them.

## Reader

*A hunched, one-eyed thing of the Archive that reads your secrets off your face.*

> A thin, grey, crouching shape with one huge wet eye, turning pages it isn't holding. It looks up, and says aloud the thing you've never told anyone.

**Where.** The Silent Archive.

**What you know**

- Creatures of the Silent Archive that read minds the way others read books.
- Some were scholars once.

**What people say**

- A Reader will trade one of your secrets for one of someone else's.
- Master Ilythis lets them be. Nobody else does.

## Hollow Scholar

*A vulture-headed ancient who traded everything for knowledge, and still wants more.*

> A hunched figure in a scholar's robe, with a bald vulture's head and fingers that have turned every page in the Archive twice.

**Where.** The Silent Archive.

**What you know**

- Ancient scholars of the Archive, cursed and immortal.
- They know nearly everything, and want the rest.

**What people say**

- They'll help you find any book, for a price you can't afford.
- There are thirteen of them. Nobody knows why thirteen.

## Hungry Lens

*A floating many-eyed thing that drinks magic out of the Archive's books, and out of you.*

> A floating eye in a ring of smaller eyes on stalks, drifting between the stacks, and wherever it looks the glow of every enchanted page goes dim.

**Where.** The Silent Archive.

**What you know**

- Eye-creatures that feed on magic in the Archive.
- They're lesser cousins of the Eye Beasts of the Sunken Archive.

**What people say**

- Put your magic items away when you hear the hum.
- Somebody brought one to Zepharyos once. Zepharyos still talks about it.

## Gibbering Margin

*A mass of mouths and eyes made of every unfinished sentence in the Archive.*

> A heaving mound of ink and flesh, covered in mouths, every one of them reciting a different book at once, getting louder as you get closer.

**Where.** The Silent Archive.

**What you know**

- A thing grown from the Archive's unwritten words.
- Listening too long scrambles your thoughts.

**What people say**

- Plug your ears and walk away.
- It's saying something true, somewhere in all that. People have gone mad trying to find it.

## Thread-grue

*A small, grinning, fish-faced thing that tangles the threads of fate, and the minds of anyone nearby.*

> A hunched little creature with a fish's face and a mouth full of needle teeth, pulling at a glowing thread and giggling. Your thoughts go wrong when it's close.

**Where.** The Loom of Eternity.

**What you know**

- Small, malicious things that crawl along the Loom's threads.
- Being near one makes it hard to think straight.

**What people say**

- Where the grues are, something bigger has been.
- They tangle threads for fun. Somewhere, a life goes wrong.

## Weft-mangler

*A many-armed horror that comes out of nowhere and cuts.*

> Nothing, and then six clawed arms and a body like a peeled shadow, already moving, already cutting.

**Where.** The Loom of Eternity.

**What you know**

- Ambush predators of the Loom, all arms and claws.
- They come out of shadow you didn't notice.

**What people say**

- If you see one, it's already too late. If you see two, run.

## Fate-seer

*A robed, bulbous-headed prophet that reads the threads and bends them toward something beyond the world.*

> A tall figure in a robe of tangled thread, with a huge, soft, swollen head and eyes that are looking at something behind you, very far away.

**Where.** The Loom of Eternity.

**What you know**

- Prophets of the star-things, found where the Loom's threads are thickest.
- They speak of a coming that will unmake everything.

**What people say**

- A fate-seer's prophecy always comes true. Usually not the way you think.
- The Black Sun cult would love to meet one.

## Thread Ghost

*A life's thread, cut loose, glowing and wandering and looking for the person it belonged to.*

> A figure of pale starlight, flickering, with the face of someone who's just about to remember who they are.

**Where.** The Loom of Eternity.

**What you know**

- Cut threads of the Loom that wander as light.
- Their touch shows you the life they were part of.

**What people say**

- If a thread ghost finds the person it belonged to, both of them change.
- There are more of them since the Cycle broke.

## Horizon Hulk

*A towering, stitched-together mass of muscle and mouths that walks the Black Horizon's edge.*

> Something twice the height of a person, built like someone made a giant from memory and got it slightly wrong, with a ring of eyes around a mouth where its chest should be.

**Where.** The Black Horizon.

**What you know**

- Brutes of the Black Horizon.
- They guard the star-things' prophets.

**What people say**

- The Echoborn have lost more people to the hulks than to anything else.
- You can hear them before you see them. You won't like the sound.

## Worm-speaker

*Worm-headed folk who came in from beyond the Horizon and want to take others back with them.*

> People-shaped figures in grey robes, whose heads are worms, a ring of tiny teeth where a face should be. They speak very politely.

**Where.** The Black Horizon.

**What you know**

- Folk from beyond the Black Horizon, who live at its edge.
- They want to bring others back with them.

**What people say**

- They'll offer you a way out of the realm. It isn't one.
- The Echoborn trade with them. Elyndria says that's fine.

## Dread-feyr

*A nightmare given a body: a mass of fear that wears the shape of what you dread most.*

> At first, a heap of something dark and wet. Then it stands up, and it is exactly the thing you were afraid of as a child.

**Where.** The Black Horizon.

**What you know**

- Things made of fear, at the edge of the Black Horizon.
- They grow from travellers' nightmares.

**What people say**

- Don't sleep near the Horizon.
- Kill one and you'll never have that nightmare again. You'll have a new one.

## Falling Star

*A burning thing that falls endlessly through the Black Horizon, and sometimes chooses where it lands.*

> A streak of white fire across a starless sky. Then it turns, and you realise it's been watching you the whole way down.

**Where.** The Black Horizon.

**What you know**

- Comets that fall forever through the Black Horizon.
- They hunt.

**What people say**

- The Echoborn time their walks between the falls.
- A falling star landed in the Fields once. There's still a crater, and it's still warm.

## The Horizon Leviathan

*A thing the size of a mountain that swims beyond the Black Horizon, and swallows whatever drifts too far.*

> Nothing, for a long time. Then the dark moves, and you understand you have been looking at its side.

**Where.** The Black Horizon.

**What you know**

- Something vast that swims at the far edge of the realm.
- It has never been seen whole.

**What people say**

- It swallowed the Echoborn's first observatory, tower and all.
- Some say it's a Watcher. The Echoborn say it's much smaller than a Watcher.

## Starlight Madness

*The stars past the Black Horizon aren't anyone's stars, and looking at them does something to you.*

> Stars that are too close, in colours with no names, moving when you aren't looking, and then very still when you are.

**Where.** The Black Horizon.

**What you know**

- Travellers under the Horizon's stars come back strange.

**What people say**

- Keep your eyes down.
- The Echoborn say the madness is the first step to seeing clearly.

## Wrong Distance

*Past the edge of the realm, distance and time stop agreeing.*

> The tower ahead gets further away as you walk toward it. Then you turn around, and it's behind you.

**Where.** The Black Horizon.

**What you know**

- At the Black Horizon, a step can take a year and a day's walk a minute.

**What people say**

- Bring a rope, and tie everyone to it.
- Sylas Vaerin says he's mapped it. His map changes every time you look.
