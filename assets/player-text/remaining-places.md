# Player Adventure Book: The Other Places

## How to use this

Same layout as the five places: **Read aloud**, **What you know** (common knowledge) and **What people say** (rumours, some true, some not). Secrets are left out, including every vault's name, place and purpose.

The read-aloud text and rumours are new writing built on the archive.

## Aetheris

> The land looks broken on purpose. Roads end in rubble. Towers lean over valleys with no towns left in them. Ruins of five different kinds of kingdom stand on the same hillside, none of them finished, all of them fought over. Wherever you go, somebody's banner flies over somebody else's ruins, and everyone you meet carries a weapon and a grudge.

**What you know**

- Aetheris is a shattered continent of ruins, scavengers, warbands, zealots and conspiracies.
- For as long as anyone can count, the world was remade over and over. A faction would gather the shards of the Ethereal Crystal and rewrite everything. People called it the Cycle of Aetherfalls.
- The Cycle ended about thirty years ago. This is Year 31 Unbound. Nothing resets any more, and history now piles up.
- There are twelve shards. Five great factions still want them.
- Magic and power have a price here: memory, name, blood or sanity.
- The first regular ships have begun crossing the Black Tides to Kaelmar.

| Dominion | Faction | Capital | Lies |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Radiant Ascendancy | Celestial Order | Empyrean Spire | North |
| The Tyrant's Dominion | Crimson Legion | Varkhaal | East |
| The Fractured Nexus | Arcane Consortium | Zepharyos | North-west |
| The Eternal Veil | Shadow Pact | Noctis | West |
| The Verdant Rebirth | Wildborn Clans | The Rootspire | South |

The Hollow Marches sit in the middle, and nobody rules them.

**Getting around**

Each dominion's border is about a week from Hollow Refuge. Each capital is about another week past its border.

**What people say**

- Before the gods there was Fire, and it still burns somewhere.
- The skeletons of gods lie where they fell, at a place called the Titan's Fall.
- There was a war nobody remembers. They call it the Bloodless War.
- A city was stolen from the surface and sunk under the sea.
- The Silent Accord whispers that the Aetherfall was a prison, built by something older than anyone guesses.

## The Radiant Ascendancy

> The plains run gold to the horizon, wheat or grass or something brighter, it's hard to tell in this light. Shrines stand at every crossroads, scrubbed white. Far to the north, a city of white stone hangs in the sky with nothing holding it up. Patrols in polished armour ride past and look at you a moment too long, as if they're counting your sins.

**What you know**

- This is the dominion of the Celestial Order. Its creed is divine rule, purity and absolute law. Its emblem is a rising golden sun.
- The Order promises peace through enlightened order. It wants a golden theocracy under a sun that never sets, with undead, demons and forbidden magic wiped out. Its enemies say dissent gets wiped out too.
- The capital is Empyrean Spire, a floating city of white stone. The High Theurgists rule from the Throne of Light. The Order's chief fortress, the Golden Aerie, stands nearby.
- The Order's leaders are Highlord Theos Valcairn, Grand Theurgist Selara Dawnveil and Inquisitor Malrik Vael.
- Its forces are radiant knights, inquisitors, seraphs and missionary healers.

**Places**

| Place | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The Radiant Expanse | Golden plains. |
| The Luminara Gate | An arch of light, said to stand at the world's edge. |
| The Purged Halls | A city erased for rebellion. |

The other four factions keep enclaves here: Bastion of the Unbroken (Legion), the Sanctum of the Everseeing (Consortium), the Eclipsed Chamber (Pact) and the Thornward Grove (Wildborn).

**Getting around**

The border is about a week from Hollow Refuge, and Empyrean Spire is about another week beyond it.

**What people say**

- The Order calls obedience "harmony".
- Highlord Valcairn once doubted the Order. Nobody says so twice within the Highlord's hearing.
- The Thornward Grove is tolerated as a shrine to Solenne, but its keepers are hiding Wildborn who fled the Order.
- Nobody lives in the Purged Halls, but lamps still get lit there.

## The Tyrant's Dominion

> Iron mountains, black and red, and every pass guarded by a fort. You hear the crowds at Varkhaal before you see the walls, a roar that rises and breaks and rises again from the Great Arena. Out here every dispute is a duel, and every village has a fighting pit. The smiths never stop. Some of the smoke rises out of the mountainsides themselves.

**What you know**

- This is the dominion of the Crimson Legion. Its creed: only the strong deserve to rule. Its emblem is a crimson blade or a clenched fist.
- The Legion promises strength that can survive anything. It wants endless war: every settlement a fortress, every dispute a duel, an arena in every city.
- The capital is Varkhaal, the Iron Citadel, with its Great Arena. Warlords meet in the Warlord's Hall. The chief fortress is the Red Bastion.
- The Legion's leaders are Warlord Garron Kael and General Orana Volkash.
- Its forces are Shock Troopers, Centurions, Scouts, gladiators and siege companies.

**Places**

| Place | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The Bloodfields | A permanent warfront on the border facing the Hollow Marches. |
| The Ruins of the Old Kings | What's left of older rulers. |
| The Anvil of Dominion | War-forges under the mountains. |

The other four factions keep enclaves here: the Last Sanctuary (Order), the Ember Archives (Consortium), the Hollow Blades' Den (Pact) and the Bloodfang Warcamp (Wildborn).

**Getting around**

The border is about a week from Hollow Refuge, and Varkhaal is about another week beyond it.

**What people say**

- Garron Kael rules from the battlefield, not the throne, through sheer brutality.
- Rivals say the Legion's "strength" just means whatever helps the Legion.
- The forges under the Anvil are older than the Legion.
- The Bloodbound Company, a mercenary brotherhood, recruits at the arenas. Blood is the only coin they trust.

## The Fractured Nexus

> The sky up here is wrong. Storms move against the wind. Islands of rock drift overhead, trailing roots and waterfalls that never reach the ground. Lightning hangs in the air a heartbeat too long. Somewhere above the clouds, a city of towers turns slowly, and the air tastes of metal and burnt paper.

**What you know**

- This is the dominion of the Arcane Consortium. Its creed: reality is a puzzle to be rewritten. Its emblem is a violet geometric eye, or a prism.
- The Consortium promises a world no longer held back by ignorance. It wants limitless magic: floating islands, shifting time, cities that move between dimensions.
- The capital is Zepharyos, City of the Unbound, among the clouds. The council sits in the Nexus Spire. The chief fortress is the Nexus Collegium.
- The Consortium's leaders are Archmagister Veylan Orithis and Archivist Lissara Wynne.
- Its forces are scholars, constructs, dimensional mercenaries and Eye Beasts.

**Places**

| Place | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The Rift of Unmaking | An elemental vortex. |
| The Echoing Halls | A labyrinth of mirrors. |
| The Repository of the Unwritten | A forbidden vault. |

The other four factions keep enclaves here: the Last Unbroken Chapel (Order), the Warbound Coliseum (Legion), the Veilfracture (Pact) and the Living Wilds (Wildborn).

**Getting around**

The border is about a week from Hollow Refuge, and Zepharyos is about another week beyond it. The high country is all storms and floating rock.

**What people say**

- Archmagister Orithis is one experiment away from going too far.
- Nobody has ever seen the true face of Lord Varkoss, the illusionist. Some say Varkoss doesn't exist at all.
- The Consortium treats catastrophe as the cost of research.
- What goes into the Repository of the Unwritten doesn't come out.

## The Eternal Veil

> The sun never quite rises here, and never quite sets. Dusk hangs over everything, purple and grey. In Noctis everyone wears a mask, and nobody uses their real name twice. Streets that led one way this morning lead somewhere else tonight. Past the last harbour lies the Black Tides, flat and dark as ink, and no light comes off it at all.

**What you know**

- This is the dominion of the Shadow Pact. Its creed: rule unseen, and truth is whatever is believed. Its emblem is a broken black circle.
- The Pact promises freedom from any permanent central control. It wants perpetual twilight, where identities shift, histories get rewritten and assassins run the cities.
- The capital is Noctis, the City of Masks, in endless dusk. The rulers meet in the Midnight Court. The chief fortress is Nightglass.
- The Pact's leaders are Grandmaster Lysander Morrow and Spymaster Tobias Vale.
- Its forces are Silent Blades, illusionists, Shadow Lashers and executioners.
- Ships cross from the Veil's ports to Kaelholm in Kaelmar. Regular crossings have only just begun.

**Places**

| Place | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The Umbral Paths | Shifting roads that lead anywhere. |
| The Veilborn Archive | A hall of stone masks. |
| The Black Tides | A sunless sea. |

The other four factions keep enclaves here: the Last Beacon (Order), the Bleeding Arena (Legion), the Woven Sanctum (Consortium) and the Fading Grove (Wildborn).

**Getting around**

The border is about a week from Hollow Refuge, and Noctis is about another week beyond it. The crossing to Kaelmar takes about two weeks.

**What people say**

- Rivals say the Pact fights control by controlling everything in secret.
- Every mask in the Veilborn Archive belonged to somebody.
- The Umbral Paths will take you anywhere, but not always where you asked.
- The Drowned Saints preach on the docks that a sunken city will rise and the sea will take back what was stolen.

## The Verdant Rebirth

> There are no roads. The forest closes behind you like a door. Roots as thick as houses climb over the bones of buildings, and moss has swallowed whole streets. Somewhere ahead a tree the size of a mountain rises out of a ruined city, and lights move in its branches. Something big is following you, quietly, just out of sight.

**What you know**

- This is the dominion of the Wildborn Clans. Their creed: civilisation is a disease. Their emblem is a green crown of roots.
- The Clans promise a world that can go on living. They want cities swallowed by forest, no kings, weaker magic and the law of the wild.
- The capital is the Rootspire, a colossal tree-city grown over a ruined capital. The elders meet at the Den of Elders. The chief fortress is the Verdant Crown.
- The Clans' leaders are High Shaman Raethan Stormcaller and Elder Myrris Thornveil.
- Their forces are beast hunters, shamans, primal spirits and living constructs of root and bone.

**Places**

| Place | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The Green Expanse | Roadless wilds. |
| Fangholt | The skeleton of a behemoth. |
| The Ravaged Pass | A haunted canyon. |

The other four factions keep enclaves here: the Hollow Shrine (Order), the Bloodfang Pit (Legion), the Verdant Sanctum (Consortium) and the Hollow Grove (Pact).

**Getting around**

The border is about a week from Hollow Refuge, and the Rootspire is about another week beyond it. There are no roads.

**What people say**

- Raethan Stormcaller, a towering lizardfolk shaman, commands storms and beasts.
- Rivals say the Clans would sacrifice a city to save a tree.
- Nobody knows what killed the behemoth at Fangholt.
- Travellers who camp in the Ravaged Pass hear an army marching all night.

## The Hollowed Veil

> The birds stop first. Then the wind. A leaf hangs in the air beside you and doesn't fall. People walk past in the mist, in clothes a hundred years out of fashion, and none of them look at you. One of them is calling a name, over and over, the same way each time.

**What you know**

- A place where time forgets to move and the lost still walk.
- Nobody agrees on where it is.

**What people say**

- Stay too long and you'll walk out to find everyone you knew has grown old.
- The lost aren't ghosts. They're people who walked in and haven't noticed yet.
- The Nameless, people erased from history, look for their names here.

## The Bloodmoon Spire

> The moon comes up red, and the mist on the moor starts to move. When it thins, a tower stands where there was nothing an hour ago, black against the red light, with one window lit at the very top. By dawn it's gone, and the grass where it stood isn't even flattened.

**What you know**

- A tower that rises from the mist only when the moon turns red.
- Nobody agrees on where it is.

**What people say**

- Whoever goes in has until the moon turns pale again to get out.
- The Black Sun cult watches for red moons. They believe an endless eclipse is coming, and that something older than the gods will come out of it.
- The light in the top window is always lit.

## The Ashen Wastes

> The ground is warm through your boots. Ash drifts like snow and never settles. The charred bones of a city stand on the skyline, towers burned hollow, and between them the horizon glows orange at noon and midnight alike. Somewhere out there, something is still burning, and it has been burning longer than anyone remembers.

**What you know**

- A burned empire where the fire never dies.
- The myths say that before the gods there was Fire, and the first flame still burns here without fuel.
- Nobody agrees on exactly where it is.

**What people say**

- Some call the First Fire creation's source. Others say it's what will be left at the end.
- The Ashen Covenant believes the world must burn to be reborn, and they're searching the Wastes for the First Fire.
- The empire didn't burn because of a war. It burned because someone found the Fire.

## The Luminara Gate

> The plains end in light. An arch stands alone against the sky, bright enough to make your eyes water, with nothing behind it but more light. The air hums. Pilgrims kneel in rows before it, and not one of them goes through.

**What you know**

- An arch of light in the Radiant Ascendancy, in the far north.
- People say it stands at the edge of the world.

**What people say**

- Nobody who's walked through has come back to say what's on the other side.
- The Celestial Order guards it. Whether it keeps people in or out depends who you ask.
- It isn't the edge of the world. It's a door.

## The Rift of Echoes

> You hear your own voice before you speak. Then you hear it again, older. The ground ahead drops away into a haze where shapes move: a battle, a wedding, a fire, a city that isn't built yet. Somebody down there turns and looks right at you, and they have your face.

**What you know**

- People call it the edge of Aetheris, where past, present and future collide.
- Nobody agrees on whether it is truly the edge of anything.

**What people say**

- The Whispering Circle says time loops, and their seers don't predict the future, they remember it. Some say they learned it here.
- What you see in the haze has already happened, or will.
- Don't answer if the echo calls your name.

## The Black Tides

> The sea is black and flat and swallows every light. No moon on the water, no stars. The crew talks in whispers in a jumble of two languages. At dusk the captain orders every lantern doused, and nobody argues. Below the keel, the water goes down further than anyone wants to think about.

**What you know**

- A sunless sea between Aetheris and Kaelmar.
- Ships cross between Kaelholm and the ports of the Eternal Veil. The crossing takes about two weeks in the dark. Regular crossings have only just begun.
- Crews light no lanterns over the trench.
- Sailors speak Tidecant, a pidgin of Common and Kaelish.

**What people say**

- A trench runs under the sea, and that's why it's dark.
- In Aetheris some say the Tides lead to another world.
- Smugglers move people across who don't want to be found.
- The Drowned Saints say a city lies sunk beneath these waters, waiting to rise.

## The World

> Unroll the map. Two continents, one sea between them. On the east, Aetheris, broken into five dominions around a lawless middle. On the west, Kaelmar, where forest and old spellwork have grown together. Between them, the Black Tides, black on every copy of this map, because nobody has ever drawn what's under them.

**What you know**

- One world, two continents: Aetheris in the east and Kaelmar in the west. The Black Tides, a sunless sea, lie between them.
- Both continents worship the same gods, use the same coin and share much of their language.
- It's 300 After Bloom (AB) in Kaelmar and Year 31 Unbound in Aetheris. It's the same year.
- The year has 363 days: twelve 30-day months, plus three festival days that belong to no month.
- Ships cross the Black Tides between Kaelholm and the Eternal Veil in about two weeks.

**The gods**

| God | Portfolio |
| --- | --- |
| Solenne, the Dawn Mother | Light, law and mercy |
| Karrath, the Red Victor | War, strength and sworn oaths |
| Ithiel, the Many-Eyed | Knowledge, magic and the stars |
| Ellun, the Maskwright | Secrets, night and freedom |
| Orrowen, the Green Antler | The wild, the seasons and beasts |
| Sephane, Keeper of the Cairn | Death, memory and names |

Three gods are dead: Maruun the Sundered Tide, Aurex the Throne-Breaker and the Nameless Smith.

**What people say**

- The gods are real and answer prayers, but ask a priest about the Cycle and you'll get nothing.
- The Rift opens on both continents. Some say it's one wound, and that the two ends meet somewhere in between.
- In Kaelmar, the roots under the ground lead to the same place.

> Lay the world out east to west and it reads like a sentence. Aetheris. The Eternal Veil's dusk coast. The Black Tides. Kaelholm on the far shore. Then Kaelmar, running west to the Rift.

**What you know**

| --- | --- |
| Centre | The Hollow Marches, the lawless heartland. Every dominion borders it. |
| North | The Radiant Ascendancy: golden plains up to the Luminara Gate. |

| East | The Tyrant's Dominion: iron mountains, with the Bloodfields facing the Marches. |
| South | The Verdant Rebirth: roadless forest. |
| West | The Eternal Veil: a dusk coast on the Black Tides. |

| In Kaelmar | What lies there |
| --- | --- |

| Centre | Open Bloom country over the under-roads. |

| East | The Eastern Deepwood, then the eastern mountains. |
| Far east | Kaelholm, then the coast and the Black Tides. |
| North | Cold highlands and ice. |
| South | Fens, lakes and river country. |

**Getting around**

- In Aetheris, each dominion's border is about a week from Hollow Refuge, and each capital about another week beyond.
- Kaelmar is about 1,500 km from the Rift to the east coast.
- The crossing between the continents takes about two weeks by ship.

**What people say**

- Nobody has mapped the far shores of either continent.
- The Rift runs under all of it.

## Kaelmar

> Forest grows through everything. Moss on the old stone roads, roots through the walls of towers that were built to last forever. Strange marks glow faintly in the ruins, lines of light that pulse when you get close, as if something is listening. The air smells of loam and spores, and here and there the trees seem to lean toward you.

**What you know**

- Three hundred years ago the Kaelith civilisation tried to move minds between bodies and conquer death. It failed. People call the failure the Shrouding.
- The failure joined spellwork, flesh and forest. The change was paused, not undone, and the Bloom grew through the ruins.
- It's now 300 AB, After Bloom.
- The big question here is who gets to choose what shape another life takes.
- Kaelith glyph-work and the Bloom are two kinds of one magic, a grammar of call and answer. It costs blood, memory or self.
- Under-roads and root paths reach a place people call the between.

| Power | What it wants |
| --- | --- |
| Kaelholm | Every old work catalogued, and the change turned back. |
| The Spore Circle | To tend the Bloom. Its Eastern Tendril burns Kaelith work. |
| The Convergence Collective | The change finished, but only for those who choose it. |
| The Ascendancy | A few minds raised up through the change, and everyone else beneath them. |

**What people say**

- The ruins aren't dead, only sleeping.
- Ships from Aetheris have started arriving at Kaelholm, and everyone wants to know what they're carrying.
- The Bloom remembers everything that happens in it.

## Kaelholm (Settlement)

> Grey stone, grey robes, grey sea. The biggest city on the continent climbs up from the harbour in terraces of archives and monasteries, every window lit with somebody reading. Clerks meet every ship at the dock with ledgers open. Somewhere above, a bell rings, and a name gets struck from the rolls.

**What you know**

- Kaelholm is the largest city on Kaelmar and the first port for ships from Aetheris. It used to be called Kaelith-East.
- It kept Kaelith's name, archives, scholar corps and tribunals. Its elders wear grey.
- Kaelholm is the strongest power on the continent. It wants every vault opened and catalogued, and the change turned back so the world goes back "into its drawers".
- Archivist-Elder Venn Tull, a gnome and the head of a tribunal, leads it.
- Its tribunals can punish someone by scraping their name from every roll. People call it being "burned".

**What people say**

- Venn Tull wants a living Kaelith guardian, and will pay anything to get one.
- The Eastern Tendril of the Spore Circle burns Kaelith work on sight, and Kaelholm's expeditions keep running into them.
- Kaelholm wants to be the first to look at anything old that comes off the ships from Aetheris.
- Once you're burned, nobody in the city will say your name aloud.

## Thornhaven

> Lamps hang in the branches, green light glowing through the skins of hollowed gourds. Smoke rises from a pit where something carved and glowing is being burned, and the people around it watch until it's ash. Nobody here carries anything made by the Kaelith. They check your pack before they let you sit down.

**What you know**

- Thornhaven is the seat of the Spore Circle's Eastern Tendril, in the Eastern Deepwood.
- The Eastern Tendril burns Kaelith work and carries lamps grown in gourds.
- Elder Sorrel leads it, an elf of about 320 who saw the Shrouding as a child.
- The Tendril opposes Kaelholm's expeditions into the old vaults.

**What people say**

- Elder Sorrel wants every piece of Kaelith work burned, and will never forgive what happened.
- The gourd-lamps don't need oil.
- Tendril scouts have been seen a long way from the Deepwood, carrying green lamps.

## The Eastern Deepwood

> The forest and the ruins have grown into one thing. Ferns with copper veins brush your knees. Towers rise out of the canopy, furred with fungus from top to bottom. The rot on the fallen logs moves when you're not looking straight at it. Then the air goes still, and the spores start to drift.

**What you know**

- Forest and Kaelith ruins grown together, in eastern Kaelmar.
- Copper-veined ferns, fungus-covered towers, thinking rot and spore-storms.
- Thornhaven, the seat of the Spore Circle's Eastern Tendril, is here, and so is the Survey Garden.

**What people say**

- The rot thinks. Some say it remembers too.
- Get caught in a spore-storm and you might not come out the same person.
- Kaelholm sends expeditions in. The Eastern Tendril burns what they find.

## The Survey Garden

> Rows of glass houses, all of them broken, and the plants have long since climbed out. Labels in old Kaelith script still hang from stakes, over things that have grown into something else. As you walk, the leaves turn to follow you, and you have the feeling you're being written down.

**What you know**

- An abandoned Kaelith herbarium, overgrown by its own test plots, in the Eastern Deepwood.
- Its plants record what passes them.

**What people say**

- Kaelholm would pay well for anyone who can read what the plants have recorded.
- Some of the old labels describe plants that haven't grown yet.

## The Eastern Mountains

> The Deepwood thins out into scree, and the mountains rise ahead, grey and white. Goliath herders watch you climb from the ridges and don't wave. Further up, you pass a camp where soldiers drill in columns, and half of them carry iron grafted onto their bodies.

**What you know**

- Mountain country east of the Deepwood.
- Goliath clans live here.
- The Ascendancy began here, as a doctrine inside a mercenary legion. It's not an army yet.
- Marshal Dace Corvane leads it, a hobgoblin mercenary captain who "keeps the count".

**What people say**

- The Ascendancy is searching these mountains for a lost Kaelith vault.
- Corvane wants an army, and sounds very reasonable when explaining why.
- The Ascendancy's soldiers are remade with iron and graft.

## The Ash Barrens

> Grey ash under grey sky. The wind lifts it in sheets and lays it down again somewhere else. Herds move across the plain in long lines, driven by orc riders who know every dip and ridge by heart. There's no water you'd want to drink, and no shade at all.

**What you know**

- The ash barrens lie east of central Kaelmar.
- Orc herders live there.

**What people say**

- The herders don't say what burned here. Ask, and they change the subject.
- The ash is deeper than any well anyone's dug.

## The Central Bloom Country

> The ground gives slightly underfoot. The Bloom has covered everything: hills, ruins and old roads, all under a soft carpet of moss and fungus that glows faintly at dusk. Here and there a stairway leads down into the earth, its walls lit with old glyphs. The whole country seems to be breathing.

**What you know**

- Open Bloom country covers the centre of Kaelmar.
- The under-roads run beneath it.
- The Bloom-Speakers of Ashvale teach how to commune with the Bloom safely.

**What people say**

- The Bloom answers when it's spoken to properly. The trouble is knowing what it will ask in return.
- There are more ways down to the under-roads than anyone has mapped.

## The Under-Roads

> The tunnel walls glow with glyphs, line after line, brightening as you pass and fading behind you. Rails run along the floor for sleds that haven't moved in three hundred years. Roots as thick as pillars hold up the ceiling, and some of them pulse faintly, like something is moving through them.

**What you know**

- Ancient tunnels, lit by glyphs, run under Kaelmar and link the thirteen old vaults.
- Rune-sleds used to run through them. Circuit-roots hold up many of the galleries.
- At one tunnel junction stands the Bloom Cathedral, which must be asked for passage.
- At a rot crossroads, the roots can carry travellers through the between, for a price.

**What people say**

- Some sleds still run, with nobody aboard.
- The roots will take you anywhere, but they always take something too.
- Kaelholm has mapped some of the under-roads. It hasn't shared the maps.

## The Bloom Cathedral

> The tunnel opens into a hall. Roots rise like columns to a ceiling you can't see, twisting in slow spirals. The light is green and soft and seems to come from everywhere. There's no door on the far side, only a wall of roots, and the longer you stand here the more certain you are that the hall is waiting for you to say something.

**What you know**

- A tunnel junction under Kaelmar, grown into a hall of root columns and slow spirals.
- You have to ask it for passage.

**What people say**

- What you have to pay to pass depends on what you're carrying.
- The Bloom-Speakers can talk to it. Some people say it talks back.

## The Northern Highlands and Ice

> The wind comes down off the ice and doesn't stop. The highlands are bare rock and frozen moss, and the only shelter is in the dwarf-halls cut into the mountainsides, their doors so well hidden you'd walk right past them. Further north, the ice runs to the end of sight.

**What you know**

- Cold highlands and ice cover northern Kaelmar.
- Dwarves hold the highlands.

**What people say**

- The dwarves have been guarding something in the mountains for longer than they'll admit.
- Out on the ice, sometimes, there are lights under the surface.

## The Southern Fens, Lakes and River Country

> The land goes soft and wet and full of birds. Reed beds stretch away into mist, cut by slow green channels. Flat-bottomed boats pass loaded with goods, halfling traders calling prices to each other across the water. Out in the deep fen, something large and slow surfaces, and a tortle on its shell-back raises a hand to you.

**What you know**

- Southern Kaelmar is fens, lakes and river country.
- Halfling traders work the waterways.
- Tortles and lizardfolk keep the fens.

**What people say**

- The halflings know every channel, and will take you anywhere if the price is right.
- Some of the lakes are deeper than they ought to be.
- The fen-keepers say the water remembers.
