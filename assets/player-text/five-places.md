# Player Adventure Book: Five Places

Nov 11, 2026 · @Nix

## How to use this

Everything below is safe to read aloud or hand to players. Secrets are left out.

Each place has three parts:

- **Read aloud.** The quoted text, for when the party first arrives.
- **What you know.** Common knowledge any local or traveller could pass on.
- **What people say.** Rumours. Some are true, some aren't, and the players don't know which.

## The Hollow Marches

> The road gives out before the land does. Past the last milestone the ground turns to churned clay and old iron, a rusted helm here, a spear shaft gone grey there, all of it half swallowed by the grass. Ruins stand on every rise. None of them match. A Legion banner snaps over one ridge, and on the next a column of white smoke climbs from somebody else's camp. Nobody owns this country. Everybody is trying to.

**What you know**

- The Marches are the heartland of Aetheris. Forgotten kingdoms once met here.
- All five dominions border it, and none of them rules it. The Celestial Order, the Crimson Legion, the Arcane Consortium, the Shadow Pact and the Wildborn Clans all keep camps and send patrols.
- Hollow Refuge is the one neutral town. Most travel starts there.
- Ashen Spire was the capital of the last kingdom. It's a ruin now, picked over by scavengers and sellswords.
- The Scar is a battlefield of rusted steel that never ends.
- Blackveil Fortress is an abandoned stronghold. People who go into its depths don't come back.
- The Sunken Archive is a library under the ground, laid out like a maze.
- Wither's Hollow is a dead forest, always in mist.
- The Cairn of the Forgotten is a field of graves with no names on them.

**Getting around**

| From Hollow Refuge to | Time | How safe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Crossroads of Fate | Half a day | Watched, usually safe |
| The Bloodmarked Fields | Half a day | Risky |
| Blackveil Fortress | 1 day | Risky |

Past those three, the roads get worse. The routes out to the Rift are openly hostile.

**What people say**

- The dead on the Scar don't stay down.
- Read too long in the Sunken Archive and you start remembering things that were never yours.
- The paths in Wither's Hollow move once you've walked past them.
- Some of the nameless graves at the Cairn stir.
- Restless spirits still walk Ashen Spire.

## Hollow Refuge

> The town looks like it was built out of whatever the last five worlds left lying around. A temple column holds up a tanner's awning. A gatehouse arch that belongs on a palace frames a pig pen. The wall around it all runs in bands of different stone, each one older or newer than the last. Somebody has hung a sign over the nearest door: a bent nail driven through a crown. Inside, people are arguing in three accents, and nobody has reached for a weapon. Here, that counts as peace.

**What you know**

- A few hundred people live here, maybe 250 to 400.
- Every faction needs a place to talk, trade and patch up its wounded, so every faction leaves the Refuge alone.

**Places in town**

| Place | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The Crooked Nail | Inn, mercenary exchange and rumour market. Mara Venn runs it. The sign is a bent nail through a crown. |
| Old Vara's Forge | Gear repairs. Vara would rather be paid in favours than coin. |
| The Lantern House | Hospital, shrine and mortuary. Weapons stay outside. Even faction soldiers do as they're told. |
| The Market of Lost Things | Battlefield salvage, old maps, strange coins and objects nobody can name. |
| The Seer's Tower | Home of the Blind Seer. The tower has no windows, because "windows work both ways". |
| The Empty House | Nobody goes in. Nobody can say who lives there. Smoke still rises from the chimney every morning. |
| The Old Wall | Built over centuries, one layer at a time. |
| The East Gate | Where faction envoys come in, and most of the trouble with them. |

**What people say**

- The locals remember things they shouldn't.
- Vara knows metals that shouldn't exist, just by the look of them.
- One stretch of the Old Wall honours a battle nobody in town remembers fighting.
- Ask enough people about the Empty House and every one of them changes the subject.

## The Bloodmarked Fields

> The grass gives up a mile out. After that it's mud the colour of old rust, cut by trenches nobody bothered to fill and stakes nobody bothered to pull. Crows work the ground in no hurry at all. Five sets of colours fly over five dug-in lines, close enough to shout insults across. Somewhere in the middle, a work crew is digging, and every line is watching them.

**What you know**

- Every faction fights here, and the fighting is over shards. They keep surfacing in this ground.
- It's half a day from Hollow Refuge, and the road is risky.
- From the Fields it's half a day to the Scar, or a full day to Wither's Hollow. Both roads are dangerous.
- Nobody holds the Fields for long.

**What people say**

- Every faction has sent somebody new this season. Something has them stirred up.
- The digging isn't about salvage. Somebody thinks there's something big under there.
- Whoever walks off the Fields with a shard won't be left alone afterwards.

## The Rift

The Rift opens in two places, one on each continent. Use the read-aloud text for whichever one the party reaches.

**In the Hollow Marches (Aetheris)**

> You hear it before you see it, a low hum you feel in your back teeth. Then the ground stops. The chasm runs off in both directions further than you can see, and there's no bottom to it. Drop a stone and you'll be waiting a long time. The air at the edge tastes of copper and tin, and the hair on your arms won't lie down.

**At Ashvale (Kaelmar)**

> It's a crack in the world. It runs out of sight both ways, and warm air breathes up out of it, steady as a sleeper's breath, even on a frost morning. Fungus glows along the lip in pale greens and blues. Between the glowing patches there's a mold you keep catching from the corner of your eye. You'd swear it's watching you.

**What you know**

- The eastern Rift sits at the far edge of the Hollow Marches. Every road there is openly hostile. It's a day from Blackveil Fortress, or half a day from the Sunken Archive or Wither's Hollow.
- The western Rift runs beside Ashvale. The town is built right on its lip.
- Ashvale's ring of thirteen standing stones stands close to the edge.

**What people say**

- In Aetheris: the Rift has something to do with how the Aetherfall began.
- Mother Thorn, a Wildborn elder, is afraid of "what the land remembers beneath the Rift".
- In Kaelmar, the Convergence Collective has its own Riftwalker, Maro Grane.
- Scholars and a few drunk sailors say the two openings are one wound. It runs under the land and drops into a trench under the Black Tides, and that's why that sea has no light.
- Somebody from Aetheris has been drawing maps of the Rift.

## Ashvale

> The town hugs the edge of the Rift like it's afraid to let go. Warm air comes up out of the crack and keeps the frost off the nearest roofs. In the middle of town, thirteen standing stones lean together in a ring, older than anything around them. The meeting hall beside them is only half finished, its timbers still raw. Out on the eastern edge, young trees stand in rows with their grafts bound in twine. Somebody near the stones is singing the same four lines over and over.

**What you know**

- Refugees founded Ashvale in 2 AB. They were led by a woman with burned palms, and she wrote the rite the town still keeps.
- Locals say everyone who sleeps in Ashvale is "a hand on the key". Ask them what the key opens and you'll mostly get a shrug.
- Children learn a four-line keeping song. It gets sung at the stones.
- Three groups run things, and they agree the rite must be kept. They don't agree on much else.

| Group | Leader | What they care about |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Boundary Keepers | Ritekeeper Orla Hesk | Keeping the rite and the song, sung right. |
| Bloom-Speakers | Master Speaker Tevan Lin | Teaching communion with the Bloom safely, to anyone who asks. Their sanctuary is on the west edge of town. |
| Wrights | Chief Wright Brenna Halvor | Tools that work. They're planting the grafted orchard on the east side. |

**Around town**

- **The east gate** and **the west road** are the ways in and out. The west road crosses a ford.
- **The old waygate** is a ruin under the west road. The Wrights pay for salvage from it.
- **Nine villages** lie in the hills to the west and south: Coldwater, Haskett's Mill, Greyfold, Low Fenn, Oakhallow, Sallow Cross, Kettleby, Brannock and Thornmere.
- **Odran Reeve** trades salt up from Low Fenn.

**What people say**

- No frost ever settles inside the ring of stones.
- Once, years back, the stones were "listening".
- Something still hums down in the old waygate.
- A green gourd-lamp has been showing up at the treeline at night.
