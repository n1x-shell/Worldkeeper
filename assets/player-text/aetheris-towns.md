# Player Adventure Book: Villages and Towns of Aetheris

## How to use this

Four villages of the Hollow Marches, and a town in each of the five dominions. Each has a short read-aloud, what you know, a few places, who you'll meet, how to get there, and rumours. Their leaders follow.

## Archwell

*A village built inside a fallen kingdom's gate, around a well that draws up water from somewhere else.*

> A stone arch taller than any tower stands alone in the grass, and the village has grown inside it like a nest in a skull. Washing hangs between the old gate-posts. In the middle of it all, a well with a bronze lid, and a queue of people waiting to see what comes up.

**What you know**

- A village of salvagers and farmers in the north-west Marches, built in the ruin of a great gate.
- Nobody knows what kingdom the gate belonged to. The stones have five different carvings, one over the other.
- Its well sometimes draws up water that tastes of the sea, or of snow, or of somewhere that isn't here.
- @[Well-Warden Ysolde Varn] keeps the well, and the village.

**Places in the village**

| Place | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The Well | Bronze-lidded and very deep. Some days the bucket comes up full of salt water, some days with snow in it. |
| The Gate Hall | The hollow inside the arch, now the village hall, market and dancehall. |
| The Five Carvings | The arch's faces, each carved by a different world. Scholars come to copy them. |

**Getting to Hollow Refuge.** A day's walk south-east, past the Ashen Spire.

**What people say**

- Last spring the well brought up a fish nobody could name. It's still alive, in a barrel.
- There's a sixth carving under the other five, and nobody can quite look at it.
- Consortium scholars pay well for a bucket from the well, on the right day.

## Tallowmarch

*A candlemakers' village on the Radiant border, where every shrine in the Marches gets its lights.*

> You smell Tallowmarch before you see it: warm wax, smoke and honey. Low houses with deep porches line a single street, and every porch has rows of candles cooling in the shade. Bees drone over the clover. At the end of the street, a little shrine with six niches burns a light in every one.

**What you know**

- A village of candlemakers and beekeepers in the north-east Marches, on the road to the Radiant Ascendancy.
- Its candles light shrines all over the Marches, and some in Empyrean Spire.
- Order pilgrims stop here on the way north. Not all of them are welcome.
- @[Chandler Ossery Thane] runs the guild, which means he runs the village.

**Places in the village**

| Place | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The Long Street | One street, half a mile long, every house a workshop. |
| The Hives | Hundreds of skeps on the hill behind the village, and the best honey in the Marches. |
| The Six-Niche Shrine | A shrine with a light for every living god, and an empty seventh niche nobody fills. |

**Getting to Hollow Refuge.** A day's walk south-west, past Blackveil Fortress.

**What people say**

- The bees won't go near the Scar. Neither will the beekeepers.
- The Order wants Tallowmarch inside its border. Tallowmarch would rather sell it candles.
- Somebody lights a candle in the seventh niche every Evernight. Nobody admits to it.

## Lastlight

*The last lit village on the road west, where travellers stop before the dusk of the Eternal Veil.*

> The road west runs toward a bruise-coloured sky, and the last thing on it before the dusk is a village of lamps. Every post, every door and every gable has one, and they're all lit, even at noon. A tiny figure on wings flits from lamp to lamp with a long-handled taper, and waves.

**What you know**

- A waystation village on the road from Hollow Refuge to Noctis, the last before the Eternal Veil's endless dusk.
- Every lamp in the village is kept lit, day and night.
- Caravans stop here to buy oil, hire guides and get their nerve back.
- @[Lamplighter Ezri] keeps the lamps, and so keeps the village.

**Places in the village**

| Place | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The Lamp Road | The village's one road, lit end to end. Walking it at night feels like noon. |
| The Last Inn | A coaching inn full of travellers going both ways, and the best place in the Marches to hire a guide into the dusk. |
| The Oil Store | Barrels of lamp oil in a stone barn, guarded like treasure. |

**Getting to Hollow Refuge.** Half a day east, on the road.

**What people say**

- If a Lastlight lamp goes out, something comes in. Nobody wants to find out what.
- The Shadow Pact pays Ezri to keep one lamp dark. Ezri says no. Ezri says no every month.
- The dusk is a little closer to the village every year.

## Stakewall

*A palisaded farming village on the edge of the Bloodmarked Fields, which has been attacked by every faction and taken by none.*

> A wall of sharpened stakes, twice a person's height, rings fields of barley and a cluster of sod-roofed houses. The gate is open, and a huge horned figure is leaning on it, chewing a stalk of grass and watching the Fields to the west. Somebody's whetstone is going, somewhere. It's always going.

**What you know**

- A farming village south-east of the Bloodmarked Fields, close to the Tyrant's border.
- Its palisade has been attacked by every faction since the Border Years, and has never fallen.
- Legion recruiters come every spring. Stakewall sends them home with bread and no recruits.
- @[Reeve Bram Hollis] leads it, and leads its wall.

**Places in the village**

| Place | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The Wall | The stake palisade, rebuilt a stake at a time. Every family keeps its own section. |
| The Bell Gate | The only gate, with a bell that every child knows how to ring. |
| The Barley Fields | Inside the wall and out. The outside fields are harvested in armour. |

**Getting to Hollow Refuge.** A day west, round the edge of the Fields.

**What people say**

- Since the shard rumour, there are diggers in the Fields every night. Stakewall can see their lanterns.
- Bram has turned down a Legion commission three times.
- Stakewall barley is the only thing both armies will buy from each other.

## Dawnmere

*A pilgrim town on the long road to the Luminara Gate, all white walls and very strict rules.*

> White walls, white roofs, white dust on the road. Dawnmere sits beside a still lake that holds the sky like a mirror, and every building faces east. Pilgrims in grey robes queue at the gate to have their papers checked, and Order knights check them slowly. Somewhere a bell is counting the hour, and everyone stops to listen.

**What you know**

- A pilgrim town in the Radiant Ascendancy, on the road from Empyrean Spire to the Luminara Gate.
- Every building faces east, so the dawn reaches every door.
- Its rules are strict, and its magistrate enforces every one.
- @[Magistrate Seline Ashby] keeps the law here.

**Places in the town**

| Place | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The Mirror Lake | A still lake that reflects the sky so perfectly that birds fly into it. |
| The Pilgrim Hall | Where travellers are fed, housed, counted and watched. |
| The Bell of Hours | A great bell that rings every hour, day and night. In Dawnmere, everything stops when it rings. |

**Getting around.** Empyrean Spire is about three days south-west; the Luminara Gate about four days north-east, on foot, as pilgrims go.

**What people say**

- A pilgrim went into the lake last year and came out on the other side of it. That's the story, anyway.
- The magistrate is a yuan-ti. The Order made her magistrate anyway. Nobody knows why.
- Pilgrims coming back from the Luminara Gate don't talk about what they saw.

## Ironwake

*A forge town under the Tyrant's mountains, where the Legion's weapons are made and its veterans go to retire.*

> The hammers start before dawn and stop after dark. Ironwake climbs a hillside in terraces of black stone, each one with a forge and a chimney, and the smoke hangs over the whole town like a second sky. Old soldiers sit on every step, arguing about battles. The young ones are at the anvils.

**What you know**

- A forge town in the Tyrant's Dominion, south of Varkhaal.
- Much of the Legion's steel is made here, and much of it is mended here.
- Legion veterans retire here, and the town is run by them.
- @[Forgemaster Kiro Vashti] holds the Forge Seat.

**Places in the town**

| Place | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The Terraces | Forge after forge up the hillside. Each terrace belongs to a different company. |
| The Veterans' Hall | Where old soldiers drink, argue and settle disputes, by duel if they have to. |
| The Proving Yard | Where every blade made in Ironwake is tested, and every smith with it. |

**Getting around.** Varkhaal is about two days north. Hollow Refuge about two weeks west.

**What people say**

- Kiro Vashti made the Warlord's greatsword, and won't make him another.
- There's a forge on the top terrace nobody works. It's still hot.
- Ironwake steel rings when it's lied to. That's the story.

## Driftholm

*A town on a floating island, moored to the mountains by chains, which goes wherever the storms take it when the chains break.*

> The island hangs in the air above the peaks, held down by four great chains, and the town on top of it is all rope bridges, wind-chimes and laundry flapping sideways. Lightning walks around the edges of the sky. A ladder of iron rungs runs up the nearest chain, and somebody at the top is waving you on.

**What you know**

- A town on a floating island in the Fractured Nexus, chained to the mountains.
- When a chain breaks, the town drifts until it's caught again. It's been as far as the Marches.
- Consortium researchers study the island's stone. The townsfolk sell them tea.
- @[Anchor-Master Wenna Gale] keeps the chains.

**Places in the town**

| Place | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The Four Chains | Each as thick as a house, anchored in the rock below. One is new, and the town still argues about the old one. |
| The Chime Market | A market where every stall has wind-chimes, so you can find it in a storm. |
| The Edge | The rim of the island, with a rope rail. Children dare each other to lean out. |

**Getting around.** Zepharyos is about two days away, by chain-ladder and mountain road, or a day if the island has drifted the right way.

**What people say**

- The island drifted once, before the Cycle broke, and came back with a different name. Nobody remembers the old one.
- Wenna Gale can call the wind. That's why she keeps the chains.
- Somebody keeps cutting the chains, one link at a time.

## Mothhaven

*A smugglers' harbour on the dusk coast, where the first ship to cross the Black Tides set out.*

> Lantern-moths gather in clouds over the harbour, drawn to the only lit windows on the coast. The town leans out over black water on stilts and piers, and everything creaks. Ships come and go without lights. On the end of the longest pier, a tortle sits fishing, and he's been watching you since you came round the headland.

**What you know**

- A harbour town on the northern dusk coast of the Eternal Veil.
- The first ship to cross the Black Tides and come back sailed from here, in Year 11.
- Smugglers, sailors and the Shadow Pact all use the port. Nobody uses their real name.
- @[Harbourmaster Gulen Saltback] keeps the harbour.

**Places in the town**

| Place | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The Long Pier | The longest pier on the coast, and the best place to hire a ship west. |
| The Moth Lantern | A tavern with one lit window, which is why it's full of moths, and sailors. |
| The Dark Dock | Where the ships that cross the Black Tides tie up. No lanterns allowed. |

**Getting around.** Noctis is about three days south, down the coast. Kaelholm is about two weeks west, across the Black Tides.

**What people say**

- The first crew to cross brought someone back with them. Nobody in Mothhaven will say where they went.
- Gulen won't sail. He says he's seen what's under the Tides.
- The Pact runs the harbour. Gulen says he does. Both are right.

## Highbough

*A Wildborn village in the forest canopy, where there are no roads and you're not welcome until someone lowers a rope.*

> The forest is quiet in a way that means you're being watched. High above, platforms and rope bridges run between the trunks, and smoke drifts through the leaves. Nobody calls down. After a long minute, a rope drops at your feet, with a knot tied in the end that you'll learn means 'one guest'.

**What you know**

- A Wildborn village in the canopy of the Green Expanse, in the Verdant Rebirth.
- There are no roads to it, and no ladders. Visitors are let up by rope, or not at all.
- It's a waystation for hunters and shamans of many clans.
- @[Elder Aelis Thornweave] speaks for it.

**Places in the town**

| Place | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The Rope Gate | Where visitors wait to be let up. The knots in the rope mean different things. |
| The Long Platform | The village hall, a platform between six trees, open to the sky. |
| The Root Shrine | Down on the forest floor, the one place in Highbough on the ground: Orrowen's shrine. |

**Getting around.** The Rootspire is about four days east through roadless forest, with a guide.

**What people say**

- Highbough has never been found by the Order. Not once.
- Aelis changes with the seasons, and so do Highbough's rules.
- Something walks on the forest floor at night that the hunters won't name.

## Well-Warden Ysolde Varn

*Archwell's well-keeper, a sea elf who came up a well and never went back.*

| People | Calling | Pronouns | From |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sea elf | Mage | She/her | Archwell |

**Looks.** A tall, grey-skinned sea elf with dark wet-looking hair that never quite dries, and a bronze key on a chain.

**Manner.** Calm, dry-humoured and very hard to surprise.

**Who they are**

- Warden of the well at Archwell, and the village's headwoman.
- Says she came up the well herself, years ago, and stayed because the bread was good.

**What people say**

- She can tell where the water came from by the taste.
- She keeps a list of everything the well has brought up. It's long.

| Half-orc | Monk | He/him | Tallowmarch |

**Manner.** Patient, kind and immovable in a bargain.

## Lamplighter Ezri

*Lastlight's fairy lamplighter, who has never once let a lamp go out.*

| People | Calling | Pronouns | From |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fairy | Mystic | They/them | Lastlight |

**Looks.** A fairy no taller than a hand, with moth-brown wings, a soot-black face and a taper twice their height.

**Manner.** Bright, bossy and fearless of anything except the dark.

**Who they are**

- Lamplighter of Lastlight, and the closest thing it has to a headperson.
- Made a bargain with something, long ago, to keep the lights burning.

**What people say**

- Ezri can light a lamp from across the street.
- They say Ezri's patron lives in the dusk, and doesn't like the lamps either.

## Elder Aelis Thornweave

*Highbough's eladrin elder, whose temper follows the seasons and whose word is law in the canopy.*

| People | Calling | Pronouns | From |

| Eladrin | Warrior | They/them | Highbough |

**Looks.** A tall eladrin whose hair turns with the seasons, green, gold, rust and white, and whose skin is patterned like bark.

**Manner.** Warm in summer, grieving in autumn, cold in winter, and wild in spring.
