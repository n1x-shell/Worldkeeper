# Peoples of the world

## How to use this

Every people found on both continents. Each section becomes that people's page in the handbook, with a one-line blurb, how they live in Aetheris and in Kaelmar, and an "At the table" note for building a character.

## Aarakocra

*Feathered folk of the high passes, who see the world from above and find it smaller than everyone says.*

Aarakocra nest in the high passes above the Green Expanse and in the storm-cliffs of the Fractured Nexus, where the Consortium pays them well to carry messages through weather no airship would try. They have little patience for borders, which they can't see from the air.

In Kaelmar they roost on the cliffs of the Eastern Mountains and the northern ice. Some families say the spore-storms gave them their wings; others say they always had them, and the Bloom only made more of them.

**Strengths and flaws.** Unmatched scouts and messengers. Restless, and uneasy under a roof.

**Culture.** Clan nests, sky-songs, and a long memory for which way the wind blew on important days.

**Common names.** Aeka, Kirru, Pell, Quill, Sarrik, Tiriel, Ulla, Zephi

**At the table.** Use the aarakocra rules.

## Aasimar

*People with a little of the divine in them, who glow when they're angry and are never quite sure why.*

Aasimar are born to ordinary families, most often in the Radiant Ascendancy, and the Celestial Order claims every one it can find. Many serve gladly. Many run. Since the Cycle broke, aasimar have been born who say they dream of a light that isn't Solenne's.

In Kaelmar aasimar are rare and quietly watched. Kaelholm records every one in its rolls, and the Continuity is said to keep a list of its own.

**Strengths and flaws.** Healers and fighters with a light of their own. Hunted for it, and held to standards nobody else is.

**Culture.** No culture of their own; they take their family's. Most carry a guide they hear in dreams.

**Common names.** Aurel, Cassiel, Ilyra, Lumen, Orlaith, Seren, Theron, Veyra

**At the table.** Use the aasimar rules.

## Stormborn

*People with a storm in their blood: hair that drifts, eyes that flicker, and a temper that brings the weather.*

Stormborn are born in thunderstorms, most often over the Fractured Nexus, where the storms never stop. The Consortium calls them a natural phenomenon and studies them. The Wildborn call them sky-children and give them to the shamans.

In Kaelmar storms are just weather, and Stormborn are rare enough that sailors take one aboard for luck.

**Strengths and flaws.** Quick, light and hard to pin down. Restless, and moody as the sky.

**Culture.** Born wherever the storm was; most drift until they find somewhere the weather suits them.

**Common names.** Aeris, Galen, Kesta, Lyre, Siro, Tamsin, Velka, Wren

**At the table.** Use the air genasi rules.

## Bugbears

*Big, quiet and much stealthier than anyone expects, until they decide to be noticed.*

Bugbears came out of the eastern hills of the Tyrant's Dominion and now turn up anywhere there's work for someone large and patient. Plenty work for the Legion. Plenty, like the innkeeper of the Crooked Nail in Hollow Refuge, very definitely don't.

In Kaelmar bugbears keep to the Eastern Deepwood and the woodcutting villages, and they've a name as trackers and guides the Scholar Corps relies on.

**Strengths and flaws.** Long-armed, strong and silent on their feet. Taken for brutes, and they've learned to use it.

**Culture.** Close-knit families, long silences, and a dry sense of humour that takes years to notice.

**Common names.** Bruk, Gorra, Hask, Mog, Nargu, Rusk, Tharg, Ulla

**At the table.** Use the bugbear rules.

## Centaurs

*Horse-bodied wanderers of the open plains, who measure the world in a day's gallop.*

Centaur herds cross the Radiant Expanse and the open land of the Hollow Marches, and their routes are older than the borders. Every dominion has tried to tax them. None has managed it twice.

In Kaelmar centaurs roam the open Bloom country, where the spore-storms first rewrote them. Their herds travel ahead of the storms, and villages watch them to know when weather's coming.

**Strengths and flaws.** Fast, strong and tireless over open ground. Uncomfortable in cities, cellars and anywhere with stairs.

**Culture.** Herds, long migrations, and star-lore for finding the way.

**Common names.** Arren, Callis, Dovren, Kaya, Merrow, Sabine, Torvald, Yessa

**At the table.** Use the centaur rules.

## Changelings

*People who can wear any face, and who sometimes forget which one was theirs.*

Changelings live hidden in every city of Aetheris, and the Shadow Pact employs more of them than anyone admits. In Noctis, where everyone wears a mask, they're the only people who don't need one. Nobody knows where they came from. Neither do they.

In Kaelmar changelings are rare, and Kaelholm's rolls make them nervous: a name in a roll is only useful if it stays attached to one face.

**Strengths and flaws.** Any face, any voice. Trusted by nobody, once found out.

**Culture.** Chosen family, private names, and a habit of keeping one face only for friends.

**Common names.** Ash, Cael, Ivy, Lark, Nim, Quill, Sev, Tam

**At the table.** Use the changeling rules.

## Deep Gnomes

*Small, stony people of the deep places, who trust rock more than anyone who lives above it.*

Deep gnomes live under the mountains of the Tyrant's Dominion, beneath the Anvil of Dominion, and in the old mines of the Hollow Marches. The Consortium hires them to find what's buried. They usually find more than they're paid for.

In Kaelmar deep gnomes live along the edges of the under-roads, and they're the only people who travel them by choice. They know the roads better than anyone, and won't go past certain doors.

**Strengths and flaws.** Hard to see and harder to surprise underground. Wary of the open sky and of strangers.

**Culture.** Small, careful clans, trading in stone and silence.

**Common names.** Beldin, Durra, Fenn, Isolde, Kurn, Murr, Senna, Thaelris

**At the table.** Use the deep gnome rules.

## Dragonborn

*Proud people with dragons' blood and dragons' breath, and the scales to prove it.*

Dragonborn clans live in the Tyrant's Dominion and the Radiant Ascendancy, where they're prized as soldiers and knights. Some count themselves among the Reptilians; most would rather you didn't. Their clan histories go back further than anyone's, and they don't agree with each other.

In Kaelmar dragonborn are mostly sailors and mercenaries off the Aetheris ships, and they turn heads in Kaelholm's streets.

**Strengths and flaws.** Strong, fierce, and a breath that can clear a room. Proud to a fault.

**Culture.** Clan honour above all. Every dragonborn knows their clan's name, and most have a story about it.

**Common names.** Arjhan, Balasar, Kava, Medrash, Nala, Rhogar, Sora, Torinn

**At the table.** Use the dragonborn rules.

## Duergar

*Grey dwarves of the deep forges, hard as their iron and twice as stubborn.*

Duergar hold the deepest forges under the Tyrant's Dominion, and the Legion's best weapons come out of their hands. They have little love for their surface cousins, and their records disagree with everyone's.

In Kaelmar duergar live under the northern highlands, below the dwarf holds, and say they were Kaelith's ironworkers. Nobody's checked.

**Strengths and flaws.** Tough, strong, and able to grow or vanish at will. Grim, and slow to forgive.

**Culture.** Forge-clans, hard bargains, and long grudges kept in iron ledgers.

**Common names.** Brakka, Duren, Gorm, Hilde, Korra, Morn, Thrain, Vessa

**At the table.** Use the duergar rules.

## Earth Genasi

*People with the earth in them: skin like stone, patience like a mountain, and a grip nobody breaks.*

Earth genasi are born in quarries, caves and battlefields, and Aetheris has plenty of all three. They're common in the Tyrant's Dominion, where the Legion's champions are as often stone as flesh, and in the Scar, where the ground itself is broken.

In Kaelmar earth genasi are born in the Eastern Mountains and under the highlands, and the Wrights hire them to move what nothing else can.

**Strengths and flaws.** Unshakeable and very hard to move. Slow to start, slower to stop.

**Culture.** Patient, practical, and not easily impressed.

**Common names.** Basalt, Dorra, Garnet, Hollis, Krag, Mica, Ronan, Slate

**At the table.** Use the earth genasi rules.

## Eladrin

*Elves whose moods turn with the seasons, and who sometimes forget which world they're standing in.*

Eladrin are rare in Aetheris and tend to turn up with no past, as if the Cycle forgot to finish them. They change with the seasons: warm in summer, grieving in autumn, cold in winter. Many have found a home with the Wildborn, and a few in stranger company.

In Kaelmar eladrin are almost unknown. The Spore Circle's eldest say the Bloom has a season they don't have a name for, and look at eladrin oddly.

**Strengths and flaws.** Fey-step and fey charm. Changeable, and hard for others to keep up with.

**Culture.** No homeland they can name. Many keep a token from each season of their lives.

**Common names.** Aerith, Faen, Ilsevel, Lirael, Naivara, Sylas, Thaelon, Vaelis

**At the table.** Use the eladrin rules.

## Fairies

*Tiny winged folk with old magic and a very strict sense of fairness.*

Fairies are found in the Verdant Rebirth's deepest woods and, more strangely, in the halls of the Eternal Veil, where they keep records nobody else can reach. Small, quick and impossible to bribe, they make excellent judges, which is why the Hollow Tribunal is rumoured to employ one.

In Kaelmar fairies live in the old woods of the Eastern Deepwood, and talk to the Bloom as if it were an old neighbour.

**Strengths and flaws.** Flight and fey magic. Tiny, and easy to underestimate, which they count on.

**Culture.** Bargains, promises, and an exact memory for both.

**Common names.** Bryony, Fenn, Ivy, Mote, Pip, Quell, Sorrel, Tink

**At the table.** Use the fairy rules.

## Firbolgs

*Gentle giants of the deep forests, who'd much rather hide than fight.*

Firbolgs live quietly in the Verdant Rebirth, hidden in the Green Expanse, and only come out when the forest needs them. They're patient, slow to speak and very hard to anger, which surprises people who haven't seen them angry.

In Kaelmar firbolgs live alongside the Bloomborn in the Deepwood, and people who can't tell them apart are corrected politely. Firbolgs were here before the Bloom. Some of them remember the Shrouding.

**Strengths and flaws.** Strong, gentle and hard to find. Shy of crowds and cities.

**Culture.** Forest clans, quiet stewardship, and the habit of leaving no trace.

**Common names.** Alder, Brennick, Dunna, Gethin, Hulda, Mossa, Oakes, Tull

**At the table.** Use the firbolg rules.

## Fire Genasi

*People with fire in their blood, warm to the touch and quick to burn.*

Fire genasi are born near volcanoes, forges and burning cities, and Aetheris has had a great many burning cities. They're common at the edge of the Ashen Wastes and the Tyrant's Dominion. The Ashen Covenant courts them. Most want nothing to do with it.

In Kaelmar fire genasi are born in the ash barrens, and the orc herders there count one in a family as good luck in winter.

**Strengths and flaws.** Fire magic, and never cold. Hot-tempered, and not always sorry.

**Culture.** Passionate, direct, and drawn to forges, kitchens and fights.

**Common names.** Ash, Cinder, Ember, Ignis, Kaela, Pyra, Sol, Yvara

**At the table.** Use the fire genasi rules.

## Githyanki

*Warriors from beyond the world, who came in through a crack in it and never went home.*

Githyanki say they came from the Astral Sea, through the Rift of Unmaking in the Fractured Nexus, and that their people are elsewhere. A few hundred live in Aetheris now, in war-bands that hire out to anyone. They have no love for the Githzerai, and say so.

In Kaelmar githyanki are almost unknown, and the few who've crossed say the Rift there feels like home.

**Strengths and flaws.** Fierce fighters with psionic gifts. Proud, cold and distrustful.

**Culture.** War-bands, silver swords, and a long, angry history nobody else knows.

**Common names.** Kaaltis, Lazhar, Marrak, Seraine, Tavra, Vaelith, Xarra, Zhessa

**At the table.** Use the githyanki rules.

## Githzerai

*Calm, disciplined people from beyond the world, who think very carefully before they act.*

Githzerai came into Aetheris through the same cracks as the githyanki and want nothing to do with them. They keep monasteries in the Echoing Halls of the Fractured Nexus and in quiet corners of every dominion, and they're much in demand as judges, tutors and bodyguards.

In Kaelmar githzerai are rare, and Kaelholm's archive-monks have welcomed the few who've come. They get on well.

**Strengths and flaws.** Discipline, focus and psionic gifts. Remote, and hard to know.

**Culture.** Monasteries, order, and the patient study of the mind.

**Common names.** Althros, Dak, Ilsa, Kalthis, Lirian, Mezzar, Vhalek, Zerra

**At the table.** Use the githzerai rules.

## Gnomes

*Small, clever and endlessly curious, with an answer for everything and a question for that.*

Gnomes in Aetheris are tinkerers and scholars, found in every Consortium tower and every market that sells something clever. They get on with everyone and trust nobody's maths but their own.

In Kaelmar gnomes are Kaelholm's glyph-wrights, and many of the oldest archive families are gnomes. They've kept the city's records since it was Kaelith-East.

**Strengths and flaws.** Quick minds and clever hands. Easily distracted, and very hard to stop talking.

**Culture.** Workshops, archives, and families that pass down a trade.

**Common names.** Bimble, Corin, Dabbin, Fennel, Nissa, Orryn, Tilda, Venn

**At the table.** Use the gnome rules.

## Goblins

*Small, quick and underestimated by everyone, which goblins find very useful.*

Goblins live in every city in Aetheris, mostly in the parts the rich don't visit. Many serve the Legion as scouts and sappers; many more run the markets, the sewers and the rumours. Most hobgoblins and bugbears have goblin cousins, and won't hear a word against them.

In Kaelmar goblins live in the fen towns and the Deepwood's edge, and they're the best boatmen on the southern rivers.

**Strengths and flaws.** Small, quick and nimble in a scrap. Looked down on, and quick to get even.

**Culture.** Big families, crowded homes, and a talent for finding what's been lost.

**Common names.** Grix, Kibba, Nott, Pikka, Rizz, Snik, Tazz, Yip

**At the table.** Use the goblin rules.

## Goliaths

*Mountain giants' kin, huge and competitive, who keep score of everything.*

Goliaths live in the high peaks of the Tyrant's Dominion and the Fractured Nexus, and they're welcome in every arena. They're often confused with the Taur'n, who have horns and are tired of the comparison. The Titans of the Rift are said to be goliaths of a kind nobody has seen for a long time.

In Kaelmar the Eastern Mountains are goliath country, and many of the Ascendancy's soldiers are goliaths who like being counted.

**Strengths and flaws.** Huge, strong and hard to knock down. Competitive about absolutely everything.

**Culture.** Mountain clans, fair contests, and a scorekeeper's memory.

**Common names.** Aukan, Eglath, Gae-Al, Kavaki, Manneo, Paavu, Thalai, Varuun

**At the table.** Use the goliath rules.

## Half-elves

*Children of two peoples, at home in both and wholly in neither.*

Half-elves are common in every city of Aetheris, and since the Cycle broke there are more of them every year, as five dominions' worth of people meet, mix and marry. They're diplomats, travellers, and the people everybody asks to settle an argument.

In Kaelmar half-elves are common in Ashvale and the hill villages, where families have mixed for generations and nobody thinks twice about it.

**Strengths and flaws.** Adaptable, charming and at home in any company. Often feel like outsiders anyway.

**Culture.** Whatever their families were, both of them.

**Common names.** Arin, Corwen, Elsa, Jory, Kiera, Lia, Seren, Tobin

**At the table.** Use the half-elf rules.

## Half-orcs

*Children of orcs and humans, with an orc's strength and a temper they keep on a short leash.*

Half-orcs grow up all over Aetheris, and the Legion recruits them hard. Plenty refuse. In Hollow Refuge and the Marches, half-orc families have run farms, inns and caravans since before the Cycle broke, and they're tired of people assuming otherwise.

In Kaelmar half-orcs are common in the ash barrens and the towns nearby, where orc and human families have mixed for centuries.

**Strengths and flaws.** Strong, tough and hard to put down. Judged on sight, everywhere.

**Culture.** Whichever family raised them, and fiercely loyal to it.

**Common names.** Dench, Holg, Krusk, Mira, Rosk, Shara, Thokk, Yevelda

**At the table.** Use the half-orc rules.

## Halflings

*Small, warm and very lucky, and found anywhere there's a good kitchen.*

Halflings live in every corner of Aetheris, mostly in the places nobody notices: farms, kitchens, back rooms and archives. They get on with everyone, and they remember everyone's name, which matters more than people think in a world that forgets.

In Kaelmar halflings are everywhere in the hills around Ashvale, and some of its oldest families are halflings who married into human ones, and the other way round.

**Strengths and flaws.** Lucky, brave and hard to frighten. Small, and overlooked.

**Culture.** Big families, good food, and long memories for kindness and slights alike.

**Common names.** Bree, Cora, Dell, Lark, Merric, Ondine, Pip, Wilma

**At the table.** Use the halfling rules.

## Harengon

*Quick, twitchy rabbit-folk, born lucky and usually already leaving.*

Harengon are common in the green country of the Verdant Rebirth and the open Marches, and they travel more than anyone. Every dominion has harengon couriers and scouts. A surprising number end up as mages; they say it's because they read fast.

In Kaelmar harengon are one of the peoples the spore-storms rewrote, and they live in the open Bloom country, close to the ground and quick to leave.

**Strengths and flaws.** Quick, lucky and always ready to jump. Restless, and easily bored.

**Culture.** Large, scattered families, and a habit of never staying anywhere too long.

**Common names.** Bramble, Clover, Dash, Fern, Hopper, Juniper, Celith, Tansy

**At the table.** Use the harengon rules.

## Hobgoblins

*Disciplined, ambitious goblin-kin, who keep order and expect it.*

Hobgoblins built much of the Crimson Legion and still run a great deal of it, including the Warlord's Hall. They value discipline, rank and honour, and they're as likely to be magistrates or merchants as soldiers.

In Kaelmar hobgoblins command mercenary companies in the eastern mountains, and the Ascendancy's marshal is one of them.

**Strengths and flaws.** Disciplined, tough and good at helping allies. Proud, and unforgiving of weakness.

**Culture.** Rank, honour, and a family's place earned and kept.

**Common names.** Darion, Garron, Kesh, Mara, Orana, Ruk, Tsevan, Vrask

**At the table.** Use the hobgoblin rules.

## Kenku

*Crow-folk who speak in borrowed sounds, and remember every one they've heard.*

Kenku live along the dusk coast of the Eternal Veil and in the crowded cities of every dominion, where they work as messengers, spies and mimics. They speak in borrowed voices and sounds, and they never forget one.

In Kaelmar kenku are another people the spore-storms rewrote, and they roost in the towers of Kaelholm, where the archives are full of sounds to collect.

**Strengths and flaws.** Perfect mimics with perfect memories. Find it hard to say anything new.

**Culture.** Flocks, borrowed voices, and a collector's love of sounds.

**Common names.** Bellchime, Creak, Ink, Marrow, Rattle, Scratch, Tidewash, Whistle

**At the table.** Use the kenku rules.

## Kobolds

*Small, clever trap-makers, devoted to each other and to dragons nobody else can see.*

Kobolds live in warrens under the Tyrant's Dominion and the Anvil of Dominion, and they build the best traps and siege-works in Aetheris. Everyone underestimates them. That's the point.

In Kaelmar kobolds live in the under-roads, where they keep strange company and stranger habits. Some of them have been down there a very long time.

**Strengths and flaws.** Clever, quick and very good at working together. Small, and easily bullied, until the traps go off.

**Culture.** Warrens, clever works, and complete devotion to their own.

**Common names.** Grik, Kib, Meepo, Nib, Sniv, Tix, Vexis, Zek

**At the table.** Use the kobold rules.

## Satyrs

*Goat-legged revellers with a song for every occasion and a joke at the worst time.*

Satyrs live in the Verdant Rebirth and wherever there's a market, a festival or a party. They make excellent merchants, musicians and liars, often all at once. The Market of Lost Things in Hollow Refuge has always had a satyr trader or two.

In Kaelmar satyrs are another people the spore-storms rewrote, and they wander the hill villages from fair to fair.

**Strengths and flaws.** Charming, quick and hard to charm in turn. Unreliable, and proud of it.

**Culture.** Music, wine, trade, and making every day a little bit of a festival.

**Common names.** Ambrose, Dorran, Fawn, Illa, Lysander, Pell, Thyme, Vine

**At the table.** Use the satyr rules.

## Sea Elves

*Elves of the deep water, who came up from under the sea when the sea god died.*

Sea elves live off the dusk coast of the Eternal Veil and in the deep water beyond. They say they came up when Maruun fell, and that the sea has been emptier ever since. Many have no love for the Drowned Saints. A few have joined them.

In Kaelmar sea elves are rare, and the few who live along the east coast near Kaelholm say they won't swim under the Black Tides.

**Strengths and flaws.** At home in deep water and able to talk to its creatures. Uneasy on dry land for long.

**Culture.** Undersea clans, old songs of the sea god, and a long grief.

**Common names.** Coral, Isra, Kael, Maren, Nerith, Sirala, Tethys, Ulmo

**At the table.** Use the sea elf rules.

## Shifters

*People with a beast in their blood, who let it out when it matters.*

Shifters live in the Verdant Rebirth, and the Wildborn Clans count many of their best hunters among them. Plenty of shifters serve the Legion too, including some of its generals. They take after a beast: wolf, bear, cat or worse.

In Kaelmar shifters are one of the peoples the spore-storms rewrote, and the Bloom-marked ones say they can feel it when they shift.

**Strengths and flaws.** Fierce, fast and hard to put down when shifted. Wild, and slow to settle.

**Culture.** Packs, hunts, and a fierce love for family.

**Common names.** Brynn, Fang, Grey, Kirra, Orana, Rook, Tavi, Wolfe

**At the table.** Use the shifter rules.

## Tabaxi

*Curious cat-folk who travel for stories and keep the best ones to themselves.*

Tabaxi wander out of the Green Expanse of the Verdant Rebirth and roam all five dominions, collecting stories, treasures and secrets. The Shadow Pact is very fond of them.

In Kaelmar tabaxi are another people the spore-storms rewrote, and they live in the Deepwood and the river country, where they trade in curiosities.

**Strengths and flaws.** Quick, agile and impossible to keep out of anything. Easily distracted by something new.

**Culture.** Stories, travel, and the joy of finding out.

**Common names.** Cloud, Ember-Eyes, Five Dawns, Moss-in-Rain, Selene, Smoke, Thorn, Whisper

**At the table.** Use the tabaxi rules.

## Tieflings

*People with a mark of the fiendish in their blood, judged for it from birth.*

Tieflings are born all over Aetheris, more often in Noctis than anywhere else, and they're distrusted everywhere except there. Many go into magic, law or crime, as the three professions where nobody asks where you came from. Each says theirs is the honest one.

In Kaelmar tieflings are rare, and Kaelholm's tribunals have a bad record of burning their names.

**Strengths and flaws.** Fiendish magic and a resistance to fire. Distrusted on sight.

**Culture.** Whatever family raised them, and a sharp sense of fairness.

**Common names.** Akmen, Damakos, Kallista, Lerissa, Mordai, Nemeia, Orianna, Talen

**At the table.** Use the tiefling rules.

## Tortles

*Patient, wandering turtle-folk, who carry their homes on their backs and see no reason to hurry.*

Tortles live along the coasts and rivers of the Eternal Veil and the Verdant Rebirth, and many make a pilgrimage across all five dominions before they settle. Some ride the wrecks out to sea and come back with stories.

In Kaelmar tortles live in the southern fens and the river country, where they trade along the waterways. They're as patient as the lizardfolk and much better company.

**Strengths and flaws.** Hard-shelled and very hard to hurt. Slow to hurry, and slower to change their minds.

**Culture.** Long wanderings, few possessions, and a love of quiet.

**Common names.** Drennal, Gura, Jappa, Krull, Nulka, Odran, Tibor, Xopa

**At the table.** Use the tortle rules.

## Tritons

*Guardians of the deep, who came up to the surface with a sense of duty and a lot of opinions.*

Tritons come from undersea kingdoms off the dusk coast of the Eternal Veil, and they surfaced, they say, to keep watch over a world that's lost its sea god. They're proud, brave, and convinced they know best.

In Kaelmar tritons are rare. The few who have come say there's something in the deep under the Black Tides they were sent to watch.

**Strengths and flaws.** Strong swimmers with water magic. Proud, and often out of their depth on land.

**Culture.** Duty, rank, and the long watch of the deep.

**Common names.** Corrin, Iskar, Jorvas, Lhossa, Marnis, Nalla, Torven, Vodos

**At the table.** Use the triton rules.

## Warforged

*Made people of iron and old work, who woke up somewhere they weren't built for.*

Warforged in Aetheris are leftovers of worlds that don't exist any more. When the Cycle broke, a few dozen of them were found standing on battlefields and in ruins, built for wars nobody remembers, and they've had to work out who to be since. Some serve the factions. Most just want to know who made them.

In Kaelmar everyone takes the warforged for Kaelith's iron vessels, and Kaelholm would very much like to examine one. They aren't vessels. Nobody can say what they are instead.

**Strengths and flaws.** Tireless and hard to hurt. Uncertain of their own past, and other people's reactions.

**Culture.** No culture but what they've chosen, and a careful way with words.

**Common names.** Anvil, Bastion, Echo, Lantern, Sentinel, Seraphis, Tally, Vessel

**At the table.** Use the warforged rules.

## Water Genasi

*People with the sea in their blood, calm as still water until they aren't.*

Water genasi are born near the sea, and since Maruun fell, more are born each year along the dusk coast and on the Black Tides themselves. Sailors count one in a crew as a blessing. The Drowned Saints count them as proof.

In Kaelmar water genasi are born along the southern fens and lakes, and the lizardfolk of the fens treat them as kin.

**Strengths and flaws.** At home in the water, and hard to drown. Moody as the tide.

**Culture.** Coast and river folk, sailors, and a deep pull towards the sea.

**Common names.** Althisa, Brine, Coral, Ilythis, Marin, Nerys, Pearl, Tide

**At the table.** Use the water genasi rules.

## Yuan-ti

*Serpent-folk with cold blood, sharp minds and a reputation they've mostly earned.*

Yuan-ti live along the dusk coast of the Eternal Veil and in the old ruins of the Radiant Ascendancy, and they're distrusted almost everywhere. Some deserve it. Most are just very good at business, which isn't the same thing.

In Kaelmar yuan-ti are almost unknown, and the few who have crossed keep their distance from the lizardfolk of the fens, who don't like them.

**Strengths and flaws.** Resistant to poison and magic. Cold, and hard to trust.

**Culture.** Old families, older grudges, and a long view of everything.

**Common names.** Asha, Riven, Selka, Ssera, Tazzen, Vashti, Xaliss, Zehra

**At the table.** Use the yuan-ti rules.

## Shadowkin

*Born from an erased past, living between light and dark.*

Shadowkin are born from an erased past and live between light and dark. They are masters of stealth, and they are distrusted everywhere. They value hidden identity, chosen family and performance. Some Shadowkin families say their ancestors came from places that no longer exist.

In Kaelmar there are almost no Shadowkin, and the few who've crossed the Black Tides say the dark there feels different: older, and listening.

**Strengths and flaws.** Masters of stealth. Distrusted everywhere.

**Common names.** Ash, Eris, Kell, Nira, Orren, Seth, Vey, Zara

**At the table.** Use the shadar-kai rules, or elf rules with the drow lineage.

## Taur'n

*Horned nomads seven to nine feet tall, honoured by the Wildborn and feared by the Legion.*

The Taur'n are horned nomads, seven to nine feet tall, with runes marked into their horns. They are warriors and storm-calling shamans. They value migration, family oaths, craftsmanship and spiritual memory. Many live in cities, and assuming they are all Wildborn annoys them.

In Kaelmar the Taur'n are known only from sailors' stories, and the first few to cross the Black Tides have been mistaken for minotaurs and spore-rewritten folk alike. They're not pleased.

**Strengths and flaws.** Warriors and storm-callers. Honoured by the Wildborn, feared in the Legion.

**Common names.** Arrok, Bera, Dorran, Kala, Mhor, Sava, Torren, Yara

**At the table.** Use the minotaur rules, or goliath rules with horns.

## Moonborn

*Wildborn druids who step between the physical and spirit worlds. Perhaps only one is left.*

The Moonborn were Wildborn druids who could step between the physical world and the spirit world. They were always few. Faelyn Moondancer is said to be the last of them.

Nobody in Kaelmar has ever seen a Moonborn. The Spore Circle's eldest say the Bloom has a spirit-world of its own, and that a Moonborn might be able to walk in it.

**At the table.** Play a Moonborn as an elf with the Fey Touched feat.
