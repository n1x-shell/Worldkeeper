# Player Adventure Book: Cities (DRAFT)

Drafted for review. Nothing here is published until this file is moved up into assets/player-text/.

## How to use this

The great cities, in the same layout as Hollow Refuge and Ashvale: **Read aloud**, **What you know**, **Places in town**, **Who you'll meet**, **Getting around** and **What people say**.

## Kaelholm (Settlement)

*The grey city of archives and tribunals, Kaelmar's largest, where the ships from Aetheris come in.*

> Grey stone, grey robes, grey sea. The biggest city on the continent climbs up from the harbour in terraces of archives and monasteries, every window lit with somebody reading. Clerks meet every ship at the dock with their ledgers open. Somewhere above, a bell rings, and a name gets struck from the rolls.

**What you know**

- Kaelholm is the largest city on Kaelmar and the first port for ships from Aetheris. It used to be called Kaelith-East.
- It kept Kaelith's name, archives, scholar corps and tribunals. Its elders wear grey.
- It is the strongest power on the continent. It wants every old vault opened and catalogued, and the change turned back so the world goes back "into its drawers".
- Its gnomes are the city's scribes and glyph-wrights.
- Its tribunals can punish someone by scraping their name from every roll. People call it being "burned".
- Glyph-cutting into living flesh is forbidden here.

**Places in town**

| Place | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The Grey Quay | The harbour. Every ship is met, searched and written down. Sailors and smugglers argue here in Tidecant. |
| The Tribunal Halls | Where the elders in grey sit in judgement. Open to the public, and always full. |
| The House of Rolls | Where every name in Kaelholm is written. A burned name is scraped out here, in front of witnesses. |
| The Scholar Corps | The college that trains Kaelholm's scholars and sends out its expeditions. Always hiring guards. |
| Glyph Row | The gnomes' street of scribes and glyph-wrights. Signs glow faintly after dark. |
| The Monasteries | Quiet houses of copying and study up on the terraces. Some take in travellers for work. |
| The Lantern Market | Night market by the docks: Aetherian goods off the ships, salt from the south, and things nobody should be selling. |
| The Drowned Bell | Dockside inn favoured by sailors from the Black Tides crossing. |

**Who you'll meet**

- @[Archivist-Elder Venn Tull], who heads one of the tribunals.
- Scholars of the Scholar Corps, hiring anyone who can fight.
- Sailors off the Aetheris ships, speaking Tidecant.

**Getting around**

| From Kaelholm to | Time | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Eternal Veil's ports, in Aetheris | About two weeks by ship | Across the Black Tides, in the dark. Regular crossings have only just begun. |
| The Eastern Mountains | West, a long road inland | Goliath country, and the Ascendancy's. |
| Ashvale and the Rift | About 1,500 km west | Right across the continent. |

**What people say**

- Venn Tull wants a living Kaelith guardian, and will pay anything to get one.
- The Eastern Tendril of the Spore Circle burns Kaelith work on sight, and Kaelholm's expeditions keep running into them.
- Kaelholm wants to be first to examine anything old that comes off the ships from Aetheris.
- Once you are burned, nobody in the city will say your name aloud.
- The House of Rolls has a second set of rolls, and nobody admits to keeping them.

## Empyrean Spire

*The floating city of white stone where the Celestial Order rules from the Throne of Light.*

> The city hangs in the sky with nothing holding it up. White towers, white walls, white bridges, all of it so bright at noon that pilgrims weep and blame the light. Platforms rise from the plain on chains of gold, loaded with petitioners, soldiers and carts of wheat. At the very top a single spire catches the sun before anything else in the world, and there are no shadows anywhere.

**What you know**

- The capital of the Radiant Ascendancy, and the seat of the Celestial Order.
- The High Theurgists rule from the Throne of Light.
- The Order's chief fortress, the Golden Aerie, stands nearby.
- Order law holds everywhere in the city. Undead, demons and forbidden magic are hunted.

**Places in town**

| Place | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The Throne of Light | Where the High Theurgists rule. Petitioners wait days for an audience. |
| The Dawn Lifts | Platforms drawn up from the plain on golden chains. The only way up for most. |
| The Pilgrims' Ward | The lowest terraces, where outsiders stay. Every visitor's name goes into the Pilgrims' Book. |
| The Hall of Mercy | The Order's great infirmary. Its missionary healers train here. |
| Solenne's Dawn Temple | The great temple of the Dawn Mother: light, law and mercy. |
| The Sunward Rest | The one inn in the Pilgrims' Ward that serves anyone, and asks no questions out loud. |
| The Golden Aerie | The Order's chief fortress, on a crag near the city. Radiant knights train there. |

**Who you'll meet**

- @[Highlord Theos Valcairn] and @[Grand Theurgist Selara Dawnveil], at the Throne of Light.
- @[Inquisitor Malrik Vael], now and then. Nobody is ever glad to see him.
- Pilgrims from every dominion, and Order knights of every people.

**Getting around**

| From Empyrean Spire to | Time | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hollow Refuge | About two weeks | A week to the border, a week across the Radiant Expanse. |
| The Luminara Gate | North, across the golden plains | Pilgrims walk it. |

**What people say**

- The Inquisition reads the Pilgrims' Book every night.
- There are no shadows at noon in the upper city, and the Order likes it that way.
- Not everyone who falls from the Spire was pushed. Most were.

## Varkhaal

*The Crimson Legion's Iron Citadel, a city of fortresses built around the Great Arena.*

> You hear Varkhaal before you see it: a roar that rises and breaks and rises again from the Great Arena. The city is all iron and red stone, every house a little fortress, every crossroads a duelling ring. Smoke climbs out of the mountainsides around it, where the forges never stop. Soldiers of every people walk the streets in Legion red, and all of them are armed.

**What you know**

- The capital of the Tyrant's Dominion, and the seat of the Crimson Legion. Called the Iron Citadel.
- The warlords meet in the Warlord's Hall. The Legion's chief fortress, the Red Bastion, stands nearby.
- Every dispute is settled by a duel. Anyone can challenge anyone.
- The Great Arena holds a tournament every year, open to anyone. The prize is a warlord's favour.

**Places in town**

| Place | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The Great Arena | The heart of the city. Fights every day, and the yearly tournament. |
| The Warlord's Hall | Where the warlords meet, and where the Warlord sometimes sits. |
| The Pits | The gladiator pits under the arena, where fighters are made and broken. |
| The Oath-Stones | Karrath's shrine, where oaths are sworn on stone and kept on pain of death. |
| Smiths' Row | Forges cut into the mountainside. The Anvil of Dominion lies further down. |
| The Broken Shield | A soldiers' tavern. A fight a night, and no hard feelings after. |
| The Red Bastion | The Legion's chief fortress, a short march from the city walls. |

**Who you'll meet**

- @[Warlord Garron Kael], when he is not at war, and his heir @[Darion Kael].
- @[General Orana Volkash], @[Champion Valrik the Undying], @[Korga Bloodfang] and @[Velkana Stormclad], usually at the arena.
- Fighters from everywhere, come for the tournament.

**Getting around**

| From Varkhaal to | Time | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hollow Refuge | About two weeks | A week to the border at the Bloodfields, a week through the iron mountains. |
| The Anvil of Dominion | Under the mountains nearby | Forge-roads, hot and crowded. |

**What people say**

- Anyone can challenge anyone in Varkhaal. Most people only do it once.
- Darion Kael holds quiet dinners with foreigners.
- The tournament draws fighters from every dominion, and some of them are spies.

## Zepharyos

*The Arcane Consortium's City of the Unbound, drifting among the clouds.*

> The city is not on the ground. It is islands of rock and tower drifting in the clouds, joined by bridges that are not always there. Some streets run sideways. One district is upside down, and the people in it seem not to mind. Lightning flickers between the spires and nobody looks up. The air tastes of metal and burnt paper.

**What you know**

- The capital of the Fractured Nexus, and the seat of the Arcane Consortium. Called the City of the Unbound.
- The council sits in the Nexus Spire. The Consortium's chief fortress, the Nexus Collegium, stands nearby.
- Magic here is limitless and unstable. Floating islands and shifting time are normal.

**Places in town**

| Place | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The Nexus Spire | Where the Consortium's council sits. |
| The Nexus Collegium | The Consortium's chief fortress and greatest school. |
| The Drifting Quarters | Districts on floating islands that rearrange every few nights. Maps sell out daily. |
| The Skybridges | Bridges of light between the islands. Mostly reliable. |
| The Prism Market | Spell components, wands and things with no names, sold by the ounce. |
| Ithiel's Observatory | Temple of the Many-Eyed: knowledge, magic and the stars. |
| The Anchor | The one inn that never moves. Nobody knows how. |

**Who you'll meet**

- @[Archmagister Veylan Orithis], at the Nexus Spire or the Collegium.
- @[Archivist Lissara Wynne], @[Magister Celith Varis], @[Sorcerer-King Jorvas Myrr], @[Pyromancer Elaris Duskbane] and @[Master Talen Voros].
- Students from every people and every dominion.

**Getting around**

| From Zepharyos to | Time | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hollow Refuge | About two weeks | A week to the border, a week through storm country. |
| The Echoing Halls and the Rift of Unmaking | Elsewhere in the Fractured Nexus | Neither is anywhere you want to be. |

**What people say**

- Half of Zepharyos floats because of something the Archmagister did by accident.
- If your island moves while you sleep, you pay rent in the new district.
- Some of the bridges lead somewhere other than the next island.

## Noctis

*The Shadow Pact's City of Masks, in endless dusk.*

> The sun never quite rises over Noctis, and never quite sets. Everything is purple and grey. Everyone is masked: porcelain, leather, lace, plain black cloth. Streets that led one way this morning lead somewhere else tonight. Music drifts from a hundred doorways. Nobody here uses their real name twice, and nobody asks you for yours.

**What you know**

- The capital of the Eternal Veil, and the seat of the Shadow Pact. Called the City of Masks.
- The rulers meet in the Midnight Court. The Pact's chief fortress, Nightglass, stands nearby.
- Masks are worn everywhere, by everyone. Going unmasked is rude, and unwise.

**Places in town**

| Place | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The Midnight Court | Where the Pact's rulers meet, and its singers perform. |
| Nightglass | The Pact's chief fortress, just outside the city. Its magistrates judge traitors. |
| The Mask Market | Masks sold, borrowed, stolen and inherited. Some come with a name attached. |
| Ellun's Hidden Temple | Temple of the Maskwright: secrets, night and freedom. Its door is never in the same place. |
| The Quiet Houses | Where secrets are bought and sold, by the whisper. |
| The Veiled Lantern | An inn where nobody sees anybody. Very popular. |

**Who you'll meet**

- @[Grandmaster Lysander Morrow], if he chooses to be met.
- @[Ilsevre the Whispering Veil], singing at the Midnight Court.
- @[Magistrate Renn Varros], at Nightglass.
- Merchants and smugglers from both continents, since the ships began crossing.

**Getting around**

| From Noctis to | Time | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hollow Refuge | About two weeks | A week to the border, a week through the dusk. |
| The dusk coast and the Black Tides | West | Ships cross to Kaelholm in about two weeks. |
| The Umbral Paths | Everywhere and nowhere | Shifting roads that lead anywhere. |

**What people say**

- Every mask in the market has been worn before.
- The Grandmaster has been seen in two places on the same night.
- Ask for the same street twice and you will get two different directions, both correct.

## The Rootspire

*The Wildborn Clans' colossal tree-city, grown over the bones of a ruined capital.*

> The tree is the size of a mountain. Its roots have swallowed a whole city, and you can still see the old streets down there, stone arches and towers lifted into the air in the grip of living wood. Houses hang from the branches like fruit. Rope bridges sway between them. Wolves pad along the lower roots, and somewhere far above, a horn sounds.

**What you know**

- The capital of the Verdant Rebirth, and the home of the Wildborn Clans. A colossal tree-city over a ruined capital.
- The elders meet at the Den of Elders. The Clans' chief fortress, the Verdant Crown, stands nearby.
- There are no roads in the Verdant Rebirth. The way here is through the forest.
- Outsiders are welcome if they behave.

**Places in town**

| Place | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The Den of Elders | Where the clan elders meet, in a hollow deep in the trunk. |
| The Root Halls | The old capital's streets and towers, held up inside the roots. Some of it is still lived in. |
| The Hanging Market | Stalls slung from the branches: furs, herbs, carvings, and very fresh meat. |
| Orrowen's Grove | Shrine of the Green Antler: the wild, the seasons and beasts. |
| The Wolf Steps | The lower roots, where the packs den. Visitors walk quickly. |
| The Verdant Crown | The Clans' chief fortress, a short climb away. |

**Who you'll meet**

- @[High Shaman Raethan "Stormcaller"] and @[Elder Myrris Thornveil], at the Den of Elders.
- @[Brynn Wolfsong], with the wolves.
- @[Faelyn Moondancer] and @[Ogrim Ironhide], when they are home.

**Getting around**

| From the Rootspire to | Time | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hollow Refuge | About two weeks | A week to the border, a week through roadless forest. |
| Fangholt and the Ravaged Pass | Elsewhere in the Verdant Rebirth | A behemoth's skeleton, and a haunted canyon. |

**What people say**

- The tree is still growing, and is slowly lifting the old city's bones into the air.
- Leave your axe at the roots, or the tree will remember.
- The old capital's king is buried somewhere in the Root Halls, and the roots never grew near him.
