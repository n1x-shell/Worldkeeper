# Families and houses

## How to use this

The families that matter on both continents. Each section becomes that family's page in the handbook.

## House Kael

*The hobgoblin warlords of Varkhaal.*

The hobgoblin warlords of Varkhaal. House Kael has held the Warlord's Hall since the Border Years, by winning every challenge for it, and the Warlord wears the proof: a mouthful of gold teeth, each one taken from a rival who lost.

**Who's who**

- @[Warlord Garron Kael|NPC-024], head of the house
- @[Darion Kael|NPC-033], his son and heir

The Kaels count their history in fights won. The house has no lands of its own, only the Legion, and every Kael is expected to earn a place in it on the sand of the Great Arena.

**What people say**

- Never ask a Kael how many teeth are left to win.
- The Warlord's son writes poetry. The Warlord pretends not to know.

## The Marrows of the Dusk Coast

*Not a family by blood.*

Not a family by blood. On the Eternal Veil's dusk coast, children left at Sephane's shrines are taken in by her carvers and given the name Marrow, because, the carvers say, a name has to go all the way to the bone. A kenku and a halfling grew up in the same foundling house and still sign their letters "your sibling".

**Who's who**

- @[Elder Marrow "the Writeless Prophet"|NPC-091], raised in the foundling house
- @[Scribe-Mother Ondine Marrow|NPC-094], raised in the foundling house

Marrows turn up everywhere: priests, clerks, sailors, a prophet who never speaks. Ask one about the others and they'll know at least one name.

**What people say**

- Every Marrow knows every other Marrow's name. It's the one thing they're taught first.
- There's a Marrow across the Black Tides, in the fens of Kaelmar. Nobody on the dusk coast can say how.

## The Hesks of Ashvale

*The Hesks have sung the keeping song at Ashvale's stones for as long as anyone has kept count, and they claim it was a Hesk who stood beside the woman with burned hands when Ashvale was founded.*

The Hesks have sung the keeping song at Ashvale's stones for as long as anyone has kept count, and they claim it was a Hesk who stood beside the woman with burned hands when Ashvale was founded. Orla is Ritekeeper, as her mother was. Her grandson Jory, half-elf on his father's side, teaches the song to every child in town.

**Who's who**

- @[Ritekeeper Orla Hesk|NPC-304], the matriarch
- @[Keeper Jory Hesk|NPC-341], her grandson

A Hesk is expected to be at every singing, sick or well, and to know every verse. They are, and they do.

**What people say**

- There's always a Hesk at the stones.
- Jory has his grandmother's eyes, and none of her silence.

## The Lins of Greyfold

*A big, loud hill family from Greyfold, spread across three villages and two peoples.*

A big, loud hill family from Greyfold, spread across three villages and two peoples. The Lins have married humans and halflings for generations, and nobody keeps track of which cousin is which, except at weddings. They've spoken to the Bloom since the first years after the Shrouding; the copper marks run in the family.

**Who's who**

- @[Master Speaker Tevan Lin|NPC-305], the eldest of his generation
- @[Lark Lin|NPC-342], his cousin, from the halfling side

A Lin table always has room for one more. The Lins mark every child's first communion with a feast.

**What people say**

- If you need help in Greyfold, ask a Lin. If you need it kept quiet, don't.
- The Bloom answers the Lins. Some say it answers them first.

## The Halvors of Haskett's Mill

*Millwrights from Haskett's Mill, who've kept the river wheels turning for five generations.*

Millwrights from Haskett's Mill, who've kept the river wheels turning for five generations. Brenna and Edda left the mill for Ashvale and the Wrights, and their parents haven't forgiven them, though they ask about the walkers in every letter.

**Who's who**

- @[Chief Wright Brenna Halvor|NPC-306], the elder sister
- @[Edda Halvor|NPC-344], the younger sister

The Halvors can fix anything, and they'll tell you so.

**What people say**

- Every wheel on the river road was built or mended by a Halvor.
- Edda's walker follows her home. It's been seen doing it alone.

## The Tulls of Kaelholm

*An old family of gnome glyph-wrights who have kept Kaelholm's archives for longer than the city has had its name.*

An old family of gnome glyph-wrights who have kept Kaelholm's archives for longer than the city has had its name. A Tull has sat on one of the city's tribunals for most of its history, and a Tull's name has never once been burned from the rolls.

**Who's who**

- @[Archivist-Elder Venn Tull|NPC-308], head of the family
- @[Corin Tull|NPC-321], her nephew

Tulls are trained to the archive from childhood. Most stay there. Corin didn't.

**What people say**

- A Tull never forgets a name. That's what worries people.
- Corin Tull has opened more Kaelith doors than anyone alive. He'd like to open one more.

## The Granes of the Ash Barrens

*An orc family of herders from the ash barrens, who had never been anyone in particular until Maro climbed down into the Rift.*

An orc family of herders from the ash barrens, who had never been anyone in particular until Maro climbed down into the Rift. Now the first Riftwalker and the eldest of the cycles are brother and sister, and the Convergence Collective is, at its heart, a family argument.

**Who's who**

- @[Maro Grane|NPC-310], the younger brother
- @[Elder Saba Grane|NPC-333], the elder sister

The Granes read the Root Texts aloud at every meal, and argue about them after.

**What people say**

- The Collective is two orcs and everyone who agrees with one of them.
- Saba is the only one who can make Maro stop writing.
## The Vale Family

*A minor house of Ashen Spire that scattered when the Cycle broke.*

The Vales were nobody much, a minor house of the old city at Ashen Spire, until the Cycle broke and the family broke with it. Now four Vales serve four masters, and each faction believes it has the only Vale worth having.

**Who's who**

- @[Brother Altharion Vale|NPC-020], of the Celestial Order
- @[Sister Elyra Vale|NPC-006], of the Celestial Order
- @[Professor Orin Vale|NPC-007], of the Arcane Consortium
- @[Tobias Vale|NPC-051], of the Shadow Pact

They still write to each other. Nobody else is allowed to read the letters.

**What people say**

- Altharion prays for the other three. They don't pray for him.
- Put any two Vales in a room and you'll learn something. Put all four in and someone will leave early.
