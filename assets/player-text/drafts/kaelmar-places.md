# Player Adventure Book: Kaelmar's old places

## How to use this

Kaelmar's old places that people know about, and the thirteen sealed vaults as Kaelholm catalogues them. What's inside stays with the GM.

## The Rite Circle

*Thirteen leaning stones in the heart of Ashvale, older than Kaelith, where the keeping song is sung.*

Thirteen stones lean together in the middle of @[Ashvale], older than Kaelith's cities, older than anything anyone can date. No frost falls inside the ring, even at Evernight. Ashvale was founded here in 2 AB by a woman with burned hands, who taught the first keeping song, and the @[Boundary Keepers] have sung it at the stones ever since.

**What people say**

- Nobody sits on the stones. Nobody can say why, but nobody does.
- Once, in 291 AB, the stones listened. Everyone there felt it.
- The keeping song has more verses than anyone sings.

## The Memory-House

*A sphere hanging in a root-lit canyon in the south-east, which trades knowledge for memories.*

Far to the south-east, near the foot of the eastern mountains, a sphere the size of a chapel hangs in a canyon lit by glowing roots. People who've found it say it's full of minds, centuries of them, and that it will tell you almost anything, for a price: not the memory itself, but how you saw it.

A small figure tends its roots and explains the terms. Most people take the deal.

**What people say**

- It's never lied. It's never told anyone everything, either.
- People who trade with it too often come back a little thinner.
- Whoever tends it has been there a very long time.

## Ashvale Waygate

*A ruined under-road station under Ashvale's west road, picked over by salvagers.*

Under Ashvale's west road, a stair goes down to a ruined halt of the under-roads, on the old line to somewhere east. Rune-sleds used to stop here. Now it's salvage country: the Wrights pay well for anything that still works, and @[Brenna Halvor|NPC-306] pays best of all.

**What people say**

- Something down there still hums.
- The Boundary Keepers don't like the Wrights digging so close to the stones.
- The old line runs east, all the way to the mountains. Nobody's walked it.

## The Dead Zone

*A stretch of crimson and black-crystal Bloom over a broken Kaelith forge, where the air burns.*

Somewhere in the Eastern Deepwood, the Bloom turns crimson and black and grows in crystals over the ruin of a Kaelith forge. The burn in the air comes and goes. Travellers who need to cross it wear warded plates and carry adaptive spores, and they don't stop.

**What people say**

- The burn is worst at dawn.
- The forge under it is split down the middle. Something came out of it, or tried to.
- Scholar Corps expeditions go round it. The long way.

## Root Crossroads and the Rot

*Where the Bloom's roots meet, and can carry a traveller a hundred miles in minutes, for a price.*

Where the Bloom's roots cross, they can carry travellers through the between, from one crossroads to another the roots know, in minutes instead of days. Everyone who rides a root-road comes back a little less themselves, unless someone holds their name for them. Near the crossroads, the Bloom's gut breaks down whatever falls in, and builds with it.

**What people say**

- Ride with someone who knows your name.
- The rot near the crossroads is building something. Nobody knows what.

## The Thirteen Vaults

*The sealed vaults of Kaelith, joined by the under-roads, which nobody has opened since the Shrouding.*

Kaelith built thirteen vaults, joined by the under-roads, for the Great Work that ended in the Shrouding. Every one of them is sealed. @[Kaelholm|FAC-301] has spent three centuries cataloguing them from the outside, and its scholars agree on twelve names. The thirteenth vault is in the old records, but nobody has ever found it.

The vaults don't open to force. Kaelholm's scholars say each one asks for something, and that the asking is the lock. What each one asks, nobody has worked out.

**What people say**

- A Kaelholm crew went into Arborhold in 22 AB, and never came out.
- Every vault door hums a note before it refuses you.
- Kaelholm wants every vault opened and catalogued. The Spore Circle wants every one burned shut.

## Arborhold

*The vault in the Eastern Deepwood where a Kaelholm crew vanished in 22 AB.*

Arborhold lies deep in the Eastern Deepwood, behind a rune gate and a marker stone, at the foot of a stair of 273 steps. A Kaelholm repair crew went in in 22 AB and never came out. Nobody has passed the gate since. @[Venn Tull|NPC-308] would very much like someone to.

**What people say**

- The crew's names are still on Kaelholm's rolls. All but one.
- Something sleeps inside. Kaelholm wants it awake.

## Cradlehorn

*A vault in the eastern mountains, where Kaelith grew its iron vessels.*

Somewhere in the eastern mountains, Kaelith grew bodies: iron vessels for minds that never came. Kaelholm's catalogue calls it the vault of vessels. Nobody has found its door.

**What people say**

- The vessels are still in there, asleep.
- Mercenaries in the eastern mountains have been asking about it.

## Mirewell

*A vault in the southern fens, which asks for a dream.*

Mirewell lies under the southern fens. The fen folk say people who sleep near it dream someone else's dreams.

**What people say**

- The Convergence Collective wants to read the Root Texts at its door.
- Low Fenn's headwoman won't go near it.

## Cinderlock

*A vault in the ash barrens, which once held the fire that ran Kaelith.*

Cinderlock lies in the ash barrens, east of the centre. Kaelholm's catalogue says its fire once ran the whole of Kaelith. The ground around it is warm all year.

**What people say**

- The orc herders winter their flocks on the warm ground, and don't dig.
- What burned there once could burn again.

## Anvilreach

*A vault in the northern highlands, where Kaelith forged something it called cores.*

Anvilreach lies in the northern highlands, under dwarf holds that were built over it on purpose. Kaelholm's catalogue says Kaelith forged thirteen of something there, and named each one.

**What people say**

- The dwarves of the highlands say they were Kaelith's masons. They say less about Anvilreach.
- Whatever was forged there, it was made to be named.

## Rimeholt

*A vault in the northern ice, which Kaelholm calls the vault of the failsafes.*

Rimeholt lies under the northern ice. Kaelholm's catalogue calls it the vault of the failsafes: whatever stopped the change, three hundred years ago, is said to be in there.

**What people say**

- Kaelholm's scholars argue about Rimeholt more than any other vault.
- If anyone opens Rimeholt, everyone should be there.

## Deepcall

*A vault deep under the centre of Kaelmar, where the under-roads meet.*

Deepcall lies deep under the centre of Kaelmar, where the under-roads meet. Kaelholm's catalogue says it holds the web itself, the roads through the between.

**What people say**

- You can hear it from the under-roads, calling.
- Root-road riders say they pass close to it, in the between.

## Saltgate

*A vault on the east coast, which Kaelholm says needs someone from across the sea.*

Saltgate lies on Kaelmar's east coast. Kaelholm's catalogue says it was the vault of far-speaking and sea-roads.

**What people say**

- Since the Aetheris ships started coming, its door has started to hum.
- The sea near Saltgate is calm in every weather.

## Greyhall

*A vault beside Kaelholm, said to hold Kaelith's archive.*

Greyhall lies right beside @[Kaelholm|SET-302], and Kaelholm was built where it is because of it. Its catalogue says Greyhall holds Kaelith's own archive, in the First Grammar.

**What people say**

- Kaelholm's archivists have been reading its door for three hundred years.
- Every Archivist-Elder swears to open it one day.

## Watchbarrow

*A vault in the western hills, said to be where Kaelith's guardians rest.*

Watchbarrow lies in the western hills, not far from Ashvale's villages. Kaelholm's catalogue calls it the vault of the long watch, where Kaelith's guardians rest between their watches.

**What people say**

- The hill folk leave food at its barrow. It's always gone in the morning.
- Something in there is still keeping watch.

## Glassmere

*A vault under the southern lakes, said to have made the glass.*

Glassmere lies under the southern lakes. Kaelholm's catalogue says the glass was made there, "made, not grown". Since the seeps started, the lake has been very still.

**What people say**

- The lake looks like glass on still days. On windy days, too.
- Since the glass came, the fishing's been strange.

## Hollowbell

*A vault in the southern river country, which sings.*

Hollowbell lies in the river country of the south. On still nights, people say, you can hear it hum one note. Kaelholm's catalogue says it holds the keys to all the others.

**What people say**

- Kaelholm's archivists have tried singing to it. It didn't sing back.
- The river folk sing back to it, on Evernight.

## Glyph-Work and the First Grammar

*Kaelith's magic of call and answer: intention, resonance, pattern, world.*

Kaelith's glyph-work follows the First Grammar: intention wakes resonance, resonance makes a pattern, and the pattern becomes the world. It is the same grammar the Bloom speaks, in another voice.

Kaelith doors listen. Outer gates may demand a sequence of notes, a tuning fork, a key or marked blood.

**Glyph-scars.** Glyphs can be carved into living flesh, and some grow on their own. Carving them is forbidden in Kaelholm, and a rite in the Collective's Warband.

**Kaelith materials.** Everstone does not rot. Hatch-metal drives roots away.

**Everyday glyph-work.** In Kaelholm and Ashvale, glyph-work is as ordinary as a lamp.

| Thing | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Lamp-glyphs | A cold, steady light inside a cut stone, hung from a nail. Every house in Kaelholm has one. |
| Speaking-stones | Matched pebbles that buzz in the palm when the other one speaks. They crackle near the glass. |
| Listening bowls | A copper bowl of water. Put your ear to it and hear the room it's aimed at. |
| Beacon-stones | One stone, one partner, one message a day. |
| Warding stones | Four grey stones set in a room's corners. Speaking-stones go dead inside. |
| Reading-lenses | A cut-glass disc that makes the First Grammar make sense, and nothing else. |
| Memory-crystals | A cloudy shard that replays one scene when held, sound and all. |
| Glyph-light | The blue fuel that runs rune-sleds and walkers. One charge lasts a day. |
| Glyph-lances | A crossbow with a glyph down the rail that throws a bolt of light. |

**At the table.** Glyph-work uses ordinary arcane spells. Spells are cast as written, with a glyph as the visible part; calligrapher's supplies are the tool for it.
