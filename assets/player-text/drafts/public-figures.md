# Player Adventure Book: Public Figures of Aetheris (DRAFT)

Drafted from the archive for review. Nothing here is published until this file is moved up into assets/player-text/.

## How to use this

The famous and infamous of Aetheris: the five factions' leaders and champions, and the faces of the cults. Each person has two parts:

- **Who they are.** What any traveller has heard.
- **What people say.** Rumours. Some are true, some aren't.

There's no read-aloud text, because the archive leaves most of these people's looks and voices to you. Pronouns are used only where the archive uses them.

## Highlord Theos Valcairn

**Who they are**

- Supreme commander of the Celestial Order, the faction that rules @[The Radiant Ascendancy].
- A paladin.

**What people say**
