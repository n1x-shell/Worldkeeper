# Player Adventure Book: Sites of the Hollow Marches (DRAFT)

Drafted for review. Nothing here is published until this file is moved up into assets/player-text/ and the records in assets/lore/drafts/marches/ move up to assets/lore/.

## How to use this

The named sites and faction camps of the Hollow Marches, in the same layout as the other places.

## Ashen Spire

*The ruined capital of the last great kingdom, picked over by scavengers and walked by restless spirits.*

> The tower is still standing, somehow: a black needle of stone above a city of collapsed roofs. Everything is grey with old ash. Scavenger flags flap on the rubble, three crews' worth, each pretending the others aren't there. As the light goes, you notice figures moving between the ruins who don't cast shadows.

**What you know**

- The capital of the last great kingdom, before it fell and the Hollow Marches became lawless.
- Now a ruin of scavengers, mercenaries and restless spirits.
- The Vale family were a minor house here.

**Places in the ruin**

| Place | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The Needle | The last standing tower, black with old fire. |
| The Throne Steps | Where the last kings sat. The throne is gone. |
| Salvage Row | Where the scavenger crews camp, trade and fight. |

**Getting around.** Somewhere in the Marches, a hard road from Hollow Refuge.

**What people say**

- Restless spirits still walk Ashen Spire.
- The spirits give warnings, but nobody understands them.
- Every crew wants to salvage the Needle first.

## The Scar

*An endless battlefield of rusted steel, where the dead do not stay down.*

> The ground is armour. Helmets, blades and breastplates, rusted together into a crust that crunches underfoot, as far as you can see. Banners nobody recognises hang off broken spears. It's very quiet. Then something under the rust shifts.

**What you know**

- An endless battlefield of rusted steel in the Hollow Marches.
- The dead soldiers there do not rest.
- Half a day from the Crossroads of Fate or the Bloodmarked Fields. Both roads are dangerous.

**Getting around**

| From the Scar to | Time | How safe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Crossroads of Fate | Half a day | Dangerous |
| The Bloodmarked Fields | Half a day | Dangerous |

**What people say**

- The dead on the Scar don't stay down.
- Nobody recognises half the banners on the Scar.
- The scavenger Torren Kell's crew won't go near it.

## Blackveil Fortress

*An abandoned stronghold whose vaults may hold relics, and whose depths nobody returns from.*

> The fortress fills the valley: black walls, black towers, gates that hang open as if it left in a hurry. Nothing flies over it. The upper halls have been picked clean. The stairs going down have not.

**What you know**

- An abandoned stronghold in the Hollow Marches.
- Its vaults may hold relics.
- People who go into its depths don't come back.

**Getting around**

| From Blackveil to | Time | How safe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hollow Refuge | 1 day | Risky |
| The Rift | 1 day | Openly hostile |

**What people say**

- Old Vara wants Blackveil metal, and pays well for it.
- There are names carved on the walls of one hall, hundreds of them.
- Somebody's planning a Blackveil job, and hiring.

## The Sunken Archive

*An underground library laid out like a maze, where reading too deeply brings back memories.*

> The stairs go down under a hill, and keep going. Then the shelves begin: shelves carved into the rock, shelves built of old doors, shelves leaning on each other in the dark, all of them full. Your lantern finds a book open on a reading stand, as if somebody just stepped away.

**What you know**

- An underground library laid out like a labyrinth, in the Hollow Marches.
- Reading too deeply brings back memories that were erased.
- Scholars of every faction want into it.

**Getting around**

| From the Archive to | Time | How safe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Crossroads of Fate | 1 day | Risky |
| The Rift | Half a day | Openly hostile |

**What people say**

- Read too long in the Sunken Archive and you start remembering things that were never yours.
- The records in the Archive contradict each other, and the scholars argue about which are forgeries.
- The Blind Seer wants a book from the Archive.
- Professor Orin Vale would give anything to reach its deepest stacks.

## Wither's Hollow

*A dead forest in endless mist, whose paths shift once you've walked past them.*

> The trees are grey and dead, and the mist never lifts. The path ahead looks clear. You walk ten steps, look back, and the path behind you is gone, and a different one is there instead. Somewhere in the mist, something is breathing.

**What you know**

- A dead, mist-bound forest in the Hollow Marches.
- Its paths shift behind travellers.
- Mother Thorn knows its trails better than anyone.

**Getting around**

| From Wither's Hollow to | Time | How safe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Bloodmarked Fields | 1 day | Dangerous |
| The Rift | Half a day | Openly hostile |

**What people say**

- The paths in Wither's Hollow move once you've walked past them.
- One path never moves, and nobody who follows it comes back the same.
- The forest knows who you are.

## The Cairn of the Forgotten

*A field of unmarked graves for people whose histories were erased. Some of them stir.*

> Low mounds of stone, row after row, none of them marked. The grass between them is very green. A few graves look freshly turned. An old woman is laying flowers on one of them, and when you ask whose it is, she says she doesn't know.

**What you know**

- A field of unmarked graves in the Hollow Marches, for people whose histories were erased.
- Some of the graves stir.
- Sephane, Keeper of the Cairn, is the god of death, memory and names.

**What people say**

- Some of the nameless graves at the Cairn stir.
- Sometimes a grave is empty, and nobody knows who climbed out.
- People find their own family names on stones that were blank the day before.

## The Crossroads of Fate

*A waystone where the roads shift but the crossing never changes, and every faction passes through.*

> The roads come in from every direction, and none of them are where they were last time. But the stone in the middle never moves: a tall grey waystone, worn smooth by hands. Travellers of every faction touch it as they pass, the way you'd touch a doorframe for luck.

**What you know**

- A waystone in the Hollow Marches where the roads shift but the crossing itself never changes.
- Every faction passes through. It is watched, and usually safe.

**Getting around**

| From the Crossroads to | Time | How safe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hollow Refuge | Half a day | Watched, usually safe |
| The Scar | Half a day | Dangerous |
| The Sunken Archive | 1 day | Risky |

**What people say**

- Everyone touches the waystone for luck. Nobody remembers who started it.
- A road appears at the Crossroads sometimes that wasn't there before, and isn't there after.
- The stone hums if you put your ear to it.

## The Sanctuary of the Last Light

*The Celestial Order's camp in the Marches: a ruined hilltop cathedral held as a holy bastion.*

> The cathedral has no roof, but its walls still stand on the hilltop, and the Order has filled the gaps with timber and faith. A golden sun banner flies from the broken bell tower. Knights drill in the nave. The doors stand open, but someone is always watching them.

**What you know**

- The Celestial Order's camp in the Hollow Marches, in a ruined cathedral on a hilltop.
- A holy bastion, and the Order's healers work from it.

**What people say**

- Sister Elyra Vale treats anyone who comes to its doors, whatever their colours.
- The Order is quietly fortifying it, as if expecting a siege.

## The Warlord's Rest

*The Crimson Legion's war camp on an old battlefield, where rank is settled by duels.*

> Tents in neat red rows, cookfires, a ring of churned mud in the middle where two soldiers are fighting and fifty are betting on it. The Legion has built its camp on top of an older battle, and the old dead are still turning up in the latrine trenches.

**What you know**

- The Crimson Legion's war camp in the Hollow Marches, on an old battlefield.
- Rank in the camp is settled by duels.
- Captain Marrek Vaal commands there.

**What people say**

- Vaal keeps his agreements, which makes the other factions trust him more than his superiors.
- Anyone can challenge for rank in the Rest. Most people only do it once.

## The Nexus Outpost

*The Arcane Consortium's ruined tower in the Marches, where reality is fragile.*

> The tower leans at an angle that makes your stomach turn. Its shadow points the wrong way. Scholars come and go with instruments that tick and hum, and nobody seems to notice that the stream beside the tower is running uphill.

**What you know**

- The Arcane Consortium's outpost in the Hollow Marches, in a ruined tower.
- Reality around it is fragile.

**What people say**

- The tower is leaning further every year.
- Professor Orin Vale works out of it, when he isn't trying to get into the Sunken Archive.
- Don't drink from the stream.

## The Veiled Chamber

*The Shadow Pact's hidden hall under a dead city, hidden by illusion.*

> You won't find it. That's the point. Somewhere under the ruins of a dead city in the Marches, the Shadow Pact keeps a hall that looks like rubble to anyone not invited. If you're reading this, someone has decided to invite you.

**What you know**

- The Shadow Pact's hidden hall in the Hollow Marches, under the ruins of a dead city.
- Illusion hides it.

**What people say**

- Vess the Grey can get you in, for a price.
- Nobody agrees which dead city it's under.

## The Wolf's Den

*The Wildborn Clans' overgrown ruin in the Marches, shared with wolves.*

> Ivy has eaten the old walls. Wolves lie in the sun on the broken stones and watch you without getting up. Wildborn sit among them as if it's the most normal thing in the world. An old firbolg woman with thorns in her hair is already telling you off for something.

**What you know**

- The Wildborn Clans' camp in the Hollow Marches: an overgrown ruin shared with wolves.
- Mother Thorn speaks for the clans there.

**What people say**

- The wolves decide who's welcome, not the Wildborn.
- Mother Thorn is afraid of what lies beneath the Rift.
