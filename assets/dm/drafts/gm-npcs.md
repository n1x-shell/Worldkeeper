# GM-only NPCs (DRAFT)

Full profiles for the 31 NPCs kept from players. None of this is published. "What people see" is the face a player meets; "The truth" is what only you know.

## The Blind Seer

**Build:** Minotaur (MotM) · Cleric (Twilight Domain) · Acolyte · he/him · from Unknown. Older than anyone can say.

**Group:** Black Sun (secretly), Hollow Refuge

**Looks.** A huge, stooped Taur'n with rune-marked horns worn smooth, and two white scars where his eyes were. He never seems to bump into anything.

**Manner.** Unhurried and kind, with a habit of answering the question you were about to ask.

**What people see.** The prophet of Hollow Refuge. Lives in the Seer's Tower, which has no windows because "windows work both ways". Put out his own eyes to see the Eternal Eclipse. People climb the tower for advice and come down quieter.

**The truth**

- He is High Prophet Azhir of the Black Sun, living under no name. Most of the Black Sun do not know where he goes between eclipses.
- He has survived more than one Aetherfall.
- Wants the truth discovered slowly enough to survive.
- Fears the thirteenth shard being found too early.

**Hook.** He will tell the party one true thing about each of them, and nothing else.

## Old Vara

**Build:** Dwarf (PHB) · Artificer (Armorer) · Artisan · she/her · from The Anvil of Dominion, the Tyrant's Dominion

**Group:** Hollow Refuge

**Looks.** A broad old dwarf with a white braid down her back and a hand of black iron that she built herself. The joints hiss when she grips a hammer.

**Manner.** Gruff, generous with favours, stingy with coin. Calls every blade "her".

**What people see.** Runs Old Vara's Forge in Hollow Refuge. Repairs anything, and would rather be paid in favours. Knows metals that should not exist just by looking at them.

**The truth**

- Remembers dying.
- Wants Blackveil metal.
- Fears losing her daughter to the Legion.

**Hook.** Her daughter serves under Captain Captain Marrek Vaal. Vara will pay well for news.

## Captain Marrek Vaal

**Build:** Centaur (MotM) · Fighter (Psi Warrior) · Soldier · he/him · from The Bloodfields, the Tyrant's Dominion

**Group:** Crimson Legion

**Looks.** A grey-coated centaur in red-lacquered barding, with a captain's braid and a scarred, careful face.

**Manner.** Formal and honest. Says exactly what he will do, then does it.

**What people see.** A Crimson Legion officer in the Hollow Marches who keeps his agreements. Every faction would rather deal with him than with his superiors.

**The truth**

- Wants the Shard of Dominion.
- Fears political irrelevance.
- Prefers a negotiated surrender to slaughter, and would be ruined if the Legion knew.

**Hook.** Old Vara's daughter is one of his soldiers. He knows who she is, and is keeping her safe without telling either of them.

## Sister Elyra Vale

**Build:** Human (PHB) · Cleric (Life Domain) · Acolyte · she/her · from Ashen Spire, the Hollow Marches

**Group:** Celestial Order, Vale family

**Looks.** A young woman with the Vale family's grey eyes, sleeves always rolled up, and a healer's satchel stitched with the Order's sun.

**Manner.** Warm, earnest and stubborn. Treats anyone who is bleeding, whatever their colours.

**What people see.** A Celestial Order healer working in the Hollow Marches, often at the Lantern House. Youngest of the Vale family.

**The truth**

- Ordered to record every Mnemonic Relic and necromancer she meets.
- Wants to protect the Marches from corruption.
- Fears her compassion being read as weakness.

**Hook.** She does not yet know that her brother Tobias Vale reads every report she sends.

## Professor Orin Vale

**Build:** Half-elf (PHB 2014) · Wizard (Order of Scribes) · Sage · he/him · from Ashen Spire, the Hollow Marches

**Group:** Arcane Consortium, Vale family

**Looks.** A lanky half-elf with ink-blacked fingers, a scholar's coat full of notebooks, and the Vale grey eyes.

**Manner.** Distracted, excitable and bad at lying.

**What people see.** An Arcane Consortium scholar working in the Hollow Marches. Second-youngest of the Vales, and the only one who is half-elf: the family took him in as a baby.

**The truth**

- Wants access to the Sunken Archive.
- Fears a rival reaching its deepest vault first.
- Has found records older than accepted history.

**Hook.** He needs bodyguards for the Sunken Archive, and he pays in copied pages.

## Vess the Grey

**Build:** Lizardfolk (MotM) · Rogue (Thief) · Charlatan · they/them · from Noctis, the Eternal Veil

**Group:** Shadow Pact

**Looks.** A grey-scaled lizardfolk in a grey cloak. Witnesses disagree on height, eye colour and how many teeth showed.

**What people see.** A Shadow Pact agent in the Hollow Marches who trades in information.

**Hook.** Two of the Vesses can be in the same room. The party may notice.

## Mother Thorn

**Build:** Firbolg (MotM) · Druid (Circle of the Stars) · Hermit · she/her · from Wither's Hollow, the Hollow Marches

**Group:** Wildborn Clans

**Looks.** A tall, bent firbolg with bark-brown skin and thorn-twigs woven through grey hair. She smells of crushed nettles.

**Manner.** Sharp-tongued and maternal. Scolds people she likes.

**What people see.** A Wildborn elder in the Hollow Marches. Wants the Marches kept from becoming an imperial battlefield. Afraid of "what the land remembers beneath the Rift".

**The truth**

- Knows unstable trails through Wither's Hollow.
- Her fear of the Rift is not superstition: she reads the stars over it, and they are wrong.

**Hook.** She can guide the party through Wither's Hollow, by paths that move.

## The founder

**Build:** Human (PHB) · Bard (College of Lore) · Scribe · she/her · from Kaelith, before the Shrouding

**Group:** Ashvale

**Looks.** Remembered as a small woman with burned palms. Ashvale's children draw her with her hands held up.

**Manner.** In the stories: blunt, tired and kind.

**What people see.** The woman with burned hands who led refugees to found Ashvale in 2 AB, and wrote the rite the town still keeps. Never named. Died about 250 years ago.

**The truth**

- Probably the last vault-keeper of Arborhold. Her palm burns resemble that keeper's final log.
- Her sealed warning lies in lead under the meeting hall, read once by each new Ritekeeper.

**Hook.** Her grave, her song and her warning are the start of the Ashvale mystery.

## The Forgotten One

**Build:** Changeling (MotM) · Wizard (Order of Scribes) · Hermit · they/them · from Aetheris, many lives ago

**Group:** None

**Looks.** Nobody remembers. A changeling's face, worn plain and forgettable, so that even people who meet them do not keep it.

**Manner.** Patient, lonely and very careful.

**What people see.** Nobody knows they exist. People avoid the Empty House without knowing why.

**The truth**

- One mortal of Aetheris who learned the truth, was erased, returned without memory and learned it again over many lives.
- Tore the Rift between continents, centuries before Kaelith, and crossed it repeatedly, the last time about 270 AB.
- Left a spore-seed in a Kaelmar communion pool for a bloodline not yet born.
- Cut the shards from the Loom, ending the Cycle, and tore a piece from their own Thread to make the thirteenth shard.
- Lives unremembered in the Empty House at Hollow Refuge, with the thirteenth shard.

**Hook.** Why a changeling and a scribe: a face nobody keeps, and a habit of writing everything down, because they have had to relearn it all so many times. Their true name and first face were never recorded anywhere; these are suggestions to keep or change.
