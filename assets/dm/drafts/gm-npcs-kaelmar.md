# GM-only notes: new Kaelmar NPCs (DRAFT)

The truth behind the cover pages, and the three NPCs with no player page at all.

## Archivist Teodor Wrenn

**Build:** Human (PHB) · Wizard (Diviner) · Scribe · he/him · from Kaelholm

**Player page:** cover page

**Looks.** A thin, balding man in archivist grey, with spectacles mended with wire and a pleasant, forgettable face.

**Manner.** Helpful, quiet and always slightly too well informed.

**Wants.** To keep the Continuity told of everything Kaelholm finds.

**The truth**

- A Continuity watcher inside Kaelholm's archives.
- He copies every expedition map and every report on the vaults, and sends them to Sentinel Hold.

## Elder Tamsin Rook

**Build:** Elf (PHB) · Druid (Circle of Spores) · Hermit · she/her · from The Central Bloom Country

**Player page:** cover page

**Looks.** A very old elf with eyes clouded white with fine crystals, and branching lines covering both arms.

**Manner.** Slow, distant, and often answering a question nobody asked.

**Wants.** To go deeper than anyone, and come back.

**The truth**

- Leads the Sixth Tendril, which goes deepest of all.
- She hears something in the Bloom that answers from under the Rift, and has told no one.

## Deputy Warden Ossian Duskmere

**Build:** Shadar-kai (MotM) · Wizard (Abjurer) · Guard · he/him · from Sentinel Hold

**Player page:** none

**Looks.** A tall Shadowkin in a hatch-metal coat, grey-skinned, with eyes like wet slate.

**Manner.** Courteous, careful and impossible to surprise.

**Wants.** Observation without exposure, for another three hundred years if need be.

**The truth**

- Keeps the Hold's seventeen locks, cut for the shape of a key.
- Thinks Crane is too willing to wake things.

## Mags Kettle

**Build:** Goblin (MotM) · Rogue (Arcane Trickster) · Charlatan · she/her · from Kettleby

**Player page:** cover page

**Looks.** A small green goblin with a cart of pots, wires and spare hinges, and a laugh like a kettle whistle.

**Manner.** Chatty, generous and everywhere at once.

**Wants.** To see the stones, and anyone who goes near them.

**The truth**

- A Continuity observer, watching Ashvale's stones and the children who dream of them.
- She reports to Sentinel Hold every new moon.

## Lock-Cutter Ennis Moor

**Build:** Deep Gnome (MotM) · Artificer (Armorer) · Artisan · they/them · from The under-roads

**Player page:** none

**Looks.** A grey deep gnome in a leather apron, with fine tools in every pocket and hatch-metal rings on every finger.

**Manner.** Absorbed, silent and endlessly patient.

**Wants.** To finish the eighteenth lock.

**The truth**

- Descended from a vault crew sealed into the under-roads at the breaking.
- Has worked out more about the thirteen keys than the Continuity has told Crane.

## Watcher Senna Glass

**Build:** Sea Elf (MotM) · Sorcerer (Aberrant Sorcery) · Sage · she/her · from The southern lakes

**Player page:** none

**Looks.** A pale sea elf with grey-green skin and eyes that reflect light like still water.

**Manner.** Dreamy, precise and unsettling.

**Wants.** To watch the lakes, and the things beneath them, and never be seen.

**The truth**

- Watches the lakes where one of the sealed vaults lies, for the Continuity.
- Has started dreaming in a voice that is not hers.
