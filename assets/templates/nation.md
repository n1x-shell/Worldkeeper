# Nation

A land defined by who rules it: a kingdom, dominion, empire or free state. Use a Region for country with no single ruler, and a Faction for the rulers themselves.

## Facts

- **Ruling faction**: Name the faction or house that rules it. Example: Crimson Legion (The Tyrant's Dominion)
- **Capital**: Name the seat of government. Example: Varkhaal, the Iron Citadel (The Tyrant's Dominion)
- **Chief fortress**: Name its strongest stronghold, if it has one apart from the capital. Example: the Red Bastion (The Tyrant's Dominion)
- **Leaders**: Name the people in charge now. Example: Warlord Garron Kael and General Orana Volkash (The Tyrant's Dominion)
- **Emblem**: Describe the sign on its banners and coins. Example: a crimson blade or a clenched fist (The Tyrant's Dominion)
- **Terrain**: List the kinds of ground found there, such as mountain, hill or urban. Example: mountain, hill, urban (The Tyrant's Dominion)
- **Danger**: Say how safe travel is, as safe, risky or deadly; with the party's level this sets how hard its encounters are. Example: risky
- **Wealth**: Say how well off it is, as poor, modest or rich; this weights the treasure found there. Example: modest

## A first look

> Guidance: A few lines to read aloud as the party crosses the border. Show what life under this rule looks and sounds like.
> Example (The Tyrant's Dominion, REG-003): "Iron mountains, black and red, and every pass guarded by a fort."

## What it stands for

> Guidance: The creed of its rulers and what they promise their people. This is what the nation's soldiers and officials will say to the party.
> Example (The Tyrant's Dominion, REG-003): "This is the dominion of the Crimson Legion. Its creed: only the strong deserve to rule."

## Who rules and how

> Guidance: Where power sits, who holds it and how decisions are made. Players need to know who to ask, who to bribe and who to avoid.
> Example (The Eternal Veil, REG-005): "The capital is Noctis, the City of Masks, in endless dusk. The rulers meet in the Midnight Court."

## Places

> Guidance: The cities, forts and sites worth knowing, each with one line. Link each to its own record.
> Example (The Tyrant's Dominion, REG-003): "The Bloodfields (permanent warfront), the Ruins of the Old Kings, the Anvil of Dominion (warforges under the mountains)."

## Rivals within its borders

> Guidance: The outsiders it tolerates inside its land: embassies, enclaves, rebels or minorities. These are where a party out of favour can find shelter.
> Example (The Eternal Veil, REG-005): "An Order lighthouse on the dusk coast, the one steady light in the Veil."

## Getting around

> Guidance: How far the border and capital are from places the party knows, and how people travel. Rough times are enough.
> Example (The Eternal Veil, REG-005): "The border is about two weeks from Hollow Refuge, and Noctis is about five days beyond it."

## What people say

> Guidance: What locals and rivals say about the nation. Include at least one thing its rulers would rather nobody said.
> Example (The Eternal Veil, REG-005): "The Umbral Paths will take you anywhere, but not always where you asked."

## Secrets (GM only)

> Guidance: What the rulers hide from their own people and from outsiders. A good secret here can turn a whole campaign.
> Example (The Purging of the Halls, HIS-013): "Some survivors escaped to the old grove on the edge of the Radiant Expanse."
