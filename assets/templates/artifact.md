# Artifact

A unique object of great power and history, with a story of its own. Use an Item for magic that could exist in more than one copy.

## Facts

- **Form**: Say what it looks like, in a few words. Example: A bone-handled scraping knife (The First Rasp)
- **First owner**: Name who made it or first carried it. Example: the first leader of the Spore Circle's Eastern Tendril (The Ember Crown of the Tendril)
- **Rules**: Say whether it has rules yet, or where they are. Example: No 2024 rules are assigned (The Walker's Bridle)

## Description

> Guidance: What it looks and feels like when someone holds it. One striking detail is enough.
> Example (The Walker's Bridle, ART-304): "A harness of iron and braided root, made for a Kaelith walker that no longer exists."

## What it does

> Guidance: Its power, in a line or two of plain words. Say what it does before saying how it works in the rules.
> Example (The Tidecaller's Heart, ART-007): "Half of a sea deity's heart. Commands water and storms."

## What it costs

> Guidance: The price of using or carrying it. A great artifact should always ask for something back.
> Example (Aegis of the Last Dawn, ART-001): "Carries the memories of fallen warriors, which can consume the bearer."

## Its history

> Guidance: Who made it, who has carried it and what it has done. A short history gives the party people to ask about it.
> Example (The First Rasp, ART-301): "Kaelholm's first, used to burn the first name from the first roll."

## Where it is (GM only)

> Guidance: Where it is now, who holds it and who would trade anything for it. Decide this even if the party is nowhere near.
> Example (The Ember Crown of the Tendril, ART-305): "Still worn, by someone in the Deepwood."
