# Cult

A group bound by a belief others find strange, forbidden or dangerous, often working in secret. Use a Faction for an open organisation, and a Deity for the power a cult worships.

## Facts

- **Allies**: Name the groups that help it or hire it. Example: any faction that can pay (The Bloodbound Company)
- **Opposed by**: Name the groups that hunt or resist it. Example: The Celestial Order, absolutely (The Black Sun)
- **Ranks**: List the roles or ranks inside it. Example: Scribe, Witness, Keeper, Guide, and Silent (The Silent Accord)
- **Leader**: Name who leads it, or who is said to. Example: Pell the Rememberer (The Whispering Circle)
- **Gathers at**: Name the place it meets or keeps its holy site. Example: the Rift of Echoes (The Whispering Circle)

## What they believe

> Guidance: The belief at the heart of the cult, in one or two lines. Write it the way a member would say it.
> Example (The Drowned Saints, CLT-003): "The sunken city will rise and the sea will take back what was stolen."

## How they work

> Guidance: How the cult recruits, meets and acts. This tells the party how they might cross its path.
> Example (The Silent Accord, CLT-006): "In small cells, quietly. They copy forbidden records so no single burning can destroy them"

## Who's known

> Guidance: The members people can name, each with one line. Link each to their own record.
> Example (The Whispering Circle, CLT-008): "Its leader is Pell the Rememberer, an old gnome who says he remembers three Aetherfalls."

## Where to find them

> Guidance: Where the cult gathers, worships or can be contacted. Give the party somewhere to go looking.
> Example (The Cults: Strongholds and Strength, RUL-660): "Pell the Rememberer keeps a house in Hollow Refuge for those who come asking."

## What people say

> Guidance: What outsiders say about the cult, true or not. Fear and rumour are half of a cult's power.
> Example (The Silent Accord, CLT-006): "If a stranger asks about your earliest memory, you've met the Accord."

## Secrets (GM only)

> Guidance: What the cult is really doing, how strong it is and how close it is to its goal. Note any split inside it.
> Example (The Cults: Strongholds and Strength, RUL-660): "They remember this year ending badly, and they disagree about how."
