# Adventure Hook

A ready idea for an adventure: a problem, a job or a mystery the party can be drawn into. Use an Adventure Location for the place where it plays out, and a Historical Event for something already over.

## Facts

- **Kind**: Say what sort of adventure it is, such as politics, travel or a heist. Example: Survival, travel (Wrong Shore)
- **Starts in**: Name the place it begins. Example: The Crossroads of Fate (Wrong Shore)
- **Can lead to**: Name where it might take the party. Example: Either continent (Wrong Shore)
- **Levels**: Give the character levels it suits. Example: 1 to 4 (Wrong Shore)

## Premise

> Guidance: The situation in one or two lines, as you would pitch it to the players. End on the question the party has to answer.
> Example (The Feather Crown, HOOK-915): "The aarakocra king of the eastern peaks has died, and three heirs want the party to judge."

## Ways to start

> Guidance: A few different ways the party could be drawn in, one line each. Several openings make it easy to fit into any campaign.
> Example (Kaelmar Hooks, Levels 11 to 16, HOOK-303): "Reach the Seventh Ward before Kaelholm does, and decide whether to wake it."

## People and places

> Guidance: The people, places and things involved, each with one line. Link each to its own record.
> Example (The Dead Man at the Crooked Nail, HOOK-001): "The Crooked Nail is the inn and mercenary exchange in Hollow Refuge, run by Mara Venn."

## Running notes

> Guidance: What to keep in mind while running it: who is moving, what it costs, the usual difficulty of checks. Short reminders, not a script.
> Example (Vault of the Shard of Dominion, HOOK-003): "All five factions are sending someone to the Fields. Captain Marrek Vaal wants the shard."

## What comes next

> Guidance: Where the story can go once this adventure is done. Link to the hooks it leads into.
> Example (The Red Excavation, HOOK-004): "It leads into Vault of the Shard of Dominion."

## Hidden truth (GM only)

> Guidance: What is really going on behind the hook. Write it plainly, so you can plant clues that point to it.
> Example (The War That Never Happened, HOOK-006): "Some of the dead fought in wars that never happened."
