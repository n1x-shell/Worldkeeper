# People

A people as players would meet them: a folk with a shared look, history and way of life, playable as a character. Use a Species for the bare biology and rules, and a Culture for a way of life shared across several peoples.

## Facts

- **Common names**: List eight or so names a player could pick from. Example: Arrok, Bera, Dorran, Kala, Mhor, Sava, Torren, Yara (Taur'n)
- **Naming style**: Say in a few words how they choose or give names. Example: a name they chose themselves (Autognomes)
- **Species rules**: Name the D&D 2024 rules species a player uses to play one. Example: firbolg (Bloomborn)
- **Found in**: Name the continents or realms where they live. Example: Aetheris and Kaelmar (Duergar)
- **Other names**: List what other peoples call them. Example: minotaurs, in Kaelmar (Taur'n)

## Who they are

> Guidance: What they look like and what sets them apart, in two or three lines. This is the picture a player starts from.
> Example (Taur'n, PPL-007): "The Taur'n are horned nomads, seven to nine feet tall, with runes marked into their horns."

## Strengths and flaws

> Guidance: What they are good at and where they fall short, in plain words. It gives players something to play up and something to play against.
> Example (Kobolds, PPL-036): "Clever, quick and very good at working together. Small, and easily bullied, until the traps go off."

## Culture

> Guidance: How they live: their families, crafts, customs and what they value. A few strong habits are worth more than a long history.
> Example (Duergar, PPL-019): "Forge-clans, hard bargains, and long grudges kept in iron ledgers."

## Where they live

> Guidance: Where they are found in each land, and how their lives differ from place to place. Give each continent or realm its own line.
> Example (Duergar, PPL-019): "In Kaelmar duergar live under the northern highlands, below the dwarf holds, and say they were Kaelith's ironworkers."

## How others see them

> Guidance: What neighbours and powers think of them, fairly or not. This shapes how a character of this people is greeted.
> Example (Bloomborn, PPL-010): "Every power in Kaelmar has an opinion about them."

## What the GM knows (GM only)

> Guidance: Anything about this people that players should not know yet, such as a hidden origin or an old debt. Leave it empty if there is nothing to hide.
