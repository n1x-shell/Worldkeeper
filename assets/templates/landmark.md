# Landmark

A single notable site out in the world: a ruin, battlefield, forest, crossing or wonder. Use a Building for a made structure people use, and a Dungeon for a site the party explores room by room.

## Facts

- **Where**: Name the region it lies in. Example: the Hollow Marches (Wither's Hollow)
- **Terrain**: Give the kind of ground, such as forest, hill or underdark. Example: forest (Wither's Hollow)
- **Danger**: Say how safe it is to visit, from safe through risky to deadly. Example: watched, and usually safe (The Crossroads of Fate)

## A first look

> Guidance: A few lines to read aloud as the party reaches it. Lead with what they see, then one thing that feels wrong or wonderful.
> Example (Wither's Hollow, LOC-034): "The trees are grey and dead, and the mist never lifts."

## What you know

> Guidance: The plain facts anyone nearby could tell a traveller. Say why people come here.
> Example (The Bloodmarked Fields, LOC-002): "Every faction fights here, and the fighting is over shards. They keep surfacing in this ground."

## Getting there

> Guidance: How far it is from places the party knows, and how safe the way is. Rough times are enough.
> Example (The Survey Garden, LOC-307): "The garden is three days from Arborhold's exit."

## What people say

> Guidance: Rumours about the site, true or not. Each should tempt the party closer or warn them away.
> Example (The Crossroads of Fate, LOC-003): "Everyone touches the waystone for luck. Nobody remembers who started it."

## Who you'll find here

> Guidance: The people or creatures the party is likely to meet, each with one line on what they are doing here. Link each to their own record.
> Example (The Bloodmarked Fields, LOC-002): "Gussie Bramblecott, runs a dig crew on the Fields for whoever pays."

## Secrets (GM only)

> Guidance: What the site hides and what it is really for. One clear secret is better than several vague ones.
> Example (Wither's Hollow, LOC-034): "One unstable path leads toward the Hollow Verge."
