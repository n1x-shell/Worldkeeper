# Shared System

Something that works the same way across the whole world: the calendar, money, languages, travel between lands or a world-wide condition. Use a Rule for a single procedure at the table, and Cosmology for the deeper shape of the world.

## Facts

- **Applies across**: Say where it holds. Example: Both continents (Money)
- **Units**: Name the units it is counted in, if any. Example: copper, silver, gold and platinum (Money)
- **Exceptions**: Name any place where it works differently. Example: Western Kaelmar relies heavily on barter (Money)

## In brief

> Guidance: The system in one line a player could remember. Start with what it means for them.
> Example (Travel between continents, SYS-905): "Two weeks by sea in the dark, or through the Rift, if you dare."

## How it works

> Guidance: The plain facts of how it works everywhere. Keep each fact to a line.
> Example (Money, SYS-902): "Each dominion and Kaelholm strikes its own faces on them, but coins are accepted by weight across every border."

## Local differences

> Guidance: Where and how it varies from place to place. These details make each land feel different.
> Example (Money, SYS-902): "Western Kaelmar still relies heavily on barter."

## At the table

> Guidance: How to use it in play, with any numbers the table needs. Point to rule records for the full mechanics.
> Example (Thinning and Unmaking, SYS-907): "A month among people who use your name takes away one level. Lost memories don't come back."

## Not yet decided

> Guidance: The parts nobody has settled yet. Listing them stops you inventing different answers in different sessions.
> Example (Travel between continents, SYS-905): "Exact sea width, route maps, vessel capacities, named ports, customs charges and travel prices are not defined."

## GM notes (GM only)

> Guidance: Anything about the system that players should not rely on, such as claims that may be false.
> Example (Ways Into and Out of the Ethereal Realm, ETH-591): "Sylas Vaerin only claims such an exit exists."
