# NPC

A single named person the party can meet, talk to or fight. Use a Family for a household of several people, and a Creature for a kind of monster rather than one individual.

## Facts

- **Pronouns**: Give the pronouns people use for them. Example: he/him (Old Corwin Ambry)
- **Species**: Name their people or species. Example: Halfling (Old Corwin Ambry)
- **Class**: Name the class they would have, if any. Example: Rogue (Merrin Rell)
- **Looks**: Give the one or two things the party notices first. Example: A tiny, ink-stained halfling with a white beard tucked into his apron (Old Corwin Ambry)
- **Manner**: Give how they talk and carry themselves, in a few words. Example: Gossipy, kind and much sharper than he lets on (Old Corwin Ambry)
- **Home**: Name the place they live or are usually found. Example: Kaelholm (Old Corwin Ambry)

## Who they are

> Guidance: What they do and what they are known for, in a line or two each. This is what anyone could tell the party about them.
> Example (Old Corwin Ambry, NPC-377): "Sells ink, quills and glyph-pigments in Lampblack, Kaelholm's quarter of scribes."

## What they want

> Guidance: The one thing they are working towards. A clear want tells you how they react to whatever the party does.
> Example (Grau Teeth-Taker, NPC-385): "A clan of his own, and the Ascendancy paying for what it did to him."

## What they fear

> Guidance: What they dread or what pressure is on them. Fear gives the party leverage, and gives the person depth.
> Example (Mara Venn, NPC-010): "Another Aetherfall is the only thing that frightens her."

## Ties and loyalties

> Guidance: Their family, friends, rivals and the groups they belong to, and where they can be found. Link each to its own record.
> Example (Mara Venn, NPC-010): "Belongs to no faction, and guards the Refuge's neutrality."

## How to play them

> Guidance: Voice, habits and a phrase or gesture you can use at the table. Keep it short enough to remember mid-scene.
> Example (The Blind Seer, NPC-001): "Unhurried and kind, with a habit of answering the question you were about to ask."

## What people say

> Guidance: Two or three things others say about them, true or not. Rumours give the party a reason to seek them out.
> Example (Grau Teeth-Taker, NPC-385): "Grau Teeth-Taker takes one tooth and lets you go. Argue, and he takes more."

## Secrets (GM only)

> Guidance: What they hide, who they really are, or what they know that nobody else does. Decide how the party might find out.
> Example (The Blind Seer, NPC-001): "Has survived more than one Aetherfall."
