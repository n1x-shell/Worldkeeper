# Culture

A shared way of life: the customs, names and values of a community that may include several peoples. Use a People for a folk defined by birth and look, and a Faction for an organisation with leaders and aims.

## Facts

- **Common names**: List eight or so names a player could pick from. Example: Alden, Mara, Kest, Lysa, Orin, Elyra, Vaal, Reed (Humans)
- **Naming style**: Say in a few words how names are chosen or given. Example: children left at Sephane's shrines are given the name Marrow (The Marrows of the Dusk Coast)
- **Species rules**: Name the D&D 2024 rules species its members usually play, or say any. Example: any; a kenku and a halfling grew up together (The Marrows of the Dusk Coast)
- **Found in**: Name the continents, realms or regions where it is lived. Example: the Eternal Veil's dusk coast (The Marrows of the Dusk Coast)

## Who they are

> Guidance: Who shares this way of life and what holds them together. One or two lines are enough.
> Example (Humans, PPL-001): "Found everywhere. The least culturally uniform people."

## Customs

> Guidance: The habits, rites and manners an outsider notices first. Pick ones a player can show at the table.
> Example (Dragonborn, PPL-018): "Clan honour above all. Every dragonborn knows their clan's name, and most have a story about it."

## What they value

> Guidance: What this community holds most dear, and what it will not forgive. Values tell players how to play someone raised in it.
> Example (Taur'n, PPL-007): "Migration, family oaths, craftsmanship and spiritual memory."

## Names and language

> Guidance: How names are given and what they mean, and any speech or script they share. This feeds every character a player makes from it.
> Example (The Marrows of the Dusk Coast, FAM-003): "a name has to go all the way to the bone"

## How others see them

> Guidance: What outsiders assume about this culture, rightly or wrongly. It gives a character something to prove or to put right.
> Example (Taur'n, PPL-007): "Many live in cities, and assuming they are all Wildborn annoys them."

## What people say

> Guidance: Sayings and rumours about them. One line that everyone knows is often enough.
> Example (The Marrows of the Dusk Coast, FAM-003): "Every Marrow knows every other Marrow's name. It's the one thing they're taught first."

## Secrets (GM only)

> Guidance: Anything about this culture that its members or outsiders do not know. Keep it small and tied to a person or place.
> Example (The Marrows of the Dusk Coast, FAM-003): "Marrow is an old fen name in Kaelmar too, and the coincidence is unexplained."
