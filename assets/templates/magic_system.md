# Magic System

A kind of magic as it works in your world: where it comes from, what it costs and who uses it. Use Cosmology for the deeper shape of the world, and a Rule for a single mechanic at the table.

## Facts

- **Source**: Say where the power comes from, in a few words. Example: the First Grammar (Glyph-Work and the First Grammar)
- **Cost**: Say what it takes from the user. Example: memory, name, blood or sanity (Magic in Aetheris)
- **Used by**: Name the people or groups who practise it. Example: Spore-bound people (The Bloom)
- **Tools**: Name the tool or focus a caster needs. Example: calligrapher's supplies (Glyph-Work and the First Grammar)
- **Rules**: Say which game rules it uses. Example: ordinary arcane spells (Glyph-Work and the First Grammar)

## How it works

> Guidance: The idea behind the magic in a few plain lines. Players should be able to explain it to each other.
> Example (Glyph-Work and the First Grammar, COS-302): "intention wakes resonance, resonance makes a pattern, and the pattern becomes the world."

## What it costs

> Guidance: The price of using it, and how the price grows with the power. A clear cost makes every spell a choice.
> Example (Magic in Aetheris, COS-020): "Power here takes something back, in memory, name, blood or sanity, and the greater the power, the more it takes."

## Who uses it

> Guidance: Who practises it, how they learn and what it does to them. Link the groups to their own records.
> Example (The Bloom, COS-301): "Communion opens a person to the Bloom, at a cost in self."

## Everyday magic

> Guidance: The small, common uses ordinary people see every day. These make the world feel lived in.
> Example (Glyph-Work and the First Grammar, COS-302): "In Kaelholm and Ashvale, glyph-work is as ordinary as a lamp."

## Where it changes

> Guidance: Places where the magic works differently, and how. A short table by place is often enough.
> Example (Magic in Aetheris, COS-020): "Each of the five dominions still carries the rule of the world its faction made, and magic bends to it."

## At the table

> Guidance: How to run it with the game's rules, in a line or two. Point to any rule records for the details.
> Example (Glyph-Work and the First Grammar, COS-302): "Glyph-work uses ordinary arcane spells. Spells are cast as written, with a glyph as the visible part"

## What people say

> Guidance: Sayings and warnings about the magic. They tell players how ordinary folk feel about it.
> Example (Magic in Aetheris, COS-020): "If a mage can't remember their mother's name, don't ask how they got so good."

## Secrets (GM only)

> Guidance: Why the magic really works the way it does. Note anything it could lead the party to discover.
> Example (Magic in Aetheris, COS-020): "The price is the Cycle's echo: the Hidden Ones' weapon was forgetting, and the world still charges in memory."
