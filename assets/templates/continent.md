# Continent

A great landmass and everything on it: its lands, powers and the feel of the place. Use a Region or Nation for one part of it, and a Realm for a place outside the ordinary world.

## Facts

- **Main lands**: Name the regions or nations it is divided into. Example: The Radiant Ascendancy, the Tyrant's Dominion, the Fractured Nexus, the Eternal Veil and the Verdant Rebirth (Aetheris)
- **Great powers**: Name the factions or rulers that matter across the whole continent. Example: Kaelholm, the Spore Circle, the Convergence Collective and the Ascendancy (Kaelmar)
- **Size**: Give a rough distance across it, so journeys can be judged. Example: about 1,500 km (Kaelmar)
- **Current date**: Give the year as people here count it. Example: Year 31 Unbound (Aetheris)
- **Starting place**: Name the place most campaigns here begin. Example: Ashvale (Kaelmar)

## A first look

> Guidance: A few lines to read aloud when the party first arrives or sees the land. Give the one image that sums the continent up.
> Example (Kaelmar, CON-301): "Forest grows through everything. Moss on the old stone roads, roots through the walls of towers that were built to last forever."

## What everyone knows

> Guidance: The facts any local could tell a stranger. Keep it to a short list the players can lean on.
> Example (Aetheris, CON-001): "Aetheris is a shattered continent of ruins, scavengers, warbands, zealots and conspiracies."

## The lands

> Guidance: How the land is laid out, north to south and coast to interior. Name the regions and link each to its own record.
> Example (Kaelmar, CON-301): "The north has cold highlands and ice; the south has fens, lakes and rivers; the centre is Bloom country above the under-roads."

## The powers

> Guidance: Who is in charge, who wants what, and where they clash. A small table of each power and its goal is often enough.
> Example (Aetheris, CON-001): "There are twelve shards. Five great factions still want them."

## Magic and the strange

> Guidance: What is unusual about magic or the supernatural here, and what it costs. This sets the tone for every spell cast on this land.
> Example (Aetheris, CON-001): "Magic and power have a price here: memory, name, blood or sanity."

## Getting around

> Guidance: How long the main journeys take and how people make them. Rough times are better than exact distances.
> Example (Aetheris, CON-001): "Each dominion's border is one to two weeks from Hollow Refuge, and each capital another five days to a week and a half past it."

## What people say

> Guidance: Rumours and legends told across the continent. Mix the true with the false, and let each point somewhere the party could go.
> Example (Kaelmar, CON-301): "The ruins aren't dead, only sleeping."

## Secrets (GM only)

> Guidance: What is really happening on this continent that the players do not know yet. Note who is behind it, so you can drop clues in other records.
> Example (Aetheris, CON-001): "The Watchers Beyond have noticed that the world is no longer hidden."
