# Realm

A place outside the ordinary world, such as a plane, a spirit world or a land of the dead. Use it for the realm as a whole, and give each distinct part of it its own record.

## Facts

- **Other names**: List what different peoples call it. Example: the between (The Ethereal Realm)
- **Ways in**: Name the doors, routes or rites that lead there from the world. Example: the Hollow Verge, Rift, Umbral Paths, Luminara Gate (The Ethereal Realm)
- **Who lives there**: Name the peoples or groups that make their home there. Example: The Cycle Breakers, Architects of Eternity, Veilborn, Titans of the Rift and Echoborn (The Ethereal Realm)
- **Party level**: Give the level a party should be before going in uninvited. Example: about level 11 and above (The Ethereal Realm)

## A first look

> Guidance: What a traveller sees, hears and feels in the first moments. Strangeness works best when it is quiet and specific.
> Example (The Ethereal Realm, CON-501): "There's no sky, only a pale light that comes from everywhere and nowhere."

## What it is

> Guidance: What this realm is, in a sentence or two, and how it relates to the ordinary world. Players should come away knowing why anyone would go there.
> Example (The Ethereal Realm, CON-501): "It's where lost and erased things go: discarded worlds, forgotten people, names nobody remembers."

## Its parts

> Guidance: The main layers, regions or domains, and how they differ. Link each one to its own record.
> Example (The Ethereal Realm, CON-501): "Its border, the Hollow Verge, is where most travellers stay."

## Getting in and out

> Guidance: The routes in from the world, who can use them, and how hard it is to come back. Note any level guidance as advice, not a wall.
> Example (The Ethereal Realm, CON-501): "A Kaelmar party may reach it by root-road from around level 5; the Verge or Rift route is suited to about level 11 and above."

## Who lives there

> Guidance: The residents, how they came to be there and what they want. A realm with people in it gives the party someone to talk to.
> Example (The Ethereal Realm, CON-501): "People live here. Some slipped out of the Cycle when an Aetherfall remade their world, and woke here."

## Dangers and rules

> Guidance: What this realm does to visitors, and any rule that works differently here. Say it plainly so players can choose to take the risk.
> Example (The Ethereal Realm, CON-501): "In the deep realm, death is final, and worse than final."

## What people say

> Guidance: Stories told about the realm by people who have never been, and by a few who have. Give the players something to hope for and something to fear.
> Example (The Ethereal Realm, CON-501): "Everything that was ever forgotten is here somewhere. Finding it is the hard part."

## Secrets (GM only)

> Guidance: The truth about the realm and the powers in it. Write what lies at its heart, even if the party may never reach it.
> Example (The Ethereal Realm, CON-501): "The Watchers are beyond the Black Horizon, not ordinary inhabitants with a monster stat block."
