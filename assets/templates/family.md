# Family

A household, house or clan bound by blood, marriage or a shared name. Use an NPC for each member, and a Faction for a group that people join by choice.

## Facts

- **Allies**: Name the groups or houses the family stands with. Example: the Crimson Legion (House Kael)
- **Opposed by**: Name the rivals the family is set against. Example: every rival for the Warlord's Hall (House Kael)
- **Seat**: Name the hall, home or town the family holds. Example: the Warlord's Hall (House Kael)
- **Head of the family**: Name who speaks for the family now. Example: Warlord Garron Kael (House Kael)

## Background

> Guidance: Where the family comes from and how it got where it is. A line or two is enough.
> Example (The Vale Family, FAM-001): "Now four Vales serve four masters, and each faction believes it has the only Vale worth having."

## Who's who

> Guidance: The members worth knowing, each with how they fit in. Link each to their own record.
> Example (The Halvors of Haskett's Mill, FAM-006): "Chief Wright Brenna Halvor, the elder sister"

## Family ways

> Guidance: The habits, traditions and expectations every member grows up with. This is what makes them recognisably one family.
> Example (The Hesks of Ashvale, FAM-004): "A Hesk is expected to be at every singing, sick or well, and to know every verse."

## Tensions

> Guidance: The quarrels inside the family, as short pairs of who wants what. These are the scenes waiting to happen.
> Example (House Kael, FAM-002): "Garron wants a son who'll fight for the Hall. Darion wants a Legion that doesn't need to."

## What people say

> Guidance: What neighbours and rivals say about the family. Include one thing the family would rather was not said.
> Example (House Kael, FAM-002): "The Warlord's son writes poetry. The Warlord pretends not to know."

## Secrets (GM only)

> Guidance: What the family hides, and which member knows it. Family secrets work best when one member would give them away.
> Example (The Halvors of Haskett's Mill, FAM-006): "The Halvor mill sits on old Kaelith foundations, and the first Halvor knew it."
