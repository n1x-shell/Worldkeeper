# Cosmology

How the world itself is made and held together: its origins, its hidden layers and the forces behind the gods. Use a Deity for a single god, and a Magic System for how spells and powers work day to day.

## Facts

- **Other names**: List what different peoples or scholars call it. Example: the Cycle of Aetherfalls (Aetheris)
- **State now**: Say whether it still works, has ended or is changing. Example: About thirty years ago it stopped (The Cycle and the Aetherfall)
- **Scale**: Say how far it reaches, in a few words. Example: seven depths, from the Hollow Verge to the Black Horizon (The Depths of the Ethereal Realm)

## The short version

> Guidance: The idea in one or two lines that a player could repeat. Start here before any detail.
> Example (The Depths of the Ethereal Realm, COS-021): "The deeper you go, the less the realm remembers being a world."

## What people believe

> Guidance: The stories and teachings ordinary people hold about it. They may disagree with each other, and with the truth.
> Example (The Ones Before, COS-034): "Every people on both continents has a story about who came before the gods, and no two stories agree."

## How it shapes the world

> Guidance: What it changes in everyday life, history and the lands. This is why it matters to someone who has never heard of it.
> Example (The Cycle and the Aetherfall, COS-903): "Five factions' worlds were left side by side, the dead stayed dead, and history began to pile up for the first time."

## At the table

> Guidance: How it affects play: where the party meets it, what it means for travel, magic or levels. Keep any rules short and point to their own records.
> Example (The Depths of the Ethereal Realm, COS-021): "With an invitation, a party can go deeper sooner."

## What people say

> Guidance: Sayings and rumours about it. A good one makes players wonder whether it is true.
> Example (The Cycle and the Aetherfall, COS-903): "Your grandmother's world might not have been this one. Ask her what she remembers."

## The truth (GM only)

> Guidance: How it really works and who is behind it. Write down what is still a mystery even to you, so you stay consistent.
> Example (The Depths of the Ethereal Realm, COS-021): "Nobody knows why the Hidden Ones and the elder gods made the world of Aetheris and Kaelmar, or what the Loom is for."
