# Creature

A kind of monster, beast or being the party might meet more than once. Use an NPC for a single named individual, and a Species for a kind that players can play.

## Facts

- **Stat block**: Name the rules stat block to start from, and any change. Example: Guard (Radiant Squire)
- **Challenge**: Give its rough challenge rating. Example: about CR 1 (Radiant Squire)
- **Found in**: Name the regions or realms where it lives. Example: The Hollow Verge (Name-Eater)
- **Associated groups**: Name any faction or cult it serves or belongs to. Example: Celestial Order (Radiant Squire)

## Overview

> Guidance: What the creature is, in one line. This is the summary a player would remember.
> Example (Name-Eater, CRE-502): "A slick of grey ooze that feeds on memories, and wears the faces of the people it has eaten."

## Appearance

> Guidance: What the party sees when it first appears. Describe it so you can read it aloud.
> Example (Black Sun Prophet, CRE-031): "Black robes, eyes painted out, and darkness pooling around their feet."

## How it behaves

> Guidance: What it does when it meets people: hunts, guards, lures or flees. This tells you how to open the scene.
> Example (Name-Eater, CRE-502): "When it rises, it has your sister's face, and it calls you by name."

## What you know

> Guidance: The plain facts a well-travelled character might know about it. These reward players who ask.
> Example (Radiant Squire, CRE-001): "They patrol the Radiant Ascendancy's roads in pairs."

## What people say

> Guidance: Rumours and warnings about it, true or not. One good warning can save a party, or lead it astray.
> Example (Radiant Squire, CRE-001): "Squires report everything to the Inquisition."

## Secret (GM only)

> Guidance: What it really is, who made it or what killing it changes. Keep any special rule here short.
> Example (Maintenance Echo, CRE-034): "The Hidden Ones' maintenance workers. They tend the hidden machinery of fate."
