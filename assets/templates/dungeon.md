# Dungeon

A site the party explores room by room: a vault, tomb, lair or sunken ruin with dangers inside. Use a Landmark for a site that is mostly seen from outside, and an Adventure Location for a place where one story plays out.

## Facts

- **Where**: Say where the entrance is. Example: under the Bloodmarked Fields (Vault of the Shard of Dominion)
- **Levels**: Give the character levels it suits. Example: 1 to 4 (Vault of the Shard of Dominion)
- **Scene DC**: Give the usual difficulty for checks inside. Example: 12 (Vault of the Shard of Dominion)
- **Rooms**: Give the number of rooms or areas. Example: five (Vault of the Shard of Dominion)
- **The prize**: Name what waits at the end for a party that gets through. Example: the Shard of Dominion (Vault of the Shard of Dominion)

## A first look

> Guidance: A few lines to read aloud at the entrance. Hint at what has already happened here and what has not.
> Example (Blackveil Fortress, LOC-032): "The upper halls have been picked clean. The stairs going down have not."

## What you know

> Guidance: What people outside know or believe about the place. This is why the party came, and what they expect to face.
> Example (Blackveil Fortress, LOC-032): "People who go into its depths don't come back."

## The way in

> Guidance: How the party gets inside: the door, the climb, the key or the trick. A good entrance is the first puzzle.
> Example (The Vault of the Nameless, LOC-016): "The stairs go down under the city, and stop at a door of stone sealed with iron bands and older wards."

## Rooms

> Guidance: Each room in order, with what is there, what can go wrong and one clue. Number them so you can find them quickly at the table.
> Example (Vault of the Shard of Dominion: the Rooms, RUL-665): "A long hall of tiles that slide when weight lands on them."

## The way out

> Guidance: How the party leaves, and who or what is waiting when they do. Leaving is often the start of the next adventure.
> Example (Vault of the Shard of Dominion: the Rooms, RUL-665): "The way out comes up in the middle of the Fields, and all five factions are waiting"

## What people say

> Guidance: Rumours about the place, told by people who never went all the way in. Some should be true.
> Example (The Sunken Archive, LOC-033): "Read too long in the Sunken Archive and you start remembering things that were never yours."

## Secrets (GM only)

> Guidance: The truth at the bottom of the dungeon: who built it, what it guards and why. Leave clues to it in the rooms above.
> Example (Blackveil Fortress, LOC-032): "One hall lists the names of soldiers who are still alive."
