# Faction

An organised group with shared aims: an order, army, guild, council or movement. Use a Cult for a group bound by a secret or forbidden belief, and a Family for a group bound by blood or name.

## Facts

- **Allies**: Name the groups it works with or trades with. Example: the Arcane Consortium, trading spells with the Pact (The Eternal Veil)
- **Opposed by**: Name the groups that stand against it. Example: the Arcane Consortium, the Shadow Pact and the Wildborn Clans (Celestial Order)
- **Ranks**: List its ranks or divisions, lowest to highest if it has an order. Example: Tendrils; the Sixth Tendril goes deepest (The Spore Circle)
- **Leaders**: Name the people in charge now. Example: Highlord Theos Valcairn, Grand Theurgist Selara Dawnveil, Inquisitor Malrik Vael (Celestial Order)
- **Seat**: Name the place it rules or works from. Example: Thornhaven (The Spore Circle)
- **Sign**: Describe its emblem. Example: A rising golden sun (Celestial Order)

## What they want

> Guidance: The world the faction is working towards, in one or two lines. Every scene with its members flows from this.
> Example (The Spore Circle, FAC-302): "To tend the conversation between people and the Bloom."

## What they promise

> Guidance: What the faction offers the people who follow it. This is why ordinary folk join or support it.
> Example (Celestial Order, FAC-001): "Peace through enlightened order."

## How they work

> Guidance: Their methods, their forces and how they treat outsiders. This tells you what they do when the party gets in the way.
> Example (Celestial Order, FAC-001): "The healers build hospitals in every dominion; the inquisitors follow them."

## Who leads

> Guidance: The leaders and how they came to lead. Link each to their own record.
> Example (The Spore Circle, FAC-302): "Its oldest elves saw the Shrouding as children."

## Belonging

> Guidance: What joining is like, what a member gains and what it costs them. Players thinking of joining will read this first.
> Example (Celestial Order, FAC-001): "A member gets protection, purpose and a place in a world that makes sense. They also get watched."

## What people say

> Guidance: What friends and enemies say about the faction. Include the line its rivals use against it.
> Example (Celestial Order, FAC-001): "Its healers will treat anyone. Its knights won't."

## Secrets (GM only)

> Guidance: What the faction hides from outsiders, or from its own members. Note who knows it.
> Example (The Enclave Compacts, HIS-014): "The Shadow Pact brokered most of the Compacts, and kept copies of every one."
