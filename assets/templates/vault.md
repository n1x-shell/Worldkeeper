# Vault

One of the sealed vaults left by an old civilisation, each holding one strand of its great work. Use a Vault Key for the shape of minds that opens it, and a Dungeon for an ordinary site to explore.

## Facts

- **Region**: Say where the vault lies. Example: The southern river country (Hollowbell)
- **Strand of the work**: Say which part of the great work it holds. Example: The thirteen keys and the notes that open locks (Hollowbell)
- **Key**: Name the shape of its key in a few words. Example: Thirteen voices singing one note (Hollowbell)
- **State**: Say whether it is sealed, open or broken, and since when. Example: Sealed at 300 AB (Hollowbell)

## Overview

> Guidance: What people know about the vault, in two or three lines. Start with what it is like to stand near it.
> Example (Hollowbell, VLT-313): "On still nights, people say, you can hear it hum one note."

## Site details

> Guidance: What is known of the vault and its surroundings: its doors, its guardians, what it once did. Only write what has been settled.
> Example (Cinderlock, VLT-305): "Its bound fire powered the web before the Shrouding."

## What people say

> Guidance: Rumours about the vault, true or not. Each should give someone a reason to want it opened, or kept shut.
> Example (Saltgate, VLT-309): "Since the Aetheris ships started coming, its door has started to hum."

## Who you'll find here

> Guidance: The people who live near it, watch it or want into it, each with one line. Link each to their own record.
> Example (Cinderlock, VLT-305): "Gorruk Ashhide, winters his flock on the warm ground over Cinderlock."

## Its key

> Guidance: The shape of the key and what it asks of the people who form it. Link to the vault key's own record.
> Example (Cinderlock, VLT-305): "What it asks: An enemy reconciled."

## What opening does

> Guidance: What happens when the vault's heart opens, and what it wakes. Say what is still undecided.
> Example (The Seed Vault, VLT-301): "Opening all thirteen hearts together in one shape wakes the whole web and the Root Core."

## Secrets (GM only)

> Guidance: What the vault hides, or what it does to the land around it, that nobody knows yet.
> Example (Cinderwell, SET-334): "Cinderlock's bound fire runs beneath it."
