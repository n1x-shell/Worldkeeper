# Region

A stretch of country with its own character, such as a forest, a range of hills or a borderland. Use a Nation when the land is defined by who rules it, and a Landmark for a single site within it.

## Facts

- **Ruling faction**: Name the faction that holds the region, or say plainly that nobody does. Example: None; five factions contest it (The Hollow Marches)
- **Compass position**: Say where it lies within its continent or realm. Example: east (The Eastern Deepwood)
- **Terrain**: List the kinds of ground found there, such as forest, hill, coastal or swamp. Example: forest (The Eastern Deepwood)
- **Danger**: Say how safe travel is, from safe through risky to deadly. Example: from watched, usually safe to openly hostile (The Hollow Marches)
- **Main places**: Name the settlements and landmarks worth a visit. Example: Thornhaven and the Survey Garden (The Eastern Deepwood)

## A first look

> Guidance: A few lines to read aloud as the party enters the region. Pick details that tell them what kind of trouble lives here.
> Example (The Hollow Marches, REG-001): "The road gives out before the land does."

## What you know

> Guidance: The plain facts a traveller would pick up on the road. Keep each one to a line, so players can scan them.
> Example (The Eastern Deepwood, REG-301): "Forest and Kaelith ruins grown together, in eastern Kaelmar."

## Places

> Guidance: The settlements, ruins and landmarks in the region, each with one line on why it matters. Link each to its own record.
> Example (The Hollow Marches, REG-001): "The Sunken Archive is a library under the ground, laid out like a maze."

## Getting around

> Guidance: The main routes, how long they take and how safe they are. A small table of journeys from a starting town works well.
> Example (The Hollow Marches, REG-001): "Past those three, the roads get worse. The routes out to the Rift are openly hostile."

## Who holds it

> Guidance: Which factions rule, patrol or fight over the region, and where their camps are. This tells the party whose banner they are under.
> Example (The Hollow Marches, REG-001): "All five dominions border it, and none of them rules it."

## What people say

> Guidance: Rumours about the region, true or not. Each one should point the party somewhere or warn them off.
> Example (The Eastern Deepwood, REG-301): "The rot thinks. Some say it remembers too."

## Secrets (GM only)

> Guidance: What the region hides: buried treasure, a hidden way, the real cause of its trouble. Note which place each secret is tied to.
> Example (The Hollow Marches, REG-001): "One unstable path leads toward the Hollow Verge."
