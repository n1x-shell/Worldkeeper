# Species

A kind of living being, described by body, senses and origin, with the rules a player uses for it. Use a People for how one folk of that species lives, and a Creature for a kind that is not played as a character.

## Facts

- **Common names**: List eight or so names a player could pick from. Example: Chak'ta, Kik'reth, Ssa-tik, Tch'kala, Vrik, Zaktik, Kkhai, T'chess (Thri-kreen)
- **Naming style**: Say in a few words how they are usually named. Example: Borrowed shapes, borrowed names (Plasmoids)
- **Species rules**: Name the D&D 2024 rules species a player uses. Example: Goliath, with horns (Taur'n)
- **Found in**: Name the continents or realms where they live. Example: the Hollow Verge and the Hollow Marches (Thri-kreen)
- **Creature type**: Give the rules creature type. Example: Humanoids (Bloomborn)

## What they are

> Guidance: The species in one or two lines, as a traveller would describe them. Start with what is most striking.
> Example (Thri-kreen, PPL-052): "Six-limbed mantis-folk found on every desert, in every world."

## Body and senses

> Guidance: How their bodies work and what they can sense or do that others cannot. Pick details a player can describe in play.
> Example (Thri-kreen, PPL-052): "Their shells change colour to match whatever they stand against."

## Strengths and flaws

> Guidance: What their bodies are good at and where they are weak, in plain words. Keep it to what matters at the table.
> Example (Thri-kreen, PPL-052): "Four arms, chitin armour, and no need for sleep. Hard to read, and harder to understand."

## Origins

> Guidance: Where they came from, as far as anyone knows. A short, uncertain answer often works better than a long one.
> Example (Bloomborn, PPL-010): "In the first years of the Bloom, the spore-storms rewrote some people into beast-folk."

## Where they live

> Guidance: Where they are found and how common they are there. Link the peoples of this species to their own records.
> Example (Bloomborn, PPL-010): "Most Bloomborn live in the open Bloom country and the Eastern Deepwood."

## At the table

> Guidance: Which rules to use and any changes for your world. One plain line saves a lot of questions.
> Example (Taur'n, PPL-007): "Use the minotaur rules, or goliath rules with horns."

## What the GM knows (GM only)

> Guidance: Anything about this species the players should not know yet, such as its true origin. Leave it empty if there is nothing to hide.
