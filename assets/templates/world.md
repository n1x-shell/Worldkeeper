# World

The whole setting in one record: its lands, gods, calendar and the big truths that hold everywhere. Make one per world, and give each continent or realm its own record.

## Facts

- **Continents**: Name the great landmasses, in the order you would point to them on a map. Example: Aetheris in the east and Kaelmar in the west (The World)
- **Seas and gulfs**: Name the waters that keep the lands apart. Example: the Black Tides, a sunless sea (The World)
- **Current date**: Give the year play begins in each calendar the players will hear. Example: 300 After Bloom (AB) in Kaelmar and Year 31 Unbound in Aetheris (The World)
- **Length of the year**: Give the number of days, so dates and journeys add up. Example: 363 days (The World)
- **Starting place**: Name the place where most campaigns begin. Example: Hollow Refuge (The World)

## A first look

> Guidance: A few lines to read aloud when the players first see the whole world, as if unrolling a map. Keep it to what anyone could see or be told.
> Example (The World, WLD-901): "Unroll the map. Two continents, one sea between them."

## What everyone knows

> Guidance: The handful of facts every character grew up with. These are the things players can rely on without asking.
> Example (The World, WLD-901): "Both continents worship the same gods, use the same coin and share much of their language."

## The lands

> Guidance: How the continents, realms and seas sit next to each other, in plain directions. A short tour helps players picture where they are.
> Example (The Layout of the World, REG-901): "Lay the world out east to west and it reads like a sentence."

## Gods and great powers

> Guidance: Who the gods are, whether they answer, and which powers reach across every land. Link each one to its own record.
> Example (The Gods, COS-909): "Both continents pray to the same gods, and the gods answer"

## Time and the calendar

> Guidance: How years, months and festivals are counted, and what year it is now. Players need this for birthdays, holy days and deadlines.
> Example (The World, WLD-901): "The year has 363 days: twelve 30-day months, plus three festival days that belong to no month."

## Getting between the lands

> Guidance: The usual ways to cross from one land to another, and roughly how long they take. This tells the party how big the world feels.
> Example (The World, WLD-901): "Ships cross the Black Tides between Kaelholm and the Eternal Veil in about two weeks."

## What people say

> Guidance: Two or three rumours about the world as a whole. They can be true, half true or wrong, and they give players questions to chase.
> Example (The World, WLD-901): "The Rift opens on both continents. Some say it's one wound, and that the two ends meet somewhere in between."

## Secrets (GM only)

> Guidance: The truths behind the world that the players should only learn through play. Write what is really going on, so every other record can stay consistent with it.
> Example (The World, WLD-901): "Since the Cycle ended, nothing hides either continent from them."
