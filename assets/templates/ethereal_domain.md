# Ethereal Domain

One region of the Ethereal Realm, at its own depth, with its own residents and dangers. Use a Realm for the Ethereal Realm as a whole, and a Landmark for a single site inside a domain.

## Facts

- **Depth**: Give its depth, counting from the border. Example: Depth 1 of 7 (The Hollow Verge)
- **Ways in**: Name how travellers find their way here. Example: a stair under a dry well (The Hollow Verge)
- **Party level**: Give the level a party should be before coming uninvited. Example: about level 11 (The Hollow Verge)
- **Places**: Name the sites within it. Example: the Nameless Asylum (The Hollow Verge)

## A first look

> Guidance: A few lines to read aloud as the party arrives. Make it strange, but quiet and specific.
> Example (The Hollow Verge, ETH-501): "a quiet country of grey grass and long shadows, full of people who look up when you arrive and are kind enough not to ask how."

## What you know

> Guidance: What travellers and stories say the domain is. Keep each fact to a line.
> Example (The Ossuary of Kings, ETH-503): "For centuries they couldn't die. Now they can, and they're afraid."

## Getting in

> Guidance: How the party can reach it, and whether they need an invitation. Say what it costs to come back.
> Example (The Ossuary of Kings, ETH-503): "A summons to the Colosseum can grant an exception to the general deep-realm access restriction."

## Places

> Guidance: The sites in the domain and what marks each one. Link each to its own record.
> Example (The Loom of Eternity, ETH-505): "Twelve colossal crystals pierce it, and one is dark."

## Who lives there

> Guidance: The residents and what they want from visitors. Give the party someone to talk to.
> Example (The Hollow Verge, ETH-501): "Refugees live here, people who remember too much to be comfortable in the world."

## What people say

> Guidance: Rumours from those who have been, and those who only wish they had. Some should be warnings.
> Example (The Black Horizon, ETH-507): "Travellers who come back from the Horizon don't talk about the stars."

## Secrets (GM only)

> Guidance: What lies at the heart of the domain and who really holds it. Keep it consistent with the realm's own secrets.
> Example (The Black Horizon, ETH-507): "Even the Hidden Ones avoid it."
