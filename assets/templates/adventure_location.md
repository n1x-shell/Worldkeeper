# Adventure Location

A place set up for one adventure to happen in: a dig site, a contested camp, a ruin with a job waiting. Use a Landmark for a lasting feature of the map, and a Dungeon for a site explored room by room.

## Facts

- **Where**: Name the place or region it sits in. Example: Bloodmarked Fields (The Red Excavation)
- **Adventure**: Name the adventure hook that brings the party here. Example: The Red Excavation (The Red Excavation)
- **Levels**: Give the character levels it suits. Example: level 1 to 4 (The Red Excavation)
- **Scene DC**: Give the usual difficulty for checks here. Example: 12 (The Red Excavation)
- **Leads to**: Name where the story goes next. Example: Vault of the Shard of Dominion (The Red Excavation)

## A first look

> Guidance: A few lines to read aloud as the party arrives. Put the trouble in plain sight.
> Example (The Bloodmarked Fields, LOC-002): "Somewhere in the middle, a work crew is digging, and every line is watching them."

## The situation

> Guidance: What is going on here right now, and who wants what. This is the problem the party walks into.
> Example (Vault of the Shard of Dominion, HOOK-003): "All five factions are sending someone to the Fields."

## Who is here

> Guidance: The people the party can talk to, hire, fight or help, each with one line. Link each to their own record.
> Example (Blackveil Fortress, LOC-032): "Corla Thresh, a smuggler hiring for the Blackveil job."

## Challenges

> Guidance: The obstacles and how they can be overcome, with the checks or creatures involved. Give more than one way past each one.
> Example (Vault of the Shard of Dominion: the Rooms, RUL-665): "Lighting the sigils in the order the factions came to the Marches (DC 12 Intelligence (History) to recall it) opens the way."

## Clues

> Guidance: What the party can find or learn here that points to the truth. Make each clue something they can see, hear or hold.
> Example (Vault of the Shard of Dominion: the Rooms, RUL-665): "the doors are carved with five banners kneeling to a sixth, blank one."

## Leads onward

> Guidance: Where the party can go next, and what pulls them there. A place that leads nowhere is a dead end.
> Example (The Red Excavation, HOOK-004): "It leads into Vault of the Shard of Dominion."

## Secrets (GM only)

> Guidance: The truth behind the situation that the party may uncover. Write it plainly, so you can judge how close they are.
> Example (The Red Excavation, HOOK-004): "The Shard of Dominion lies beneath the Fields at campaign start."
