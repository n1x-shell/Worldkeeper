# Building

A single made structure that people use: a fortress, temple, school, inn, forge or house. Use a Settlement for the town around it, and a Dungeon for a structure the party explores room by room.

## Facts

- **Held by**: Name the faction, family or person who owns or holds it. Example: Arcane Consortium (The Nexus Collegium)
- **Where**: Say where it stands. Example: near Zepharyos (The Nexus Collegium)
- **Used for**: Say what it is for, in a few words. Example: war-forges (The Anvil of Dominion)
- **Keeper**: Name the person who runs it day to day. Example: Forge-Mother Ilda Cragg (The Anvil of Dominion)

## A first look

> Guidance: A few lines to read aloud as the party arrives. Show its size, its state and who is about.
> Example (The Verdant Crown, LOC-055): "It is not built. It is grown: a fortress of living trees woven together, their trunks thick as towers, their branches knotted into walls."

## What it is

> Guidance: What the building is, who uses it and why it matters. One or two plain lines are enough.
> Example (The Sanctuary of the Last Light, LOC-036): "The Celestial Order's camp in the Hollow Marches, in a ruined cathedral on a hilltop."

## Inside

> Guidance: The rooms, halls or features worth knowing, and anything unusual about how it is built. This helps when the party wanders in.
> Example (The Nexus Collegium, LOC-048): "The Collegium floats in tiers above a chasm, joined by bridges of light."

## What people say

> Guidance: Rumours about the building and the people in it. Give the party a reason to look closer.
> Example (The Anvil of Dominion, LOC-047): "The deepest forges are older than the Legion."

## Who you'll find here

> Guidance: The people the party is likely to meet, each with one line on what they do here. Link each to their own record.
> Example (The Sanctuary of the Last Light, LOC-036): "Brother Abelard Ashgrove, works beside Sister Elyra Vale and treats anyone at the door."

## Secrets (GM only)

> Guidance: What the building hides: a sealed room, a hidden resident, its real purpose. Tie it to a room or a person.
> Example (The Empty House, LOC-001): "Mother Thorn refuses to enter."
