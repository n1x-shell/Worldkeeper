# Deity

A god or other power that is worshipped and answers prayers. Use a Cult for a group devoted to a power, and Cosmology for how the gods fit into the shape of the world.

## Facts

- **Portfolio**: Say what the god keeps, in a few words. Example: Light, law and mercy (Solenne, the Dawn Mother)
- **State**: Say whether the god is living, dead, sleeping or lost. Example: Living (Orrowen, the Green Antler)
- **Sign**: Describe the holy symbol. Example: A pair of antlers, often with leaves growing from them (Orrowen, the Green Antler)
- **Domains for clerics**: Name the cleric domains the god grants. Example: Nature (Orrowen, the Green Antler)
- **Holy days**: Name the festivals and their dates. Example: Seedwake, on First Seed 1, and Harvest Home, on Harvest 30 (Orrowen, the Green Antler)
- **Worshipped by**: Name the people and groups who pray to them most. Example: Farmers, hunters, herders, foresters, midwives, and the Wildborn Clans (Orrowen, the Green Antler)

## Who they are

> Guidance: The god in one or two lines, as a believer would put it. This is what a priest says first.
> Example (Orrowen, the Green Antler, DEI-905): "The wild, the seasons and beasts. The god of the Wildborn Clans, and of every farmer who ever watched the sky for rain."

## Where they are worshipped

> Guidance: The temples, shrines and holy places, and what they look like. Give the party somewhere to visit.
> Example (Orrowen, the Green Antler, DEI-905): "In groves, fields and at the edge of the woods."

## How people pray

> Guidance: The rites, offerings and rules of worship. A player cleric will use these in every session.
> Example (Orrowen, the Green Antler, DEI-905): "Outdoors, never under a roof. The first of every harvest and the first blood of every hunt go back to the ground."

## Priests and the faithful

> Guidance: How priests dress and behave, and what the god asks of ordinary believers. Small habits bring a faith to life.
> Example (Orrowen, the Green Antler, DEI-905): "Hunters ask the animal's pardon before the kill. His priests wear antlers of wood or bone."

## What people say

> Guidance: Sayings and beliefs about the god among ordinary folk. They show how the faith feels from the street.
> Example (Orrowen, the Green Antler, DEI-905): "Orrowen doesn't punish. He just lets the winter in."

## Secrets (GM only)

> Guidance: What the god really is, what it remembers, or what its priests do not know. Write it so you can answer a cleric's hardest question.
> Example (The Gods, COS-909): "Each Aetherfall rewrote the gods along with everything else, so no god remembers the earlier worlds"
