# Settlement

A place where people live together: a village, town or city. Use a Building for a single house, inn or temple within it, and a Region for the country around it.

## Facts

- **Population**: Give a rough number of people, so the party knows what to expect. Example: about 40,000 (Kaelholm)
- **Ruled by**: Name the person, council or faction in charge. Example: Warden Brin Oakhallow (Oakhallow)
- **What people say**: Give the one rumour every visitor hears first. Example: Nobody owns Cinderwell. Nobody fights there. (Cinderwell)
- **Region**: Name the region or nation it belongs to. Example: The Hollow Marches (Hollow Refuge)
- **Terrain**: List the kinds of ground in and around it, such as urban, forest or coastal. Example: urban, grassland (Hollow Refuge)

## A first look

> Guidance: A few lines to read aloud as the party arrives. Show one or two details that only this place has.
> Example (Oakhallow, SET-324): "The road goes into the trees and the light goes green."

## What you know

> Guidance: The plain facts anyone in the area could tell a stranger. Keep each one to a line.
> Example (Hollow Refuge, SET-001): "Every faction needs a place to talk, trade and patch up its wounded, so every faction leaves the Refuge alone."

## Who runs it

> Guidance: Who keeps order, how they were chosen and what they care about. The party will deal with these people sooner or later.
> Example (Ashvale, SET-301): "Three groups run things, and they agree the rite must be kept. They don't agree on much else."

## Places in town

> Guidance: The inns, shops, temples and odd corners worth visiting, each with one line on what it is. Link people who work there to their own records.
> Example (Dawnmere, SET-019): "A great bell that rings every hour, day and night. In Dawnmere, everything stops when it rings."

## Getting there

> Guidance: How far it is from the places around it, and how people travel. Rough times are enough.
> Example (Oakhallow, SET-324): "About two weeks' walk east, through the woods."

## What people say

> Guidance: Two or three more rumours heard in the streets. Each should give the party a reason to ask a question or visit somewhere.
> Example (Hollow Refuge, SET-001): "Ask enough people about the Empty House and every one of them changes the subject."

## Secrets (GM only)

> Guidance: What the town or its leaders hide. Tie each secret to a place or person in town, so the party can stumble on it.
> Example (Dawnmere, SET-019): "Seline Ashby is the Order's most reliable magistrate precisely because she has no faith at all, only law."
