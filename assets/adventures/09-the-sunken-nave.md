Title: The Sunken Nave
Arc: The Eclipse
Hook: CLT-003
Levels: 6 to 8 (written for level 6)
Linked: CLT-003, NPC-087, NPC-090, NPC-089, NPC-088, NPC-091, LOC-006, LOC-005, ART-007, DEI-907, CLT-002, NPC-084, CRE-032, CRE-031, LOC-902, COS-032, COS-033

## Premise
Once a year, at the lowest tide, the sea off the dusk coast of the Eternal Veil draws back far enough to dive to the Abyssal Hollow [LOC-006], the drowned city that lies intact beneath it, its people missing. The Drowned Saints [CLT-003] believe it's their sunken city, the one that will rise when Maruun [DEI-907] wakes. This year, Father Iskar [NPC-087] means to go down, with the half of Maruun's heart the Saints have finally found, and bring back the other.

The truth: the Tidecaller's Heart [ART-007] is half a sea god's heart, and it hungers for its missing half. Whether the Hollow is the Saints' sunken city stays open. What's certain is that the Hollow lies over the trench, where the realm is thin, and a Black Sun prophet is waiting at its door.

## Hook
- A fishing village asks the party to bring back the young people the Saints took for "baptism" at the Sunken Nave.
- Sirala "the Deep Mourner" [NPC-090], who grieves for everyone she kills, quietly asks the party to stop Iskar: she believes waking Maruun will drown everyone she's mourning for.
- Whoever has the Tidecaller's Heart, if it isn't the Saints, finds it beating harder every night, pulling west.

## Stakes
- **The baptised.** A dozen converts from the coast, to be drowned at the Hollow's door at the lowest tide, so they can come back holy. Some will. Most won't.
- **The Heart.** If both halves are joined, something wakes. What, exactly, is up to the GM: Maruun, a storm that doesn't end, or nothing at all.
- **The Return.** Vhalek "the Silent Watcher" [NPC-084] sits at the Hollow's door, over the thin place. His faithful need only a shard fragment for their rite, and the Saints have pulled one out of the Titan's Fall's bones.

## Opposition
- **The Drowned Saints:** Father Iskar, Drennal "the Barnacled Knight" [NPC-089], who will let go anyone who promises to light a candle for the drowned, and Althisa "the Saltborn" [NPC-088], who calls the tide.
- **Drowned Saints [CRE-032]**, the returned, rising from the water.
- **Vhalek's faithful:** a Black Sun Prophet [CRE-031] and 4 Cultists (Monster Manual), and Vhalek himself, who never acts. Meeting his eyes is the danger.
- **The tide.** The lowest tide lasts four hours. Then the sea comes back.

## Clues
- Elder Marrow "the Writeless Prophet" [NPC-091] has written one word in the sand at the Nave's door, and the tide hasn't taken it: "Half."
- Iskar's sermon says the city will rise when "the heart is whole". He doesn't say whose heart.
- The shard fragment came from the Titan's Fall [LOC-005], from a god's rib. The grave-robbers who sold it to the Saints are still on the beach, frightened.
- A grey-robed githzerai sits on a rock at the Hollow's edge and doesn't move when the water comes. Locals have stopped looking at him.

## Obstacles
- Reaching the Hollow means walking out across the sea floor for an hour as the tide falls, then diving 60 feet. Water Breathing, a Drowned Saint's blessing, or holding breath (DC 14 Constitution saves every 10 minutes) all work.
- The Hollow's streets are intact, lit faintly from below, and empty. Moving quietly through them takes a DC 14 Dexterity (Stealth) check, or the returned notice.
- Vhalek's eyes: anyone who meets them makes a DC 15 Wisdom save or sees the end of all things, and is Frightened for a minute.

## Encounters
- **The Sunken Nave.** At low tide the Saints' cathedral stands on the wet sand, and the converts wait inside. Drennal guards the door and will let the party pass if they promise to light a candle for the drowned. Otherwise, 2 Drowned Saints rise from the flooded aisles.
- **The Walk Out.** Crossing the sea floor as the tide falls: Althisa calls a swell to slow the party, and the returned rise in the shallows (1 Drowned Saint for each character).
- **The Door of the Hollow.** Vhalek's faithful perform their rite at the edge of the trench, the shard fragment held up to an open sky that's really a hundred feet of water. The rite takes 3 rounds. Breaking the circle, or taking the fragment, stops it.
- **The Heart (climax).** Deep in the Hollow, in a hall of drowned pews, Father Iskar holds up the Tidecaller's Heart, and something in the dark answers. The party has until the tide turns to decide what to do with the Heart, and with Iskar.

## Rewards
- The baptised, alive, and the gratitude of the coast.
- The Tidecaller's Heart, or the knowledge of where its missing half lies.
- The shard fragment, if taken from the rite.
- Standing: -1 with the Drowned Saints whatever happens; +1 with the Shadow Pact if the coast stays quiet.

## Approaches
- **Free the converts.** Stealth and speed at the Nave, before the tide falls.
- **Talk to Sirala.** She'll show the party the way through the Hollow, and turn her back while they take the Heart.
- **Let Iskar finish.** And find out what wakes.

## Consequences
- **If the halves are joined,** the GM decides what wakes. At the least, the sea off the dusk coast is never quite still again.
- **If the Heart is taken,** the Saints hunt the party for it, politely at first.
- **If Vhalek's rite succeeds,** the Return moves one stage (see What the Black Sun Knows [COS-033]), unless the Blind Seer has quietly arranged for it to fail.
- **Whether the Hollow is the Saints' city** stays open, whatever happens down there.

## If ignored
The tide goes out, and comes back. Twelve converts walk into the sea. Three come back. Every bell on the dusk coast rings a half-beat late.

## Scaling
- **Level 8.** Iskar fights (use his fight card), and two more Drowned Saints guard the Heart.
- **Levels 9 to 10.** Something answers the Heart from the dark: a Memory-Drinker [CRE-541] rising from the trench.
