Title: Echoes in the Sand
Arc: The Eclipse
Hook: FAC-504
Levels: 8 to 10 (written for level 8)
Linked: LOC-504, FAC-504, NPC-069, ETH-503, CRE-505, CLT-002, NPC-086, CRE-031, SYS-907, RUL-653, COS-032, COS-033

## Premise
A summons arrives for the party: an invitation to fight at the Colosseum of Echoes [LOC-504], in the Ossuary of Kings [ETH-503], where the Titans of the Rift [FAC-504] fight copies of themselves. A summons is an invitation into the deep realm, and the Titans send them out often. It is also the most dangerous invitation there is: in the deep realm, death is final, and the dead are Unmade.

The truth: Titan-King Varuun [NPC-069] didn't send this summons. A Black Sun prophet did, through the Titans, because a bout at the Colosseum is the only way to bring outsiders into the deep realm with a shard fragment in their gear. The prophet needs the party's arrival to open the Summoning Arch, and the Ossuary's open sky for the rite. The prophet is Isolde "the Last Ember" [NPC-086], and she doesn't want to do it.

## Hook
The summons is a black card, delivered by whatever way is least likely: under a door, in a fish, in a dream. It names each character, and says: "Come and meet yourselves." Refusing a Titans' summons is possible. Nobody has done it twice.

## Stakes
- **The party.** Each character fights their own echo. Bouts are to yielding; killing blows are allowed but not required. Outside the bouts, the deep realm's rules hold: death is final (see Thinning and Unmaking [SYS-907]).
- **Isolde.** She foresees the end and doesn't want it. She has been ordered to perform her rite here, and she will, unless someone stops her, or gives her a reason to stop herself.
- **The Return.** The deep realm is as thin as the world gets.

## Opposition
- **The echoes:** for each character, an echo that knows every move they know (use the character's own statistics; the echo has Advantage on its first attack roll, and knows any secret the character has kept from the party).
- **The Titans of the Rift,** who are not enemies, but who fight anyone who breaks the Colosseum's rules.
- **Titan-King Varuun,** who doesn't fight guests unless they insult the Sands.
- **The rite:** Isolde, a Black Sun Prophet [CRE-031] who watches her, and 4 Cultists (Monster Manual) in the stands.

## Clues
- The summons is written in a hand that isn't Varuun's. The Titans know it, and say nothing.
- A small deep gnome with a lantern sits in the highest tier for every bout and never cheers.
- Echoes in the Mirror Pens whisper about "the one who's come to end it", and none of them mean the party.
- The Champions say a summons always brings exactly the number of fighters the Sands can hold. This one brought one more.

## Obstacles
- **Getting there.** The summons opens the Summoning Arch for the party, once. Getting home needs Varuun's leave, a win on the Sands, or another way out of the deep realm (journey times are in Journeys in the Ethereal Realm [RUL-653]).
- **The Mirror Pens.** Before a bout, each character must walk past their echo in the still pools. A DC 15 Wisdom save, or the echo learns one thing the character hoped it wouldn't.
- **The crowd.** More spectators than there are people. Finding Isolde among them takes a DC 16 Wisdom (Perception) check, or a Champion's help.

## Encounters
- **The Summoning Arch.** The party arrives; the Champions greet them with courtesy and warnings. The rules: yield, and live. Kill, and be remembered.
- **Your Own Echo.** Each character's bout, on the Sands, one at a time or all at once (the party chooses; Varuun prefers all at once). Use the Echo [CRE-505] traits, and each character's own numbers.
- **The Rite in the Stands.** During the last bout, Isolde begins her rite, the shard fragment in her lantern, her face turned to the Ossuary's open sky. It takes 3 rounds. Breaking it, taking the fragment, or talking her out of it (DC 18 Charisma (Persuasion), or automatic if a character has beaten their echo without killing it) stops it.
- **Varuun's Judgement (climax).** The Titan-King rises in his box and asks why the party broke the Sands' order. The answer decides whether they go home.

## Rewards
- A Champion's place on the rail: once, the character may send their echo to fight for them, anywhere.
- Varuun's leave to come and go through the Summoning Arch.
- Isolde, freed of her rite, as an ally who sees the end and wants to stop it.
- Standing: +1 with the Titans of the Rift for each character who yielded honourably or won.

## Approaches
- **Fight fair.** Beat each echo, and keep the Sands' order.
- **Find Isolde first.** Skip the bouts, or win them fast, and get to the stands before the rite starts.
- **Talk to the echoes.** An echo knows the character's secrets. It also knows what the character wants. Some echoes yield to the truth.

## Consequences
- **If Isolde's rite succeeds,** the Return moves one stage (see What the Black Sun Knows [COS-033]), unless the Blind Seer has quietly arranged for it to fail. Isolde weeps.
- **If Isolde is freed,** she tells the party what she has foreseen, and that she isn't the only prophet ordered to perform a rite this year.
- **If a character died on the Sands,** they were Unmade. Everyone else writes one true sentence about them.

## If ignored
Refusing the summons is possible. A second summons comes, with only one name on it.

## Scaling
- **Level 10.** The echoes have the Echo [CRE-505] traits and each character's numbers plus 20 Hit Points.
- **Levels 11 to 14.** Varuun himself challenges the party's champion (use his fight card), and the rite is performed by two prophets.

Kind: encounter
Linked: CRE-505

> Under the stands, still pools lie in rows, each one reflecting someone who isn't standing over it. As you pass, the reflections turn their heads and follow you. One of them smiles your smile, a little before you do.

# Scene: The Sands
Kind: encounter
Linked: NPC-069, CRE-505

> White sand under an open, starless sky. Tiers of white stone go up and up, full of a crowd that's bigger than any crowd could be. High above, in a box carved for a giant, a goliath larger than any living goliath rests his chin on his fist and watches you. Across the sand, you walk out to meet yourself.

Linked: NPC-086, CRE-031, CLT-002

> High in the stands, a small deep gnome stands up with a lantern in both hands, and lifts it to the sky. The light in it isn't a flame: it's a splinter of crystal, burning. She's crying. She opens her mouth to say a name.
