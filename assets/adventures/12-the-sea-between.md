Title: The Sea Between
Arc: The Thirteen Vaults
Hook: VLT-309
Levels: 5 to 6 (written for level 5)
Linked: VLT-309, NPC-459, FAC-301, NPC-308, FAC-004, CRE-312, CRE-305, NPC-301, FAC-303, NPC-309, LOC-902, CON-301

## Premise
Saltgate [VLT-309] is the vault of far-speaking and the sea-roads, on Kaelmar's east coast. Like every vault, it has been sealed for three hundred years, and like every vault its key is not a thing but a shape of willing minds. Saltgate's asks for two strangers from opposite shores, and the sea between: one person of Kaelmar and one of Aetheris. Until the ships began to cross, nobody alive could make that key.

The truth: Lightkeeper Annis Tarrow [NPC-459] has known for years that the door hums loudest when one particular Aetheris captain is in port. It's Captain Wilhelmina Corr, of the *Lodestar*. Annis asks every captain her name because she's been looking for the other half of the key. Now Kaelholm has noticed too.

## Hook
- Annis asks the party to keep Captain Corr ashore one more night. She won't say why.
- Archivist-Elder Venn Tull [NPC-308] of Kaelholm [FAC-301] hires the party to escort a Scholar Corps team to Saltgate and "catalogue the door".
- A Shadow Pact [FAC-004] factor offers the party good money to be standing at Saltgate's door when it opens, and to tell the Pact what's inside first.

## Stakes
- **The sea-roads.** Saltgate's strand is far-speaking and the sea-roads. Opened, the far-speaking stones work across the Black Tides [LOC-902] for the first time in three hundred years. Whoever holds the vault holds the only voice between the continents.
- **Captain Corr.** She is half the key, and has no idea. Kaelholm would like to keep her; the Pact would like to own her.
- **Annis.** She has waited twenty years for this, and is afraid of what she'll have to give.

## Opposition
- **The Scholar Corps,** under a Kaelholm tribunal's seal (use Mage and 4 Guards from the Monster Manual), who mean to catalogue the vault before anyone else touches it, and to take Captain Corr to Kaelholm "for her safety".
- **Pact smugglers** (use 3 Silent Blade Agents [CRE-014] and a Veil Adept [CRE-012]), who want the door opened on their terms.
- **The vault's wards:** a Vault Ward [CRE-312] and 2 Walkers [CRE-305] in halt livery, who test anyone who reaches the heart without the key.

## Clues
- Annis's log: every captain's name for twenty years, and one name underlined, again and again.
- The door hums a low note when Corr is near. It hums a second note when someone from Kaelmar who loves the sea stands beside her.
- The door's carving shows two figures on two shores holding the ends of one rope, and waves between them.
- A Continuity watcher has been seen on the cliffs, writing. Warden Ilsabet Crane [NPC-309] knows the moment a vault wakes.

## Obstacles
- **The key.** One person of Kaelmar and one of Aetheris must stand at the heart together, awake, willing and honest, with the sea's sound in the room: the third part of the key is the sea between them, and it must be heard. A lie, a charm or a forced hand keeps the heart closed for a day. No roll replaces it.
- **Corr's trust.** She has to be told the truth, and choose. She'll want to know what it costs her (nothing she can see, but she'll never quite be a stranger to Kaelmar again).
- **The tide.** The vault's sea-door opens only at low water.

## Encounters
- **The Night Visitors.** The Pact comes for Captain Corr at the inn on the night before low water: 3 Silent Blade Agents who want her alive.
- **The Corps at the Door.** The Scholar Corps has fenced the vault. They'll let the party through with a Kaelholm writ, or none at all, and fight if Corr is taken past them.
- **The Halt Walkers.** Inside, 2 Walkers in halt livery ask for tickets, and the Vault Ward tests anyone who can't show the key.
- **The Heart (climax).** The key is made. As the vault wakes, both factions arrive at once, and the far-speaking stones begin to speak.

## Rewards
- A pair of Saltgate's far-speaking stones (they work as Sending Stones, and reach across the Black Tides).
- Annis Tarrow's friendship, and the vault's records of the old sea-roads: a safer lane over the trench, if anyone dares to sail it.
- Standing: +1 with Kaelholm if the Corps catalogues the vault, or +1 with the Shadow Pact if the Pact hears first; not both.

## Approaches
- **Bring the key quietly.** Get Corr and a Kaelmar companion to the heart at low water before either faction moves.
- **Bargain with Kaelholm.** Venn Tull wants the vault catalogued, and will trade a writ for the first copy of the records.
- **Let the Pact open it.** And find out what the Pact does with the only voice across the sea.

## Consequences
- **The vault opens.** The far-speaking stones work across the Black Tides. In a month, every captain on the crossing wants a pair, and the dark toll has a rival.
- **The Seventh Ward stirs.** Asleep in Arborhold, it turns over in its cradle. The Continuity notices.
- **One vault opened alone wakes only its own strand.** Nothing else in Kaelmar changes.

## If ignored
Kaelholm takes Captain Corr to the city "for her safety" and tries to make the key with her. Without someone who loves her, and the sea, it doesn't work. She sails away angry, and the *Lodestar* doesn't come back.

## Scaling
- **Level 6.** Add a second Vault Ward in the heart, and a fourth Silent Blade.
- **Levels 7 to 10.** The Pact sends a Nightglass Executioner [CRE-015], and the vault's heart is guarded by a Hunter [CRE-307].
