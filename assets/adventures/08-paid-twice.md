Title: Paid Twice
Arc: The Eclipse
Hook: CLT-004
Levels: 5 to 7 (written for level 5)
Linked: LOC-002, CLT-004, NPC-073, NPC-074, NPC-075, NPC-076, NPC-077, FAC-002, FAC-005, NPC-005, CLT-002, NPC-083, CRE-031, COS-032, COS-033, SHD-001

## Premise
Two armies are dug in across the Bloodmarked Fields [LOC-002], Legion on one side and Wildborn on the other, and neither will move. Both hired Bloodbound Company swords to break the deadlock. Both are paying. Nobody is fighting.

The truth: Selka the Pactmother [NPC-073] has quietly bought every Company contract on both sides. Whatever happens next on the Fields, the Company is paid twice, and Selka would prefer that what happens next is a long, expensive war. Her plan for it is in her ledger, the Red Ledger's copy, kept in her own hand. Meanwhile, out in the mud between the lines, a Black Sun prophet is digging for something else.

## Hook
Any of these:
- Captain Marrek Vaal [NPC-005] or a Wildborn war-chief hires the party to find out why their Bloodbound swords won't fight.
- Garek "the Coinbinder" [NPC-076] quietly hires them to steal Selka's ledger before Selka notices Garek has been skimming.
- A Fields dig crew asks for guards: a woman in black has been buying every shard splinter they turn up, and the crews who refused her have gone missing.

## Stakes
- **The Fields.** If Selka's plan works, the Fields become a war that never ends, and the Company owns both sides of it.
- **The Company.** Its one law is that a contract can't be broken. Prove Selka broke faith with her own clients, and the Company turns on her.
- **The Return.** The woman in black is Seraine "the Eclipse Blade" [NPC-083] of the Black Sun. A rite needs a shard or a fragment of one, and the Fields are full of splinters.

## Opposition
- **Bloodbound swords** (use Veteran and Bandit Captain from the Monster Manual), on both sides, who'd rather be paid to wait.
- **Lira "the Red Whisper"** [NPC-075], sent to make sure nobody reads the ledger.
- **Yulrath "the Warhound"** [NPC-077], on a chain, to be let loose if the deadlock breaks the wrong way.
- **Varik "the Bloodforged"** [NPC-074], who will fight one formal duel, by its terms, and no more.
- **Seraine's faithful** (2 Black Sun Prophets [CRE-031] and 4 Cultists from the Monster Manual), digging for splinters by night. Seraine herself won't fight a party this strong: she leaves when her rite fails.

## Clues
- The Legion's paymaster and the Wildborn's tally-keeper both pay the Company on the same day, by the same courier.
- Bloodbound pickets on both sides share a fire at night, in the middle, where nobody's supposed to be.
- Garek's abacus has two rows of black beads that always add up to the same total.
- Dig crews who sold their splinters to "the woman in black" were found with their eyes covered.
- Selka's ledger, written in her own hand, lists both armies under one heading: "Clients: the war."

## Obstacles
- Crossing between the lines by day is suicide. By night, it's only dangerous (DC 14 Dexterity (Stealth) for the party, or a hard fight with whoever spots them).
- The Red Ledger's copy travels with Selka, and Selka is never where the Company says she is.
- Neither army will believe the party without the ledger, or a Bloodbound sword who'll swear to it.

## Encounters
- **The Middle Fire.** At night, the party walks into the Bloodbound pickets' shared fire between the lines: 3 Veterans and a Bandit Captain from both sides, eating together. They fight to keep the secret, or talk, if the party talks first.
- **Lira in the Dark.** Lira comes for whoever is carrying questions about the ledger. She strikes once, from the best moment, and leaves if she misses.
- **The Splinter Pits.** Seraine's faithful at a dig at midnight: 2 Black Sun Prophets and 4 Cultists, with a cart of shard splinters. Destroying the splinters, or taking them, stops this rite.
- **The Challenge (climax).** Once the party has the ledger, Varik issues a formal challenge to their best fighter: until one yields. If the challenger wins, Varik reads the ledger aloud to the Company himself, and the Company turns on Selka. If Varik wins, he keeps the ledger, and the war goes on. Yulrath's chain is held by whoever wins.

## Rewards
- 1,000 gp from whichever army hired the party, or Garek's gratitude and a line of credit at any Company paymaster.
- Selka's ledger: blackmail on half the powers of Aetheris.
- The shard splinters, if taken: each counts as a shard fragment for anything that needs one.
- Standing: +1 with whichever army hired the party; with the Bloodbound Company, +1 if Selka falls, or -2 if she learns who did it.

## Approaches
- **Expose Selka.** Get the ledger in front of the Company. Contracts are sacred; Selka's broke faith.
- **Make peace.** Get the ledger in front of both armies at once. They stop paying, and the Fields go quiet for a season.
- **Take the contract.** Buy Selka's contracts out with something she wants more. She'll listen. She always listens.

## Consequences
- **If Selka falls,** Varik takes the Company, and keeps every contract to the letter, which is worse for some people than Selka ever was.
- **If the armies learn,** they stop paying, and the Fields go quiet for a season. The digging gets easier, for everyone.
- **If Seraine's faithful get away with the splinters,** she performs her rite at the Rift a month later, unless someone stops her there. If she succeeds, the Return moves one stage (see What the Black Sun Knows [COS-033]). Unless the Blind Seer sabotages it, quietly, as he does.

## If ignored
The Fields stay dug in for a year, the Company paid twice the whole time. Seraine gets her splinters. Somewhere in the Marches, every bell rings a half-beat late.

## Scaling
- **Level 7.** Add a second Bandit Captain at the Middle Fire, and Lira brings a second Silent Blade.
- **Levels 8 to 10.** Seraine stays to fight at the Splinter Pits (use her fight card), and Yulrath is let loose at the start of the Challenge.
