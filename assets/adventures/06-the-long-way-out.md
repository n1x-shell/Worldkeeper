Title: The Long Way Out
Arc: The Loose Thread
Hook: HOOK-931
Levels: 2 to 4 (written for level 2)
Linked: LOC-506, ETH-501, NPC-376, NPC-374, NPC-394, NPC-371, NPC-372, NPC-404, NPC-400, NPC-403, CLT-007, NPC-095, CRE-501, CRE-502, CRE-504, CRE-505, CRE-014, REG-001, LOC-003, HOOK-931

## Premise
The Lamplighter is himself again, from the grey grass onward, and he knows his name: Lemmy Carrow [NPC-376]. The silver thread on his wrist leads off across the Verge toward the other two ends, and he wants to deliver something he can't remember. To tie the thread back, he has to reach the waystone of the Crossroads of Fate [LOC-003], in Aetheris. Getting him out of the Hollow Verge and across the world is the adventure the Asylum has been dreading.

The truth: the Name-Eater didn't wander in. The Hollow Tribunal [CLT-007] sent it, to see who would come for a half-unmade courier, and whether a cut thread of the Loom could be tied again. They learned about the thread from Dannick Thrale [NPC-394], the Veilborn runner who reports to them on Sister Nobody [NPC-374] because he needs their money to buy his sister back from the Fields. Now the Tribunal wants Lemmy themselves.

## Hook
Keeper Lantern [NPC-371] asks the party, gravely, to take the Lamplighter home: "The lamps can wait. He can't." Sister Nobody adds, privately: "A Name-Eater that old doesn't wander. Something sent it. Be careful who you trust on the road." Lemmy just wants to go. He keeps his satchel packed by the Threshold Door.

## Stakes
- **Lemmy.** He is the only living end of the thread. If he's Unmade on the way, the thread can never be tied back.
- **The Asylum's secret.** If the Tribunal learns that Sister Nobody was once one of their judges, they'll come for her too.
- **Dannick.** He's not a bad man. He's a desperate one. What happens to him depends on what the party does when they find out.

## Opposition
- **Tribunal hunters**, masked and silent, two of them at first (use Silent Blade Agent [CRE-014], with grey masks and the ability to speak a verdict instead of Sneak Attack: DC 12 Wisdom save or Frightened until the end of its next turn).
- **Greylings [CRE-501]**, drawn to a man whose name was nearly gone.
- **An Echo of Lemmy [CRE-505]**, left behind in the Verge thirty-one years ago, which wants his life back.
- **Threshold Hounds [CRE-504]**, who don't oppose anyone. They decide who passes.

## Clues
- Dannick has more money than a runner should, and he's spent none of it.
- The masked hunters know Lemmy's name. Nobody outside the Asylum should.
- Ooma [NPC-403], who remembers every story word for word, remembers Dannick asking about the Lamplighter's thread, three nights before the Name-Eater came.
- Gauge's [NPC-400] chart shows the way to where the Rift opens into Aetheris. It's right about where, and wrong about how far.
- The Echo wears Lemmy's face as it was thirty-one years ago, and knows the courier's tune with the extra bar.
- At the Rift's mouth, the air smells of the Hollow Marches: wet grass and old iron.

## Obstacles
- The Verge's paths move. Without a guide, the party loses a day on every leg. Tessk [NPC-372] or Tch'kala [NPC-404] can guide, for a price or for friendship.
- Lemmy can't hurry. He's half unmade, and walks like a man much older. Once per day, he must make a Thinning save; the party can make it for him, as an anchor, if one of them holds his name.
- Saying Lemmy's name aloud in the Verge draws every Greyling for a mile.

## Encounters
- **Leaving the Asylum.** Two Greylings [CRE-501] wait in the grass outside the Threshold Door, turning toward any name spoken. At level 3 or higher, four.
- **The Echo on the Path.** An Echo [CRE-505] with Lemmy's young face steps out of the grey and asks him for his life back. It fights only if Lemmy refuses, and it is perfectly reasonable until then.
- **The Hunters.** On the second night, the Tribunal's hunters come for Lemmy. Two hunters, and a Greyling [CRE-501] they've brought on a leash, which turns toward Lemmy wherever he hides. At level 3 or higher, the thing on the leash is a Name-Eater [CRE-502]. They fight to take him alive.
- **The Threshold Hounds (climax).** At the Rift's mouth, two Threshold Hounds [CRE-504] decide who passes. They'll let the living through. They won't let a half-unmade man through without someone vouching for his name. Meanwhile the hunters catch up for a last try.

## Rewards
- Lemmy, alive, in Aetheris, a short walk from the Crossroads of Fate.
- The Asylum's gratitude: +1 standing with the Veilborn [FAC-503], and Sister Nobody's trust.
- If Dannick is saved rather than exposed: a friend in the Verge who knows every path, and a sister to rescue from the Fields (an adventure of its own).
- A Tribunal mask, which lets its wearer pass as one of them once, before the Tribunal notices it's missing.

## Approaches
- **Fast and quiet.** A guide, no names spoken, and a hard pace. Lemmy's Thinning saves get harder (+1 DC each day).
- **Slow and safe.** Take the long paths. The hunters catch up sooner.
- **Turn the hunter.** Unmasked, a hunter is a frightened clerk from a court nobody remembers. A party that spares one learns the Tribunal wants to know who can tie a cut thread, and why.
- **Find the informer.** A party that suspects Dannick can confront him before the road (DC 13 Wisdom (Insight)) or catch him leaving a message on the road.

## Consequences
- **Lemmy reaches Aetheris.** The thread on his wrist pulls toward the Crossroads, half a day away. The other two ends, wherever they are, pull toward him. Tying them back is the end of the Loose Thread.
- **Dannick exposed.** The Asylum throws him out. The Tribunal uses him up. His sister stays lost.
- **Dannick saved.** He tells the party everything he told the Tribunal, and that Justicar Quell [NPC-095] asked about the thread in person.
- **The Tribunal now knows the party's names.** Justicar Quell watches them from now on.

## If ignored
Lemmy leaves alone one night, through the Threshold Door. The Tribunal's hunters are waiting. A week later the Asylum's lamps go out again, and this time nobody remembers they were ever lit.

## Scaling
- **Level 3 or 4.** Three hunters, and their leader is a Veil Adept [CRE-012] in a grey mask. The Echo brings two Greylings.
- **Levels 5 to 10.** The Tribunal sends an Abandoned Future [CRE-524] in Lemmy's own shape, the courier he would have been if he'd arrived on time. At the Rift, Justicar Quell [NPC-095] waits in person, and judges the party.

# Scene: The Satchel by the Door
Kind: social
Linked: LOC-506, NPC-371, NPC-374, NPC-376, NPC-403

> The Lamplighter sits on the bench by the Threshold Door with his satchel on his knees, as if the coach will be along any minute. The silver thread on his wrist lies across the floor and out under the door, taut as a fishing line. "I'm late," he says, when you sit down beside him. "I've been late for thirty-one years. I'd like to stop."

Lantern asks the party to take him. Sister Nobody gives her warning. Ooma, if asked, remembers everything anyone has said about the Lamplighter, including who asked about his thread.

# Scene: Names in the Grass
Kind: encounter
Linked: ETH-501, CRE-501

> The grey grass goes on forever under a sky like an old sheet. Somewhere out there, faint figures turn, all at once, toward you. Someone has said a name.

Greylings drift in from every side when a name is spoken. A party that learns quickly to talk without names gets through this leg without a fight.

# Scene: Himself, Younger
Kind: encounter
Linked: CRE-505, NPC-376

> A young man steps out of the mist ahead, in a courier's cap and coat, with burned fingers and a satchel exactly like Lemmy's. He smiles at Lemmy the way you smile at an old friend. "You've been wearing my life," he says, kindly. "I'd like it back now."

The Echo is reasonable, sad and absolutely certain it deserves to finish its life. If Lemmy talks to it, the party learns what the message was: "Tell them I'm coming home." If the party fights it, Lemmy grieves for a day.

# Scene: Masks in the Night
Kind: encounter
Linked: CLT-007, CRE-014, CRE-501, CRE-502, NPC-394

> The fire burns low. Out in the grey, two grey masks catch the light: no faces behind them, just masks, at head height, coming closer. Behind them something on a leash drips.

The hunters speak only to deliver a verdict: "The courier is required." After the fight, whatever the party does, Dannick is somewhere he shouldn't be: on the path behind them, or leaving a message under a stone.

# Scene: The Rift's Mouth
Kind: climax
Linked: CRE-504, REG-001, LOC-003

> The grass ends at a tear in the grey, tall as a tower, and through it you can see green: wet grass, old iron, a crow on a broken standard. The Hollow Marches. Two black hounds lie across the opening, and they lift their heads as you come.

The hounds let the living pass. For Lemmy, someone must say his name and mean it: the character who does takes his Thinning save for him. Then the hunters come for their last try, with the green world one step away.
