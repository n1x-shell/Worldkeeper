Title: The First Fire
Arc: The Eclipse
Hook: CLT-001
Levels: 7 to 9 (written for level 7)
Linked: CLT-001, LOC-004, NPC-078, NPC-082, NPC-080, NPC-081, NPC-079, NPC-040, CLT-002, NPC-085, CRE-030, CRE-031, FAC-001, COS-032, COS-033

## Premise
The Ashen Covenant [CLT-001] is gathering its fourth expedition into the Ashen Wastes [LOC-004], to find the First Fire that burns there without fuel. Three expeditions have failed. This one has Vexis's maps, Hadrek's faith and Aedrin's protection, and it will get there.

The truth: the Covenant is splitting. Hadrek "the Emberborn" [NPC-078] wants to carry the First Fire out of the Wastes and burn the world with it. Elder Thaelon "the Last Spark" [NPC-082] wants only to see it again. Aedrin "the Cinderfather" [NPC-080], who can't die, will follow whichever of them stands beside the Fire at the end. What the First Fire is stays open. What the GM knows is that the realm is thin around it, and a fallen paladin of the Black Sun wants it for a rite.

## Hook
- The Celestial Order [FAC-001] hires the party to follow the expedition and make sure no fire comes out of the Wastes.
- Elder Thaelon asks the party to come with him, as guards, as witnesses, and because he trusts strangers more than his own people now.
- Vexis "the Infernal Architect" [NPC-081] sells the party a copy of the maps, and a warning: Vexis has been paid to make the Fire portable, and doesn't want to be the one who did it.

## Stakes
- **The world.** If Hadrek carries the Fire out, the Covenant has its rebirth, and cities burn.
- **The Covenant.** If Thaelon wins, the Covenant becomes something other than a fire cult. If Hadrek wins, it becomes what everyone already thinks it is.
- **The Return.** Cassiel "the False Dawn" [NPC-085], who decided the gods lied, has joined the expedition in disguise. The Fire is older than the gods, and the place is thin.

## Opposition
- **The Wastes.** A burned empire where the fire never dies: heat, ash storms and ruins still burning after a thousand years.
- **Hadrek's zealots:** Ashen Zealots [CRE-030], led by Hadrek and Sister Kallisa "the Pyre-Singer" [NPC-079].
- **Cassiel's faithful:** 2 Black Sun Prophets [CRE-031], hidden among the Covenant, and Cassiel, who won't fight a party this strong: he leaves when his rite fails.
- **Aedrin,** who stands with whoever stands with the Fire, and can't be killed.

## Clues
- Thaelon remembers the First Fire from before the gods. He's never said how.
- Vexis's maps have a second route marked in a different ink: someone wanted to arrive before the expedition.
- One of the expedition's preachers prays facing away from the fire, at the sky, at noon.
- The ruins of the Wastes are carved with the same scene over and over: a flame, and seven figures kneeling round it, and an eighth figure standing.

## Obstacles
- The Wastes' heat: each day, every character makes a DC 14 Constitution save or gains a level of Exhaustion, unless they have Vexis's fire-cloaks or resist Fire.
- Ash storms: once a day (on a 1-2 on a d6), an ash storm blinds everything beyond 10 feet for an hour.
- The Kiln: the Covenant's temple, a dead Legion forge-town where the fires never go out, and the only way into the Wastes the expedition will use.

## Encounters
- **Leaving the Kiln.** The expedition departs in a column of singing zealots. Hadrek's faithful test the party: a test of faith by fire (DC 14 Wisdom save to walk the coals, or a fight with 4 Ashen Zealots if refused).
- **The Burning Ruins.** An ash storm drives the expedition into ruins that are still on fire. Fire-touched things live there (use Fire Elementals and Magma Mephits from the Monster Manual).
- **The Rite in the Ash.** Near the First Fire, Cassiel's faithful break away to perform their rite: a prophet's true name spoken at the open sky, with a shard fragment, in the heat of the Fire. The rite takes 3 rounds. Breaking the circle, or taking the fragment, stops it.
- **The First Fire (climax).** A flame on bare stone, burning without fuel, as tall as a tower. Hadrek reaches for it with a vessel Vexis built. Thaelon kneels. Aedrin stands between them, waiting to see who wins.

## Rewards
- An ember of the First Fire, if the party takes one: it never goes out, and no fire can harm whoever carries it while it burns (the GM decides its full nature).
- Vexis's thanks, and anything Vexis can build in a month.
- The Order's gratitude, if no fire comes out of the Wastes.
- Standing: +1 with the Celestial Order or -1, depending; the Ashen Covenant splits, and the party has friends in whichever half they helped.

## Approaches
- **Back Thaelon.** Help him reach the Fire first, and see it. Aedrin stands with him, and Hadrek's zealots break.
- **Stop Hadrek.** Destroy Vexis's vessel, or stop Hadrek in person.
- **Let it burn.** Let Hadrek carry the Fire out, and follow it, ready.

## Consequences
- **If Hadrek carries the Fire out,** the Covenant has its rebirth, and the first city it reaches burns. Aedrin walks beside it.
- **If Thaelon sees it,** he says what he remembers, quietly, to the party alone. What he says is the GM's to decide. The Covenant becomes a pilgrimage.
- **If Cassiel's rite succeeds,** the Return moves one stage (see What the Black Sun Knows [COS-033]), unless the Blind Seer has quietly arranged for it to fail.
- **What the First Fire is** stays open, whatever happens.

## If ignored
The expedition reaches the Fire. Hadrek comes back with it. Somewhere in the Tyrant's Dominion, a town burns, and every bell in it rings a half-beat late.

## Scaling
- **Level 9.** Hadrek fights (use their fight card), and Pyromancer Elaris Duskbane [NPC-040] has come to see the Fire too, on Hadrek's side.
- **Level 10.** Cassiel stays to fight at the rite (use his fight card).

# Scene: The First Fire
Kind: climax
Linked: NPC-078, NPC-082, NPC-080

Hadrek walks toward it, singing, carrying a vessel of black iron. Elder Thaelon kneels, and weeps. Between them, a crowned figure wrapped in fire turns his head to watch you, and waits.
