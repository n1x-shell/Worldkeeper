Title: The Dreaming Fen
Arc: The Thirteen Vaults
Hook: VLT-302
Levels: 6 to 7 (written for level 6)
Linked: VLT-302, NPC-453, NPC-310, FAC-304, FAC-305, NPC-311, CRE-306, CRE-309, CRE-312, CRE-313, NPC-301, REG-305

## Premise
Mirewell [VLT-302], in the southern fens [REG-305], is the vault of taking the impression of a living mind: the first step of Kaelith's Great Work. Its key asks for the dreamer, the waker, and one who remembers both. Someone must give up a dream to open it.

The truth: Agathe Mudge [NPC-453], the fen-witch who sleeps near Mirewell, has been selling dreams for years. They're the vault's own: thousands of impressions of Kaelith minds, leaking out through her sleep. The vault is dreaming of being opened, by one particular person. Maro Grane [NPC-310] of the Convergence Collective [FAC-304] wants to read the Root Texts aloud at its door, with witnesses. Marshal Dace Corvane [NPC-311] of the Ascendancy [FAC-305] wants the impressions themselves.

## Hook
- A dream bought from Agathe shows a character a door in the fen, and someone they love waiting on the other side.
- Maro Grane asks the party to be his witnesses at Mirewell: a reading of the Root Texts needs people who don't believe them.
- Fen-folk ask for help: people who bought Agathe's dreams have started sleepwalking into the marsh.

## Stakes
- **The impressions.** Mirewell holds impressions of Kaelith minds: not people, but perfect copies of how they thought. The Ascendancy wants them to fill the vessels of Cradlehorn. The Collective wants them as witnesses to the change. Kaelholm would like them catalogued and shut away.
- **The dreamer.** Someone must give up a dream to open the vault: a real one, the kind you have every night and wake up glad of. It doesn't come back.
- **The sleepwalkers.** A dozen fen-folk wandering toward Mirewell in their sleep.

## Opposition
- **Corvane's detachment** (use Veteran and Scout from the Monster Manual, 6 in all), with an empty vessel on a cart, waiting for the door to open.
- **Half-made [CRE-306]** in the reeds, drawn by the dreams, who think the impressions are meant for them.
- **The Scattered [CRE-309]**, minds without bodies, who will take the sleepwalkers' bodies if nobody stops them.
- **The vault's wards:** a Vault Ward [CRE-312], and a Thing from the Between [CRE-313] that has crept in through the dreaming.

## Clues
- Every dream Agathe sells has the same door in it.
- The sleepwalkers all say the same name in their sleep. It's the name of the person the vault is dreaming of.
- Maro Grane's copy of the Root Texts has a chapter about Mirewell that he wrote before he'd ever heard of it.
- Corvane's cart carries a vessel with an empty face, and a tally-board with one space left to fill.

## Obstacles
- **The key.** The dreamer, the waker, and one who remembers both: one character gives up a dream, another wakes them, and a third must remember both the dream and the waking. Awake, willing and honest. No roll replaces it.
- **The fen.** Moving through the deep fen takes a guide or a DC 15 Wisdom (Survival) check each hour; failure costs an hour and a fight.
- **Sleep.** Anyone who sleeps within a mile of Mirewell dreams the vault's dreams (DC 15 Wisdom save or wake an hour later, somewhere else).

## Encounters
- **The Sleepwalkers.** At dusk, the fen-folk walk into the marsh, and 2 of the Scattered follow them, looking for a way in. Waking a sleepwalker takes their true name.
- **The Reading.** Maro Grane reads the Root Texts aloud at the vault's door. Half-made rise from the reeds to listen, and to take.
- **Corvane's Cart.** The Ascendancy detachment moves to take the door the moment it opens. They'll talk first: Corvane always does.
- **The Dreaming Heart (climax).** Inside, the impressions hang in the air like mist with faces. The Thing from the Between is feeding on them. The party chooses what to do with them before Corvane's vessel reaches the room.

## Rewards
- What the impressions remember: the Great Work as Kaelith's ordinary people saw it, and one answer to one question about Kaelith (the GM chooses).
- Agathe's gratitude: she can sleep without selling anything, for the first time in years.
- Maro Grane's friendship, if the party witnessed honestly, whatever they believed.
- Standing: +1 with the Convergence Collective or -1 with the Ascendancy, depending on what happens to the impressions.

## Approaches
- **Seal it again.** Open the vault, then let the impressions rest. Nobody gets them.
- **Give them a voice.** Let the impressions speak to whoever will listen. The Collective is delighted.
- **Let Corvane have one.** One impression, in one vessel. Someone from three hundred years ago wakes up in a body made for war.

## Consequences
- **The vault opens.** The fen stops dreaming. Agathe sleeps.
- **If an impression goes into a vessel,** the Ascendancy has its first soldier with a Kaelith mind, and Corvane's count goes up by one.
- **The Seventh Ward stirs again.** It whispers, in its sleep, a name from the impressions.

## If ignored
The sleepwalkers reach Mirewell. The Scattered take three of them. Corvane opens the door with someone else's dream, and fills his vessel.

## Scaling
- **Level 7.** Add a Shambling Mound [CRE-303] in the deep fen, and a second Thing from the Between.
- **Levels 8 to 10.** Corvane comes in person (use his fight card), with a Hunter [CRE-307], and the empty vessel on his cart is a Vessel [CRE-311] that wakes if an impression is put into it.
