Title: The Lightless Crossing
Arc: The Loose Thread
Hook: HOOK-931
Levels: 2 to 4 (written for level 2)
Linked: SET-001, REG-005, SET-022, NPC-369, LOC-053, NPC-437, LOC-902, VLT-309, NPC-459, CRE-032, CRE-033, CRE-014, NPC-376, HOOK-931, COS-032

## Premise
The thread's end the party took from the dead man at the Crooked Nail won't stop tugging east, across the Black Tides, toward Ashvale and the second end. Ships cross from the ports of the Eternal Veil to Kaelmar in about two weeks of dark, with every lantern doused over the trench. The party has to get to the coast, find a ship that will take them, and survive the crossing.

The truth: the thread glows. Only faintly, and only in true dark, but over the trench nothing is darker. Harbourmaster Gulen Saltback [NPC-369] of Mothhaven [SET-022] lit the only lantern on the first crossing in Year 11, and something in the Rift beneath the Tides looked back. It has spoken to him ever since, and he keeps every light off the water so it can't find the ships. A silver thread of the Loom is a light it would very much like to find.

## Hook
The thread does the hooking: every night it pulls east, a little harder. If the party needs more, Mara Venn [NPC-010] gives them a name, the Crooked Nail's own contact in Mothhaven ("ask for Gulen, and don't believe a word he says about the Pact"), and a letter of credit for passage.

Or Courier-Captain Shae Darrow [NPC-437] finds them first, with a letter (see the Umbral Paths scene).

## Stakes
- **The thread.** If the thread is lost overboard, it sinks toward the trench, and the second and third ends feel it go dark. It can be found again only at the bottom of the Black Tides.
- **The ship and its crew.** Twenty sailors who trusted the party's word that they carried nothing dangerous.
- **Gulen.** He has kept the Tides dark for twenty years. If the thing below finds a ship by the party's light, he will believe it was his fault.

## Opposition
- **Something below the Tides**, never seen: a pull toward the dark, voices in the bilges, and the water rising where it shouldn't. What it is stays undefined.
- **Drowned Saints [CRE-032]**, who come up the anchor chain when the light shows: the drowned who answer what's below.
- **Thread Mites [CRE-033]**, drawn to the loose end, which follow the party aboard in a crate of salt fish.
- **Pact smugglers** (Silent Blade Agents [CRE-014]) who want to know what the party is carrying that Gulen is so afraid of.

## Clues
- The thread glows in true dark. A character who wraps it and sits in a sealed hold can see a faint silver light through three layers of cloth.
- Gulen, asked about the first crossing, says only: "One lantern. Mine." He won't talk about what he saw.
- In Mothhaven they say the first crew brought someone back with them. They won't say who. (Gulen did. He knows it.)
- Shae Darrow's letter is addressed to Lemmy Carrow, Courier, care of the Crooked Nail, and dated thirty-one years ago. It says: "Come home by the west road. They're keeping your room."
- On the third night out, the ship's bell rings a half-beat late, and nobody touched the rope. (The first omen of the Return.)
- Captain Wilhelmina Corr's ship always makes landfall near Saltgate [VLT-309], whatever course she sets. She thinks it's the currents.

## Obstacles
- No captain will carry a passenger Gulen has refused, and Gulen refuses anyone whose cargo "shows in the dark".
- Over the trench, any light at all draws the pull. A character carrying the thread must make a DC 12 Wisdom save each night or wake on deck, at the rail, with the thread in their hand.
- Tidecant, the sailors' pidgin of Common and Kaelish: a party that can't speak it pays twice for everything and hears half of what the crew whispers.

## Encounters
- **The Umbral Paths (optional, any time before the coast).** Shae Darrow offers to take the party to Mothhaven by the Shadow Pact's roads in two days instead of nearly three weeks, for a favour later. On the way, a Shadow Lasher [CRE-016] hunting the Paths takes an interest in the thread. Shae fights beside them.
- **The Pier (Mothhaven).** Pact smugglers (two Silent Blade Agents [CRE-014], a third on a rooftop at level 3 or higher) try to steal the "glowing cargo" from the party's room by night. They fight to get away, not to kill.
- **The Salt-Fish Crate (first week at sea).** A swarm of Thread Mites (1d4 + 2 [CRE-033]) bursts from the cargo, drawn to the thread. Any sailor they bite forgets the name of a friend aboard.
- **Up the Anchor Chain (over the trench, climax).** When the thread's light shows, two Drowned Saints [CRE-032] climb the anchor chain in the dark, and at level 3 or higher a third comes up through the bilges. The water around the ship rises in a slow swell toward the light. If the light is hidden again (wrapped, sunk in a sealed box, swallowed by someone brave), the swell drops and the Saints fight on alone.

## Rewards
- Passage to Kaelmar, landing within a few days' walk of Ashvale.
- Gulen's trust, and his story, if the party keeps the light hidden over the trench: he lit the lantern, it looked back, and he has listened to it ever since. He owes them, and will hide them in Mothhaven if they ever need it.
- Captain Corr's friendship and a standing berth on her ship; and a mystery: why Saltgate hums when she's near.
- Shae Darrow's letter, which Lemmy will want, and which proves to anyone in the Refuge that he was real.
- Standing: +1 with the Shadow Pact if the smugglers walk away unhurt.

## Approaches
- **Talk Gulen round.** Convince him the light can be hidden (DC 14 Persuasion, with Advantage if they show they've tested it in the dark). He gives them a lead-lined box and a captain who owes him.
- **Smuggle it.** Take passage with another captain and keep the thread hidden. Gulen finds out on the pier, and comes aboard himself rather than let them sail without him.
- **The fast way.** Shae and the Umbral Paths get them to the coast quickly, but the Pact now knows exactly what they carry.
- **The brave way.** Over the trench, a character holds the thread in their mouth all night, in the dark. It works. They don't sleep, and they dream the courier's tune for a week.

## Consequences
- If the thing below found the ship, Gulen never forgives himself, and the next ship to cross is lost. The crossings stop for a season unless the party does something about it.
- If Gulen trusts them, he tells them what the voice in the dark says to him: it is "keeping a room" too.
- Captain Corr lands them near Saltgate. Annis Tarrow is waiting on the shore with a lamp, and asks the captain her name, as she always does.
- The party arrives in Kaelmar with the thread, the letter, and a feeling that the bells of both continents are a little late.

## If ignored
The thread's pull grows each week. After a month, the character carrying it sleepwalks toward the coast, and wakes on a pier in Mothhaven with no memory of the road.

## Scaling
- **Level 3 or 4.** Add a third smuggler, a third Drowned Saint, and an Eclipse-Touched [CRE-540] stowaway among the passengers who wants the thread for the Black Sun.
- **Levels 5 to 10.** The pull from the trench becomes a Memory-Drinker [CRE-541] rising under the keel. Its first tentacle comes over the rail on the last night.

# Scene: The Pull East
Kind: scene
Linked: SET-001, NPC-010, NPC-376

> The thread is warm in your hand, the way it has been since the dead man faded at the waystone. Tonight, for the first time, it pulls. Gently, the way a dog pulls toward home. East. Always east. Past the Bloodmarked Fields, past the mountains, toward the sea nobody sails at night.

Mara Venn listens, and goes quiet. She gives them the letter of credit, and the name. If anyone mentions the room she keeps, she says she's keeping it for someone, and she doesn't know who.

# Scene: The Umbral Paths
Kind: encounter
Linked: LOC-053, NPC-437, CRE-016

> The road goes into shadow and comes out somewhere else. The light's wrong, the birds are wrong, and the kenku walking beside you hasn't stopped talking in other people's voices since you set off.

Shae Darrow knows every turning, and gets nothing wrong except the date. She has a letter for "a courier named Carrow". If the party asks how she came by it, she says she's carried it nine years. The letter is dated thirty-one years ago.

# Scene: Mothhaven Pier
Kind: social
Linked: SET-022, NPC-369

> Lantern-moths clot round the only lit windows on the coast. The town creaks on its stilts. On the end of the longest pier, an old tortle sits fishing, and he's been watching you since you came round the headland.

Gulen asks what they're carrying before they've said a word. If they lie, he knows. He takes them to the end of the pier at moonset, has them hold out what they carry, and watches the water. If they've hidden it well, he nods. If not, the water under the pier goes very still, and he says, quietly, "No."

# Scene: Night Visitors
Kind: encounter
Linked: CRE-014

The Pact's people come for the cargo the night before sailing. Run the fight in the party's room and on the rooftops. If the party catches one, she says the Pact doesn't want the thing, it wants to know why Gulen is afraid of it. Nobody has seen him afraid before.

# Scene: The Crate of Salt Fish
Kind: encounter
Linked: CRE-033

> Four days out, the sea goes from grey to black, and the crew stops talking above a whisper. In the hold, something ticks inside a crate of salt fish.

The Thread Mites come out at dusk. If the party protects the crew, the sailors start calling them "the quiet ones" and share their Tidecant.

# Scene: Over the Trench
Kind: climax
Linked: LOC-902, CRE-032, COS-032

> Every lantern is out. The captain stands at the wheel with her eyes closed, steering by the feel of the swell. Below the keel, the water goes down further than anyone wants to think about. The ship's bell rings once, a half-beat late, and nobody is near the rope.

Whoever carries the thread makes the Wisdom save. Then the light shows, however briefly, and the Drowned Saints come up the chain. Keep the swell rising one step a round: on the third step the deck tilts toward the light, and on the fifth the sea comes over the rail. Hiding the light drops it back a step each round.

# Scene: Landfall
Kind: scene
Linked: VLT-309, NPC-459

> Dawn, of a sort. Grey cliffs, a calm sea in a storm, and on the headland a single lamp burning in broad daylight. A woman in oilskins stands beside it, watching the ship come in as if she's been expecting it for years.

Annis Tarrow asks Captain Corr her name, as she asks every captain. This time, when the captain answers, the cliffs hum: one low note, through the rock. Annis writes something in her log. The thread in the party's hand pulls west, inland, toward Ashvale.
