Title: The Hum Under the West Road
Arc: The Loose Thread
Hook: HOOK-301
Levels: 1 to 10 (written for level 1)
Linked: SET-301, LOC-305, NPC-306, NPC-304, NPC-305, NPC-341, LOC-301, LOC-303, FAC-306, FAC-307, FAC-308, NPC-376, HOOK-931

## Premise
Chief Wright Brenna Halvor [NPC-306] pays for salvage from the ruined waygate [LOC-305] under Ashvale's west road. It's an under-road halt on the old line from Cradlehorn, and something down there still hums. Everyone in town has heard it at night through the floorboards. Lately it's louder.

The truth: the hum is a voice. On the night the shards were cut from the Loom, the courier Lemmy Carrow [NPC-376] was halfway along the Crossroads road, which was coming out that night under Ashvale's west road. He was calling ahead, as couriers did, to say he was nearly there. The waygate caught the call and has been trying to deliver it ever since. It is the second end of the loose thread.

## Hook
Brenna offers 15 gp each, and first pick of one salvaged piece, for a cart of working parts: "Brass, glass that isn't the bad kind, anything with a hum in it. Especially the thing with the hum."

Two others find the party before they go down. Ritekeeper Orla Hesk [NPC-304] asks them, quietly, to come back and tell her what the hum sounds like, and nobody else. Master Speaker Tevan Lin [NPC-305] asks them to listen to it properly, "not with ears."

## Stakes
- **Ashvale's three groups.** Brenna wants the parts, Orla wants the hum hidden, and Tevan wants it heard. Whatever the party brings up decides who's angry with whom for a season.
- **The tune.** The hum has the tune of Ashvale's keeping song, with one more bar at the end that nobody in town has ever heard. If the wrong ears hear it, the song's secret is less secret.
- **The thread.** If the waygate's heart is torn out without care, the call stops forever, and this end of the thread with it.

## Opposition
- **The waygate itself**, which wants to finish its delivery and will pull the nearest traveller along the line to do it.
- **Its keepers**, a halt-guard Walker [CRE-305] still on duty and the Thread Mites [CRE-033] the loose thread draws.
- **Mild pressure in town.** Orla and Brenna, each sure they're right.

## Clues
- The hum keeps the tune of the keeping song. Any Ashvale child would know it. Jory Hesk [NPC-341] knows it first.
- It isn't a machine sound. Close up, it's someone humming with their mouth closed, the way you do when your hands are full.
- The halt's signal board still shows one arrival due, from "Crossroads, shifting." The time on it is thirty-one years old.
- The roots in the walls are circuit-roots [CRE-353]. Listening through them (Wisdom (Perception), DC 12) brings snatches of a man's voice: "...nearly there... keep the room..."
- At the heart of the halt, a silver thread runs from the speaking-stone into the floor, then simply stops. Thread Mites cluster on it like bees on a comb.
- The extra bar at the end of the tune is a name, hummed. Anyone who has heard "Lemmy" said aloud (at the Crooked Nail, say) recognises its shape.

## Obstacles
- The stair down is half-collapsed. Getting a cart down needs a rope and a plan, and getting it back up needs both twice.
- A seep of glass [CRE-354] has opened in the platform. Crossing near it calls for a Dexterity (Acrobatics) check, DC 10, and stepping in means the Thinning save [SYS-907].
- The waygate's pull. When the hum swells, the nearest creature makes a Charisma save, DC 11, or is drawn 10 feet toward the speaking-stone. At the stone, the save is the Thinning save instead.

## Encounters
Three fights, and the last one comes down the line at the party.
- **The platform.** Four Thread Mites in a cloud, drawn to anyone carrying another end of the thread (they flock to it) or to the most recently bonded pair in the party.
- **The signal room.** A halt-guard Walker that still checks tickets. A courier's token (from the Market of Lost Things), a convincing ticket (Deception or Persuasion, DC 13) or the right word (the waybill's code) gets the party past it. Otherwise it fights until reduced to half, then stands aside.
- **The track (on the way to the heart).** Three Half-made [CRE-306] stand on the line in lockstep, facing the stone like passengers waiting for a train. They turn as one when anyone passes between them and the hum, and fight in lockstep: they all move and attack on the same initiative count.
- **The heart (climax).** Whatever the party does at the speaking-stone (tears out the heart, frees the call or seals it), the waygate tries one last time to complete its delivery, and everything on the line comes with it. The hum becomes a roar, the pull's save happens every round, and down the dark track come a fresh cloud of Thread Mites, two more Half-made, and the Walker if it's still standing, all trying to reach the stone. The party has to hold the brass ring for three rounds while it settles.

## Rewards
- Brenna's pay, plus a cart of good brass, lenses and working fittings worth 60 gp to the Wrights.
- First pick for each character: a **courier's lantern** that never needs oil (it does go out near glass), or a **halt key**, which opens any under-road door that hasn't been sealed with a rite.
- The **thread's second end**, if the party frees the call instead of cutting it. The voice goes into whatever they offer it: a shell, a music box, a bottle, their own throat. Whoever carries it can hum the tune, and the thread pulls toward the other ends.
- Standing: +1 with whichever of Ashvale's groups gets its way, and -1 with any group that is lied to.

## Approaches
- **Salvagers** strip the halt and leave the heart. The hum goes on, and Brenna is pleased.
- **Listeners** commune with the call through the roots (Tevan's way, a Thinning save) and hear the courier's last message whole.
- **Keepers** seal the halt, as Orla wants. They tell her what the tune was, and the hum is quieter after.
- **Fighters** hold the line at the stone while someone else does the delicate work.
- **Bold** characters lay a hand on the speaking-stone and let the waygate try to deliver them. It can't, because the line ends in the Hollow Verge now, but they see the grey hall full of beds and the lamp. Thinning save, then it lets go.

## Consequences
- If the heart is torn out, the call stops. Brenna gets the best part she's ever salvaged, and the thread has one end fewer. Tying it back later costs more (see The Loose Thread [HOOK-931]).
- If the party tells Orla the tune has an extra bar, she goes very still and asks them never to sing it in town. She doesn't say why. She's thinking of the founder's warning, about thirteen keys gathered as one shape.
- If they tell Tevan everything, he wants to come with them to wherever the thread goes next. He's a useful friend, and a target for the Spore Circle's watchers.
- The green gourd-lamp at the treeline (the Eastern Tendril [FAC-302]) has seen everything. Someone is now interested in the party, and in the thread.

## If ignored
The hum gets louder each night. After a week children in Ashvale start humming the extra bar in their sleep. After two, the waygate pulls a Wright down the stairs to "deliver" him, and he comes back up a little Thinner, humming. The groups start blaming each other.

## Scaling
- **Levels 3 and 4.** Add a Maintenance Echo [CRE-034] in the signal room instead of the Walker, and a second cloud of Mites. The pull's save rises to DC 13.
- **Levels 5 to 10.** The seep of glass has grown into a doorway, and a Thing from the Between [CRE-313] has come through it to feed on the call. Half-made [CRE-306] drift along the old line in lockstep, drawn by the hum. The Eastern Tendril sends someone down after the party to take the voice for the Spore Circle. The pull's save is DC 15, and failing it at the stone means a free trip along the line to the Hollow Verge and back, which costs a Thinning level automatically.

# Scene: Three Requests
Kind: social
Linked: NPC-306, NPC-304, NPC-305, NPC-341

> Ashvale smells of cut wood and wet earth. Halfway down the west road, where the cobbles give way to packed dirt, there's a square of new planking over an old stair, weighted down with stones. If you stand on the planks and wait, you can feel it through your boots: a low, steady hum, like a held note.

Brenna meets the party at the planks with a cart and a coil of rope. Orla finds them at the well, and Tevan at the edge of the Bloom-Speakers' garden. Let each ask in their own way, and let the party decide whose terms they accept. They can accept all three.

Jory Hesk, if the party passes the stones, is teaching children the keeping song. He stops when he hears the hum through the ground, frowns, and starts again.

# Scene: The Stair and the Platform
Kind: location
Linked: LOC-305, LOC-303

> The stair turns twice and opens onto a long platform of fitted stone. Roots have broken through the walls and grown into neat lines, like cables. A track runs off into the dark in both directions, and a cold draught comes up it from the east, from very far away. The hum is louder here. It has a tune.

The seep of glass is on the platform's edge: a patch of floor that reflects nothing. The Thread Mites are here, ticking over the stones in a little cloud.

# Scene: The Signal Room
Kind: encounter
Linked: CRE-305

A narrow room with a slate board on the wall. Painted on it are arrivals and departures for stations whose names have been scratched off, all except one: "CROSSROADS (shifting). Due." The Walker stands by the board in old halt livery, asking for tickets in a voice like a door.

Settle it by ticket, by trick or by force. If the party shows it a courier's token, it salutes and says, "Late," and nothing else.

# Scene: The Passengers
Kind: encounter
Linked: CRE-306

> Past the signal room the track runs on into the dark, and on it, between the rails, three figures stand in a row facing the hum, perfectly still, like passengers waiting for a train that's very late. Their clothes are thirty years out of date and their skin is the grey of wet clay. As you come closer, all three heads turn at once.

The Half-made fight in lockstep, on one initiative count. They don't chase anyone off the track. A clever party can lure them onto the seep of glass, which takes them.

# Scene: The Speaking-Stone
Kind: climax
Linked: NPC-376, HOOK-931

> At the end of the line, where the track runs into a wall of packed earth and roots, a tall grey stone stands in a ring of brass. A silver thread comes out of the ground at its foot, runs up into the stone, and stops. The hum comes from the stone. Close to, it isn't a hum at all. It's someone humming with their mouth closed, patiently, the way you do while you wait for a door to open. Every so often it swells, and you feel it pull at you.

Through the roots, or by laying a hand on the stone, a character can hear the call whole: "Ashvale, west road. This is Carrow, off the Crossroads. Nearly there. Keep the room. Tell them I'm—" Then the tune again, and the extra bar, which is his name.

The party's choice here is the adventure:
- **Tear out the heart**, the brass ring. Brenna's prize. The call stops.
- **Free the call** into a vessel. A Charisma (Performance) check, DC 12, to hum it out of the stone and into something, or a Thinning save for whoever lets it in. They carry the thread's second end.
- **Seal it**, as Orla wants. The call goes on, quieter, for years.

Whichever they choose, the waygate fights it. Run the climax encounter: three rounds of pull and of everything on the line coming at the stone, while someone keeps working. Then the roar drops, and it's quiet enough to hear water dripping.

# Scene: Back Up the Stair
Kind: scene

Whatever the party chose, three people are waiting at the top of the stair, and each wants to know what the hum was. What the party tells each of them is the real ending.

If the party carries the voice, it hums at night, softly. When they face east, toward the sea and the Crossroads, the tune goes up. When they face the Rift, it goes down.
