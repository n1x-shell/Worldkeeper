Title: Nobody Decides Alone
Arc: The Thirteen Vaults
Hook: VLT-307
Levels: 7 to 9 (written for level 7)
Linked: VLT-307, NPC-457, FAC-301, NPC-308, FAC-302, NPC-307, FAC-304, NPC-310, FAC-305, NPC-311, FAC-303, NPC-309, CRE-312, CRE-310, CRE-307, NPC-301, NPC-302, REG-304, SET-330

## Premise
Rimeholt [VLT-307], in the northern ice, is the vault of the failsafes and the Original Directive: the rules that paused Kaelith's change half-finished, three hundred years ago. Its key is the strangest of all: three who disagree, and all say yes. Nobody decides alone.

The truth: Ice-Watcher Sorin Icemark [NPC-457], Kaelholm's watcher at Rimeholt, has noticed that the frost-writing on the door changes every day. It's counting down. On Evernight, the failsafes will weaken, and the change the Directive paused will start, slowly, to move again. Opening the vault resets the count. Opening it needs three people who want different things for Kaelmar to agree, honestly, on the same thing.

## Hook
- Sorin sends a message south by Kaunna of Whitecairn: the door is counting, and he has a month.
- Warden Ilsabet Crane [NPC-309] of the Continuity [FAC-303] approaches the party in secret. She knows what the countdown means, and she can't be one of the three: the Continuity only watches.
- The glass seeps across Kaelmar begin to widen, a little more each week.

## Stakes
- **Kaelmar.** If the count reaches zero on Evernight, the change resumes, slowly: more glass, more Half-made, the Bloom growing faster. Nobody will notice at first.
- **The three.** Kaelholm wants the change turned back; the Eastern Tendril wants every Kaelith work burned; the Collective wants the change finished, freely chosen. Each must say yes to the same thing, honestly. A lie keeps the vault shut for a day, and there aren't many days.
- **The Directive.** What the Original Directive says is the GM's to write. Whoever reads it first will use it.

## Opposition
- **The ice.** The northern ice in midwinter: cold, storms and crevasses.
- **The Ascendancy** [FAC-305]: Marshal Dace Corvane [NPC-311] wants the count to reach zero, and sends a strike force (use 4 Veterans and a Hunter [CRE-307]) to make sure nobody reaches the door in time.
- **The vault's wards:** 2 Vault Wards [CRE-312], and a dormant Guardian [CRE-310] that wakes only if the heart is forced.
- **Each other.** The three won't agree easily, and each will try to bend the party to their side.

## Clues
- The frost-writing is in the First Grammar. Door-Reader Thessaly Ammond could read it; Sorin can count it.
- Elder Sorrel [NPC-307] saw the Shrouding as a child, and remembers the day the change stopped. She remembers something she won't say until she trusts the party.
- Maro Grane [NPC-310] has written a chapter called "The Pause". In it, the pause is a kindness, not a prison.
- Archivist-Elder Venn Tull [NPC-308] has a Kaelholm copy of the Directive's first line. It is the only line Kaelholm has ever had.

## Obstacles
- **The key.** Three who disagree, and all say yes. The party must find what Kaelholm, the Eastern Tendril and the Collective can all honestly agree to: resetting the count, and nothing more, may be the only thing. Each envoy must be awake, willing and honest at the heart. No roll replaces it.
- **Bringing them.** Venn Tull, Sorrel and Grane (or envoys they trust) must travel together to Rimeholt, through Hearthdeep and past Whitecairn, and survive each other on the way.
- **The cold.** In the ice, each day, a DC 15 Constitution save or a level of Exhaustion, unless the party is equipped for it (Kaunna can equip them).

## Encounters
- **The Envoys' Camp.** Three envoys, one fire, and a long night. Run it as a negotiation: each envoy has a want, a fear and a line they won't cross. The party has to find the yes they share.
- **The Ascendancy on the Ice.** Corvane's strike force attacks the column in a storm, aiming for the envoys, not the party.
- **The Counting Door.** At Rimeholt, the 2 Vault Wards test the three envoys, not the party: each must answer one question truthfully.
- **The Directive (climax).** The heart opens. The count stops. The Directive is there to read. Who reads it first?

## Rewards
- The count reset: the failsafes hold for another three hundred years, or until someone opens all thirteen vaults.
- The Original Directive, or the first reading of it.
- An alliance, for a season, between three powers who hate each other.
- Standing: +1 with each of Kaelholm, the Spore Circle and the Convergence Collective; -2 with the Ascendancy.

## Approaches
- **The honest yes.** Find the one thing all three can agree on, and nothing more.
- **Bend one.** Push one envoy into agreeing to more than they believe. The heart stays shut for a day, and the clock runs.
- **Let it run.** Let the count reach zero, and see what the change does when it starts again.

## Consequences
- **The vault opens.** The count resets. The glass seeps stop widening.
- **The Directive.** Whoever reads it first knows exactly what the failsafes allow, and what would undo them.
- **The Seventh Ward wakes for a moment.** It opens its eyes in Arborhold, says one word, and sleeps again. The Continuity hears it. Nobody else does.
- **The Root Core** [NPC-302] does not wake: one vault opened at a time never wakes it.

## If ignored
Evernight comes. The count reaches zero. Nothing happens, for a month. Then the glass seeps begin to grow, and a child in Ashvale is born with a glyph on her palm.

## Scaling
- **Level 9.** Corvane leads the strike force in person (use his fight card).
- **Level 10.** The dormant Guardian wakes when the envoys argue in the heart, and must be calmed, not fought.
