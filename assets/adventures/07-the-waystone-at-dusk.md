Title: The Waystone at Dusk
Arc: The Loose Thread
Hook: HOOK-931
Levels: 4 to 5 (written for level 4)
Linked: LOC-003, NPC-376, NPC-010, NPC-422, CLT-007, NPC-095, CRE-014, CRE-502, CRE-033, CRE-012, SET-001, NPC-437, NPC-369, HOOK-931, HOOK-010, COS-032

## Premise
The three ends of Lemmy Carrow's thread have come together: his body's end from the Crooked Nail, his voice, and Lemmy himself. The thread can be tied back only where it snapped, at the waystone of the Crossroads of Fate [LOC-003], at dusk, with all three ends present. The Crossroads is about three days from Hollow Refuge, watched, and usually safe.

The truth: the Hollow Tribunal [CLT-007] has been waiting for exactly this. It wants to know whether a cut thread of the Loom can be tied again, and who can do it. When the three ends come together at the waystone, the Hollow Court appears, as it does wherever a judgement is due, and Justicar Quell [NPC-095] sits in judgement on whether the thread may be retied.

The GM also knows this: the Shard of Balance is hidden inside the waystone. Tying a cut thread puts something back into the world, and the shard balances it by moving something else a little. That is why the world shifts to make room.

## Hook
The ends do the hooking: once two are together, the third pulls so hard that whoever carries it can barely sleep. Lemmy wants to go home. Mara Venn [NPC-010] doesn't know why, but she has taken a lamp and gone to the Crossroads to wait, and she won't say who for.

## Stakes
- **Lemmy.** Whole again, or at rest. Either way, his thirty-one years of being late end here.
- **The party.** The Tribunal will know their names, and whether they can do what nobody should be able to do.
- **The world.** If the thread is tied, something the party knows to be true becomes a little different, and far away something notices.

## Opposition
- **Thread Mites [CRE-033]**, in swarms, drawn by three ends in one place.
- **The Hollow Tribunal.** Justicar Quell presides, and doesn't fight the innocent. The court's hunters do the rest: 3 masked hunters (use Silent Blade Agent [CRE-014], who can speak a verdict instead of Sneak Attack: DC 12 Wisdom save or Frightened until the end of its next turn), led by a grey-masked Veil Adept [CRE-012], with a Name-Eater [CRE-502] on a leash.
- **Time.** The thread must be tied at dusk. Dusk lasts about half an hour.

## Clues
- Orlo Pennick [NPC-422], who keeps the shelter by the waystone, has a book of every road that has appeared at the Crossroads. One page, thirty-one years old, records "a courier's road, coming out under Ashvale". It was crossed out the next morning, because the road had never existed.
- The waystone is warm at dusk, and it hums the courier's tune when the voice comes near.
- Mara's lamp is the one she lights every ninth night in the Crooked Nail. She doesn't know why tonight is a ninth night. It is.
- Masked figures have been seen at the Crossroads for three evenings, standing still at the edge of the light.

## Obstacles
- **The ends.** If an end was lost, the arc's costs apply (see The Loose Thread [HOOK-931]). If the body faded or the voice was cut, a character must stand in for what's missing: one Thinning level and one memory of the player's choosing. If Lemmy was Unmade, the thread can't be tied, and only letting it go remains.
- **The road.** The roads to the Crossroads shift. Arriving by dusk takes a DC 13 Wisdom (Survival) check, or Orlo's book.
- **The court.** Nothing can be tied while the court sits. The trial must end first, one way or another.

## Encounters
- **The Road at Dusk.** Two swarms of Thread Mites (use 2d4 + 2 Thread Mites each [CRE-033]) come out of the grass as the party nears the Crossroads, drawn to the ends. Any creature they bite forgets the name of someone in the party until the next dawn.
- **The Hollow Court (a trial, not a fight).** Run as a skill challenge: the party needs 5 successes before 3 failures, DC 13. Each piece of evidence the party has gathered gives one automatic success when presented well:
  - Shae Darrow's [NPC-437] letter to "Lemmy Carrow, Courier, care of the Crooked Nail";
  - Harbourmaster Gulen Saltback's [NPC-369] story of the first crossing, if he told it;
  - Orlo Pennick's crossed-out page;
  - Mara's locked room, and the cap in it, if Mara agrees to speak;
  - the message itself, "Tell them I'm coming home", if the party learned it.

  Arguments use Persuasion, Insight, History or Religion. A character can instead swear to stand in for anything the thread lacks, which counts as a success and costs a Thinning save.
- **The Verdict.** On a success, Quell rules the thread may be tied, once, and the court is gone before the gavel stops ringing. On a failure, or if the party defies the court, the hunters and the leashed Name-Eater fight to take Lemmy. Quell watches, and strikes only someone the court has found guilty.

## Rewards
- Lemmy, whole or at rest (see the last scene).
- Mara Venn's gratitude, and a room at the Crooked Nail that is always kept for the party.
- Whatever the court leaves behind: a grey mask, or the verdict itself, written on a page that can't be burned.
- If the thread is let go: the silver guide, a hand's length of thread that always points toward the Loom of Eternity. Whoever carries it has Advantage on checks to find the way through the Ethereal Realm's depths.
- Standing: +1 with the Veilborn [FAC-503] if Lemmy is whole.

## Approaches
- **Win the trial.** Gather the evidence across the earlier adventures, and argue well.
- **Defy the court.** Fight the hunters at dusk, with the clock running. Win, and the thread can still be tied, but the Tribunal will remember.
- **Bargain.** Quell will hear an offer: the court lets the thread be tied if a character agrees to answer one question for the Tribunal, truthfully, one day. Nobody knows what the question will be.

## Consequences
- **Tied back.** Lemmy is whole: body, voice and self. He remembers everything. He walks into the Crooked Nail at dusk, and Mara Venn remembers him. The world shifts slightly to make room: something the party knows to be true is now a little different, and the GM chooses what. Far away, a Watcher or one of the Hidden Ones notices that a cut thread has been retied, and someone will come to see who did it. This opens the arc of the Door Beyond (see Major Arcs and Ways to Start [HOOK-010]).
- **Let go.** Lemmy chooses to rest. He asks the party to deliver his message to Mara, and fades at the waystone, gently and gladly. The party keeps the silver guide. The Door Beyond still opens.
- **The Tribunal.** Whatever the verdict, the court now knows the party's names and what they can do. If they defied it, its hunters come back.
- **The omen.** At the moment the thread is tied or let go, every bell in the Hollow Marches rings a half-beat late. It is the Return's omen [COS-032] for whatever stage it has reached; it does not move the Return on.

## If ignored
Lemmy goes to the Crossroads alone, at dusk, with whatever ends he can carry. The court sits anyway. Nobody argues his case. The next morning Orlo Pennick finds a satchel on the waystone and a new page in his book, already crossed out.

## Scaling
- **Level 5.** Add a fourth hunter, and a second Name-Eater that arrives when the court has ruled.
- **Levels 6 to 10.** The court is judged by Quell and two more masked judges (use Veil Adept [CRE-012] for each), and the thing on the leash is an Abandoned Future [CRE-524] wearing Lemmy's face. The trial needs 7 successes before 3 failures, DC 15.

# Scene: Three Ends
Kind: scene
Linked: NPC-376, NPC-010, SET-001

> The thread on your wrist has stopped tugging. It's pulling now, straight and taut, toward the Crossroads. Beside you, Lemmy has his satchel over his shoulder and his cap on straight, the way a man dresses to go home. Somewhere ahead, the courier's tune hums under your feet.

If Mara is with the party, she walks a little apart, carrying her lamp, and doesn't say who she's waiting for.

# Scene: The Road at Dusk
Kind: encounter
Linked: CRE-033

> The road has moved again. The grass on either side is moving too, in a wave, toward you: thousands of tiny pale things, ticking like clocks.

The Thread Mites go for whoever carries an end. Every bite makes the bitten forget a party member's name until dawn, so the party may be fighting beside someone they can't name.

# Scene: Orlo's Book
Kind: social
Linked: NPC-422, LOC-003

> A small stone shelter leans against the waystone, with a lamp in the door. An old man sits inside with a book as thick as a doorstep, writing down the roads as they come.

Orlo shows the crossed-out page if asked about couriers, or about the night the Cycle broke. He'll tear it out and give it to them if they ask nicely: it's the only page he's ever crossed out.

# Scene: The Hollow Court
Kind: encounter
Linked: CLT-007, NPC-095, CRE-014, CRE-502

> The light goes grey. Around the waystone, without a sound, a courtroom has appeared: benches of old dark wood, a high bench, and behind it a very small figure in a blank mask. Masked figures stand at the edges of the light. One of them holds a leash. Something at the end of it is sniffing the air for a name. "The court will hear," says the small figure, "whether what was cut may be tied."

Run the trial. Let each piece of evidence land: the letter read aloud, Gulen's one lantern, the cap from Mara's locked room. Quell speaks only to ask questions, and the questions are always fair.

# Scene: Tie It or Let It Go
Kind: climax
Linked: NPC-376, HOOK-931, HOOK-010, COS-032

> The court is gone. The sun is a red line on the edge of the Marches. Lemmy holds out his hands for the ends of his own thread, and looks at you, and waits. "Your choice as much as mine," he says. "You carried them."

Whatever they choose, every bell in the Marches rings a half-beat late. If the thread is tied, Lemmy laughs out loud and says "the road" as if there were only one, and Mara Venn, standing in the dusk with her lamp, says his name.
