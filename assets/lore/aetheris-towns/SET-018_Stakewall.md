# Stakewall

## Overview

A palisaded farming village on the edge of the Bloodmarked Fields, which has been attacked by every faction and taken by none. Part of The Hollow Marches [REG-001].

## Leaders

Reeve Bram Hollis

## Places

- **The Wall.** The stake palisade, rebuilt a stake at a time. Every family keeps its own section.
- **The Bell Gate.** The only gate, with a bell that every child knows how to ring.
- **The Barley Fields.** Inside the wall and out. The outside fields are harvested in armour.

## Getting there

A day west, round the edge of the Fields.

## What people say

- Since the shard rumour, there are diggers in the Fields every night. Stakewall can see their lanterns.
- Bram has turned down a Legion commission three times.
- Stakewall barley is the only thing both armies will buy from each other.

## GM notes

Stakewall's wall stands on the line of an older wall from a forgotten world. It has never fallen because it has, in a sense, already been defended.
