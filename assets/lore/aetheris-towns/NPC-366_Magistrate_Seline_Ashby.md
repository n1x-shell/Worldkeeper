# Magistrate Seline Ashby

## Public role and identity
- Magistrate of Dawnmere, appointed by the Celestial Order.
- Has never once lost a ruling on appeal.

## Goal
A town where the law is the same for everyone, including the Order.

## Known relationships and locations
Dawnmere [SET-019]

## Appearance and voice
A slender yuan-ti with pale gold scales, a magistrate's white collar and spectacles she doesn't need. Precise, courteous and utterly unbending.

## Pressure and secrets
Seline's sorcery came from a clockwork order older than the Celestial Order, from an erased world. The Order thinks it is divine. It isn't.
