# Anchor-Master Wenna Gale

## Public role and identity
- Anchor-Master of Driftholm: keeper of the chains that hold the island down.
- Climbs the chains every day to check them, without a rope.

## Goal
That Driftholm stays where it's put, at least until she says otherwise.

## Known relationships and locations
Driftholm [SET-021]

## Appearance and voice
A wiry Stormborn woman with sky-blue skin, hair that streams upward, and rope burns on both palms. Cheerful, fearless and always in a hurry.

## Pressure and secrets
Wenna knows who is cutting the chains: a Consortium researcher who wants the island to drift to the Rift of Unmaking.
