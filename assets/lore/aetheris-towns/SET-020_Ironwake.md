# Ironwake

## Overview

A forge town under the Tyrant's mountains, where the Legion's weapons are made and its veterans go to retire. Part of The Tyrant's Dominion [REG-003].

## Leaders

Forgemaster Kiro Vashti

## Places

- **The Terraces.** Forge after forge up the hillside. Each terrace belongs to a different company.
- **The Veterans' Hall.** Where old soldiers drink, argue and settle disputes, by duel if they have to.
- **The Proving Yard.** Where every blade made in Ironwake is tested, and every smith with it.

## Getting there

Varkhaal is about two days north. Hollow Refuge about two weeks west.

## What people say

- Kiro Vashti made the Warlord's greatsword, and won't make him another.
- There's a forge on the top terrace nobody works. It's still hot.
- Ironwake steel rings when it's lied to. That's the story.

## GM notes

The cold forge on the top terrace is fed by the same fire the Ashen Covenant seeks. Kiro knows, and keeps it shut.
