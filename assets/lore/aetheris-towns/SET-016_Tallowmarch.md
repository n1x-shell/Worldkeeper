# Tallowmarch

## Overview

A candlemakers' village on the Radiant border, where every shrine in the Marches gets its lights. Part of The Hollow Marches [REG-001].

## Leaders

Chandler Ossery Thane

## Places

- **The Long Street.** One street, half a mile long, every house a workshop.
- **The Hives.** Hundreds of skeps on the hill behind the village, and the best honey in the Marches.
- **The Six-Niche Shrine.** A shrine with a light for every living god, and an empty seventh niche nobody fills.

## Getting there

A day's walk south-west, past Blackveil Fortress.

## What people say

- The bees won't go near the Scar. Neither will the beekeepers.
- The Order wants Tallowmarch inside its border. Tallowmarch would rather sell it candles.
- Somebody lights a candle in the seventh niche every Evernight. Nobody admits to it.

## GM notes

The seventh niche is for the Nameless Smith. The village's founders remembered it; their grandchildren don't.
