# Well-Warden Ysolde Varn

## Public role and identity
- Warden of the well at Archwell, and the village's headwoman.
- Says she came up the well herself, years ago, and stayed because the bread was good.

## Goal
To find out where the well goes, without falling in.

## Known relationships and locations
Archwell [SET-015]

## Appearance and voice
A tall, grey-skinned sea elf with dark wet-looking hair that never quite dries, and a bronze key on a chain. Calm, dry-humoured and very hard to surprise.

## Pressure and secrets
Ysolde did come up the well, from the waters under the Black Tides, the same way Sirala of the Drowned Saints says she did. She doesn't trust the Saints.
