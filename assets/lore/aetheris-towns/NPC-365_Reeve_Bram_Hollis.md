# Reeve Bram Hollis

## Public role and identity
- Reeve of Stakewall, and captain of its wall.
- A farmer, mostly. A fighter when the bell rings.

## Goal
That Stakewall stays nobody's.

## Known relationships and locations
Stakewall [SET-018]

## Appearance and voice
A huge brown-furred Taur'n with one horn broken short and runes cut into the other, in a farmer's smock with a greataxe never far away. Slow-spoken, good-humoured and absolutely immovable.

## Pressure and secrets
Bram's runes are a Taur'n family oath to guard this land, sworn by an ancestor in a world that no longer exists.
