# Highbough

## Overview

A Wildborn village in the forest canopy, where there are no roads and you're not welcome until someone lowers a rope. Part of The Verdant Rebirth [REG-006].

## Leaders

Elder Aelis Thornweave

## Places

- **The Rope Gate.** Where visitors wait to be let up. The knots in the rope mean different things.
- **The Long Platform.** The village hall, a platform between six trees, open to the sky.
- **The Root Shrine.** Down on the forest floor, the one place in Highbough on the ground: Orrowen's shrine.

## Getting there

The Rootspire is about four days east through roadless forest, with a guide.

## What people say

- Highbough has never been found by the Order. Not once.
- Aelis changes with the seasons, and so do Highbough's rules.
- Something walks on the forest floor at night that the hunters won't name.

## GM notes

Highbough shelters Order deserters on their way to the Thornward Grove's network.
