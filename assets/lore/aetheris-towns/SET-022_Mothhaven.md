# Mothhaven

## Overview

A smugglers' harbour on the dusk coast, where the first ship to cross the Black Tides set out. Part of The Eternal Veil [REG-005].

## Leaders

Harbourmaster Gulen Saltback

## Places

- **The Long Pier.** The longest pier on the coast, and the best place to hire a ship west.
- **The Moth Lantern.** A tavern with one lit window, which is why it's full of moths, and sailors.
- **The Dark Dock.** Where the ships that cross the Black Tides tie up. No lanterns allowed.

## Getting there

Noctis is about three days south, down the coast. Kaelholm is about two weeks west, across the Black Tides.

## What people say

- The first crew to cross brought someone back with them. Nobody in Mothhaven will say where they went.
- Gulen won't sail. He says he's seen what's under the Tides.
- The Pact runs the harbour. Gulen says he does. Both are right.

## GM notes

Gulen was a sailor on the Lanternless Crossing, the only one who lit a lantern over the trench, and lived. He saw the Rift, and something in it looked back.
