# Elder Aelis Thornweave

## Public role and identity
- Elder of Highbough, among the Wildborn Clans.
- Decides who is let up the rope.

## Goal
That Highbough is never found by anyone it doesn't want.

## Known relationships and locations
Highbough [SET-023]

## Appearance and voice
A tall eladrin whose hair turns with the seasons, green, gold, rust and white, and whose skin is patterned like bark. Warm in summer, grieving in autumn, cold in winter, and wild in spring.

## Pressure and secrets
Aelis has no memory before the Cycle broke. They woke up in the Green Expanse with a bark-patterned skin and a name nobody gave them.
