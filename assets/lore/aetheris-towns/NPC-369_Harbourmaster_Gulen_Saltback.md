# Harbourmaster Gulen Saltback

## Public role and identity
- Harbourmaster of Mothhaven.
- Sailed on the first crossing of the Black Tides, in Year 11.

## Goal
That every ship that leaves Mothhaven comes back.

## Known relationships and locations
Mothhaven [SET-022]

## Appearance and voice
An old tortle with a barnacled shell, a harbourmaster's chain of office, and a fishing line always in the water. Slow, sardonic and watchful. Never in a hurry, never surprised.

## Pressure and secrets
His patron is something in the Rift beneath the Tides. It speaks to him in the dark, and he keeps the lanterns out so it can't find the ships.
