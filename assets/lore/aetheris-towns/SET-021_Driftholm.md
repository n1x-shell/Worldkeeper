# Driftholm

## Overview

A town on a floating island, moored to the mountains by chains, which goes wherever the storms take it when the chains break. Part of The Fractured Nexus [REG-004].

## Leaders

Anchor-Master Wenna Gale

## Places

- **The Four Chains.** Each as thick as a house, anchored in the rock below. One is new, and the town still argues about the old one.
- **The Chime Market.** A market where every stall has wind-chimes, so you can find it in a storm.
- **The Edge.** The rim of the island, with a rope rail. Children dare each other to lean out.

## Getting there

Zepharyos is about two days away, by chain-ladder and mountain road, or a day if the island has drifted the right way.

## What people say

- The island drifted once, before the Cycle broke, and came back with a different name. Nobody remembers the old one.
- Wenna Gale can call the wind. That's why she keeps the chains.
- Somebody keeps cutting the chains, one link at a time.

## GM notes

The island is a shard-scarred fragment of an older world; its stone resists rewriting. The Consortium wants it cut loose and studied.
