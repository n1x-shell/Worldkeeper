# Forgemaster Kiro Vashti

## Public role and identity
- Forgemaster of Ironwake, and holder of the Forge Seat.
- A Legion veteran, retired, who still fights in the Red Games every Midsummer.

## Goal
Steel worth carrying, made by smiths worth trusting.

## Known relationships and locations
Ironwake [SET-020]

## Appearance and voice
A broad copper-scaled dragonborn with a leather apron over old Legion armour, and a hammer in each hand. Loud, blunt, generous and quick to anger.

## Pressure and secrets
Kiro made the Warlord's greatsword and fears what Garron Kael will do with an heir who won't fight.
