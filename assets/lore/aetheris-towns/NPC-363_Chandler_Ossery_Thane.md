# Chandler Ossery Thane

## Public role and identity
- Master of the chandlers' guild, and so headman of Tallowmarch.
- Trained once in a monastery he won't name. Still keeps its hours.

## Goal
To keep Tallowmarch out of the Order's border, and on its customers' list.

## Known relationships and locations
Tallowmarch [SET-016]

## Appearance and voice
A big, slow-moving half-orc with burn-scarred hands, a leather apron and a beard that smells of honey. Patient, kind and immovable in a bargain.

## Pressure and secrets
Ossery trained with the Celestial Order's healers and left after the Purging of the Halls. The Order has not forgotten him.
