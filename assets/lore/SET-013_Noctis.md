# Noctis

## Established description

The City of Masks, in endless dusk. Capital of the Eternal Veil and seat of the Shadow Pact. The rulers meet in the Midnight Court. The Pact's chief fortress, Nightglass, stands nearby.

## Travel

About a week beyond the dominion's border, which is itself about a week from Hollow Refuge.
