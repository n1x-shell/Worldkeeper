# The Lins of Greyfold

## Background
A big, loud hill family from Greyfold, spread across three villages and two peoples. The Lins have married humans and halflings for generations, and nobody keeps track of which cousin is which, except at weddings. They've spoken to the Bloom since the first years after the Shrouding; the copper marks run in the family.

## Family members
- Master Speaker Tevan Lin [NPC-305], the eldest of his generation
- Lark Lin [NPC-342], his cousin, from the halfling side

## Family ways
A Lin table always has room for one more. The Lins mark every child's first communion with a feast.

## Tensions
- Tevan teaches caution. Lark wants to go deeper than he'll allow.
- Half of Greyfold is a Lin cousin, and they all have an opinion.

## What people say
- If you need help in Greyfold, ask a Lin. If you need it kept quiet, don't.
- The Bloom answers the Lins. Some say it answers them first.

## GM notes
The Lins hear the Bloom more clearly than most families, because one of their founders was a Bloom-born child of the first years, and nobody outside the family knows.
