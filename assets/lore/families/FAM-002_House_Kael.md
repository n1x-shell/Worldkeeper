# House Kael

## Background
The hobgoblin warlords of Varkhaal. House Kael has held the Warlord's Hall since the Border Years, by winning every challenge for it, and the Warlord wears the proof: a mouthful of gold teeth, each one taken from a rival who lost.

## Family members
- Warlord Garron Kael [NPC-024], head of the house
- Darion Kael [NPC-033], his son and heir

## Family ways
The Kaels count their history in fights won. The house has no lands of its own, only the Legion, and every Kael is expected to earn a place in it on the sand of the Great Arena.

## Tensions
- Garron wants a son who'll fight for the Hall. Darion wants a Legion that doesn't need to.
- Every Kael heir has fought in the Red Games. Darion hasn't, yet.

## What people say
- Never ask a Kael how many teeth are left to win.
- The Warlord's son writes poetry. The Warlord pretends not to know.

## GM notes
If Darion inherits, he'll be challenged within the week. He knows it. He's been quietly counting who would stand with him.
