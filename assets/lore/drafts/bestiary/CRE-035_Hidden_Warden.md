# Hidden Warden

## Overview

A stone guardian that guards the Hidden Ones' secrets, and erases what it takes.

## Appearance

A faceless stone giant that steps out of nowhere.

## Associated groups

Hidden Ones

## Game statistics (GM)

Start from: Stone Golem that teleports and can erase one carried item over several rounds.
Threat: 3 hearts on the cards, CR 5 to 8.

## Secret (GM only)

Guards the Hidden Ones' places. It can erase an item a person is carrying over several rounds.
