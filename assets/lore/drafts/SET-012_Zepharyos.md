# Zepharyos

## Established description

The City of the Unbound, among the clouds. Capital of the Fractured Nexus and seat of the Arcane Consortium. The council sits in the Nexus Spire. The Consortium's chief fortress, the Nexus Collegium, stands nearby.

## Travel

About a week beyond the dominion's border, which is itself about a week from Hollow Refuge.
