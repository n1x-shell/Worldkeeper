# The Keeping Hymnal

## Description
An iron-bound songbook of the Boundary Keepers, with every verse of the keeping song but one.

## Lore power
Sung from at the stones, its verses calm anything sleeping, anywhere within a mile.

## Lore cost
Whoever sings from it can't stop until the song is done, and the song is very long.

## Where it is (GM)
In Orla Hesk's keeping. The missing verse is in her head.

## Mechanics
No 2024 rules are assigned. Build them from the power and the cost when the party finds it.
