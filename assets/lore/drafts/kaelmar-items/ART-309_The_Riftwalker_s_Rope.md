# The Riftwalker's Rope

## Description
A coil of grey rope that Maro Grane took down into the Rift, and brought back up.

## Lore power
Always reaches the bottom, of anything.

## Lore cost
Whoever climbs it comes back with something missing: a memory, a word, a fear.

## Where it is (GM)
Maro Grane keeps it. He won't lend it, and he won't say what he lost.

## Mechanics
No 2024 rules are assigned. Build them from the power and the cost when the party finds it.
