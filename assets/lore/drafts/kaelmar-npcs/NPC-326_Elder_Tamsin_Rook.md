# Elder Tamsin Rook

## Public role and identity
- An elder of The Spore Circle.
- Saw the Shrouding as a child.

## Goal
To go deeper than anyone, and come back.

## Known relationships and locations
The Spore Circle [FAC-302]; The Central Bloom Country [REG-303]

## Appearance and voice
A very old elf with eyes clouded white with fine crystals, and branching lines covering both arms. Slow, distant, and often answering a question nobody asked.

## Secrets (GM only)
- Leads the Sixth Tendril, which goes deepest of all.
- She hears something in the Bloom that answers from under the Rift, and has told no one.
