# Tessk the Wayfinder

## Public role and identity
- Guides travellers through the Hollow Verge, for a price.
- Left a githyanki war-band, and won't say why.

## Goal
To find the path her war-band took, and not follow it.

## Known relationships and locations
The Nameless Asylum [LOC-506]; Veilborn [FAC-503]

## Appearance and voice
A lean, yellow-skinned githyanki with a shaved head, bare feet and a silver cord tied around one wrist. Dry, quick and uninterested in small talk.

## Pressure and secrets
Tessk's war-band went past the last threshold uninvited. She was the only one who turned back.
