# Lamplighter Ezri

## Public role and identity
- Lamplighter of Lastlight, and the closest thing it has to a headperson.
- Made a bargain with something, long ago, to keep the lights burning.

## Goal
That no lamp in Lastlight ever goes out.

## Known relationships and locations
Lastlight [SET-017]

## Appearance and voice
A fairy no taller than a hand, with moth-brown wings, a soot-black face and a taper twice their height. Bright, bossy and fearless of anything except the dark.

## Pressure and secrets
Ezri's patron is a fey of the old Wildborn world who wants the dusk held back as much as Ezri does. Kyrra the Shadeborn knows of it.
