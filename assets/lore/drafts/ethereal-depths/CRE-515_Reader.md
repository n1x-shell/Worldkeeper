# Reader

## Overview

A hunched, one-eyed thing of the Archive that reads your secrets off your face.

## Appearance

A thin, grey, crouching shape with one huge wet eye, turning pages it isn't holding. It looks up, and says aloud the thing you've never told anyone.

## Where

The Silent Archive, depth 4 of 7.
