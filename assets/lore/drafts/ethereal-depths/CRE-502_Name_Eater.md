# Name-Eater

## Overview

A slick of grey ooze that feeds on memories, and wears the faces of the people it has eaten.

## Appearance

A puddle that isn't water, sliding toward you across the grey grass. When it rises, it has your sister's face, and it calls you by name.

## Where

The Hollow Verge, depth 1 of 7.

## Game statistics (GM)

Start from: Oblex Spawn (MotM) for the small ones; Adult Oblex (MotM) for an old one. About CR 2 to 5.

## Secret (GM only)

A character whose memory is eaten makes the Thinning save at the GM's choice. Killing the oblex returns one eaten memory to someone nearby.
