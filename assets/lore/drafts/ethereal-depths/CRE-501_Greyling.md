# Greyling

## Overview

The faded ghost of someone the world forgot, still looking for anyone who remembers.

## Appearance

A grey figure at the edge of sight, its face smudged like a thumbprint on a window. It turns when you say any name at all.

## Where

The Hollow Verge, depth 1 of 7.

## Game statistics (GM)

Start from: Shadow (MM), with a whisper of a forgotten name instead of Strength Drain: Wisdom save or forget a name you know until a long rest. About CR 1/2.

## Secret (GM only)

Greylings are the residue of the Unmade and the erased. A greyling given a true name becomes a Memory Ghost for one night and can tell one thing it remembers.
