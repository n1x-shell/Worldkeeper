# Larva Prophet

## Overview

A swollen, worm-wrapped thing that was once a fate-seer, and has seen too far.

## Appearance

A robed figure that is mostly worms now, writhing out of the sleeves and the hood, chanting in a voice made of a thousand smaller voices.

## Where

The Black Horizon, depth 7 of 7.

## Game statistics (GM)

Start from: Star Spawn Larva Mage (MotM). About CR 16.

## Secret (GM only)

A larva prophet can open a door through the Black Horizon. What is on the other side is up to you; the Watchers' nature stays undefined.
