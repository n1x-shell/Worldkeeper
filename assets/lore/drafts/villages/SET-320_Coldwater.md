# Coldwater

## Overview

One of the nine settlements in the hills west and south of Ashvale [SET-301]: Ashvale's home villages, rivals and trade partners. A shepherds' village high in the hills west of Ashvale, around a spring that never freezes. Known for the best wool in the hills.
