# Varkhaal

## Established description

The Iron Citadel, with its Great Arena. Capital of the Tyrant's Dominion and seat of the Crimson Legion. Warlords meet in the Warlord's Hall. The Legion's chief fortress, the Red Bastion, stands nearby.

## Travel

About a week beyond the dominion's border, which is itself about a week from Hollow Refuge.
