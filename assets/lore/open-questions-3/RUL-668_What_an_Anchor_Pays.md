# What an Anchor Pays

## What this is
The price an anchor pays for taking someone's Thinning save, which Thinning and Unmaking [SYS-907] established in principle. It is also on the handbook's House Rules page and its Thinning page.

## The rule [HOMEBREW]
- **The anchor makes the save** at the traveller's DC. If it succeeds, both are safe.
- **If it fails, the anchor takes the Thinning:** their own Thinning rises by one, and they strike one of their own memories.
- An anchor can do this once per day for any one person, and only for someone whose true name they know.
