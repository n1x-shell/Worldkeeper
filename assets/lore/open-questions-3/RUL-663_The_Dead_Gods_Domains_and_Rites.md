# The Dead Gods: Domains and Rites

## What this is
Portfolios, cleric domains, worshippers, symbols and rites for the three dead gods. Domains come from the Player's Handbook (2024), Xanathar's and Tasha's; the handbook shows the domains and what players would know.

## Maruun, the Sundered Tide [DEI-907]
- **Domains.** Tempest; Grave, for the Drowned Saints.
- **Symbol.** A wave breaking upward, or a closed eye under water.
- **Temples and rites.** No temple stands for her. The Drowned Saints keep the Sunken Nave, drown their converts on the Sundering (Monsoon 1), and teach that what the sea took, it will give back.
- **Divine statistics.** None: she's dead. Her heart, the Tidecaller's Heart, is all that's left of her power.

## Aurex, the Throne-Breaker [DEI-908]
- **Portfolio.** Ambition, and the price of it: anyone who reaches for a throne.
- **Domains.** War; Order.
- **Worshippers.** Nobody, openly. Usurpers, pretenders and ambitious officers pray to him in private, and soldiers spit for luck when they pass the Throne.
- **Symbol.** A crown split down the middle.
- **Temples and rites.** No temples. A would-be ruler leaves a coin at the foot of the Shattered Throne before making a move. Most never collect it.

## The Nameless Smith [DEI-909]
- **Portfolio.** Making: the forge, craft, and things made to last.
- **Domains.** Forge; Knowledge.
- **Worshippers.** Smiths everywhere, without a name to call it. Every forge is its shrine.
- **Symbol.** A blank smith's mark.
- **Rites.** The first blow on every new blade is struck in silence (already in the handbook). There are no feast days. Some smiths leave their forge burning one night a year, unfed, for the Smith.
