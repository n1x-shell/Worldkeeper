# Where the Legendary Sites Are

## What this is
The GM's answers to where Aetheris's legendary sites really are. The handbook still says nobody agrees, and no map pins are placed, so players still have to find them.

## The sites
- **The Ashen Wastes.** East of the Tyrant's Dominion, about three weeks from Hollow Refuge. The Covenant's Kiln stands at its western edge.
- **The Titan's Fall.** On the dusk coast of the Eternal Veil, south of Noctis, where the gods' bones run down the cliffs into the Black Tides. Grave-robbers work the bones at low tide.
- **The Abyssal Hollow.** Under the sea off the dusk coast, north of the Titan's Fall, in water shallow enough to dive at the lowest tide of the year. Whether it is the Drowned City still stays open.
- **The Crucible of Renewal.** Beneath the iron mountains of the Tyrant's Dominion, below the deepest mines of the Anvil of Dominion. The way in is a lava tube the Legion sealed.
- **The Nameless Forge.** Directly beneath the Cairn of the Forgotten. The Nameless Choir has been singing over it for years without knowing. The graves are warm in winter.
- **The Shattered Throne.** It moves. It stands on a hill beside whichever road the next would-be ruler of the Marches will take, which is why soldiers keep passing it, and why nobody agrees where it is.
- **The Bloodmoon Spire.** In the mists of Wither's Hollow. It rises only when the Wanderer, the small red moon, is full.
- **The Rift of Echoes.** North-west, beyond the Fractured Nexus, where the land ends in haze. The Whispering Circle's seers go there.
- **The Vault of the Nameless.** Under Ashen Spire, the ruined capital of the last kingdom. The scratching comes from inside.
- **The Hollowed Veil.** A valley in the Eternal Veil north of Noctis, where the dusk never changes and the lost walk. The Umbral Paths lead into it if you ask them to.
