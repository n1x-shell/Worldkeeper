# Thornhaven and Hollow Refuge: Layouts

## What this is
Thornhaven's size and layout, and Hollow Refuge's government and streets. Thornhaven's population and the Lantern Council are also in the handbook.

## Thornhaven [LOC-308]
- **Population.** About 150 members of the Eastern Tendril, and their families.
- **Layout.** A ring of great trees in the Eastern Deepwood, joined by rope bridges, with gourd-lamps in every branch.
  1. **The Checking Gate**, where every pack is searched for Kaelith work.
  2. **The Burning Pit**, at the centre, where Kaelith work is burned while the Tendril watches.
  3. **The Listening Roots**, a hollow under the biggest tree, for communion.
  4. **Sorrel's Lodge**, in the oldest tree.
  5. **The Gourd Gardens**, where the lamps are grown.

## Hollow Refuge [SET-001]
- **Government.** The Lantern Truce is the only law. **The Lantern Council** keeps it: the five factions' envoys and one **Warden of the Lamp**, chosen by the Refuge's own people, who meet at the Lantern House on the first day of each month. The Warden can expel anyone who draws a weapon inside the walls. The current Warden is Mara Venn, who says she didn't want it.
- **Streets.** One main street, the **Gate Road**, runs in from the East Gate, where the envoys arrive, to the Lantern House at the centre. The Crooked Nail stands just inside the gate. The Market of Lost Things fills the square in front of the Lantern House. Old Vara's Forge is on the lane behind the market. The Old Wall rings the whole town, its oldest stretch on the north side. The Seer's Tower stands at the north edge, and the Empty House is at the end of a quiet lane off the square that nobody seems to walk down.
