# Empyrean Spire

## Established description

A floating city of white stone. Capital of the Radiant Ascendancy and seat of the Celestial Order. The High Theurgists rule from the Throne of Light. The Order's chief fortress, the Golden Aerie, stands nearby.

## Travel

About a week beyond the dominion's border, which is itself about a week from Hollow Refuge.
