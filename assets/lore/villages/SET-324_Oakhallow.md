# Oakhallow

## Overview

One of the nine settlements in the hills west and south of Ashvale [SET-301]: Ashvale's home villages, rivals and trade partners. A woodland village among ancient oaks in the hills west of Ashvale. Foresters, hunters and charcoal-burners, wary of the Spore Circle.
