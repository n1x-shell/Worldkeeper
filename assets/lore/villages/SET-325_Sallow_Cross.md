# Sallow Cross

## Overview

One of the nine settlements in the hills west and south of Ashvale [SET-301]: Ashvale's home villages, rivals and trade partners. A crossroads market village south of Ashvale where the hill roads meet. Its market is the biggest in the hills outside Ashvale.
