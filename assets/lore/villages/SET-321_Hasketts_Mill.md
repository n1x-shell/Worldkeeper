# Haskett's Mill

## Overview

One of the nine settlements in the hills west and south of Ashvale [SET-301]: Ashvale's home villages, rivals and trade partners. A mill village on the river south-west of Ashvale. Its mill, built and run by the halfling Haskett family, grinds for half the villages.
