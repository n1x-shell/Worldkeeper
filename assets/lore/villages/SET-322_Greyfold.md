# Greyfold

## Overview

One of the nine settlements in the hills west and south of Ashvale [SET-301]: Ashvale's home villages, rivals and trade partners. A small stone village in the southern hills, with a shrine to Solenne that keeps the oldest records of births and deaths in the hills. More children are born there with light under their skin than anywhere else.

Elder Mott Greyfold
