# Low Fenn

## Overview

One of the nine settlements in the hills west and south of Ashvale [SET-301]: Ashvale's home villages, rivals and trade partners. A fen village of stilt houses on the edge of the southern marshes, south of Ashvale, where the salt trade passes through. Tortles, lizardfolk and halflings live there together.
