# Bone Herald

## Overview

A dead court's messenger, all claws and courtesy, sent to fetch you.

## Appearance

A tall skeletal thing in rotten court livery, with claws like scythes, bowing very low. It has an invitation for you.

## Where

The Ossuary of Kings, depth 3 of 7.

## Game statistics (GM)

Start from: Boneclaw (MotM). About CR 12.

## Secret (GM only)

A Bone Herald's invitation is a valid invitation into the deep realm.
