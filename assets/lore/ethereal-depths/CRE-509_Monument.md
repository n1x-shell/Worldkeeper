# Monument

## Overview

A statue of you, celebrating something you never did, that would very much like you to stay.

## Appearance

A statue twenty feet high, unmistakably you, raising a sword over a plaque that names a battle you've never heard of. Its head turns to follow you.

## Where

The Fields of Lost Realities, depth 2 of 7.

## Game statistics (GM)

Start from: Stone Golem (MM). About CR 10.

## Secret (GM only)

Reading the plaque calls for the Thinning save. On a failure, the character gains a memory of the battle in place of a real one.
