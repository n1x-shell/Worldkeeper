# Fate-seer

## Overview

A robed, bulbous-headed prophet that reads the threads and bends them toward something beyond the world.

## Appearance

A tall figure in a robe of tangled thread, with a huge, soft, swollen head and eyes that are looking at something behind you, very far away.

## Where

The Loom of Eternity, depth 5 of 7.

## Game statistics (GM)

Start from: Star Spawn Seer (MotM). About CR 13.

## Secret (GM only)

A fate-seer can read a character's thread and tell them one true thing about their future, then pull on it. The Black Sun's eclipse and the star spawn's "coming" may be the same thing, or may not.
