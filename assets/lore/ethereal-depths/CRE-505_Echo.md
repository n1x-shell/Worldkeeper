# Echo

## Overview

A copy of someone who passed through the Verge, left behind and trying to finish their life.

## Appearance

A stranger who looks exactly like someone you know, and who knows things only they would know, except the last few years.

## Where

The Hollow Verge, depth 1 of 7.

## Game statistics (GM)

Start from: Doppelganger (MM). About CR 3.

## Secret (GM only)

Echoes are why the Colosseum of Echoes works. A Colosseum Echo background character may be one.
