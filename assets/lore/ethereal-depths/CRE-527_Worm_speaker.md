# Worm-speaker

## Overview

Worm-headed folk who came in from beyond the Horizon and want to take others back with them.

## Appearance

People-shaped figures in grey robes, whose heads are worms, a ring of tiny teeth where a face should be. They speak very politely.

## Where

The Black Horizon, depth 7 of 7.

## Game statistics (GM)

Start from: Psurlon and Psurlon Ringer (BAM). About CR 3 to 8.

## Secret (GM only)

The worm-speakers are what the Echoborn could become. Elyndria knows it, and doesn't care.
