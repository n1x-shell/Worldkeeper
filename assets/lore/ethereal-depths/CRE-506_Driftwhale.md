# Driftwhale

## Overview

A vast, gentle beast that swims the silver between the wreck-islands of the Fields.

## Appearance

Something the size of a ship turns slowly over the wreckage, its belly lit like a lantern, and sings a long low note that you feel in your teeth.

## Where

The Fields of Lost Realities, depth 2 of 7.

## Game statistics (GM)

Start from: Kindori (BAM). About CR 7.

## Secret (GM only)

A driftwhale will carry a party between regions of the deep realm if asked kindly, and only if the party is invited somewhere.
