# Corridor Cloak

## Overview

A thing like a black manta that lives in the corridors behind reality and wraps anyone who wanders.

## Appearance

A dark cloak hanging on a hook in a corridor that goes on too long. Then it opens, and there are eyes along the hem.

## Where

The Citadel of the Hidden, depth 6 of 7.

## Game statistics (GM)

Start from: Cloaker (MM). About CR 8.

## Secret (GM only)

The Hidden Ones keep them as door-wardens. They obey anyone wearing a Warden's blank mask.
