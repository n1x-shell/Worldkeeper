# Starlight Madness

## Overview

The stars past the Black Horizon aren't anyone's stars, and looking at them does something to you.

## Appearance

Stars that are too close, in colours with no names, moving when you aren't looking, and then very still when you are.

## Where

The Black Horizon, depth 7 of 7.

## Game statistics (GM)

Start from: Hazard: each hour under the wrong stars, Wisdom save (DC 13 at the edge, 18 deep in); on a failure, a short-term madness (DMG), and on three failures a long-term one.
