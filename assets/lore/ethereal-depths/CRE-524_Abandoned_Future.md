# Abandoned Future

## Overview

A future the Hidden Ones discarded, given body in the pools of the Citadel, and very angry about it.

## Appearance

Someone who looks almost like you, older, in clothes from a world that never was, rising out of a pool of silver and asking why you chose this life instead of theirs.

## Where

The Citadel of the Hidden, depth 6 of 7.

## Game statistics (GM)

Start from: Oblex (Elder Oblex, MotM), shaped as a person who never existed. About CR 9.

## Secret (GM only)

Each is a future one of the player characters might have had. Killing it closes that future for good.
