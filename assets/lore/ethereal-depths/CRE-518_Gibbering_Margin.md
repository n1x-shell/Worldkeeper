# Gibbering Margin

## Overview

A mass of mouths and eyes made of every unfinished sentence in the Archive.

## Appearance

A heaving mound of ink and flesh, covered in mouths, every one of them reciting a different book at once, getting louder as you get closer.

## Where

The Silent Archive, depth 4 of 7.

## Game statistics (GM)

Start from: Gibbering Mouther (MM). About CR 2.

## Secret (GM only)

This is the first true Far Realm creature a party meets. It is how the Archive smells when the Black Horizon is near.
