# Threshold Hound

## Overview

A black hound that guards the Verge's paths, and decides who goes deeper.

## Appearance

A dog-shaped hole in the light, padding beside you just out of reach. It stops at a doorway and looks at you, and waits.

## Where

The Hollow Verge, depth 1 of 7.

## Game statistics (GM)

Start from: Shadow Mastiff (MotM). About CR 2.

## Secret (GM only)

The hounds were bred by the Hidden Ones to herd the Awakened. Since the Cycle broke, they answer to nobody.
