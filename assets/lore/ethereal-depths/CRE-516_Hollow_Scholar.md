# Hollow Scholar

## Overview

A vulture-headed ancient who traded everything for knowledge, and still wants more.

## Appearance

A hunched figure in a scholar's robe, with a bald vulture's head and fingers that have turned every page in the Archive twice.

## Where

The Silent Archive, depth 4 of 7.

## Game statistics (GM)

Start from: Nagpa (MotM). About CR 17.

## Secret (GM only)

The Hollow Scholars were Hidden Ones' archivists, cursed when they read too far. They want the untitled book.
