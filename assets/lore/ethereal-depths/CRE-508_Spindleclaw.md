# Spindleclaw

## Overview

Spider-bodied slavers that sail the Fields in ships made of other people's worlds.

## Appearance

An eel-headed thing on eight jointed legs, wearing a breastplate from one world and a crown from another, with a line of chained captives behind it.

## Where

The Fields of Lost Realities, depth 2 of 7.

## Game statistics (GM)

Start from: Neogi Hatchling, Neogi and Neogi Master (MotM). About CR 3 to 6.

## Secret (GM only)

The spindleclaws trade with the Architects of Eternity. The Architects don't ask where the workers come from.
