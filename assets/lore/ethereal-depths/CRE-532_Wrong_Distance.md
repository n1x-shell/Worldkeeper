# Wrong Distance

## Overview

Past the edge of the realm, distance and time stop agreeing.

## Appearance

The tower ahead gets further away as you walk toward it. Then you turn around, and it's behind you.

## Where

The Black Horizon, depth 7 of 7.

## Game statistics (GM)

Start from: Hazard: each hour of travel, roll a d6. 1: a day passes. 2-5: an hour. 6: no time at all. Navigation checks have Disadvantage.
