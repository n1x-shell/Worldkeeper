# Crowned Dead

## Overview

A dead monarch of an erased kingdom, still holding court, and terrified of finally dying.

## Appearance

A skeleton in three crowns, one on top of the other, on a throne of other thrones. Its courtiers are bones. Its voice shakes.

## Where

The Ossuary of Kings, depth 3 of 7.

## Game statistics (GM)

Start from: Skull Lord (MotM). About CR 15.

## Secret (GM only)

Each Crowned Dead knows one true thing about the Cycle and will trade it for protection.
