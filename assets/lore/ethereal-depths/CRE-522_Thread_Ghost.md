# Thread Ghost

## Overview

A life's thread, cut loose, glowing and wandering and looking for the person it belonged to.

## Appearance

A figure of pale starlight, flickering, with the face of someone who's just about to remember who they are.

## Where

The Loom of Eternity, depth 5 of 7.

## Game statistics (GM)

Start from: Starlight Apparition (BAM). About CR 12.

## Secret (GM only)

Thread ghosts are lives the Loom has let go: the Unmade, the erased, and people whose fate someone tweaked. Who did the tweaking is the Loom's mystery.
