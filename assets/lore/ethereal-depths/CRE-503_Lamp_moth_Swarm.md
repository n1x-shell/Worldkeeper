# Lamp-moth Swarm

## Overview

A cloud of pale moths that eats light, and then whatever was standing in it.

## Appearance

Grey moths, thousands of them, settling on every lamp until the flame gutters out under their bodies. Then they turn toward the warmest thing left.

## Where

The Hollow Verge, depth 1 of 7.

## Game statistics (GM)

Start from: Swarm of Insects (MM), dealing radiant damage and putting out non-magical light. About CR 1/2.

## Secret (GM only)

They also gather to anyone who has just remembered something, as if a memory were a light.
