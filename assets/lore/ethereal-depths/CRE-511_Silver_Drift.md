# Silver Drift

## Overview

A current in the silver void between the Fields' islands, which carries off anything it catches.

## Appearance

A shimmer in the empty air between the islands, like heat over a road. Loose wreckage drifts into it and is gone.

## Where

The Fields of Lost Realities, depth 2 of 7.

## Game statistics (GM)

Start from: Hazard: astral current. A creature caught in it is carried 1d4 miles a round unless it succeeds on a DC 13 Strength (Athletics) check.
