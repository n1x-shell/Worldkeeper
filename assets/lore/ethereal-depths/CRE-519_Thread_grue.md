# Thread-grue

## Overview

A small, grinning, fish-faced thing that tangles the threads of fate, and the minds of anyone nearby.

## Appearance

A hunched little creature with a fish's face and a mouth full of needle teeth, pulling at a glowing thread and giggling. Your thoughts go wrong when it's close.

## Where

The Loom of Eternity, depth 5 of 7.

## Game statistics (GM)

Start from: Star Spawn Grue (MotM). About CR 1/4.

## Secret (GM only)

Star spawn are an infection from beyond the Black Horizon. A thread a grue tangles is a real life in Aetheris or Kaelmar going wrong. The Loom's sentinels hunt them.
