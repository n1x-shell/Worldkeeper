# The Ethereal Orb

## What it is
The Ethereal Orb is the Ethereal Crystal made whole: the twelve shards [COS-906] reforged into one. Every Aetherfall of the Cycle [COS-903] was someone forming the Orb and telling the Loom of Eternity [ETH-505] what to weave. Breaking the Cycle cut the shards from the Loom, but it didn't stop the Orb from being made. Since the Cycle broke, it can only be made in the Ethereal Realm, outside the world.

This supersedes the World Canon Rules' line that the Orb is removed. See Owner Decisions (Worldkeeper) [DEC-910].

## Forming it
- **The shards.** All twelve, held by one person, player or not. That includes the Lost Twelfth Shard [SHD-012], which nobody remembers.
- **The road.** The bearer carries them into the deep realm. Getting there needs an invitation, as for anyone, or the thirteenth shard.
- **The forge.** Someone or something in the realm forms the Orb, at a place the bearer has to find. Who and where is the GM's choice (see Candidates).
- **The weight.** Every shard the bearer carries charges its own price [RUL-002] for as long as they carry it. Carrying twelve is a campaign in itself.

## The Ethereal Orb (twelve shards): ascension
- The bearer ascends. They leave their body and become a god: a new power in the Ethereal Realm, shaping the world from outside it.
- They remake Aetheris and Kaelmar in their image, as every Aetherfall did. The new world worships them, and remembers no other.
- It lasts until the god falls: dies, is defeated, or doubts. Then the Orb splits into shards again, the shards scatter, and the Cycle starts over.
- The other powers in the realm see a rival, and some of them have been waiting for one.

## The Greater Ethereal Orb (thirteen shards): the world healed
- **The thirteenth shard must choose its bearer.** Nobody can take it. The Forgotten One [NPC-901] can give it, or it can choose someone, and it only chooses a bearer who means to free the world rather than rule it.
- **It heals both continents.** In Aetheris the scars of the Cycle close and the five dominions settle into one world. In Kaelmar the paused change is finished gently, by consent: the Bloom stays part of the land and its people, but the vaults, the glass and the Root Core go quiet for good, and nobody is rewritten again.
- **It closes the Rift** at both ends, and every way into the Ethereal Realm.
- **It gives everyone back their names and memories.** Everyone the Cycle erased, and everyone Unmade, is remembered again: names on the stones, families who knew them, histories restored. The dead stay dead, but nobody is forgotten.
- **The price.** Everyone who came into the realm with the bearer is shut in when it closes, for the rest of their mortal lives. The world is saved by people who never see it again.
- **After.** Nobody in the world can start the Cycle again. The powers in the realm are shut out, not gone.

## The bargain
The Hidden Ones say the world can be free, remembered or safe, and only two at once [COS-915]. The Ethereal Orb makes the world safe on one god's terms, but neither free nor remembered. Leaving the shards scattered keeps it free, but neither safe nor remembered. The Greater Orb makes it all three, and the price is paid by the people who stay behind. Whether that proves the Hidden Ones wrong, or only proves how much it costs, is for the table.

## The powers
Each wants something, and most ignore a shard-bearer until they are tested.
- **The Hidden Ones** [FAC-007] want the twelve reforged and the Cycle back. They will help a twelve-shard bearer, quietly, until a thirteenth shard appears. Then they oppose it with everything they have.
- **The elder gods** and **the Watchers** [COS-904] have their own aims, left to the GM. Some may want the Orb made; some may want it never made; one may want to be the forge.
- **The residents.** The Cycle Breakers will fight any twelve-shard Orb. The Architects of Eternity want the Crucible to be the forge. The Titans want the bearer to prove themselves at the Colosseum. The Echoborn don't care which Orb, so long as someone opens a door.

## Candidates for the forge (GM)
Pick one, or none, and keep the others as rumours.
- **The Loom's dark crystal.** Twelve colossal crystals pierce the Loom of Eternity, and one is dark. Set the shards there, and the Loom forms the Orb itself. The Loom Sentinels [CRE-533] guard it.
- **Vyrithar, the Weaver of Veils** [NPC-002], the Loom's keeper, who forms it for whoever arrives with all twelve, as the Hidden Ones always have.
- **An elder god in the Nameless Sepulcher** [LOC-509]. Its seals are breaking. It will forge the Orb, and ask something in return.
- **The Nameless Smith's last fire** [DEI-909]. The god who made the gods' weapons is dead, but something of its forge burns in the realm, and it remembers how.

## Other endings
- **Leave it as it is.** The shards stay scattered and the world stays Unbound: free, but still quietly shaped by unseen forces, and always one gathering away from the Cycle.
- **Destroy the shards.** Someone finds a way. No Orb, ever again, and no Cycle. The world loses something too; the GM decides what. Suggestions: magic in Aetheris dims; the erased can never be remembered; whoever destroys them is Unmade.
- **Bargain with a power.** Give the shards to the Hidden Ones, an elder god or the Watchers, in exchange for something. What they do with them is theirs to decide.

## What the world knows
Every faction knows the old story: gather the twelve shards, make the Crystal whole, and whoever holds it remakes the world. That's why they want them. Scholars now say that since the Cycle broke, the twelve would have to be carried beyond the world, into the Ethereal Realm, to do it. Almost nobody knows a thirteenth shard exists, and nobody knows about the Greater Orb.
