# The Rootspire

## Established description

A colossal tree-city over a ruined capital. Capital of the Verdant Rebirth and home of the Wildborn Clans. The elders meet at the Den of Elders. The Clans' chief fortress, the Verdant Crown, stands nearby.

## Travel

About a week beyond the dominion's border, which is itself about a week from Hollow Refuge.
