# The Harmony Mirror

## Description
A hand mirror whose glass came from one of the seven seeps.

## Lore power
Shows the holder the world as it would be in the harmony: everyone safe, everyone joined, nobody alone.

## Lore cost
Each look costs a level of Thinning, and people come back to look again.

## Where it is (GM)
Kaelholm confiscated one in 297 AB. It wasn't the only one.

## Mechanics
No 2024 rules are assigned. Build them from the power and the cost when the party finds it.
