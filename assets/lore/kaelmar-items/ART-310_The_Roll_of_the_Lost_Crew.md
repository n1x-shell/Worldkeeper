# The Roll of the Lost Crew

## Description
A Kaelholm roll with the names of the 22 AB repair crew, one of them scraped out.

## Lore power
Read aloud, each name brings the reader that person's last memory.

## Lore cost
The reader keeps the memory, and loses one of their own to make room.

## Where it is (GM)
The Tulls keep it in a locked drawer. One name on it is a Tull's.

## Mechanics
No 2024 rules are assigned. Build them from the power and the cost when the party finds it.
