# The Reading Glass of Kaelith

## Description
A palm-sized lens of perfect glass in a silver frame, older than Kaelholm.

## Lore power
Reads any glyph, living or dead, including the ones cut into people.

## Lore cost
After a while, the wearer sees glyphs on everyone, all the time, and can't stop reading them.

## Where it is (GM)
Lost with the repair crew in Arborhold in 22 AB.

## Mechanics
No 2024 rules are assigned. Build them from the power and the cost when the party finds it.
