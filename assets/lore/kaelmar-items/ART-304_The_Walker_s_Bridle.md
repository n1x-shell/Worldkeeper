# The Walker's Bridle

## Description
A harness of iron and braided root, made for a Kaelith walker that no longer exists.

## Lore power
Commands any Kaelith construct that wears it, or that hears its bells.

## Lore cost
The construct remembers being commanded, and so do its makers, wherever they are.

## Where it is (GM)
The Wrights would trade almost anything for it. So would the Ascendancy.

## Mechanics
No 2024 rules are assigned. Build them from the power and the cost when the party finds it.
