# The Ember Crown of the Tendril

## Description
A circlet of charred root, worn by the first leader of the Spore Circle's Eastern Tendril.

## Lore power
Anything Kaelith-made that its wearer touches crumbles to ash.

## Lore cost
The wearer starts to want to touch everything.

## Where it is (GM)
Still worn, by someone in the Deepwood.
