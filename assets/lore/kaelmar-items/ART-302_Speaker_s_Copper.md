# Speaker's Copper

## Description
A bracelet of copper wire, the first binding-line ever made, worn by the first Bloom-Speaker.

## Lore power
Lets its wearer commune as deeply as they like, once, and come all the way back.

## Lore cost
The Bloom remembers the wearer afterwards, and calls to them in every grove.

## Where it is (GM)
Tevan Lin knows it exists. He doesn't know who has it.

## Mechanics
No 2024 rules are assigned. Build them from the power and the cost when the party finds it.
