# The Thirteen Vaults

## Overview
The thirteen Kaelith vaults, joined by the Under-Roads [LOC-303], as Kaelmar knows them: twelve catalogued by Kaelholm [FAC-301] from outside, and a thirteenth in the old records that nobody has found.

## The vaults
Arborhold [VLT-303], Cradlehorn [VLT-304], Mirewell [VLT-302], Cinderlock [VLT-305], Anvilreach [VLT-306], Rimeholt [VLT-307], Deepcall [VLT-308], Saltgate [VLT-309], Greyhall [VLT-310], Watchbarrow [VLT-311], Glassmere [VLT-312], Hollowbell [VLT-313]. The unfound thirteenth is the Seed Vault [VLT-301].

## GM notes
Players get each vault's region and Kaelholm's catalogue name for its strand. Keys, interiors and the Seed Vault stay GM-only.
