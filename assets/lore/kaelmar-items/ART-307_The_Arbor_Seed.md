# The Arbor Seed

## Description
A seed the size of a fist, warm, and heavier than it should be.

## Lore power
Planted, it grows a living house overnight, with rooms, doors and a hearth that never needs wood.

## Lore cost
The house is a little alive. It has opinions about its guests.

## Where it is (GM)
The Bloom-Speakers have planted one. It's their meeting house, and it likes Tevan.

## Mechanics
No 2024 rules are assigned. Build them from the power and the cost when the party finds it.
