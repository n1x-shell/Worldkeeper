# Safety Tools

## What this is
The table's safety tools: lines and veils, the pause, the open door, check-ins, and 'your character, your call'. The player-facing text is in the handbook.

## GM notes
Lists the world's darker themes (memory loss, being forgotten, possession, body horror, war, cults, grief) so the table can set lines and veils.
