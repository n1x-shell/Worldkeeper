# Starting in the Nameless Asylum

## What this is
A player's guide to beginning a campaign at the Nameless Asylum [LOC-506], in the Hollow Verge. The player-facing text is in the handbook.

## GM notes
Pairs with the opening adventure The Lamplighter. Nothing in the guide reveals the residents' secrets or the Loose Thread. Characters start with an Ethereal Background [RUL-501].
