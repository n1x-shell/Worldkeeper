# The Cults: Strongholds and Strength

## What this is
Where each of the eight cults is based, what it has, how many it numbers and how far it has got (GM only). Numbers are the GM's best count for the whole of Aetheris, not a census.

## The Ashen Covenant [CLT-001]
- **Headquarters.** **The Kiln**: a dead Legion forge-town at the edge of the Ashen Wastes, rebuilt as a temple of fires that never go out. Vexis "the Infernal Architect" made them permanent, and is paid by the year.
- **Resources.** Tithes from converts made after every fire; the Kiln's undying furnaces, which can forge and burn things nothing else can; maps of the Wastes from three failed expeditions.
- **Strength.** About 600 sworn: some 200 at the Kiln, a hundred wandering preachers and fire-singers, and about 300 quiet sympathisers in the cities, who light fires when asked.
- **Where they've got to.** A fourth expedition into the Ashen Wastes is being gathered to reach the First Fire. The Covenant is splitting: Hadrek wants to carry the Fire out and burn the world with it; Elder Thaelon wants only to see it. Aedrin the Cinderfather, who can't die, will lead whichever side wins.

## The Black Sun [CLT-002]
- **Headquarters.** No temple. The faithful meet in the dark: caves, cellars and shuttered chapels, a different one each new moon. The real centre is the Seer's Tower in Hollow Refuge, where High Prophet Azhir lives as the Blind Seer (as already established).
- **Resources.** Azhir's foresight; the prophets' four rites, each from a different dream; deserters from the Celestial Order, brought in by Cassiel; and shard fragments, which every rite needs.
- **Strength.** About 400 faithful, growing by about a third after every eclipse (the rumour says it doubles). Four prophets, each with their own following.
- **Where they've got to.** The prophets compete to perform their rites. Azhir quietly sabotages the ones he can (as already established), so the cult moves slower than any prophet wants. Seraine and Cassiel suspect someone is holding them back.

## The Drowned Saints [CLT-003]
- **Headquarters.** **The Sunken Nave**: a half-drowned cathedral on the dusk coast of the Eternal Veil, reachable on foot only at low tide. At high tide only the bell tower shows. They also pray at the Kneelers, on the Black Tides.
- **Resources.** The devotion of a dozen fishing villages; two ships that cross the Tides without lanterns; and the returned: the drowned who came back, whom the faithful treat as holy.
- **Strength.** About 1,000 faithful along the coast, some 40 of them returned. Father Iskar leads; the returned are its priests.
- **Where they've got to.** They drown converts every Sundering (Monsoon 1). They're searching the sea floor for the sunken city. Whether it's the Abyssal Hollow stays undefined.

## The Bloodbound Company [CLT-004]
- **Headquarters.** **The Red Ledger**: a fortified caravanserai in the Hollow Marches, a day from Hollow Refuge, where every contract the Company has ever signed is kept in blood. Recruiters work the Bleeding Arena and the Legion's other pits.
- **Resources.** Garek's war-chest, which funds wars and collects on them; contracts with four of the five great factions at once; a reputation for always collecting.
- **Strength.** About 800 swords in twelve companies of sixty or so, plus hired auxiliaries for big jobs. Varik commands in the field; Selka the Pactmother decides.
- **Where they've got to.** Selka has quietly bought every Company contract on both sides of the Bloodmarked Fields. Whatever happens there next, the Company gets paid twice.

## The Nameless Choir [CLT-005]
- **Headquarters.** They gather at **the Cairn of the Forgotten**, among the unmarked graves, and sing to the ones that stir.
- **Resources.** Nobody remembers them, which makes them perfect messengers and terrible witnesses. Their songs, which carry what they've lost. The Cantor.
- **Strength.** Perhaps 120. Nobody can count them, including them.
- **Where they've got to.** They're looking for a way into the Nameless Forge, where the first name was erased. They believe whoever erased them learned how there.

## The Silent Accord [CLT-006]
- **Headquarters.** None, on purpose. Scribe-Mother Ondine Marrow lives in Hollow Refuge. Copies of the forbidden ledger are hidden in about forty places across Aetheris.
- **Resources.** The ledger and its copies; archivists inside the Arcane Consortium; safe houses run by the cells' Keepers.
- **Strength.** About thirty cells of three to eight (as already established), so roughly 150 people. No cell knows more than two others.
- **Where they've got to.** They're assembling the evidence for a thirteenth shard. They're one cell away from asking the Blind Seer directly, which would be a mistake.

## The Hollow Tribunal [CLT-007]
- **Headquarters.** **The Hollow Court**: a courtroom in the Hollow Verge that appears wherever a judgement is due. Nobody finds it; it finds them.
- **Resources.** Name-Eaters, which they keep on leashes; masked hunters (as in The Long Way Out); informers like Dannick Thrale, paid in what they most need; and the masks themselves, which let a hunter pass as someone else once.
- **Strength.** Seven judges, of whom Justicar Quell is the only one with a name, about thirty hunters, and informers nobody has counted. Sister Nobody was once one of the judges (as already established).
- **Where they've got to.** They want to know whether a cut thread of the Loom can be tied again, and who can do it. The Loose Thread has their full attention.

## The Whispering Circle [CLT-008]
- **Headquarters.** **The Rift of Echoes** (as already established), where the seers go to remember. Pell the Rememberer keeps a house in Hollow Refuge for those who come asking.
- **Resources.** Memories of earlier turns of time, which are right more often than anyone likes; listeners in every faction; Pell.
- **Strength.** About 60 seers and twice as many listeners.
- **Where they've got to.** They remember this year ending badly, and they disagree about how. Half want to warn everyone; half think warning everyone is how it went wrong last time.
