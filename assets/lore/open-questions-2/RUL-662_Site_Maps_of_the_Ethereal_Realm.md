# Site Maps of the Ethereal Realm

## What this is
Short keyed maps of the Ethereal Realm's named places (GM only): the main areas of each, in order from the way in. Journey times are in Journeys in the Ethereal Realm [RUL-653].

## The Rift's Edge [LOC-501]
A loose ring of fragments around a seam in the air. The fragments move between visits; the Seam doesn't.
1. **The Doorway with No House.** The usual way in from the Fields.
2. **The Road from Nowhere.** A stretch of cobbled road that leads round the ring. Walking it the wrong way brings you back to the Doorway.
3. **The Warm Kitchen.** The Baker's kitchen, with no house around it.
4. **The Drifting Rooms.** A bedroom, a schoolroom, a chapel, floating at head height. They rearrange each visit (roll a d6 for which one is in reach).
5. **The Seam.** At the centre: a crease in the air where some say the Rift's two ends meet. It hums.

## The Crucible of Creation [LOC-502]
A walled city on a hill of salvage, built in rings.
1. **The Salvage Gate.** Wardens weigh and record everything brought in.
2. **The Market of Remnants.** Just inside: traders in pieces of lost worlds.
3. **The Bridge of Many Worlds.** Seven spans from seven worlds, joining the outer ring to the inner.
4. **The Fixing Yards.** Where salvage is made unerasable. Loud, hot, and closed to outsiders.
5. **The Drafting Tower.** Kaelos's tower, all windows, covered in plans.
6. **The Foundation Stone.** At the centre: the first thing the Architects fixed. Nothing has ever moved it.

## The Fractured Throne [LOC-503]
Pieces of a palace floating over the Ossuary of Kings, joined by nothing anyone can see.
1. **The Broken Steps.** The landing, and the debating floor. Reach it by the Ossuary's bone stair, or by flying.
2. **The Hanging Gallery.** Portraits of the first rulers, every face erased.
3. **Eryndor's Alcove.** A niche where the Eldest sits and listens.
4. **The Seat.** The throne itself, split down the middle. Nobody sits in it.
5. **The Gap.** Between the pieces: a fall into the Ossuary, a long way down.

## The Colosseum of Echoes [LOC-504]
An arena of white stone in the Ossuary, with tiers that hold more people than there are.
1. **The Summoning Arch.** Where the Summoned arrive. A summons opens it; nothing else does.
2. **The Champions' Rail.** The lowest tier, for those who've beaten their echo.
3. **The Mirror Pens.** Under the stands: still pools where the echoes wait for their originals.
4. **The Sands.** The arena floor, where everyone fights themselves.
5. **Varuun's Box.** High above the Sands, carved for a giant.

## The Shadowed Sanctum [LOC-505]
It shows itself only to those it judges worthy, and its plan changes for each.
1. **The Door that Finds You.** Wherever the worthy are, a door; the Doorward waits behind it.
2. **The Corridor behind Reality.** Long, quiet, and lined with views of the world from the wrong side.
3. **The Chamber of Seats.** Where the Hidden Ones meet. How many seats there are stays open.
4. **The Still Room.** Where the Cycle was kept running. Silent now.

## The Nameless Asylum [LOC-506]
One long, low house of grey stone, laid out along a single hall.
1. **The Front Door.** Under the sign that's been repainted so often the wood is hollow.
2. **The Long Table.** The hall: meals, and the reading of names every evening.
3. **The Remembering Rooms.** The east wing: small quiet rooms for telling other lives.
4. **The Lost Shelf.** Along the west corridor.
5. **The Dormitories.** Upstairs, more rooms than the house should hold.
6. **Lantern's Garden.** Out the back, grey grass and a bench.
7. **The Threshold Door.** At the end of the garden path: it opens onto whichever part of the Verge you most need. Usually.

## The Veil of Eternity [LOC-507]
An endless plain under the wrong stars. There's no layout, only distance.
1. **The Last Footprints.** Where the Black Horizon's ground gives way to the plain; footprints lead out and don't come back.
2. **The Wanderer's Path.** A line worn into the plain over forty years. It doesn't go anywhere.
3. **The Star-Wells.** Pools that reflect stars, but not the ones overhead.
4. **The Edge.** If there is one. What lies beyond stays open.

## The Observatory of the Last Question [LOC-508]
A tall tower at the Black Horizon, every lens turned outward.
1. **The Camp.** Tents at the tower's foot for visitors and Walkers about to leave.
2. **The Question Hall.** Where the Questioners ask the last question. They won't tell visitors what it is.
3. **The Lens Galleries.** Floor after floor of lenses, tended day and night.
4. **The Chart Room.** Thirty years of star-charts of the Horizon. (The stars are going out: see The Ethereal Factions: Size and Ranks [RUL-661].)
5. **The Top Lens.** The largest, at the summit, pointing at nothing anyone else can see.

## The Nameless Sepulcher [LOC-509]
An obsidian tomb alone on black ground. You hear it before you see it.
1. **The Approach.** A plain of black glass, cracking underfoot more loudly every year.
2. **The Ring of Seals.** Standing stones of obsidian round the tomb, each cut with a seal. Some are already broken.
3. **The Door.** No handle, no hinges, and warm.
4. **Inside.** Left open. Who or what lies there stays undefined.
