# Lemmy Carrow

## Public role and identity
- A Crossroads courier from Hollow Refuge, who walked the shifting road for pay before the Cycle broke, and came back with maps of places that weren't there.
- For thirty-one years, the Lamplighter of the Nameless Asylum, who woke on the Verge's grey grass with a satchel and no name.

## Goal
To deliver the message he was carrying. He can't remember what it was.

## Known relationships and locations
The Nameless Asylum [LOC-506]; Mara Venn [NPC-010]

## Where his thread runs
From Hollow Refuge [SET-001] and the Crooked Nail, along the Crossroads of Fate [LOC-003], to the Ashvale Waygate [LOC-305].

## Appearance and voice
A lean man of about forty, weathered, in a long courier's coat with a waystone knot on the band of his cap. His fingertips are burned from thirty years of wicks. Dry and cheerful. He says "the road" as if there were only one.

## Pressure and secrets
- On the night the Forgotten One cut the shards from the Loom, Lemmy was halfway along the Crossroads road, which was coming out that night under Ashvale's west road. His thread snapped into three: his body sits at the Crooked Nail, his voice is caught in the Ashvale waygate, and the rest of him walked into the Hollow Verge. See The Loose Thread [HOOK-931].
- Mara Venn kept a room for him every ninth night, for years. She was his friend, perhaps more. She doesn't remember him, and keeps his cap in her locked room.
- The message was to himself, care of the Nail: he meant to say it in person. It was "Tell them I'm coming home."
