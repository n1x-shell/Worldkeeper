# The Loose Thread

## What it is
One thread of the Loom of Eternity [COS-905] has come loose, and one end of it lies in each of the three starting places. It is the thread of Lemmy Carrow [NPC-376], a Crossroads courier who was halfway along the shifting road on the night the shards were cut from the Loom. He was only half unmade, and the thread snapped into three:

| End | Where | Opening adventure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| His body | The Crooked Nail, Hollow Refuge [SET-001] | The Dead Man at the Crooked Nail |
| His voice | The waygate under Ashvale's west road [LOC-305] | The Hum Under the West Road |
| Himself, without his past | The Nameless Asylum [LOC-506] | The Lamplighter |

## Running the openings
- **Any order, any time.** Each opening stands alone and can be run whenever the party is nearby, at any level from 1 to 10. Each has notes for levels 3 and 4, and for 5 to 10.
- **Each end comes loose when someone comes near it.** The dead man appears the morning the party is at the Nail, the hum swells when salvagers go down, and the Name-Eater reaches the Lamplighter when the party is at the Asylum.
- **The ends know each other.** Whoever carries one end feels the others tug: toward Ashvale across the sea, toward the grey of the Verge, toward the Nail. Carrying two, a character hears the courier's tune at night. Thread Mites [CRE-033] and things from the between are drawn to anyone carrying an end.
- **A party that starts in one place** finds the other two ends as the campaign carries them across the world. The tug is a reason to go.

## Gathering all three
The thread can be tied back only where it snapped: at the waystone of the Crossroads of Fate [LOC-003], at dusk, with all three ends present. That means Lemmy himself, his voice in whatever holds it, and the thread's end from the Nail. Getting the Lamplighter out of the Hollow Verge and across the world to the Crossroads is an adventure of its own.

At the waystone the party chooses.

### Tie it back
Lemmy is whole: body, voice and self. He remembers everything, including the message: "Tell them I'm coming home." He walks into the Crooked Nail at dusk and Mara Venn remembers him.

The world shifts slightly to make room. Something the party knows to be true is now a little different (the GM chooses one thing), and somewhere far away a Watcher [COS-904] or one of the Hidden Ones [FAC-007] notices that a cut thread has been retied. Someone will come to see who did it.

### Let it go
Lemmy chooses to rest. He asks the party to deliver his message for him, to Mara, and then fades at the waystone, gently and gladly. The thread doesn't vanish. It shortens to a hand's length of silver that always points toward the Loom of Eternity [ETH-505]. It is the one sure guide in a realm where distances lie. Whoever carries it has Advantage on checks to find the way through the Ethereal Realm's depths [COS-021].

Either way, this opens the arc of the Door Beyond (see Major Arcs and Ways to Start [HOOK-010]).

## If an end is lost
- **The body faded** (the party never followed the dead man) or **the voice was cut** (the waygate's heart was torn out): the thread can still be tied back, but a character must give it a part of themselves to stand in for what's missing. That costs one Thinning level [SYS-907] and one memory of the player's choosing.
- **The Lamplighter was Unmade:** the thread can't be tied back. Letting it go is the only ending, and the silver guide is shorter and fainter (it works once per day).

## GM notes
- Mara Venn, Ashvale's three groups and the Asylum's residents each care about a different end. Bringing them together, even only in conversation, is half the fun.
- Don't explain the thread early. Let the players put the courier together from a cap, a tune and a man with burned fingers.
