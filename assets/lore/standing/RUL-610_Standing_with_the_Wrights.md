# Standing with the Wrights

## What they value
Tools that work, salvage that's useful, and people who fix things instead of fearing them.

## Hostile
No Wright will mend your gear or sell to you, and the walkers at the orchard gate won't let you pass.

## Distrusted
Repairs cost double, and take twice as long.

## Initiate
Repairs at cost, and a fair price for salvage.

## Trusted
A listening bowl that hears through walls, or a warding stone, made for you. From level 5, a Bastion in the Wrights' yard, with a workshop.

## Exalted
A working Walker [CRE-305] to carry gear or guard your camp, and first look at anything the Wrights recover.

## Champion
Named a Chief Wright's Friend. Brenna Halvor builds you one thing to your own design, within reason, and the Wrights follow you into any vault. The Keepers think you'll wake something.
