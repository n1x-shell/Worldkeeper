# Standing with the Boundary Keepers

## What they value
The rite sung correctly, at every singing, and nobody asking why.

## Hostile
Barred from the stones and from Ashvale's meeting hall. Orla Hesk tells the town you're dangerous, and the town listens.

## Distrusted
Nobody in Ashvale will sing the keeping song where you can hear it.

## Initiate
Welcome at the singings. Jory Hesk teaches you the song, and anyone who knows it has Advantage on their first Thinning save in Ashvale.

## Trusted
The Keepers' wardings: a stone over your door that turns away things from the between. From level 5, a Bastion in Ashvale.

## Exalted
Orla tells you one thing she has never told Jory. The Keepers will hold a rite for you, which can quiet one thing that has woken.

## Champion
Named a Hand on the Key, and taught the verses nobody else is taught. Whatever is under Ashvale knows your voice. The Bloom-Speakers think you have been told too much.
