# Standing with the Echoborn

## What they value
Questions asked about what lies beyond the realm, and answers shared.

## Hostile
They think you want to keep everyone inside, and lock you out of the Observatory.

## Distrusted
They answer your questions with questions.

## Initiate
A seat at the Observatory of the Last Question [LOC-508], and one honest answer about the realm per visit.

## Trusted
An Echoborn reading of the stars: once per adventure, ask the GM one yes-or-no question about the Ethereal Realm, answered truly. From level 5, a Bastion beside the Observatory.

## Exalted
Elyndria the Echo [NPC-072] shares what she believes lies beyond, and why the realm may be another layer of control.

## Champion
Named Last Questioner. When you reach the realm's edge, the Echoborn will be waiting to go with you.
