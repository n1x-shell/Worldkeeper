# Standing with the Veilborn

## What they value
Everyone remembered by somebody, and nobody left to die forgotten.

## Hostile
The Asylum's door doesn't open for you, and the Veilborn forget you on purpose.

## Distrusted
You may sleep at the Asylum, but nobody will remember your name.

## Initiate
A bed at the Nameless Asylum [LOC-506] whenever you need it. Your names are spoken at the evening count.

## Trusted
Written in the Asylum's book: once per long rest, Advantage on a Thinning save. From level 5, a Bastion within the Asylum's walls.

## Exalted
Kyrra the Shadeborn [NPC-070] speaks to one of the Unmade for you, once per adventure.

## Champion
Named Rememberer. While any Veilborn lives, you can't be Unmade: the first time your Thinning would reach 6, it stays at 5 instead, once.
