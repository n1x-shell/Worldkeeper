# Standing with the Cycle Breakers

## What they value
Freedom from every cycle, and anyone who helps keep the old one broken.

## Hostile
They think you want the Cycle back. At the Fractured Throne [LOC-503], you are an enemy.

## Distrusted
No Breaker will tell you anything true about the realm.

## Initiate
Welcome at the Fractured Throne. A Breaker will tell you which roads in the border layer are safe this week.

## Trusted
A Breaker guide into the deep realm, and their word for you to anyone who asks. From level 5, a Bastion in the Throne's ruins.

## Exalted
Eryndor the First Shard [NPC-063] will speak with you. The Breakers fight beside you against anyone trying to remake the Cycle.

## Champion
Named Unbound. The Breakers would follow you to the Loom itself. Whoever wants the Cycle back, they will come for you first.
