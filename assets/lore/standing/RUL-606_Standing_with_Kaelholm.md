# Standing with Kaelholm

## What they value
Order, records and catalogues: every vault opened, every thing named and put in its drawer.

## Hostile
Burned: a tribunal scrapes your names off every roll in Kaelholm. No one there will trade with, house or speak to a burned name.

## Distrusted
Grey-robed clerks follow you, and every permit takes a month.

## Initiate
A reader's token for Kaelholm's public archives, and a clerk who'll look things up for a fee.

## Trusted
A surveyor's commission: Kaelholm pays for maps and finds from the vaults, and lends you guides and gear for an expedition. From level 5, a Bastion at a Kaelholm survey station.

## Exalted
A tribunal will hear your case, or a friend's, and the restricted archives open. Kaelholm will fund one expedition per adventure.

## Champion
Named an Elder's Hand. Archivist-Elder Venn Tull consults you, and your word can unburn one name. The Spore Circle's Eastern Tendril marks you as Kaelholm's.
