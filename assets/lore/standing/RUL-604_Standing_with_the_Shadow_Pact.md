# Standing with the Shadow Pact

## What they value
Secrets kept, debts paid, and no questions asked about who you were.

## Hostile
A contract on you, posted quietly. Someone close to the party may already have taken it.

## Distrusted
Your secrets are for sale, and someone is buying. Pact fences won't touch your goods.

## Initiate
A grey silk ribbon, which gets you into Pact safehouses for a night and lets you sell to Pact fences at a fair price.

## Trusted
Forged papers and a false name in any city of the Eternal Veil. One rumour a session from Pact whisperers. From level 5, a Bastion in a Pact safehouse.

## Exalted
Passage on the Umbral Paths, once per adventure. A Silent Blade Agent [CRE-014] shadows anyone you name for a week.

## Champion
Named a Broken Circle. You learn one true secret about any person you ask after, once per adventure, and the Pact will make one person disappear from the records for you, once. Every other great faction marks you as the Pact's, if it ever finds out.
