# Standing with the Bloom-Speakers

## What they value
Communion taught carefully to anyone who asks, and nobody hurt by learning.

## Hostile
Tevan Lin warns every Speaker against you, and the sanctuary's grafted gate won't open to your hand.

## Distrusted
Speakers won't teach or guide you, and say why to anyone who asks.

## Initiate
Lessons in safe communion. You may take the copper-dark binding lines, which give Advantage on saves against the Bloom's influence.

## Trusted
A Speaker goes with you to listen where you can't. Once per adventure, they reach one mind through the Bloom. From level 5, a Bastion in the sanctuary's garden.

## Exalted
Tevan himself teaches you, and the Speakers will stand with you against the Keepers or the Wrights in any dispute in Ashvale.

## Champion
Named a Master Speaker. You can teach communion yourself, and the Bloom answers you more readily than it answers most. The Keepers think you are taking it too far.
