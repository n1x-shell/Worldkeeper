# Standing with the Wildborn Clans

## What they value
The wild left whole, beasts treated as kin, and cities kept small.

## Hostile
The forest itself is set against you in the Verdant Rebirth: trails close, beasts hunt you, and Briar Wolves [CRE-018] follow your camp.

## Distrusted
No clan will guide you. Foraging in Wildborn lands always comes up short.

## Initiate
Guest-right at Wildborn camps, and a guide through any forest the clans know. Mother Thorn will speak with you.

## Trusted
A beast companion's loyalty (a mount or hunting animal). Herbalists' remedies at no cost. From level 5, a Bastion at a Wildborn camp.

## Exalted
A Spirit Bear [CRE-019] or a band of Root Hunters [CRE-017] answers your call for one fight per adventure. The trails through Wither's Hollow open for you.

## Champion
Named Root-Kin. The High Shaman's council hears you, the Verdant Crown shelters you, and the clans will rise to defend one place you name, once. Every other great faction marks you as the Clans'.
