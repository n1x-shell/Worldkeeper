# Standing with the Spore Circle

## What they value
The conversation between people and the Bloom kept gentle, and Kaelith work left in the ground.

## Hostile
The Bloom reads you as a stranger: in the Circle's lands, Bloom-shift [CRE-352] takes you wherever it grows, and the Eastern Tendril's lamps follow your camp.

## Distrusted
No druid of the Circle will heal or guide you, and spore remedies are refused.

## Initiate
A gourd-lamp to carry. It marks you as a guest, and the Circle's guides lead you safely past spore-storms.

## Trusted
Taught the first steps of communion: once per long rest, you can ask the Bloom one simple question about what has passed through a place. From level 5, a Bastion beside a Circle grove.

## Exalted
A Myconid [CRE-301] circle shelters and hides you, and the Circle's druids lift one lasting change from a spore-storm, once per adventure.

## Champion
Named a Seventh Tendril, a rank the Circle has never given before. Elder Sorrel speaks to you as an equal, and the Bloom itself will carry one message anywhere in Kaelmar. Kaelholm marks you as the Circle's.
