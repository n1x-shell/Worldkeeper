# Faction Standing

## How it works
The party has a standing with each faction it deals with: a number, Renown, kept for the whole party. It starts at 0. The GM tracks it in the campaign's Faction Standing page, and players can see their own standing in the journal.

## The ranks
| Rank | Renown | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hostile | -10 or less | The faction acts against you openly. |
| Distrusted | -3 to -9 | Doors close, prices rise, and you are watched. |
| Neutral | -2 to 2 | You are nobody to them, yet. |
| Initiate | 3 to 9 | A friend of the faction. Small help, and a contact. |
| Trusted | 10 to 24 | One of theirs. Real help, and a place to stay. From level 5, a Bastion in one of their camps. |
| Exalted | 25 to 49 | Among their best. They act for you, and ask a lot of you. |
| Champion | 50 or more | A legend in their ranks. Few ever reach it, and rivals take note. |

Each faction's own page (Standing with...) says what each rank brings.

## Gaining and losing Renown
- **+1** for a small favour: a message carried, a rival embarrassed, a job done well.
- **+2** for a real service: a mission that serves the faction's aims, a life saved that it values.
- **+3 to +5** for a deed that changes its fortunes: a shard recovered, a fortress held, a leader saved.
- **-1 to -5** for working against it, the same scale in reverse. Lying to it and being found out costs at least 2.
- **Rivals.** The five great factions of Aetheris watch each other. A deed that helps one by harming another raises one and lowers the other by the same amount.
- **Pacing.** As a guide, a party that serves one faction steadily reaches Trusted around level 5, Exalted around level 11, and Champion only near the end of a campaign.

## What a rank can't do
A rank never forces a player's choice. A faction can ask, reward and threaten. It can't make a character obey.
