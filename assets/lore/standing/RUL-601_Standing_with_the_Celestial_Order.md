# Standing with the Celestial Order

## What they value
Order kept, oaths honoured, the undead put down and forbidden magic reported.

## Hostile
Named a heretic. Inquisitors carry your descriptions, and Order healers turn you away from every shrine in the Radiant Ascendancy.

## Distrusted
Questioned at every Order checkpoint. Healing at Order shrines costs double, and is given last.

## Initiate
Free healing at any Order shrine, and lodging in its hostels. A Dawnward Healer [CRE-002] will travel with you for a day's work.

## Trusted
A writ of passage through the Radiant Ascendancy. Order smiths sell silvered and radiant-blessed weapons at cost. From level 5, a Bastion at an Order chapterhouse.

## Exalted
A Radiant Squire [CRE-001] escort when you ask, and an audience with Sister Elyra Vale's superiors. Once per adventure, an Order priest casts a spell of up to 3rd level for you, without cost.

## Champion
Titled Sword of the Dawn. The Highlord hears you in person, Inquisitors defer to you, and the Order will send an Avenger Seraph [CRE-004] once, when it truly matters. Every other great faction marks you as the Order's.
