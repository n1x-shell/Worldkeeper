# Standing with the Architects of Eternity

## What they value
Pieces of lost worlds brought in, and builders who make things that last.

## Hostile
You are material, to them, and they'll collect you for the Crucible.

## Distrusted
They'll trade only at robbery prices, and watch what you carry.

## Initiate
Trade at the Crucible of Creation [LOC-502]: salvage from the Fields bought at a fair price.

## Trusted
An Architect builds one item for you from the remains of a lost world. From level 5, a Bastion built of lost-world stone, which can't be erased.

## Exalted
Kaelos the Forgotten Star [NPC-066] receives you, and the Architects shelter you from anyone in the realm except the Hidden Ones.

## Champion
Named Builder. Your name is set in a wall of the world they're making, where nothing can erase it. Your Thinning can never take you past 5.
