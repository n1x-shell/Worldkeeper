# The First Morning

## When

Year 1 Unbound (270 AB).

## What happened

The morning after the Cycle broke, Aetheris woke and stayed as it was. Nothing reset. Men and women killed the night before were still dead at dawn, and their families waited three days for them to walk back in, the way the dead always had. They didn't.

By the end of the first week, scouts had found the other dominions sitting where nothing had been: Legion banners a day's ride from Order shrines, Wildborn forest growing over a Consortium road. Five worlds had become one, stitched at the edges. The Hollow Marches, in the middle, belonged to none of them.

## What people say

- Old soldiers call it the Long Week: seven days when nobody knew if the world would come back the way it was.
- The dead of that first year are buried with their faces up, so they can see they've been left alone.

## GM notes

Nobody remembers who broke the Cycle. The Forgotten One cut the shards from the Loom about 270 AB, then made their home in the Empty House at Hollow Refuge. The Watchers beyond the Black Horizon noticed the same morning.
