# The Breaking of the Text

## When

Year 18 Unbound (287 AB).

## What happened

In 287 AB, a copy of the Root Texts reached a mercenary legion in the eastern mountains. Its captain, Dace Corvane, read the same pages as the Collective and came to the opposite answer: the change shouldn't be offered to everyone, only to the few who could hold the rest. The Ascendancy began as a reading group in a soldiers' camp. It's still mostly that, for now.

## What people say

- Two readings of one text, and already hatred.
- Corvane keeps a count of everything: men, coin, days. Nobody knows what the count is for.

## GM notes

Corvane's copy was annotated in a hand that wasn't Grane's. Someone wanted the text to split.
