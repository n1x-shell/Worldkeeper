# The Burned Expedition

## When

Year 25 Unbound (294 AB).

## What happened

In 294 AB, Kaelholm's scholars sent their largest expedition yet into the Eastern Deepwood, to catalogue Kaelith ruins. They found the ruins, and the Spore Circle's Eastern Tendril found them. The Tendril burned every piece of Kaelith work the expedition had gathered, and sent the scholars home alive, carrying nothing.

Kaelholm's expeditions have run into the Tendril ever since.

## What people say

- Vellum House still hires guards for every expedition, and pays better each year.
- The Tendril left a message: the old work stays burned.

## GM notes

One piece wasn't burned. A scholar carried it home sewn into his coat, and Kaelholm has kept it sealed.
