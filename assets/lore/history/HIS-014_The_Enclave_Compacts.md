# The Enclave Compacts

## When

Years 10 to 14 Unbound (279 to 283 AB).

## What happened

Once the borders held, each faction wanted a foothold inside the others. Over five years, one bargain at a time, each faction won the right to keep an enclave in every rival's dominion: a chapel, a forge-hall, a library, a den or a grove, tolerated because the other side wanted the same favour back.

The enclaves are embassies, refuges and spy-nests all at once. Most of them are all three.

## What people say

- Every enclave was built with a back door. Every host knows where it is.
- If you need to leave a dominion quietly, the rival enclave is the first place to try.

## GM notes

The Shadow Pact brokered most of the Compacts, and kept copies of every one.
