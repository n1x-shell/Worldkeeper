# The Lantern Truce

## When

Year 2 Unbound (271 AB).

## What happened

In the second year, the five factions needed somewhere to trade back their dead. They met at Hollow Refuge, in the old hospital that is now the Lantern House, and agreed one rule: weapons stay outside. Every envoy left a blade at the door, and every envoy walked out alive.

The rule held. Hollow Refuge has been neutral ground ever since, because every faction has needed it at least once, and none can afford to be the one that broke it.

## What people say

- Each faction swears it called the meeting. Each faction's envoy is said to have been the last one to hand over their blade.
- Faction soldiers still leave their weapons at the Lantern House door, even when nobody's watching.

## GM notes

None of the five envoys remembers who sent the invitations. The handwriting matched none of them.
