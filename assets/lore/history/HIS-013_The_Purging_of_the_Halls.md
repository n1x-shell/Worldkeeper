# The Purging of the Halls

## When

Year 9 Unbound (278 AB).

## What happened

In the ninth year, a city in the Radiant Ascendancy closed its gates on the Order's tax collectors and its inquisitors. The Celestial Order answered with light. When it was over, the city was still standing, empty and white, with every name scraped off every door. It's called the Purged Halls now. Nobody lives there.

## What people say

- Pilgrims to Empyrean Spire are walked past the Purged Halls, so they understand.
- Survivors say it was one inquisitor, and one night. The Order says it was justice.

## GM notes

Some survivors escaped to the old grove on the edge of the Radiant Expanse. That's how the Thornward Grove began sheltering people who flee the Order.
