# The First Glass

## When

Year 27 Unbound (296 AB).

## What happened

In 296 AB, shepherds in the hills found glass growing out of bare rock, smooth and warm, where there had never been any. Over the next year, seeps of it turned up in seven places across Kaelmar. Doors of glass, some of them, that open to an open hand.

## What people say

- Whoever opens a glass door comes back with a little less of themselves.
- Nobody has found an eighth seep. People are looking.

## GM notes

The glass is the Root Core dreaming. It seeps where the sleeping change comes closest to the surface.
