# Githzerai

## Established identity
Calm, disciplined people from beyond the world, who think very carefully before they act.

## In Aetheris
Githzerai came into Aetheris through the same cracks as the githyanki and want nothing to do with them. They keep monasteries in the Echoing Halls of the Fractured Nexus and in quiet corners of every dominion, and they're much in demand as judges, tutors and bodyguards.

## In Kaelmar
In Kaelmar githzerai are rare, and Kaelholm's archive-monks have welcomed the few who've come. They get on well.

## Strengths and flaws
Discipline, focus and psionic gifts. Remote, and hard to know.

## Culture and names
Monasteries, order, and the patient study of the mind.

Common names: Althros, Dak, Ilsa, Kalthis, Lirian, Mezzar, Vhalek, Zerra.
