# Centaurs

## Established identity
Horse-bodied wanderers of the open plains, who measure the world in a day's gallop.

## In Aetheris
Centaur herds cross the Radiant Expanse and the open land of the Hollow Marches, and their routes are older than the borders. Every dominion has tried to tax them. None has managed it twice.

## In Kaelmar
In Kaelmar centaurs roam the open Bloom country, where the spore-storms first rewrote them. Their herds travel ahead of the storms, and villages watch them to know when weather's coming.

## Strengths and flaws
Fast, strong and tireless over open ground. Uncomfortable in cities, cellars and anywhere with stairs.

## Culture and names
Herds, long migrations, and star-lore for finding the way.

Common names: Arren, Callis, Dovren, Kaya, Merrow, Sabine, Torvald, Yessa.
