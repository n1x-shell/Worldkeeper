## Secret (GM only)

War-built bodies over two metres tall, each burning around a rebirth core. They sing in tones, can fold small to pass as a large person, and lend their fire to old constructs. Every one is a fragment of the same mind and believes itself whole. All are asleep. The Seventh Ward is one.
