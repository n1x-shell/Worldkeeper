# Distances in Aetheris

## What this is
How far things are, how long the journeys take, and the scale of the world map. The player-facing text is in the handbook.

## The rule
A day's travel is about 24 miles (40 km) at a normal pace on foot, 18 at a slow pace and 30 at a fast one, and about 50 miles by ship. The Hollow Marches are about a month across, roughly 800 miles (1,300 km): the Crossroads of Fate [LOC-003] is about three days from Hollow Refuge [SET-001], the Bloodmarked Fields [LOC-002] about a week, and Blackveil Fortress [LOC-032] about a week and a half. The dominions' borders are one to two weeks from the Refuge (the Verdant Rebirth about a week, the Tyrant's Dominion a week and a half, the others about two weeks), and their capitals two to three and a half weeks: the Rootspire about two weeks, Varkhaal about two and a half, Noctis nearly three, Empyrean Spire and Zepharyos about three and a half. Aetheris is about 1,300 miles (2,100 km) across and Kaelmar about 1,000 miles (1,600 km); Kaelholm to Ashvale is about 800 miles (1,300 km), and Ashvale's villages are two to four weeks from the town.

## The world map
The world map is drawn to scale, about 2,700 miles (4,300 km) across: 0.8 miles to a pixel of the map. It shows the known world, the two continents and the Black Tides between them; the world itself is larger, and the far places lie beyond its frame. Every travel time here, on the map's routes and in the handbook is measured from it.

## Limits
Travel times are what travellers say: roads, weather, terrain and danger make any journey longer or shorter. The Ethereal Realm's map is not to scale; distances there lie (Journeys in the Ethereal Realm [RUL-653]).
