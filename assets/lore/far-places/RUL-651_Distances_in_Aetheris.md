# Distances in Aetheris

## What this is
How to turn Aetheris's travel times into distances. The travel times stay canon. The player-facing text is in the handbook.

## The rule
A day's travel is about 24 miles (40 km) at a normal pace on roads, and less in the Hollow Marches. The Marches are about four days across, roughly 100 miles (160 km). Each dominion's border is about a week from Hollow Refuge [SET-001], roughly 170 miles (270 km), and each capital about a further week, roughly 340 miles (550 km) from the Refuge. Aetheris is about 1,200 km (750 miles) across, of a size with Kaelmar's 1,500 km.

## Limits
The world map is not drawn to scale. Route labels (travellers' times) remain the measure.
