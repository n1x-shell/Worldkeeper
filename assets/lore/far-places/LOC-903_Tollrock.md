# Tollrock

## Overview
A bare grey rock a day out from the Eternal Veil [REG-005], with a Shadow Pact [FAC-004] customs post, a cistern and a lighthouse put out every evening. It is the last light ships see before the dark. The Pact collects the dark toll here (see Crossing the Black Tides [RUL-652]).
