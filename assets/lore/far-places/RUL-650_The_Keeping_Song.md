# The Keeping Song

## What this is
The four lines of Ashvale's keeping song, sung at the Rite Circle [LOC-301]. The player-facing text is in the handbook.

## The words
> Thirteen stand and one lies under,
> hush the root and hold the thunder.
> Every hand upon the key:
> never thirteen, never three.

## What it means [SECRET]
The song is the rite of a living lock. "One lies under" is the Root Core [NPC-302]. "Every hand" is everyone who sleeps in Ashvale. The last line is the founder's warning about the thirteen keys, in a form nobody recognises: never thirteen minds joined as one shape, and never the three-mind key of a vault.

## Rule [HOMEBREW]
Singing the song at the stones counts as hearing your true name against Thinning [SYS-907] for anyone who has slept in Ashvale: Advantage on the save, once per day.
