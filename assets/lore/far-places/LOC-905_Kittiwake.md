# Kittiwake

## Overview
A green, windy island off the Kaelmar coast, about five days' sail south of Kaelholm [SET-302]: fishers, gulls, and the place Tidecant was born, when Aetheris smugglers and Kaelish fishers first had to bargain. Kaelholm [FAC-301] keeps a catalogue office here.
