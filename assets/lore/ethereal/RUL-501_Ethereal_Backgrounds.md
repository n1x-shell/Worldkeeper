# Ethereal Backgrounds

## Use
Five HOMEBREW backgrounds for characters who start in the Ethereal Realm, built like the setting's other custom backgrounds: three ability score choices, two skills, one tool and one origin feat from the 2024 Player's Handbook.

| Background | Skills | Tool | Origin feat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Slipped the Cycle | Perception, Survival | Herbalism Kit | Lucky |
| Asylum Ward | Insight, Medicine | Calligrapher's Supplies | Healer |
| Fields Salvager | Investigation, Sleight of Hand | Tinker's Tools | Crafter |
| Colosseum Echo | Athletics, Intimidation | One gaming set | Savage Attacker |
| Root-road Stray | Nature, Arcana | Cartographer's Tools | Alert |

## Starting in the realm
A group that starts here begins at the Nameless Asylum [LOC-506] in the Hollow Verge [ETH-501], in the border layer. The deep realm needs an invitation, as for anyone. The level guidance in Ways Into and Out of the Ethereal Realm [ETH-591] is for travellers arriving from outside, not for residents.

## GM notes
A Colosseum Echo may be the copy, not the original. Decide with the player.
