# Keeper Lantern

## Public role and identity
- Keeper of the Nameless Asylum, in the Hollow Verge.
- Woke on the Verge's grey grass after an Aetherfall, with no memory of who made it, or why.

## Goal
That everyone in the Asylum is remembered, by somebody.

## Known relationships and locations
The Nameless Asylum [LOC-506]; Veilborn [FAC-503]

## Appearance and voice
A tall warforged of blackened iron in a grey wool cloak, with a small lamp burning behind the grille of its chest. Gentle, patient and very hard to move.

## Pressure and secrets
Lantern was built for the Celestial Order's army in a world the Order won, and erased when that world was. Veyra the Unchained knows the make.
