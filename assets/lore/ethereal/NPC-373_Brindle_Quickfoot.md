# Brindle Quickfoot

## Public role and identity
- Trades in salvage from the edge of the Fields of Lost Realities.
- Runs the Lost Shelf at the Nameless Asylum.

## Goal
To make enough to buy a door back to the world, and somewhere to put it.

## Known relationships and locations
The Nameless Asylum [LOC-506]; Veilborn [FAC-503]

## Appearance and voice
A sandy-furred harengon in a coat of patched velvet, with a trader's pack bigger than he is. Chatty, quick and honest, mostly.

## Pressure and secrets
Brindle has sold something from the Fields to the Architects that he shouldn't have: a piece of a world the Hidden Ones erased on purpose.
