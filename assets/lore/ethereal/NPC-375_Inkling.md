# Inkling

## Public role and identity
- Copies down every life told in the Remembering Rooms.
- Born in the Hollow Verge, and has never seen the world.

## Goal
To see the world his books are about.

## Known relationships and locations
The Nameless Asylum [LOC-506]; Veilborn [FAC-503]

## Appearance and voice
A small, scruffy young kenku with ink-black feathers and ink-black fingers, never without a quill. Eager, shy and full of other people's voices.

## Pressure and secrets
Inkling's copies are turning up in the Silent Archive, in books that used to be blank.
