# Sister Nobody

## Public role and identity
- Healer at the Nameless Asylum, and a cleric of Sephane.
- Forgot her own face and name long ago, and stopped looking.

## Goal
That nobody in the Asylum dies forgotten.

## Known relationships and locations
The Nameless Asylum [LOC-506]; Veilborn [FAC-503]

## Appearance and voice
A changeling whose face is a little different for everyone who looks at her, in a plain grey habit with Sephane's three stones on a cord. Warm, practical and endlessly patient.

## Pressure and secrets
Sister Nobody was once a Hollow Tribunal judge. She chose to forget. The Tribunal has not.
