# Old Corwin Ambry

## Public role and identity
- Sells ink, quills and glyph-pigments in Lampblack, Kaelholm's quarter of scribes.
- Has sold ink to every Archivist-Elder for fifty years.

## Goal
One honest roll in Kaelholm, before he dies.

## Known relationships and locations
Kaelholm [SET-302]; Lampblack

## Appearance and voice
A tiny, ink-stained halfling with a white beard tucked into his apron and blue fingertips that never wash clean. Gossipy, kind and much sharper than he lets on.

## Pressure and secrets
Corwin keeps a list of every burned name in Kaelholm, written in an ink that can't be scraped and can only be read by lamp-glyph light. Hesper Ondry [NPC-378] brings him the names. The Rasp would burn him for it.
