# Dannick Thrale

## Public role and identity
- Runs the grey grass for the Veilborn, bringing in the newly forgotten.
- Knows the Verge's paths nearly as well as Tessk.

## Goal
Enough coin to buy his sister back from the Fields.

## Known relationships and locations
The Veilborn [FAC-503]; the Nameless Asylum [LOC-506]

## Appearance and voice
A lean grey tabaxi with a lantern on a pole and a scarf over his nose against the Verge's dust. Quick, kind to newcomers, and nervous around authority.

## Pressure and secrets
Dannick takes money from the Hollow Tribunal to report on Sister Nobody [NPC-374]. He hates himself for it, and he needs the money: his sister is lost in the Fields of Lost Realities.
