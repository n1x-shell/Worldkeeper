# Sister Imra of the Cairns

## Public role and identity
- Keeps the cairns on the high peaks, each with a goliath name and a number.
- Climbs to a different peak each month to change one number.

## Goal
That the count is kept true.

## Known relationships and locations
The Eastern Mountains [REG-302]

## Appearance and voice
A lean old goliath with frost-white markings, wrapped in grey wool, carrying a chisel and a single stone. Quiet, serene and unhurried. Answers questions with a count.

## Pressure and secrets
The cairns have counted down since before the Cycle. It's the goliaths' oldest charge: count the Return. The count started at seven, one for each stage of the Return [COS-032], and Imra changes it whenever the Return moves. She doesn't know what happens at zero, only that her teacher's teacher was afraid of it.
