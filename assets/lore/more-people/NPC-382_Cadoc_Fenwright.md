# Cadoc Fenwright

## Public role and identity
- Former Scholar Corps expedition sergeant.
- Hires out as a guide to the Deepwood's edge, and no further.

## Goal
Never to go back into Arborhold.

## Known relationships and locations
Kaelholm [FAC-301]; the Scholar Corps

## Appearance and voice
A broad grey dwarf with a glyph-lance he won't sell, a missing ear and a flask he never puts down. Gruff, honest and drunk by noon.

## Pressure and secrets
Cadoc was on the expedition sent after the lost repair crew of 22 AB's trail, a generation ago. He came back alone. In Arborhold he heard voices reciting the crew's names, all of them, including one the rolls don't have.
