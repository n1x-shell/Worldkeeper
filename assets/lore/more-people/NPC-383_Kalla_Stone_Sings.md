# Kalla Stone-Sings

## Public role and identity
- Song-keeper of a goliath clan in the eastern mountains.
- Sings each clan's lineage at its gatherings.

## Goal
The clan's songs kept whole, and the feud ended by singing, not blood.

## Known relationships and locations
The Eastern Mountains [REG-302]

## Appearance and voice
A tall goliath with grey stone-patterned skin, a drum on her back and every finger ringed in carved bone. Patient, solemn and very funny in private.

## Pressure and secrets
The oldest goliath song has the tune of Ashvale's keeping song. The clans guarded the Cradlehorn line before the Shrouding, and their song kept the same lid. Kalla doesn't know that. She does know the song stops working near Cradlehorn now.
