# Ashe-of-No-Turning

## Public role and identity
- Stands watch at the Fractured Throne [LOC-503] for the Cycle Breakers [FAC-501].
- Still wears the armour of an army that no longer exists.

## Goal
That no one ever remakes the world again.

## Known relationships and locations
The Cycle Breakers [FAC-501]; the Fractured Throne [LOC-503]

## Appearance and voice
A tall woman in dented armour of an unknown order, with a broken sword-oath carved into her breastplate. Stern, tireless and haunted.

## Pressure and secrets
Ashe made one choice in that last battle that she would give anything to undo. The Unburied Sovereign [NPC-902] has found her in her dreams and offered her exactly that, if she'll let it through the Throne's wards. She hasn't said yes. She hasn't said no.
