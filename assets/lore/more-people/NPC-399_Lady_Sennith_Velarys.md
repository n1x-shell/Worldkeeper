# Lady Sennith Velarys

## Public role and identity
- Holds the Starlit Court in a palace from a world whose sky was all stars, on a wreck-island in the Fields of Lost Realities.
- Pays travellers well for news of Aetheris, and better for news of the gods her people left the world to find.

## Goal
To hear her gods answer again.

## Known relationships and locations
The Fields of Lost Realities [ETH-502]; the Architects of Eternity [FAC-502]

## Appearance and voice
A tall astral elf in silver court dress a thousand years out of fashion, with whole constellations turning in her eyes. Gracious, patient, and faintly sad about everything.

## Pressure and secrets
Her court hasn't heard its gods in three Aetherfalls. To keep the court in its splendour, Sennith sells the void's starlight to the Architects of Eternity, a little at a time, and her courtiers' eyes are dimming for it. None of them has noticed yet.
