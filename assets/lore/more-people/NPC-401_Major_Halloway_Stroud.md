# Major Halloway Stroud

## Public role and identity
- Commands the Twelfth Regiment of Nowhere, a giff company that hires out its guns to driftship captains.
- Means to find the giff homeworld, and asks every salvager he meets about it.

## Goal
To march the regiment home, wherever home is.

## Known relationships and locations
The Fields of Lost Realities [ETH-502]

## Appearance and voice
A vast grey giff in a red coat with brass buttons, a pistol on each hip and a moustache waxed to points. Booming, courteous, and keen on proper drill.

## Pressure and secrets
The Major found one of the giff's petrified gods drifting in the void, and he hasn't told the regiment what it was. The regiment's powder is ground from its body. It's the best powder in the realm, and the Major has begun to dream in a voice that isn't his.
