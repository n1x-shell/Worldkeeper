# Vantis Orm

## Public role and identity
- Watches the stars over the Black Horizon from the Observatory of the Last Question [LOC-508].
- Keeps the Echoborn's star-count.

## Goal
To see what lies past the Horizon, and survive it.

## Known relationships and locations
The Echoborn [FAC-505]; Elyndria the Echo [NPC-072]

## Appearance and voice
A pale, thin astral elf with star-charts tattooed on both arms and stars moving in eyes that don't blink often enough. Quiet, precise and growing afraid.

## Pressure and secrets
Stars are going out over the Black Horizon, one at a time, in a slow sweep: the Shepherd of Quiet Stars [COS-031] coming in. Vantis's patron is a voice from past the Horizon that promises them the answer to the last question. It's a Voice from Beyond [CRE-543].
