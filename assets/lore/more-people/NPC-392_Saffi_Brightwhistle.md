# Saffi Brightwhistle

## Public role and identity
- Buys salvage for the Architects of Eternity at the Verge's edge.
- Brindle Quickfoot's best customer, and rival.

## Goal
Everything worth having, at the right price.

## Known relationships and locations
The Architects of Eternity [FAC-502]; Brindle Quickfoot [NPC-373]

## Appearance and voice
A neat little gnome in a spotless coat, with a jeweller's loupe and a ledger on a chain. Cheerful, precise and impossible to haggle with.

## Pressure and secrets
Saffi works for the Architects and for the Hidden Ones at once. She told the Hidden Ones about the erased-world piece Brindle sold, and they're coming for it, and for him.
