# Hesper Ondry

## Public role and identity
- Runs the Blank Page, a tavern under the Tollmouth where nobody asks your name.
- Sings the old ballads the Copyhouses stopped copying.

## Goal
That nobody who's been burned has to be alone.

## Known relationships and locations
Kaelholm [SET-302]; the Blank Page

## Appearance and voice
A tall, red-skinned tiefling with one broken horn, a waistcoat of patched velvet and a voice like smoke. Warm, wry and hard to rattle. Remembers every face.

## Pressure and secrets
Hesper runs a quiet way out for burned names: over the hills to Ashvale, where a burned name is just a name. The Rasp suspects. Mirela Sand [NPC-320] knows, and has let it run, because one of the names was her brother's.
