# Dov Sallis

## Public role and identity
- Works the Tollmouth docks by day and fences salvage by night.
- Knows every smuggler who crosses the Black Tides.

## Goal
Enough coin to leave Kaelholm, and never quite enough.

## Known relationships and locations
Kaelholm [SET-302]; the Tollmouth

## Appearance and voice
A lean young man with a shaved head, a sailor's tattoos and very calm, very tired eyes. Charming, quick and oddly peaceful, as if he's somewhere better.

## Pressure and secrets
Dov has two Harmony Mirrors [ART-306], smuggled in from Low Fenn, and looks into one every night. He is Thinning, and happy. He sells looks to others for silver, which is why people in the harbour are coming home Thinner.
