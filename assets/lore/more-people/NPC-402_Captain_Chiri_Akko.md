# Captain Chiri Akko

## Public role and identity
- Captains the driftship *Rope-and-Wish*, crewed by her whole extended family.
- Ferries passengers and cargo from the edge of the Verge out into the Fields of Lost Realities.

## Goal
To keep the family fed, and the ship in one piece.

## Known relationships and locations
The Fields of Lost Realities [ETH-502]; the Crucible of Creation [LOC-502]; Saffi Brightwhistle [NPC-392]

## Appearance and voice
A wiry hadozee with grey-brown fur, a captain's hat that's too big for her and three knives on a bandolier. Cheerful, quick and impossible to pin down on a price.

## Pressure and secrets
Chiri smuggles people out of the Crucible of Creation, workers who want to leave the Architects, which the Architects don't allow. She hides them among Saffi Brightwhistle's cargo. Saffi doesn't know, and the Architects are beginning to count.
