# Lio Danter

## Public role and identity
- Announces the bouts at the Colosseum of Echoes, and takes the bets.
- Sells Colosseum summonses: the easiest invitation into the deep realm.

## Goal
The best show in the realm, every night.

## Known relationships and locations
The Titans of the Rift [FAC-504]; the Colosseum of Echoes [LOC-504]

## Appearance and voice
A slim man in a coat of gold thread from a dead king's court, with a voice that fills the arena without trying. Theatrical, charming and entirely mercenary.

## Pressure and secrets
Lio sells summonses to anyone, including Black Sun prophets looking for a way into the deep realm for their rites, and he knows exactly what they want them for.
