# Grau Teeth-Taker

## Public role and identity
- Leads a band of deserters and clanless goliaths in the high passes.
- Takes a tooth from everyone he robs.

## Goal
A clan of his own, and the Ascendancy paying for what it did to him.

## Known relationships and locations
The Eastern Mountains [REG-302]; the Ascendancy [FAC-305]

## Appearance and voice
A huge goliath with Ascendancy brands burned off his arms and a necklace of teeth. Loud, cruel, and fair by his own rules.

## Pressure and secrets
Grau stole the map of Cradlehorn's under-road gate from Siege-Engineer Yarrow Kask [NPC-339] when he deserted. He'll trade it, for the right price, or the right fight.
