# Old Tolliver Grey

## Public role and identity
- Keeps the lamps and the records of the Fractured Throne.
- Remembers seven worlds, and lived in all of them.

## Goal
To write it all down before he forgets any of it.

## Known relationships and locations
The Cycle Breakers [FAC-501]; Eryndor the First Shard [NPC-063]

## Appearance and voice
A tiny, ancient gnome in seven layers of robes from seven worlds, with a lamp on a pole and ink on his nose. Rambling, kind and sharp in flashes.

## Pressure and secrets
Tolliver knows Eryndor wasn't the first to break free of the Cycle. The first was the Unburied Sovereign, before there was a Cycle to break, and the Breakers' founding story is a lie Eryndor told so nobody would go looking for it.
