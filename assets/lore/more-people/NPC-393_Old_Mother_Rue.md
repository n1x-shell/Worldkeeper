# Old Mother Rue

## Public role and identity
- Tells lives in the Remembering Rooms of the Nameless Asylum [LOC-506].
- Remembers more lives than anyone, and the oldest.

## Goal
To tell her oldest life to someone who will understand it.

## Known relationships and locations
The Veilborn [FAC-503]; the Nameless Asylum [LOC-506]; Inkling [NPC-375]

## Appearance and voice
A tiny, ancient elf wrapped in a shawl of grey grass, with eyes like a deep well. Gentle, wandering and sometimes very frightening.

## Pressure and secrets
Mother Rue's oldest life was in the first civilisation, at the court of its last ruler. She half-remembers the ruler's erased name (one of the pieces needed to bind the Unburied Sovereign [NPC-902]), and she dreams of the Grey Mother (Ysh'thaleen [DEI-911]) most nights.
