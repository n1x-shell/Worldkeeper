# Tch'kala

## Public role and identity
- Hunts and guides in the Hollow Verge, and knows its paths nearly as well as Tessk.
- Never sleeps, so she keeps the Nameless Asylum's night watch with Keeper Lantern.

## Goal
She says she's looking for her desert world, deeper in.

## Known relationships and locations
The Hollow Verge [ETH-501]; the Nameless Asylum [LOC-506]; Keeper Lantern [NPC-371]; Tessk the Wayfinder [NPC-372]

## Appearance and voice
A tall thri-kreen whose shell turns grey to match the Verge's grass, with a bone spear and a necklace of desert stones. Still, watchful, and dry in a way people take a while to notice.

## Pressure and secrets
Tch'kala has already found her desert world, deep in the Fields. Every thri-kreen there was a version of her, and they all knew her name. She left before they could ask her to stay, and she won't go back. She still tells people she's looking.
