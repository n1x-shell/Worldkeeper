# Magister Ilse Varnholt

## Public role and identity
- Prosecutes before Kaelholm's tribunals.
- Is hunting a Black Sun cell she says has come over from Aetheris.

## Goal
The Black Sun burned out of Kaelholm, root and branch.

## Known relationships and locations
Kaelholm [FAC-301]; the tribunals

## Appearance and voice
A sharp-featured gnome in tribunal grey with a heavy veil of powder on her face and a voice like a closing ledger. Precise, relentless and very convincing.

## Pressure and secrets
Ilse is the Black Sun in Kaelholm. She dreamed the Door-Maker three years ago, and the powder hides the eclipse-ring around her eyes (Eclipse-Touched [CRE-540]). She prosecutes the cell's rivals and anyone getting close, and is gathering shard-fragments for an eclipse-rite at a seep of glass.
