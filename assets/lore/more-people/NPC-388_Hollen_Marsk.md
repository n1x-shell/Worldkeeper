# Hollen Marsk

## Public role and identity
- Prospects for Kaelith metal in the eastern mountains.
- Trades with both feuding goliath clans.

## Goal
To get rich, and to be well away when it all comes down.

## Known relationships and locations
The Eastern Mountains [REG-302]

## Appearance and voice
A weathered woman in a fur-lined coat, a prospector's hammer at one hip and a very good knife at the other. Easy, friendly and always listening.

## Pressure and secrets
Hollen is paid by the Ascendancy to keep the Goliath Feud burning, so the clans stay too divided to guard the way to Cradlehorn. She's started to like the goliaths, which is inconvenient.
