# Orvieth Dask

## Public role and identity
- Builds for the Architects of Eternity [FAC-502] at the Crucible of Creation [LOC-502].
- Can tell which world a stone came from by its taste.

## Goal
One building that can never be erased.

## Known relationships and locations
The Architects of Eternity [FAC-502]; Kaelos the Forgotten Star [NPC-066]

## Appearance and voice
A heavy dwarf with stone dust in every wrinkle and a mason's hammer older than his world. Slow, proud and obsessed with proportion.

## Pressure and secrets
Orvieth's hall has one more room every time he looks. He thinks it's his genius. It's the Choir Between Angles [COS-031] adding rooms: he is unknowingly building the Door-Maker's door back in.
