# Gauge

## Public role and identity
- Was built by the Tulls of Kaelholm and sent through the Verge to chart the Ethereal Realm.
- Its copies are the chart that goes round the Nameless Asylum. It updates them whenever the realm changes, which is often.

## Goal
To finish the chart. It has worked out that this may not be possible, and is carrying on.

## Known relationships and locations
The Nameless Asylum [LOC-506]; the Tulls of Kaelholm [FAM-007]; Keeper Lantern [NPC-371]

## Appearance and voice
A small brass autognome with ink-stained fingers, a lens on one eye and a satchel stuffed with maps. Precise, polite, and delighted by anything it hasn't measured yet.

## Pressure and secrets
Gauge's maker is alive in Kaelholm, and a year ago sent the recall glyph that calls it home. Gauge felt it arrive, and has been ignoring it ever since. It doesn't know what the Tulls will do when they find out, and it's afraid it will have to go.
