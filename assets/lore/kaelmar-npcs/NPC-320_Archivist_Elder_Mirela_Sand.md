# Archivist-Elder Mirela Sand

## Public role and identity
- An Archivist-Elder of Kaelholm, who sits on the Sitting.
- Keeper of the Rasp, where burned names are scraped from the rolls.

## Goal
Every name in its proper drawer, and none forgotten by accident.

## Known relationships and locations
Kaelholm [FAC-301]; Kaelholm [SET-302]

## Appearance and voice
A stout dwarf in tribunal grey with a white braid wound into a crown, and an iron stylus she uses for nothing but scraping. Slow, formal and absolutely certain. Reads every charge aloud twice.
