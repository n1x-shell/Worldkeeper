# Ives Varrow

## Public role and identity
- A glyph-wright of Lampblack, Kaelholm's quarter of scribes.
- Descended from the glyph-carved, as many Kaelmar tieflings are.

## Goal
To read the First Grammar fluently, before anyone forbids it.

## Known relationships and locations
Kaelholm [FAC-301]; Kaelholm [SET-302]

## Appearance and voice
A tiefling with faint glyph-marks grown along their horns, ink-black fingers and a magnifying lens on a chain. Precise, shy and passionate about lettering.
