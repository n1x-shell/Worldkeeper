# Keeper Jory Hesk

## Public role and identity
- A keeper of the Boundary Keepers of Ashvale.
- Grandson of Ritekeeper Orla Hesk.
- Teaches the keeping song to the children.

## Goal
That every child in Ashvale knows the song by heart.

## Known relationships and locations
Boundary Keepers [FAC-306]; Ashvale [SET-301]

## Appearance and voice
A gangly half-elf of twenty with a fiddle on his back and his grandmother's grey eyes. Cheerful, earnest and very bad at keeping secrets.
