# Graftwright Desh

## Public role and identity
- Graft-surgeon of The Convergence Collective.
- Cuts glyphs and fits grafts for those who choose them.

## Goal
That every graft is chosen, and healed well.

## Known relationships and locations
The Convergence Collective [FAC-304]; The Ash Barrens [REG-306]

## Appearance and voice
A huge bugbear with delicate surgical tools in a roll at his belt, and a neat iron graft running down his own spine. Soft-spoken, careful and very kind.
