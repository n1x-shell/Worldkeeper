# Captain Tor Varga

## Public role and identity
- Captain of the Warband of The Convergence Collective.
- Rides with half-living constructs and the beast-folk of the barrens.

## Goal
To keep the Collective safe from the Ascendancy, whatever it takes.

## Known relationships and locations
The Convergence Collective [FAC-304]; The Ash Barrens [REG-306]

## Appearance and voice
A grey-coated centaur in grafted barding, with a lance of hatch-metal and an iron graft along one foreleg. Blunt, loyal and funny in a dry way.
