# Elder Mott Greyfold

## Public role and identity
- Elder and shrine-keeper of Greyfold.
- Keeps a record of every birth and death in the village.

## Goal
Every name written down, and every soul seen off properly.

## Known relationships and locations
Greyfold; Greyfold

## Appearance and voice
A white-bearded dwarf in a faded sun-yellow stole, with spectacles and a ledger of births and deaths. Kind, fussy and precise about dates.
