# Hollin

## Public role and identity
- A Bloomborn druid of The Spore Circle.
- Runs messages between the Tendrils, through the roots.

## Goal
To keep the Tendrils talking to each other.

## Known relationships and locations
The Spore Circle [FAC-302]; The Eastern Deepwood [REG-301]

## Appearance and voice
A slender figure of moss and pale bark, with a crown of tiny white mushrooms and amber eyes. Gentle, curious and fond of terrible puns.
