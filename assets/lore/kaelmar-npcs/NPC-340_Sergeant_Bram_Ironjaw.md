# Sergeant Bram Ironjaw

## Public role and identity
- A sergeant of the drilled columns of The Ascendancy.
- One of the remade: soldiers rebuilt with iron and graft.

## Goal
To rise, and to make his family proud.

## Known relationships and locations
The Ascendancy [FAC-305]; The Eastern Mountains [REG-302]

## Appearance and voice
A broad young man with an iron jaw, cords of grafted metal through both forearms and a farmer's sunburn. Proud, drilled and secretly homesick.
