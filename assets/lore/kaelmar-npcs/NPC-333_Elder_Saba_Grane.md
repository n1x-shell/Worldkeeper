# Elder Saba Grane

## Public role and identity
- An elder of the cycles of The Convergence Collective.
- Sister to Maro Grane.

## Goal
That nobody is ever made to change, and nobody is stopped from choosing it.

## Known relationships and locations
The Convergence Collective [FAC-304]; The Ash Barrens [REG-306]

## Appearance and voice
A broad grey-green orc with glyph-cuts healed silver on both arms, and a Root Text bound in hide at her hip. Steady, warm and immovable. The calm to her brother's fire.
