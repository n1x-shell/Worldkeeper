# Healer Ama Doss

## Public role and identity
- A healer of The Spore Circle in the southern fens.
- Heals with membranes that remember the body's intended form.

## Goal
To teach Bloom healing to every village before winter.

## Known relationships and locations
The Spore Circle [FAC-302]; The Southern Fens, Lakes and River Country [REG-305]

## Appearance and voice
A tawny tabaxi with copper binding-lines along her forearms and a satchel of living membranes in damp moss. Warm, practical and impatient with fools.
