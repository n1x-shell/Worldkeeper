# Mags Kettle

## Public role and identity
- A travelling tinker of Ashvale and the nine villages.

## Goal
To see the stones, and anyone who goes near them.

## Known relationships and locations
The Continuity [FAC-303]; Ashvale [SET-301]

## Appearance and voice
A small green goblin with a cart of pots, wires and spare hinges, and a laugh like a kettle whistle. Chatty, generous and everywhere at once.

## Secrets (GM only)
- A Continuity observer, watching Ashvale's stones and the children who dream of them.
- She reports to Sentinel Hold every new moon.
