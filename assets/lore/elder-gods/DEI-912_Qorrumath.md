# Q'orrumath, the Keeper of Endings

## What he is [SECRET]
He sleeps under the Fields of Lost Realities [ETH-502], and the Fields are his hoard. Every world the Cycle discarded drifts there because he can't let go of anything that ended. A golden cathedral lying sideways in a Legion fortress is one of his keepsakes.

## Sign
A broken ring, with both halves kept.

## In the schism
He wanted nothing to change, ever, after the first civilisation ended. He voted to drive the teachers out, and has regretted keeping their things ever since.

## What he wants
To keep. Every ending, every piece. Salvagers who take from the Fields are, in his dreams, thieves.

## If he wakes
The wreck-islands draw together into one mass. The driftwhales go still. Anything ever taken from the Fields begins to pull back toward them, wherever it is, including salvage that has been anchored in the world.

## His name
Spoken in the Fields, his name makes the nearest wreck-island turn, slowly, to face the speaker, and everything loose on it rolls toward them.
