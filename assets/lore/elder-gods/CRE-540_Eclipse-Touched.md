# Eclipse-Touched

## Overview
A Black Sun cultist who has dreamed a teacher too many times, and come back changed. Their shadow falls toward the Black Horizon, wherever they stand.

## Appearance
A robed figure with a ring of black skin around each eye, like an eclipse. When they speak a true name, their mouth goes dark inside.

## Where
Black Sun cells on both continents, and wherever an eclipse-rite is under way.

## Game statistics (GM)
Start from: Cultist Hierophant (2024 Monster Manual), or Priest with darkness instead of radiance. About CR 3 to 5. Once per day, they can speak a teacher's name: every creature within 30 feet makes a DC 13 Wisdom save or is Frightened for 1 minute.

## Secret (GM only)
Killing one at an eclipse-rite ends the rite. Letting one finish a name moves the Return one stage [COS-032].
