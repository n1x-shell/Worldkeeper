# Shaal-Uthrex, the Unknotter

## What they are [SECRET]
The maker of the Loom of Eternity [ETH-505]. They knew the knots of fate better than anything, and they taught the first civilisation how to undo the knot of death. The people stopped dying. That was the first lesson, and the first thing the Watchers noticed.

## Sign
A cord with a knot coming undone.

## In the schism
The first of the three teachers, and the first to leave. Since they went, the Loom has had no maker to tend it, only Vyrithar [NPC-002], its steward.

## What they want
To finish what they began: the seed grown up, deathless, and safe. They believe their Guest can make it safe.

## Their Guest
The Hunger in the Weave. See The Guests [COS-031].

## Their return
Thread-grues gather where they're coming in. Fate-seers stop seeing anything past a certain day. In the world, people who should have died don't, and are not glad.

## Their name
Spoken anywhere near the Loom, their name loosens every knot within reach: rope, lace, and the knots of fate that hold a creature's death in place.

## Laws of Existence
In addition to Immutable Existence and the Unseen Gaze (Elder Gods at the Table [RUL-621]). Save DCs follow its reach.

| Law | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Unknotted Death** | Creatures in their reach don't die at 0 hit points. They keep making Death Saving Throws; after three failures they stand up deathless, neither dead nor alive, until their knot is retied. Healing restores only half. |
| **Fraying** | Fate stops working: Heroic Inspiration, Bardic Inspiration and the Lucky feat do nothing, and divination tells nothing about anything more than a day away. |
| **Loose Threads** | The first time each turn a creature rolls a 1 on a d20, the effect lands on a different thread: a random creature within 30 feet suffers it instead. |
| **Reality Erosion** | The Loom slackens around them. Every creature in their reach has Disadvantage on Death Saving Throws and on saves against Thinning. |

## Manifestation
**The Slack Thread.** A tall figure made of loosened cord, every strand of it a life coming undone, holding a knife that cuts fate. Start from the Solar (Monster Manual), with radiant damage replaced by necrotic, and its Flying Sword a blade that cuts a creature's thread (Thinning save on a hit). Defeating it doesn't kill the god; it pushes the god's reach down a step for a year and a day.

## How it can be stopped
- **Sever the way.** Sew shut the slack place in the Loom where they and the Hunger come in, with the Loom Needle [ITM-022]. It takes the Needle's only use.
- **Turn them back.** Show them what deathlessness has done: bring them someone kept alive by the Hunger, who wants to die, and let them hear it. They are a teacher; they can still learn. They go back past the Horizon, and the Hunger with them.
- **Speak the true name.** Spoken while holding a knotted cord, it ties them in their own knot.
