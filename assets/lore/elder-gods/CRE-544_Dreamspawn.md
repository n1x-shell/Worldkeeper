# Dreamspawn

## Overview
Something a sleeping elder god dreamed, that walked out of the dream. Each of the four sleepers' depths has its own.

## Appearance
It depends on the dreamer: a child made of grey grass in the Verge, a thing of stitched-together salvage in the Fields, a faceless page-boy in the Ossuary, a figure of loose pages in the Archive. All of them move like sleepwalkers.

## Where
The four shallow depths, more often as the sleepers stir (The Return, stage 2).

## Game statistics (GM)
Start from: Gibbering Mouther for a small one, Nothic for a larger one (Monster Manual). About CR 2 to 5. A creature that kills one has strange dreams that night: the GM shows them one true glimpse of the elder gods.

## Secret (GM only)
Waking a Dreamspawn (it can be done, with a DC 15 Charisma (Performance) check and the sleeper's true name) wakes its dreamer a little too.
