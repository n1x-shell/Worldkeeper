# What the Black Sun Knows

## The creed, and what it's about [SECRET]
The Black Sun [CLT-002] says an eternal eclipse is coming, and something older than the gods will emerge from it. They're right. The eclipse is the Return [COS-032]: the Black Horizon's stars going out as the One Who Called Out comes home, and her Guest with her. "Something older than the gods" is the three teachers.

## Who knows what
- **The faithful** know the creed, and the eclipse-rites. They believe the Eclipse is a cleansing, and that the old ones will reward those who opened the way. They don't know about the Guests.
- **The prophets** (Seraine, Vhalek, Cassiel, Isolde the Last Ember) have each dreamed one of the teachers. They argue about which is the true one. Each has a different rite, and each rite moves the clock.
- **High Prophet Azhir,** the Blind Seer [NPC-001], knows the most. He has survived more than one Aetherfall, and in the gap between two of them he heard the Shepherd of Quiet Stars answer. He put out his own eyes so as not to see it again. He knows what the Guests are, and that the Return can't be stopped, only slowed. He leads the cult so that it moves slowly. "The truth discovered slowly enough to survive" is his whole policy.

## The eclipse-rites
A rite needs a shard or a fragment of one, a place where the Ethereal Realm is thin (the Rift, a Hollow Verge entrance, a seep of glass), and a teacher's true name spoken under an open sky. A successful rite moves the Return one stage. Azhir quietly sabotages the ones he can.

## Using it
- The cult is a source of heralds, and of knowledge nobody else has.
- Azhir is a possible ally, and a possible enemy: he wants the party to slow the Return, not stop it, because he doesn't believe it can be stopped, and he's afraid of what happens to anyone who tries.
- Whether Azhir is right is up to the table.
