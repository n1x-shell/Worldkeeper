# Ixh-Nomereth, the Reader Below

## What it is [SECRET]
It sleeps under the Silent Archive [ETH-504], and the Archive is its mind. It has a book for every erased soul because it reads everything, and remembers what it reads. The books read their visitors because, even asleep, it is reading. The untitled book with the shard-shaped hollow is the one it hasn't finished.

## Sign
An open book, the pages blank, with an eye drawn on the spine.

## In the schism
It read the teachers' lessons after the first civilisation fell, all of them, and has been trying ever since to understand why they did it.

## What it wants
To finish reading. It wants to know how the story ends, and it won't help anyone end it early.

## If it wakes
Every book in the Archive opens at once. Anyone carrying writing, anywhere in the realm, hears it read aloud. It will answer any question asked of it truthfully, and in exchange it reads the asker, all the way through.

## Its name
Spoken in the Archive, its name makes the nearest blank book fill with the speaker's life, from the beginning.

## Laws of Existence
In addition to Immutable Existence and the Unseen Gaze (Elder Gods at the Table [RUL-621]). Save DCs follow its reach.

| Law | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Being Read** | Each hour in its reach, a creature makes an Intelligence save. On a failure, one of its secrets is written in a book in the Archive, where any reader can find it. |
| **Only the True Word** | Anything written in its reach rewrites itself overnight to what's true. Lies on paper vanish; maps redraw; forged papers name their forger. |
| **Reality Erosion** | Magic is unreliable here. Whenever a creature casts a spell, roll a d20: on a 1 to 3, it becomes a different spell of the same level, which the GM chooses from a book in the Archive. |
| **Dream Contagion** | A creature that finishes a long rest in its reach makes an Intelligence save. On a failure it wakes having read a book it never saw: proficiency in one skill until its next long rest, and one language forgotten for as long. |

## Manifestation
**The Reading.** A figure of loose pages as tall as the stacks, its face an open book, its voice everyone you've ever read. Start from the Sphinx of Lore (Monster Manual), raised to about CR 20, with riddles replaced by questions about your own life. Defeating it doesn't kill the god; it pushes the god's reach down a step for a year and a day.

## How it can be stopped
- **Return it to dormancy.** Give it an ending to read. The untitled book with the shard-shaped hollow is the story it can't finish. Bring the true account of how the Cycle ended (the Forgotten One's, in their own words) and it reads, and rests.
- **Break the vessel.** Burn the untitled book. It is the only book in the Archive that will burn. It costs the realm its record of every erased soul.
- **Speak the true name.** It is written on its own spine, in a script that reads you while you read it.
