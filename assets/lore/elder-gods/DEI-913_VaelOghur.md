# Vael-Oghur, the Crowned Silence

## What she is [SECRET]
She sleeps under the Ossuary of Kings [ETH-503], and the Ossuary is her court. Rulers who learned the truth of the Cycle came here because, in her dream, a court is where things are decided. She has never decided anything. That is her nature: the pause before a verdict, held forever.

## Sign
An empty throne, facing away.

## In the schism
She judged the teachers, and gave no verdict. The other three took her silence as a sentence, and the teachers left. She has dreamed of that silence ever since.

## What she wants
A verdict she can live with, on the teachers, on the first civilisation and on the people.

## If she wakes
The dead kings stand. The Colosseum of Echoes falls silent. She will hear one case, and the people's champion had better be ready, because her verdict holds.

## Her name
Spoken in the Ossuary, her name makes every crowned dead within earshot kneel, and wait.
