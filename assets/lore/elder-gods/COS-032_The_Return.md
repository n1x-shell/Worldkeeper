# The Return

## A clock, not a date
The three teachers and their Guests are coming back, but not on any schedule. Track the Return as a clock of seven stages, in the campaign's Faction Agendas (as a plan for the Black Sun [CLT-002], seven segments). It moves only when you move it, at the moments below.

## What moves it
- The party, or anyone, travels into the deep realm (depth 2 or deeper) for the first time: one stage.
- A Black Sun eclipse-rite succeeds: one stage.
- A seal of the Nameless Sepulcher [LOC-509] breaks (there are three): one stage each.
- A sleeper wakes: one stage.
- Someone speaks a teacher's true name under an open sky: one stage.

## What slows it
Nothing reverses it, but it can be held: a failed eclipse-rite, a sleeper kept asleep, a seal mended (only the Hidden Ones know how). Each holds the clock for a season.

## The stages
1. **Signs.** Stars flicker at the Black Horizon. Bells in the world ring a half-beat late. Black Sun prophets dream of a black star.
2. **The sleepers stir.** The shallow depths change (see each sleeper's "If they wake"), lightly at first. Greylings fall silent, wreck-islands turn, crowned dead kneel at nothing.
3. **Heralds.** The Guests' heralds appear in the deep realm, then in the Hollow Verge. The Black Sun grows bolder.
4. **The first Guest at the Horizon.** One of the Guests becomes visible from the Black Horizon. Its signs (see The Guests [COS-031]) start reaching the world, faintly.
5. **The Sepulcher opens.** The Unburied Sovereign [NPC-902] walks. It goes looking for the shards.
6. **The door.** The Door-Maker's door stands open at the Black Horizon. The teachers can be met, argued with, bargained with, and stopped.
7. **The Return.** The teachers come through, and the Guests with them.

## If anyone forms the Orb
Forming the Ethereal Orb, the Greater Orb or the Lesser Orb (see The Ethereal Orb [COS-022]) is the loudest thing that can happen in the Ethereal Realm. The clock jumps to stage 6 at once, wherever it was, and the door opens at the forge. Whoever forms the Orb meets the returners there, newly a god or not, and the Guests take an interest.

- A new god (twelve shards) is a rival the teachers didn't expect, and one the Guests would like to bargain with.
- The Greater Orb (thirteen) closes the realm. Whether that shuts the returners out with everyone else, or in with the party, is the last question of the campaign.
- The Lesser Orb's catastrophe is exactly the kind of hole a Guest can come through.
