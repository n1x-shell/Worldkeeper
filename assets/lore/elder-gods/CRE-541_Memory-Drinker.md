# Memory-Drinker

## Overview
Herald of the Hunger in the Weave. It drinks the knot of death out of a creature, and the memory of dying with it.

## Appearance
Something like an eel the size of a barge, hanging in the black sea of the Loom, skin grey and shining like wet thread. It has no eyes. It has a great many mouths, and it remembers through all of them.

## Where
The Loom of Eternity [ETH-505], then the Fields, then wherever the Hunger is coming in.

## Game statistics (GM)
Start from: Aboleth (2024 Monster Manual). About CR 10. Replace its enslaving with this: a creature it hits makes a DC 16 Wisdom save or can't die until the Memory-Drinker is destroyed; it drops to 0 hit points and stays conscious, Incapacitated, in pain.

## Secret (GM only)
It is deathless, as its Guest wants everything to be. It can only be killed in a place where something has just died.
