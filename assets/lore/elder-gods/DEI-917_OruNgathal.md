# Oru-Ngathal, the One Who Called Out

## What she is [SECRET]
The deepest and the farthest. She once sang the Black Horizon [ETH-507] into place as the realm's edge. She taught the first civilisation to call beyond the edge, to see what else was out there. Something answered. That was the Watchers.

## Sign
A mouth, open, with a star in it.

## In the schism
The third teacher, and the most to blame. She went past the Black Horizon herself, to answer for it, and has been out there longest.

## What she wants
To call again, and this time be answered by something that can stand against the Watchers.

## Her Guest
The Shepherd of Quiet Stars. See The Guests [COS-031].

## Her pupil
The Unburied Sovereign [NPC-902] was hers. It lies in the Nameless Sepulcher, at the edge she sang, listening for her voice.

## Her return
Starlight Madness spreads past the Black Horizon. People in the world hear singing in the silence after a bell. The Black Sun's prophets dream of a black star rising, and they're right.

## Her name
Spoken under an open sky, her name carries farther than any sound should. Something hears it. Not always her.

## Laws of Existence
In addition to Immutable Existence and the Unseen Gaze (Elder Gods at the Table [RUL-621]). Save DCs follow its reach.

| Law | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **The Call** | Each night in her reach, a creature makes a Wisdom save. On a failure, it walks in its sleep for a mile toward the Black Horizon. |
| **Under Wrong Stars** | Starlight Madness [CRE-531] works everywhere under her sky, at her DC. |
| **Answer** | Any spell that sends words (Message, Sending, Telepathy and the like) is heard by something else too. Roll a d6: on a 1, something from beyond answers instead. |
| **The Silence After the Bell** | Sound goes wrong. Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing have Disadvantage, and Dexterity (Stealth) checks have Advantage, for everyone. |

## Manifestation
**The Singer.** A mouth among the stars, open, with a star in it, and below it a body of night as tall as a tower. Start from the Empyrean (Monster Manual), with its voice as its weapon: every attack is a note that can be heard a mile away. Defeating it doesn't kill the god; it pushes the god's reach down a step for a year and a day.

## How it can be stopped
- **Break the vessel.** Her pupil anchors her return. The Unburied Sovereign [NPC-902] is listening for her voice; destroy it, or turn it, and she has nobody in the world to sing to.
- **Turn her back.** Answer her call with silence: every bell in a city, or a whole people at a vigil, silent for one night while she sings. It tells her the seed doesn't want what she's bringing. She goes.
- **Speak the true name.** Spoken under an open sky, her name carries. Spoken in a sealed room with no window, it binds her.
