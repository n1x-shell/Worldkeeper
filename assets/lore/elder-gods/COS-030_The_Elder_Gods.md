# The Elder Gods

## What they are [SECRET]
The elder gods made the world. Seven of them, older than the Hidden Ones, older than the gods people pray to, older than the Ethereal Realm, which is the shape of their dreaming. They made Aetheris and Kaelmar, and they made the Loom of Eternity [ETH-505] to keep it.

They made it as a **hiding place**. Out past the Black Horizon, the Watchers [COS-904] had ended a kind of life wherever they found it: life that grows, remembers and changes. The elder gods saved the last seed of it and hid it in a world of their own making. That seed is the people of Aetheris and Kaelmar. The world is a nursery, the Loom is its lock, and the Ethereal Realm is the wall around it.

## The stewards and the children
- **The Hidden Ones** [FAC-007], first called the Elders of the Ethereal Throne, are the elder gods' stewards. They were left in charge when the makers withdrew. The Cycle was the stewards' answer, never the makers': keep the people from ever growing stable enough to be noticed. They meant it kindly. It became a cage.
- **Today's gods** (Solenne, Karrath, Ithiel, Ellun, Orrowen, Sephane) are the elder gods' children, made to tend the people. Every Aetherfall rewrote them along with everything else, and none remembers its makers. The three dead gods (Maruun, Aurex and the Nameless Smith) died trying to find out who made them. See The Gods [COS-909].

## The schism
The seven fell out over the **first civilisation** [COS-901]. Three of them loved the seed too much to leave it small. They taught the first civilisation in secret: how to unknot death, how to build doors out of the world, how to call beyond it. The first civilisation conquered death, found the Ethereal Realm and called beyond it, and the Watchers heard. Almost nothing survived.

The other four never forgave them. The three teachers were driven out, or left in shame, past the Black Horizon. The four stayed, and sleep.

## The return
The three teachers are coming back. Out past the Black Horizon, with nothing to stand on, they made bargains with things older and stranger than themselves, and the price of each bargain, or its ally, is coming with them. These are **the Guests** [COS-031].

The teachers think they are bringing the seed a way to grow up at last, out from under the Watchers, the stewards and the Cycle. They don't understand what they've promised in return.

- **Their pupil** waits for them. The last ruler of the first civilisation, the Unburied Sovereign [NPC-902], lies in the Nameless Sepulcher [LOC-509] at the Black Horizon, and its seals are breaking. It wants to finish the reach that drew the Watchers: wake the elder gods, and take their place.
- **The Black Sun** [CLT-002] has heard the returners coming, and calls it the Eternal Eclipse. See What the Black Sun Knows [COS-033].
- **The sleepers are stirring.** The broken Cycle and the waking Sepulcher trouble their dreams. As each wakes, its depth changes.

The return is a clock, not a date. See The Return [COS-032].

## Their names [SECRET]
The elder gods' names were never meant for human mouths. Written down, they look wrong; spoken aloud in the Ethereal Realm, they are heard. Speaking a sleeper's true name in its own depth makes it stir (see each record). The titles are what the Hidden Ones call them.

## At the table
The elder gods and the Guests have Laws of Existence instead of statistics, manifestations that can be beaten, and ways to be stopped that don't involve killing them. See Elder Gods at the Table [RUL-621].

## What mortals know
Fragments: old myths that disagree, children's rhymes on both continents, and the Black Sun's creed. See the legend, The Ones Before [COS-034].
