# Kth-Varaneh, the Door-Maker

## What he is [SECRET]
He built the Ethereal Realm's walls and corridors, and the Citadel of the Hidden [ETH-506] is the workshop he left behind, which the stewards moved into. He taught the first civilisation how to build doors out of the world. They built them, walked through, and found the Ethereal Realm.

## Sign
A door frame with no door and no wall.

## In the schism
The second teacher. He left with the first and has spent the time since building a door back in, from the outside.

## What he wants
A door the seed can walk out of, into everything beyond, with nothing hunting it.

## His Guest
The Choir Between Angles. See The Guests [COS-031].

## His return
Corridors in the Citadel lead to places that weren't there. Doors appear in the world, standing alone in fields, and they're locked from the other side. Hidden Wardens go missing.

## His name
Spoken at any door, his name opens it, and also opens it onto somewhere else.
