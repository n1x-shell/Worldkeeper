# Elder Gods at the Table

## Invincible doesn't mean unstoppable
An elder god or a Guest has no Strength, no Armor Class and no hit points. It has **Laws of Existence**: rules that reality obeys near it. The party can't kill one. They can stop one: break its hold on the world, bind it, or send it back to sleep, while reality comes apart around them. The climax isn't reducing something to 0 hit points. It's doing something impossible in time.

## Three layers
1. **Influence.** The god's Laws, working on everything in its reach. This is how the party first meets one: as a place where the rules have changed.
2. **Manifestation.** A fraction of the god given a body: an avatar with a stat block, built from the baseline in its record. It can be defeated, banished or destroyed. That doesn't kill the god, but it knocks the god's reach back one step and buys a year and a day.
3. **Heralds, vessels and servants.** Prophets, champions, possessed bodies and creatures changed by nearness: Eclipse-Touched, Memory-Drinkers, Angle-Walkers, Voices from Beyond and Dreamspawn [CRE-540 to CRE-544], and anything the GM builds. They are the escalating threats a party can fight all campaign.

## Every elder god and Guest has these two Laws
- **Immutable Existence.** No damage, spell, effect or wish can harm, kill, banish or alter the true entity. Only the ways under "How it can be stopped" work.
- **The Unseen Gaze.** A creature that looks at the true entity (not its manifestation) makes a Wisdom save against the god's DC. On a failure, it gains a long-term madness (Dungeon Master's Guide) and is Stunned until the end of its next turn. On a success, it can't make itself look again for 24 hours.

## How far the Laws reach
Reach depends on how awake the god is, which follows the Return [COS-032]:

| Reach | When | Where its Laws work | Save DC |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Dreaming** | Asleep, or far away (stages 1 to 2) | Its own depth only, faintly | 15 |
| **Stirring** | Waking, or at the Black Horizon (stages 3 to 5) | Its depth fully, and the Hollow Verge, the Rift and seeps of glass | 18 |
| **Present** | Awake, or through the door (stages 6 to 7) | Its depth, and every place in the world within a day's travel of a thin place | 21 |

Defeating a manifestation moves the god down one step for a year and a day.

## How a god can be stopped
Each god's record names the ways that work on it. They are drawn from these:
- **Break the vessel.** Destroy the thing that anchors its mind in reality: a relic, a place, a herald, a person.
- **Sever the way.** Close the connection between its depth and the world: a door, a seal, a thread.
- **Speak the true name.** A god's true name is never written. Recover it in pieces (from Dreamspawn, the Silent Archive, the Black Sun's prophets, the Unburied Sovereign) and speak it whole, in its depth, to bind it: it can't act for as long as the speaker keeps holding the name (Concentration, as on a spell), or forever, if the speaker gives up their own name to hold it (Thinning to 6).
- **Rewrite a Law.** Sacrifice something irreplaceable (a shard, a true memory, a life freely given, a god's favour) to strike one of its Laws out of existence for good.
- **Return it to dormancy.** Put a sleeper back to sleep, or turn a returner back past the Horizon, for a thousand years. Each record says what that takes.

## Running the climax
Give the party a clear impossible task (bind it, break the vessel, close the door) and a clock of rounds while its Laws work on them, its manifestation fights them and its heralds come. Success should cost something real.
