# The Unburied Sovereign

## Who it was [SECRET]
The last ruler of the first civilisation [COS-901]: the one who conquered death for its people, built doors out of the world and called beyond it, all on the teachers' lessons. It was the One Who Called Out's [DEI-917] pupil. When the Watchers came, it was the only one who couldn't die, and so the stewards sealed it in the Nameless Sepulcher [LOC-509], at the edge of the Black Horizon, where nothing could find it. Its name was erased with its civilisation.

## What it is now
Something that has been awake in the dark for longer than the Cycle has run. It doesn't remember its face. It remembers every lesson.

## What it wants
To finish the reach that drew the Watchers: wake the elder gods, all seven, and take their place among them. The shards are its way in. An Orb formed in the realm is a door, and it means to be standing in it.

## Its seals
Three seals hold the Sepulcher. They are breaking, one for each of the Return's quickening stages. When the last breaks, it walks (The Return [COS-032], stage 5).

## Running it
- It has no stat block. If it must be fought, it is a campaign's last fight, and its statistics are the GM's to build (start from a lich of the highest tier, or the Larva Prophet [CRE-526], and go up).
- It is reasonable, patient and persuasive. It will offer the party exactly what they want, and it will mean it, for as long as they are useful.
- It knows the teachers are coming back. It doesn't know about the Guests. When it learns, it will be afraid for the first time in an age, and may, briefly, be an ally.
