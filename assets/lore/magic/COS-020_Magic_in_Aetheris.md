# Magic in Aetheris

## Established identity
Magic in Aetheris works as it does anywhere: the gods grant spells, mages study them, and the wild has its own. What marks Aetheris is the price. Power here takes something back, in memory, name, blood or sanity, and the greater the power, the more it takes.

## How it feels in each dominion
Each dominion still carries the rule of the world its faction made, and magic bends to it:
- The Hollow Marches: ordinary gear is unreliable.
- The Radiant Ascendancy: healing comes easily; necromancy and dark magic struggle, and are illegal.
- The Tyrant's Dominion: blades bite deeper.
- The Fractured Nexus: spells hit harder, and sometimes tear the world.
- The Eternal Veil: shadows hide you, and illusions are hard to see through.
- The Verdant Rebirth: the wild is easy; machines and arcane spells struggle.

See Dominion Regional Effects [RUL-003] for the rules.

## The shards and the scarred
The shards [COS-906] are the greatest magic in the world, and the costliest. Anyone who touches one is Shardscarred [COS-908].

## Aether Scars
Ruins and records full of leftover contradictions: a wall that remembers a battle that hasn't happened, a map with two rivers where there is one. Scholars collect them. Sensible people walk round them.

## GM notes
The price is the Cycle's echo: the Hidden Ones' weapon was forgetting, and the world still charges in memory. Nothing here is a new rule beyond the existing regional effects and shard costs.
