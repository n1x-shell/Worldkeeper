# Owner Decisions (Worldkeeper)

## How to read this
Decisions the owner has made since the archive was reconciled. Where one changes something the archive says, the archive's text is left as it was and this register says which reading is current. See also Resolved by Owner [DEC-902] and the Canon Conflict Register [DEC-903].

## The Loom of Eternity shapes both continents (November 2026)
**Current reading.** The Loom of Eternity [ETH-505] is what governs the fates of the people of Aetheris and Kaelmar alike. It shapes the peoples and the world around them. It can be tweaked, though by whom and how is a mystery. It is protected and guarded.

**What it changes.** The Loom as a force [COS-905] and the Loom [ETH-505] say Kaelmar was never covered by the Loom. That line is superseded: Kaelmar is on the Loom too.

**What stays.** The Forgotten One [NPC-901] cut the shards from the Loom about 270 AB, and that broke the Cycle: the shards can no longer tell the Loom to remake the world. The Loom itself still weaves.

**What nobody knows.** Why the Hidden Ones [FAC-007] and the elder gods made the world of Aetheris and Kaelmar, and what the Loom is for. Breaking the Cycle didn't answer either question. The people and the world are still shaped, or at least influenced, by unseen forces.

## The Ethereal Realm lies outside the world (November 2026)
The Ethereal Realm [CON-501] lies outside the world. Whoever holds power there could restart the Cycle, create new worlds, or destroy reality itself. It holds cosmic powers: the Watchers [COS-904], the Hidden Ones, the elder gods and other entities, each with its own conflicts and goals. They mostly overlook people coming and going, unless tested. Now that the Cycle is broken, reaching the realm is possible. See The Depths of the Ethereal Realm [COS-021].

## The Ethereal Orb and the endings (November 2026)
The Orb is back. The World Canon Rules [WLD-902] removed the books' Orb; that line is superseded. The Ethereal Orb is the Ethereal Crystal made whole. Since the Cycle broke, it can only be formed in the Ethereal Realm. Twelve shards form the Ethereal Orb: its bearer ascends and remakes the world until they fall, and then the Cycle restarts. Thirteen form the Greater Ethereal Orb: it heals both continents, closes the Rift and the realm, and restores every name and memory, and everyone who came is shut in the realm for life. Full details in The Ethereal Orb [COS-022].
