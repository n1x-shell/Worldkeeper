# Owner Decisions (Worldkeeper)

## How to read this
Decisions the owner has made since the archive was reconciled. Where one changes something the archive says, the archive's text is left as it was and this register says which reading is current. See also Resolved by Owner [DEC-902] and the Canon Conflict Register [DEC-903].

## The Loom of Eternity shapes both continents (November 2026)
**Current reading.** The Loom of Eternity [ETH-505] is what governs the fates of the people of Aetheris and Kaelmar alike. It shapes the peoples and the world around them. It can be tweaked, though by whom and how is a mystery. It is protected and guarded.

**What it changes.** The Loom as a force [COS-905] and the Loom [ETH-505] say Kaelmar was never covered by the Loom. That line is superseded: Kaelmar is on the Loom too.

**What stays.** The Forgotten One [NPC-901] cut the shards from the Loom about 270 AB, and that broke the Cycle: the shards can no longer tell the Loom to remake the world. The Loom itself still weaves.

**What nobody knows.** Why the Hidden Ones [FAC-007] and the elder gods made the world of Aetheris and Kaelmar, and what the Loom is for. Breaking the Cycle didn't answer either question. The people and the world are still shaped, or at least influenced, by unseen forces.

## The Ethereal Realm lies outside the world (November 2026)
The Ethereal Realm [CON-501] lies outside the world. Whoever holds power there could restart the Cycle, create new worlds, or destroy reality itself. It holds cosmic powers: the Watchers [COS-904], the Hidden Ones, the elder gods and other entities, each with its own conflicts and goals. They mostly overlook people coming and going, unless tested. Now that the Cycle is broken, reaching the realm is possible. See The Depths of the Ethereal Realm [COS-021].

## The Ethereal Orb and the endings (November 2026)
The Orb is back. The World Canon Rules [WLD-902] removed the books' Orb; that line is superseded. The Ethereal Orb is the Ethereal Crystal made whole. Since the Cycle broke, it can only be formed in the Ethereal Realm. Twelve shards form the Ethereal Orb: its bearer ascends and remakes the world until they fall, and then the Cycle restarts. Thirteen form the Greater Ethereal Orb: it heals both continents, closes the Rift and the realm, and restores every name and memory, and everyone who came is shut in the realm for life. Full details in The Ethereal Orb [COS-022]. Eleven of the twelve with the thirteenth form the Lesser Ethereal Orb: a mad god, an unfinished Aetherfall, the realm spilling into the world and mass forgetting, flavoured by whichever shard is missing, until the god falls. In the world, the legends about the thirteenth disagree on purpose.

## The elder gods (November 2026)
Seven elder gods made the world, older than the Hidden Ones and the gods. They made it as a hiding place from the Watchers [COS-904], and what they hid is the people: the last seed of a kind of life the Watchers ended everywhere else. The Loom is its lock. The Hidden Ones are their stewards, and the Cycle was the stewards' idea. Today's gods are their children and don't know it.

The seven split over the first civilisation [COS-901]. Three taught it to cheat death, build doors out of the world and call beyond it, which drew the Watchers. Those three left past the Black Horizon. The other four sleep, one under each of the four shallow depths of the Ethereal Realm. The three are coming back, bringing Guests they bargained with out there: vast, incomprehensible things that think the world has been promised to them. Their return is a clock that advances in play, and it jumps to the end if anyone forms an Orb. The Nameless Sepulcher holds the first civilisation's last ruler, the teachers' pupil, who wants to take the elder gods' place. The Black Sun's eclipse is the Return; High Prophet Azhir knows, and is slowing his own cult. Mortals know only fragments. The elder gods have alien names. They and the Guests have Laws of Existence instead of statistics: invincible, not unstoppable. Their manifestations and heralds can be fought; the gods can be bound by their true names, cut off, put back to sleep or turned back. See The Elder Gods [COS-030] and Elder Gods at the Table [RUL-621].

## The Bloom-shaped species belong to Kaelmar (November 2026)
The Bloom happened in Kaelmar, so the species its spore-storms rewrote are Kaelmar's: the Bloomborn [PPL-010], Centaurs [PPL-015], Harengon [PPL-033], Kenku [PPL-035], Satyrs [PPL-037], Shifters [PPL-039] and Tabaxi [PPL-040]. Each carries the mark "Shaped by the Bloom" (tag origin/bloom). Those also found in Aetheris still appear under Aetheris. The Aarakocra [PPL-011] are left as they were: whether the Bloom shaped them is still disputed.

## Sixteen callings, one for every class (November 2026)
The archive's ten callings stand as written. Six more join them so that every class has a calling: Reaver (barbarian) [CAL-011], Loresinger (bard) [CAL-012], Votary (cleric) [CAL-013], Wildsoul (sorcerer) [CAL-014], Pactbound (warlock) [CAL-015] and Wright (artificer) [CAL-016]. Each calling's page lists the subclasses that fit it, from the Player's Handbook (2024), Xanathar's Guide to Everything, Tasha's Cauldron of Everything and Eberron: Forge of the Artificer. Spelljammer and Monsters of the Multiverse add no classes. Subclasses written for the 2014 rules are marked by book and used with the GM's permission; where the 2024 Player's Handbook reprints one, its version is the one used.

## The Spelljammer species live in the Ethereal Realm (November 2026)
The six species of Spelljammer: Adventures in Space belong to the Ethereal Realm, with their book origins carried into the world: the Astral Plane's silver void is the silver void of the Fields of Lost Realities, and lost homeworlds are worlds the Cycle threw away. Astral Elves [PPL-047] left Aetheris during an Aetherfall to be nearer their gods; Autognomes [PPL-048] are made by Kaelholm's glyph-wright gnomes and sent into the realm; the Giff [PPL-049] come from a discarded world whose forgotten gods drift petrified in the void; the Hadozee [PPL-050] come from Yazir, a forest world adrift in the Fields; Plasmoids [PPL-051] are native to the void and nobody knows how; Thri-kreen [PPL-052] appear on every desert world in the Fields. The Air Genasi are left for later.

## The Ethereal Realm's people and its new species (November 2026)
The realm's people now include its new species. Vantis Orm [NPC-397] is an astral elf and Brakka Twice-Born [NPC-395] a giff; their secrets are unchanged. Six people join them, one of each species: Lady Sennith Velarys [NPC-399] (astral elf), Gauge [NPC-400] (autognome), Major Halloway Stroud [NPC-401] (giff), Captain Chiri Akko [NPC-402] (hadozee), Ooma [NPC-403] (plasmoid) and Tch'kala [NPC-404] (thri-kreen). The archive's own people of the realm keep the species the archive gives them. On the pages of the people added by Worldkeeper, the Calling column names a calling (Mage, Wright, Loresinger and so on) rather than a class.

## The November audit (November 2026)
An audit of people, hooks and loot made these changes. Three people added by Worldkeeper are renamed so that no two people share a name they shouldn't: Little Quell is now Little Hush [NPC-398], Old Pell Ambry is now Old Corwin Ambry [NPC-377], and Scout-Captain Hesk Varn is now Scout-Captain Teague Dunmarsh [NPC-338]. A surname is reused only where the lore means it to be (a family, or Sel Marrow's unexplained tie to the Marrows). Every person's handbook page now gives a class and the name the world knows them by (see Classes first, below); the archive's own records are unchanged. In the treasure pools, items now sit at their rulebook rarity: All-Purpose Tool, +1 is uncommon, Well of Many Worlds is legendary, the Staff of the Woodlands is rare, and the Moon Sickle that has no common version is replaced by Boots of False Tracks.

## Classes first, callings as the world's names (November 2026)
This supersedes the sixteen callings. The handbook is organised by the thirteen classes of the books in use, because players and the GM think in classes. A calling is what the world calls someone, not a class: a fighter is a Warrior, a wizard a Mage, a rogue a Silent Blade. Each class has a page listing every subclass and the names the world uses. The six callings added earlier now name classes: Reaver is the Barbarian [CAL-011], Loresinger the Bard [CAL-012], Votary the Cleric [CAL-013], Wildsoul the Sorcerer [CAL-014], Pactbound the Warlock [CAL-015] and Wright the Artificer [CAL-016]. Druid [CAL-017], Fighter [CAL-018] and Wizard [CAL-019] are added. The archive's ten callings stand as written: Rogue, Ranger, Paladin and Monk are also class pages, and Warrior, Mage, Shaman, Necromancer, Spellblade and Mystic are kept as the world's names. On every person's handbook page, the table gives their Class and what they're Known as; the class comes from the GM's NPC builds, except where it would give away a secret (Harbourmaster Gulen Saltback [NPC-369] and Old Ash [NPC-361] show Unknown).


## Residents of the landmarks, vaults and buildings (November 2026)
Every landmark, vault and building now has someone there: 62 new people (NPC-405 to NPC-466, in assets/lore/residents), one or two to a place, each with a public face and, where it fits, a GM-only secret. Places the handbook calls lost or empty keep that reading: the Titan's Fall, the Nameless Forge, the Shattered Throne, the Purged Halls and the Shadowed Sanctum have residents known only to the GM. Nobody lives inside a sealed vault; each vault has a watcher outside it. Eight faction members move into the fortresses their pages already place them in: Captain Marrek Vaal (the Warlord's Rest), Professor Orin Vale (the Nexus Outpost), Vess the Grey (the Veiled Chamber), Mother Thorn (the Wolf's Den), Knight-Commander Edric Thalmos (the Golden Aerie), Riven the Red (the Red Bastion), Tormek Ironheart (the Anvil of Dominion) and Magistrate Renn Varros (Nightglass).
