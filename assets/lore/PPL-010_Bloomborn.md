# Bloomborn

## Established identity

People of bark, moss, root and fungus. In the first years of the Bloom, the spore-storms rewrote some people into beast-folk. A few they rewrote further, all the way into the Bloom's own grammar. Their descendants are the Bloomborn.

They are people, not the Bloom's moods. They have names, families, grudges and opinions, and they choose for themselves.

**Strengths and flaws:** Patient, hardy and hard to kill. Natural growers and healers. Slow to trust, slower to forget, and they always smell faintly of rain on soil.

## Culture and names

Most Bloomborn live in the open Bloom country and the Eastern Deepwood. Many walk with the Spore Circle. Some work Ashvale's orchards beside the Wrights.

Every power in Kaelmar has an opinion about them. Kaelholm studies them with fascination and suspicion. The Convergence Collective calls them proof that the change can be lived. Plenty of ordinary folk just find them restful company.

Bloomborn grow no husk against the Bloom, since they are already part of it. They feel its moods the way others feel weather.

A very few have crossed to Aetheris, where nobody knows what to make of them.

**Common names:** Alder, Bracken, Corrin, Fennick, Hollin, Rue, Sedge, Tansy, Thistle, Wren

## D&D adaptation

Play a Bloomborn with the firbolg rules from *Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the Multiverse*, or build one with the custom lineage rules in *Tasha's Cauldron of Everything*. They are Humanoids.

## Shared-world relationship

Aetheris and Kaelmar share species. Bloomborn are native to Kaelmar, and almost unknown in Aetheris.
